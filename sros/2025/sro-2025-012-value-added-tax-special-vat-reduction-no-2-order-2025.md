# Value Added Tax (Special VAT Reduction) (No. 2) Order, 2025

SAINT CHRISTOPHER AND NEVIS
STATUTORY RULES AND ORDERS
No. 12 of 2025

Value Added Tax (Special VAT Reduction) (No. 2) Order, 2025.

[Published 9th July 2025, Extra-Ordinary Gazette No. 37 of 2025]
In exercise of the powers conferred by section 27(2) of the Value Added Tax Act,
Cap.18.47 the Minister responsible for Finance makes the following Order:

### 1.

CITATION.
This Order may be cited as the Value Added Tax (Special VAT Reduction) (No. 2)
Order, 2025.

### 2.

INTERPRETATION.
In this Order the term “Act” means the Value Added Tax Act, Cap. 18.47.

### 3.

VAT REDUCTION DAYS.
On Friday the 15th day of August, 2025, Saturday the 16th day of August, 2025,
Friday the 12th day of December, 2025 and Saturday the 20th day of December, 2025, the VAT
rate shall be reduced to a rate of 5% on all sales of vatable goods commencing from 12.01
a.m. to 11:59 p.m. on each day.

### 4.

ELIGIBILITY FOR VAT REDUCTION DAYS.
(1) In order to qualify for the special VAT reduction, all eligible VAT registered
businesses shall submit an application to the Inland Revenue Department, to participate in
the VAT Reduction Days on or before Monday the 11th day of August 2025 for the VAT
Reduction Days of 15th and 16th August, and on or before Monday the 8th day of December
for the VAT Reduction Days of 12th and 20th December 2025.
(2) When the Inland Revenue Department receives the VAT Discounted Day
Application from the registered VAT business, then a response shall be communicated to the
taxpayer within two working days.
(3) The sale or supply of services shall not be eligible for the special rate of VAT
reduction.
(4) The sale or supply of alcoholic beverages, cigarettes and other tobacco products
shall not be eligible for the special rate of VAT reduction.
(5) The sale or supply of firearms and ammunition products shall not be eligible for the
special rate of VAT reduction.

(6) The sale or supply of vehicles shall not be eligible for the special rate of
VAT reduction on 15th and 16th August, 2025.
(7) On Friday 12th December 2025 and Saturday 20th December 2025, the sale or
supply of vehicles shall be eligible for the reduced VAT rate on the first fifty thousand
dollars ($50,000) of the value and VAT will be applicable at the standard rate of 17% on the
remaining balance.

### 5.

INELIGIBILITY OR DISQUALIFICATION.
(1) A VAT registered business that has not filed all of its VAT tax returns or settled all
of its outstanding tax liabilities or made arrangements with the Inland Revenue Department
to do so, prior to each Discounted VAT Rate Day, shall not be eligible to sell goods at the
special rate of 5%.
(2) The provisions of subsection (1) of this section shall also be applicable to a
VAT registered business that has submitted objections but has not satisfied those
objections as it relates to submitting 50% of the tax in dispute.
(3) If a business is subsequently discovered to have had a tax liability with the Inland
Revenue Department subsequent to the communication of acknowledgement referred to in
section 4 of this Order, that business will be denied eligibility for the special rate of VAT and
any sale on that day will be deemed to be VAT inclusive at the standard rate of 17%.

### 6.

REPORTING.
(1) Supplies at the special rate of VAT reduction by eligible VAT registered businesses
should be reported by those businesses on the Value of Reduced Rate 5% Supplies line of
the VAT Return for the August 2025 and December 2025 tax periods.
(2) VAT collected at the special rate of VAT reduction by eligible VAT registered
businesses should be reported by those businesses on the VAT payable on supplies at 5%
line of the VAT Return for the August 2025 and December 2025 tax periods if filing
manually.

### 7.

SPECIAL EXCHANGE RULES.
(1) The special VAT rate of 5% shall only be applicable to the days indicated and shall
not extend to any subsequent exchange of goods or credits for goods bought on those days.
(2) Goods sold on the VAT reduction days, specified in section 3, shall be physically in
stock to qualify for the discounted VAT rate.

### 8.

VAT REDUCTION ON SCHOOL SUPPLIES.
(1) The VAT rate shall be reduced on the importation, sale or supply of eligible school
supplies, for the period Tuesday the 1st day of July 2025 to Tuesday the 30th day of
September 2025.
(2) The VAT rate shall be reduced to a rate of 0%.
(3) Eligible school supplies are set out in the Schedule.
(4) The sale or supply of eligible school supplies by VAT registered businesses shall be
reported by those businesses on the Zero-rated Supplies line of the VAT Return for the
relevant tax period.

(5) VAT registered businesses shall submit a Concession Sales Schedule with their
VAT Return for the relevant tax period, containing details of school supplies sold for each
tax period.
