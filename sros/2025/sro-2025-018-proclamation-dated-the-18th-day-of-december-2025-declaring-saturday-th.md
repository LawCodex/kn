# Proclamation dated the 18th day of December, 2025 declaring Saturday the 27th day of December, 2025 to be a public half holiday in Saint Christopher and Nevis on the occasion of the 2025 National Junior Carnival Parade

SAINT CHRISTOPHER AND NEVIS
STATUTORY RULES AND ORDERS
No. 18 of 2025
Proclamation dated the 18 th day of December, 2025 declaring Saturday the
27 th day of December, 2025 to be a public half holiday in Saint Christopher
and Nevis on the occasion of the 2025 National Junior Carnival Parade.
BY THE GOVERNOR-GENERAL OF
SAINT CHRISTOPHER AND NEVIS
A PROCLAMATION
DAME MARCELLA ALTHEA LIBURD
Governor-General
[Published 24th December 2025, Official Gazette No. 68 of 2025]
WHEREAS it is enacted by section 5 of the Public Holidays Act, Cap. 23.23 that it
shall be lawful for the Governor-General, on the advice of the Cabinet, to issue a proclamation
appointing a special day or part of a day to be reserved as a public holiday in
Saint Christopher and Nevis or in an island, town, district or village thereof:
AND WHEREAS the Governor-General, on the advice of the Cabinet of
Saint Christopher and Nevis considers it expedient that Saturday the 27 th day of
December, 2025 from 12 noon should be observed and kept as a public half holiday in
Saint Christopher and Nevis on the occasion of the 2025 National Junior Carnival Parade
NOW THEREFORE, I, DAME MARCELLAALTHEA LIBURD, Dame Grand Cross
of the Order of Saint Michael and Saint George, Justice of the Peace, Governor-General of the
Federation of Saint Christopher and Nevis do with the advice of the Cabinet, in exercise of
the powers conferred on me by the said section of the said Act, appoint Saturday the
27 th day of December, 2025 to be reserved and kept as a public half holiday in
Saint Christopher and Nevis
AND all our officers and loving subjects in Saint Christopher and Nevis and all others
whom it may concern are hereby required to take due notice hereof and to give their ready
obedience accordingly.

LS

GIVEN under my hand and the Public Seal of
Saint Christopher and Nevis at Government
House, this 18th day of December, 2025 in
the fourth year of His Majesty’s reign.

GOD SAVE THE KING!
