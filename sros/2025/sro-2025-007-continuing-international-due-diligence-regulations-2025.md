# Continuing International Due Diligence Regulations, 2025

SAINT CHRISTOPHER AND NEVIS

STATUTORY RULES AND ORDERS
No. 7 of 2025
CONTINUING INTERNATIONAL DUE DILIGENCE REGULATIONS

In exercise of the power conferred under section 10 of the National Defence Council Act,
Cap 19.20, the Minister makes the following Regulations:
[Published 24 th April 2025 - Official Gazette No.20 of 2025]

### 1.

CITATION.

These Regulations may be cited as the Continuing International Due Diligence
Regulations, 2025.

### 2.

INTERPRETATION.
In these Regulations:
“Act” means the National Defence Council Act, Cap 19.20;
“citizen” means a citizen of Saint Christopher and Nevis, including holders of
citizenship by way of investment pursuant to section 3(5) of the Saint Christopher
and Nevis Citizenship Act, Cap. 1.05;
“CIDD Unit” means the Continuing International Due Diligence Unit established under
these Regulations;
“Director” means the Director of the CIDD Unit;
“Minister” means the Prime Minister;
“Ministry” means the Ministry with responsibility for national security;
“National Security Advisor” means the National Security Advisor appointed pursuant
to section 8 of the Act;
“international agency” includes Interpol, the Central Intelligence Agency of the United
States of America, and any other international law enforcement agency responsible
for disrupting criminal networks through intelligence gathering,
anti-money laundering or anti-terrorist financing.

### 3.

ESTABLISHMENT OF CONTINUING INTERNATIONAL DUE DILIGENCE UNIT.

There is hereby established a Continuing International Due Diligence Unit within the
Ministry responsible for national security.

### 4.

FUNCTIONS OF THE CIDD UNIT.
The CIDD Unit shall have the following functions:

(a) advise, and make recommendations to, the National Security Advisor, on
proposed policies and strategies on national security and on matters
pertaining to national security with respect to Citizens;
(b) coordinate national security policies with the National Security Advisor;
(c) be responsible for gathering and collating information and intelligence on
national security matters involving Citizens;
(d) alert the National Security Advisor of any Citizen investigated for, or charged
with, financial or other crimes internationally;
(e) be responsible for gathering and presenting operational information and
advising the National Security Advisor on the effectiveness of law
enforcement agencies in countering threats to national security with respect to
citizens and on the implementation of relevant laws;
(f) coordinate anti-money laundering and anti-terrorist policies in collaboration
with the National Security Advisor, Financial Intelligence Unit and the White
Collar Crime Unit of the Royal Saint Christopher and Nevis Police Force;
(g) liaise with, and report to, international law enforcement, anti-money laundering
and anti-terrorist financing agencies when Citizens are being investigated for
committing, or have been arrested or charged with, financial or other crimes
internationally;
(h) retrieve passports cancelled under the Passports and Travel Documents Act,
Cap. 6.04; and
(i) develop policies to mitigate and address risks associated with Citizens who
obtained their citizenship via the Saint Christopher & Nevis Citizenship By
Investment Program.

### 5.

DUTIES OF THE DIRECTOR.

(1) The Director of the CIDD Unit shall be appointed by the Minister and shall report
directly to the National Security Advisor.
(2) The Director shall:
(a) manage the operations of the CIDD Unit, including intelligence gathering and
analysis;
(b) prepare and submit reports and recommendations to the National Security
Advisor;
(c) serve on any other committees, boards or authorities that deal with national
security, as directed by the Minister;
(d) represent the Government as directed by the Minister at national, regional and
international meetings whose subject impacts on the national security of
Saint Christopher and Nevis;
(e) stay informed about international financial crime trends and collaborate with
relevant agencies to enhance the unit's strategic approach; and
(f) ensure CIDD Unit compliance with these Regulations.

### 6.

MONITORING AND REPORTING REQUIREMENTS.

(1) The National Security Advisor shall establish protocols for the CIDD Unit, to fulfil
its functions.
(2) All findings, including investigations or charges involving citizens, shall be
reported to the National Security Advisor within three working days of discovery.
(3) The CIDD Unit shall prepare an annual report outlining its activities, findings, and
recommendations.

### 7.

DUE DILIGENCE PROCEDURES.

(1) Citizens who obtained citizenship through the Citizenship by Investment Program
shall be subject to periodic due diligence checks conducted by the CIDD Unit.
(2) The due diligence checks may include:
(a) background checks;
(b) collaboration with international agencies to verify compliance with the laws of
the countries in which they reside and visit, and international laws; and
(c) retrieval of cancelled passports found to be misused or associated with illicit
activities.

### 8.

INTERNATIONAL COLLABORATION.

(1) The CIDD Unit shall maintain partnerships with relevant international agencies to
enhance its capacity to address cross-border financial crimes.
(2) The CIDD Unit may share intelligence and receive information to mitigate risks
posed by Citizens engaged in unlawful activities.

### 9.

OFFENCES AND PENALTIES.

Any person who obstructs the work of the CIDD Unit or provides false
information during investigations commits an offense and is liable on summary conviction to
a fine not exceeding EC$50,000.

### 10.

FORMS.
The Minister may prescribe forms to facilitate the operations and reporting
requirements of the CIDD Unit under these Regulations.
Made the 23rd day of April 2025.
