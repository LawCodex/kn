# Labour (Minimum Wage) Act (Amendment of Schedules) Order, 2025

SAINT CHRISTOPHER AND NEVIS
STATUTORY RULES AND ORDERS
No. 10 of 2025

LABOUR (MINIMUM WAGE) ACT
(AMENDMENT OF SCHEDULES) ORDER, 2025

[Published 3rd July 2025, Extra-Ordinary Gazette No. 36 of 2025]
In exercise of the powers conferred by section 3 of the Labour (Minimum Wage)
Act, Cap.18.19 the Minister on the advice of the Cabinet makes the following Order:

### 1.

Citation.

This Order may be cited as the Labour (Minimum Wage) Act (Amendment of Schedules)
Order, 2025.

### 2.

Interpretation.
In this Order, “Act” means the Labour (Minimum Wage) Act, Cap. 18.19.

### 3.

Amendment of Schedules.

The Act is amended in the Second, Third, Fourth, Fifth, Sixth, Seventh and Eighth
Schedules by replacing the expression, “ten dollars and seventy-five cents per hour”, with
the expression, “twelve dollars and fifty cents per hour”.

Made this 3rd day of July 2025.

MARSHA HENDERSON
Minister responsible for Labour
