# Proclamation dated the 29th day of July 2025, declaring Tuesday the 5th day of August, 2025 to be a public holiday in Saint Christopher and Nevis in recognition of the 51st Year of the Nevis Culturama Festival 2025

SAINT CHRISTOPHER AND NEVIS
STATUTORY RULES AND ORDERS
No. 14 of 2025
Proclamation dated the 29 th day of July 2025, declaring Tuesday the 5th day of August, 2025
to be a public holiday in Saint Christopher and Nevis in recognition of the 51st Year of the
Nevis Culturama Festival 2025.
BY THE GOVERNOR-GENERAL
OF SAINT CHRISTOPHER AND NEVIS
A PROCLAMATION
DAME MARCELLA ALTHEA LIBURD
GCMG, JP
GOVERNOR-GENERAL

[Published 31st July 2025, Official Gazette No. 42 of 2025.]
WHEREAS it is enacted by section 5 of the Public Holidays Act, Cap. 23.23 that it shall be
lawful for the Governor-General, on the advice of the Cabinet, to issue a proclamation appointing a
special day or part of a day to be reserved as a public holiday in Saint Christopher and Nevis or in any
island, town, district or village thereof
AND WHEREAS the Governor-General, on the advice of the Cabinet of Saint Christopher and
Nevis considers it expedient that Tuesday the 5th day of August, 2025 should be observed and kept as
a public holiday in Saint Christopher and Nevis in recognition of the 51st Year of the Nevis Culturama
Festival 2025
NOW, THEREFORE, I, Dame Marcella Althea Liburd, Dame Grand Cross of the Order of
Saint Michael and Saint George, Justice of the Peace, Governor-General of the Federation of
Saint Christopher and Nevis, do with the advice of the Cabinet, in exercise of the powers conferred on
me by the said section of the said Act, appoint Tuesday the 5th day of August, 2025 to be reserved and
kept as a public holiday in Saint Christopher and Nevis
AND all our officers and loving subjects in Saint Christopher and Nevis and all others whom it
may concern are hereby required to take due notice hereof and to give their ready obedience accordingly.

LS

GIVEN under my hand and the Public Seal of
Saint Christopher and Nevis, at Government
House this 29 th day of July 2025, in the
third year of His Majesty’s reign.
GOD SAVE THE KING!
