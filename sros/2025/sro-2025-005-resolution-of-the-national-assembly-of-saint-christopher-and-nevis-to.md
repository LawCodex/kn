# Resolution of the National Assembly of Saint Christopher and Nevis to approve Statutory Rule and Order, No. 4 of 2025 made by the Minister under section 27(2) of the Value Added Tax Act, Cap. 18.47…

SAINT CHRISTOPHER AND NEVIS
STATUTORY RULES AND ORDERS
No. 5 of 2025
Resolution of the National Assembly of Saint Christopher and Nevis to approve Statutory
Rule and Order, No. 4 of 2025 made by the Minister under section 27(2) of the Value
Added Tax Act, Cap. 18.47 in respect of a Special Vat Reduction from the
1st day of January, 2025.

[Published 27th March 2025, Official Gazette No. 15 of 2025]
WHEREAS it is provided by section 27(2) of the Value Added Tax Act, Cap. 18.47
that the Minister with responsibility for Finance may calculate the rates of tax at such other
rate as the Minister may by Order specify and that such order shall be subject to the
Affirmative Resolution of the National Assembly
AND WHEREAS the Minister of Finance considered it expedient to introduce a
measure to create a six-month fiscal stimulus with effect from the 1st day of January, 2025
until the 30th day of June, 2025 and made a pronouncement to that effect during the
Appropriation Address in December 2024
AND WHEREAS by Statutory Rule and Order, No. 4 of 2025 made by the Minister
with responsibility for Finance under section 27(2) of the Value Added Tax Act in respect
of a Special VAT reduction rate of 13% on sales of vatable goods and services ordinarily
subject to the standard rate of 17%, which took effect from the 1st day of January 2025 until
the 30th day of June, 2025
NOW BE IT RESOLVED THEREFORE that the National Assembly of
Saint Christopher and Nevis hereby affirms the Order of the Minister of Finance to reduce
the rate of VAT to a rate of 13% with effect from 1st January, 2025 until 30th June, 2025.
Approved by the National Assembly this 21st day of March, 2025.

TREVLYN STAPLETON
Clerk of the National Assembly
