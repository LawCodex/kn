# Non-Government Organisations (Forms) Regulations, 2025

SAINT CHRISTOPHER AND NEVIS
STATUTORY RULES AND ORDERS
No. 17 of 2025

Non-Government Organisations (Forms) Regulations, 2025

In exercise of the powers conferred by section 22 of the Non-Government Organisations
Act, Cap. 20.59, the Minister makes the following Regulations:
[Published 11th December 2025, Official Gazette No. 66 of 2025]

### 1.

CITATION.

This Regulations may be cited as the Non-Government Organisations (Forms)
Regulations, 2025.

### 2.

INTERPRETATION.
In these Regulations “Act” means the Non-Government Organisations Act, Cap. 20.59.

### 3.

Forms.

The forms set out in the Schedule shall be completed on an annual basis and submitted
to the Registrar of Non-Government Organisations.

## SCHEDULE

COMPILATION INSTRUCTIONS
1\.

In accordance with Section 15 of the Financial Services Regulatory Commission
Non-Government Organisations Act, Cap 20.59., the attached forms should be completed
on an annual basis and submitted to the Registrar of NGOs located at the Financial
Services Regulatory Commission, St. Kitts Branch (FSRC) by September 30th in each
year within four months after the end of the financial year. These forms apply to NGOs
with revenue not exceeding EC$270,000.

2\.

These statements should reflect the accrual basis of accounting.

3\.

All figures should be reported in Eastern Caribbean Dollars and entered as whole
numbers.

### 4.

All cells that have formulae within are password protected. Other cells can have data
entered into them.
