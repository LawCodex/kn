# RESOLUTION OF THE NATIONAL ASSEMBLY TO APPROVE VALUE ADDED TAX (Special VAT Reduction) (No. 2) Order, 2025

SAINT CHRISTOPHER AND NEVIS
STATUTORY RULES AND ORDERS
No. 13 of 2025

RESOLUTION OF THE NATIONAL ASSEMBLY TO APPROVE
VALUE ADDED TAX (Special VAT Reduction) (No. 2) Order, 2025.

[Published 17th July 2025, Official Gazette No. 40 of 2025]
WHEREAS it is provided by section 27(2) of the Value Added Tax Act, Cap. 18.47,
that the Minister may calculate the rates of tax at such other rate as the Minister may by
Order specify and that such order shall be subject to the affirmative resolution of the
National Assembly;
AND WHEREAS the Ministry of Finance has considered it expedient to introduce
certain measures to create a fiscal stimulus and reduce the cost of certain goods for consumers;
AND WHEREAS the Value Added Tax (Special Vat Reduction) (No. 2) Order 2025
(hereinafter “the Order”) was published on the 9th day of July 2025 in Extra-Ordinary Gazette
No. 37 of 2025 assigned Order No. 12 of 2025;
NOW BE IT RESOLVED THEREFORE that the National Assembly of
Saint Christopher and Nevis hereby affirms the Order of the Minister of Finance to reduce
the rate of VAT—
(a) on eligible school supplies as described in the Order from the 1st day of
July 2025 to the 30th day of September 2025 to a rate of 0%; and
(b) on eligible vatable goods as described in the Order on the 15 th day of
August 2025; the 16th day of August 2025; the 12th day of December 2025;
and the 20th day of December 2025; to a rate of 5%.

Approved by the National Assembly this 10th day of July, 2025.

TREVLYN STAPLETON
Clerk of the National Assembly
