# Immigration (Eligibility for Annual Residency) Order, 2025

SAINT CHRISTOPHER AND NEVIS
STATUTORY RULES AND ORDERS
No. 16 of 2025
Immigration (Eligibility for Annual Residency) Order, 2025
In exercise of the powers conferred by section 5 of the Immigration Act, Cap. 6.02,
the Minister makes the following Order—
[Published 28th November 2025, Extra Ordinary Gazette No. 64 of 2025]

### 1.

Citation.

This Order may be cited as the Immigration (Eligibility for Annual Residency)
Order, 2025.

### 2.

Interpretation.
In this Order, “Act” means the Immigration Act, Cap. 6.02.

### 3.

Categories of persons eligible for annual residency.

For the purposes of section 5(2) of the Act, the following categories of persons may
apply for permission to become annual residents of Saint Christopher and Nevis—
(a) a person who—
(i) lawfully entered into Saint Christopher and Nevis;
(ii) lawfully resided in Saint Christopher and Nevis;
(iii) has no criminal record in any jurisdiction; and
(iv) has been granted three consecutive work permits in three consecutive years
immediately preceding the date of application;
(b) a person who—
(i) lawfully entered into Saint Christopher and Nevis;
(ii) lawfully resided in Saint Christopher and Nevis;
(iii) has no criminal record in any jurisdiction; and
(iv) has attended a primary school or secondary school in Saint Christopher
and Nevis recognised by the Ministry of Education for a period of three
consecutive years;
(c) a person who—
(i) lawfully entered into Saint Christopher and Nevis;
(ii) has no criminal record in any jurisdiction; and
(iii) has been residing in Saint Christopher and Nevis for a period of not less
than one calendar year as at the date of his or her application;

provided that such application shall be made on or before the 1st day of May, 2026.

### 4.

Application fee.

An application made under section 3(c) of this Order shall be accompanied by payment
of a non-refundable application fee of two thousand dollars.

### 5.

Preservation of application for persons approaching one year residency.
(1) Notwithstanding section 3(c), a person who—
(a) lawfully entered Saint Christopher and Nevis on or before the 28th day of
November 2025;
(b) has no criminal record in any jurisdiction; and
(c) is capable of meeting the requirements of section 3(c) but has not yet attained
one calendar year of residence at the time of application,

may submit an application for annual residency on or before the 1st day of May, 2026,
accompanied by the non-refundable application fee referred to in section 4.
(2) An application made under subsection (1) shall be deemed to preserve the status of
that person as an applicant under section 3(c), and the Minister shall consider the application
upon that person attaining the period of one calendar year of residence in Saint Christopher
and Nevis.

### 6.

Commencement.
This Order shall come into operation on the 1st day of December 2025.
Made this 28th day of November, 2025.
