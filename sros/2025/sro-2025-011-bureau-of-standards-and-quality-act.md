# Bureau of Standards and Quality Act

SAINT CHRISTOPHER AND NEVIS
STATUTORY RULES AND ORDERS
No. 11 of 2025

Bureau of Standards and Quality Act (Labelling of Goods) (Forms) Regulations, 2025.

In exercise of the powers conferred by section 51(1)(c) and (l) of the Bureau of
Standards and Control Act, Cap. 23.15, the Minister makes the following Regulations.
[Published 10 th July 2025 - Official Gazette No .37 of 2025.]

### 1.

Citation.
These Regulations may be cited as the Bureau of Standards and Quality Act, (Labelling
of Goods) (Forms) Regulations, 2025.

### 2.

Interpretation.
In these Regulations, “Act” means the Bureau of Standards and Quality Act, Cap. 23.15.

### 3.

Forms.

Where a person is desirous of importing goods that qualify as Special Orders into
Saint Christopher and Nevis, that person shall complete and submit to the Bureau the form
set out in the Schedule.

Made this 4th day of July 2025.

Denzil Douglas
Minister responsible for Consumer Affairs
