# Proclamation dated the 23rd day of September, 2025 declaring Thursday the 2nd day of October, 2025 to be a public half holiday in Saint Christopher and Nevis as a mark of respect on the passing of…

SAINT CHRISTOPHER AND NEVIS
STATUTORY RULES AND ORDERS
No. 15 of 2025
Proclamation dated the 23 rd day of September, 2025 declaring Thursday the
2nd day of October, 2025 to be a public half holiday in Saint Christopher and
Nevis as a mark of respect on the passing of Sir Edmund Wickham Lawrence,
GCMG, OBE, CSM, JP former Governor-General of Saint Christopher and Nevis.
BY THE GOVERNOR-GENERAL OF
SAINT CHRISTOPHER AND NEVIS
A PROCLAMATION
DAME MARCELLA A. LIBURD
GOVERNOR-GENERAL

[Published 24th September 2025, Extra-Ordinary Gazette No. 51 of 2025.]
WHEREAS it is enacted by section 5 of the Public Holiday Act, Cap. 23.23 that it shall
be lawful for the Governor-General, on the advice of the Cabinet, to issue a proclamation
appointing a special day or part of a day to be reserved as a public holiday in Saint Christopher
and Nevis or in an island, town, district or village thereof
AND WHEREAS the Governor-General, on the advice of the Cabinet of Saint Christopher
and Nevis considers it expedient that Thursday 2nd day of October, 2025 from 12 noon
should be observed and kept as a public half holiday in Saint Christopher and Nevis as a
mark of respect on the passing of Sir Edmund Wickham Lawrence, GCMG, OBE, CSM, JP
former Governor-General of Saint Christopher and Nevis
NOW THEREFORE, I, Dame Marcella Althea Liburd, Dame Grand Cross of the Order of
Saint Michael and Saint George, Justice of the Peace, Governor-General of the Federation of
Saint Christopher and Nevis, do with the advice of the Cabinet, in exercise of the powers
conferred on me by the said section of the said Act, appoint Thursday 2nd day of October, 2025
to be reserved and kept as a public half holiday in Saint Christopher and Nevis
AND all our officers and loving subjects in Saint Christopher and Nevis and all others
whom it may concern are hereby required to take due notice hereof and to give their ready
obedience accordingly.

LS

GIVEN under my hand and the Public Seal of
Saint Christopher and Nevis at
Government House, this 23rd day of
September 2025, and in the fourth year
of His Majesty’s reign.
GOD SAVE THE KING!
