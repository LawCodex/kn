# Resolution of the National Assembly of Saint Christopher and Nevis to Approve Loan for Expansion of Needsmust Power Plant Project from the Saudi Fund for Development

SAINT CHRISTOPHER AND NEVIS
STATUTORY RULES AND ORDERS
No. 35 of 2024
RESOLUTION OF THE NATIONAL ASSEMBLY OF SAINT CHRISTOPHER
AND NEVIS TO APPROVE LOAN FOR EXPANSION OF NEEDSMUST POWER
PLANT PROJECT FROM THE SAUDI FUND FOR DEVELOPMENT
[Published 26th September 2024, Extra Ordinary Gazette No. 59 of 2024]
WHEREAS it is provided by section 48 of the Finance Administration Act, Cap. 20.13
that money shall not be raised on the credit of the Government except under the authority of
an Act of Parliament or of a Resolution of the National Assembly;
AND WHEREAS the Government, pursuant to its Sustainable Island State Agenda,
has prioritized a project which aims to expand and increase the production capacity of the
current Needsmust Power Plant, by building a dual-fuel power plant with a capacity of
18MW, which will contribute to providing a flexible hybrid power generation platform to
improve efficiency and burn clean fuel to support the Federation’s transition to a sustainable
energy future (“the Expansion of Needsmust Power Plant Project”);
AND WHEREAS the successful implementation of the Expansion of Needsmust Power
Plant Project is anticipated to contribute to lower energy prices, economic growth, and job
creation across the Federation;
AND WHEREAS the Expansion of Needsmust Power Plant Project will contribute
significantly to achieving the United Nations Sustainable Development Goals amd advancing
the Federation’s commitment to a future of clean, affordable and renewable energy in its
transition into a Sustainable Island State;
AND WHEREAS the Government has entered into a Loan Agreement in which the
Saudi Fund for Development has agreed to lend one hundred fifty million (150,000,000)
Saudi Riyals, equivalent to forty million (40,000,000) United States Dollars, to finance the
reasonable cost of the goods and services required for the Expansion of Needsmust Power
Plant Project;
NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED by the National Assembly of
Saint Christopher and Nevis that the raising of forty million (40,000,000) United States
Dollars on the credit of the Government from the Saudi Fund for Development to finance the
reasonable cost of the goods and services required for the carrying out the Expansion of
Needsmust Power Plant Project is hereby authorized.
Approved by the National Assembly this 2nd day of May 2024.
TREVLYN STAPLETON
