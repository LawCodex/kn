# Proclamation dated the 25th day of July 2024, declaring Tuesday the 6th day of August, 2024 to be a public holiday in Saint Christopher and Nevis in recognition of the 50th Year of the Nevis Culturama Festival 2024

SAINT CHRISTOPHER AND NEVIS
STATUTORY RULES AND ORDERS
No. 22 of 2024
Proclamation dated the 25 th day of July 2024, declaring Tuesday the 6th day of August, 2024
to be a public holiday in Saint Christopher and Nevis in recognition of the 50th Year of
the Nevis Culturama Festival 2024.
BY THE GOVERNOR-GENERAL
OF SAINT CHRISTOPHER AND NEVIS
A PROCLAMATION
DAME MARCELLA ALTHEA LIBURD
GCMG, JP
GOVERNOR-GENERAL

[Published 25th July 2024, Extra-Ordinary Gazette No. 43 of 2024.]
WHEREAS it is enacted by section 5 of the Public Holidays Act, Cap. 23.23 that it shall be
lawful for the Governor-General, on the advice of the Cabinet, to issue a proclamation appointing a
special day or part of a day to be reserved as a public holiday in Saint Christopher and Nevis or in any
island, town, district or village thereof
AND WHEREAS the Governor-General, on the advice of the Cabinet of Saint Christopher and
Nevis considers it expedient that Tuesday the 6th day of August, 2024 should be observed and kept as
a public holiday in Saint Christopher and Nevis in recognition of the 50th Year of the Nevis Culturama
Festival 2024
NOW, THEREFORE, I, Dame Marcella Althea Liburd, Dame Grand Cross of the Order of
Saint Michael and Saint George, Justice of the Peace, Governor-General of Saint Christopher and
Nevis, do with the advice of the Cabinet, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by the said section
of the said Act, appoint Tuesday the 6th day of August, 2024 to be reserved and kept as a public
holiday in Saint Christopher and Nevis
AND all our officers and loving subjects in Saint Christopher and Nevis and all others whom it
may concern are hereby required to take due notice hereof and to give their ready obedience accordingly.

LS

GIVEN under my hand and the Public Seal of
Saint Christopher and Nevis, at Government
House this 25 th day of July 2024, in the
second year of His Majesty’s reign.
GOD SAVE THE KING!
