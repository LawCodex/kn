# Value Added Tax (Special VAT Reduction) Order, 2024

SAINT CHRISTOPHER AND NEVIS
STATUTORY RULES AND ORDERS
No. 8 of 2024
Value Added Tax (Special VAT Reduction) Order, 2024.

In exercise of the powers conferred on him by section 27 (2) of the Value Added Tax Act,
Cap 18.47, the Minister responsible for Finance makes the following Order:
[Published 11th April 2024, Extra Ordinary Gazette No. 19 of 2024]

### 1.

CITATION.
This Order may be cited as the Value Added Tax (Special VAT Reduction) Order, 2024.

### 2.

INTERPRETATION.
In this Order, “Act” means the Value Added Tax Act, Cap. 18.47.

### 3.

VAT REDUCTION DAY.

On Friday the 12th day of April 2024, the VAT rate shall be reduced to a rate of 5% on
all sales of vatable goods commencing from 12.01 a.m. to 11:59 p.m. on that day.

### 4.

ELIGIBILITY FOR VAT REDUCTION DAY.

(1) In order to qualify for the special VAT reduction, all eligible VAT registered
businesses shall submit an application to the Inland Revenue Department, to participate in
the VAT Reduction Day on or before Wednesday the 10th day of April 2024.
(2) When the Inland Revenue Department receives the VAT Discounted Day Application
from the registered VAT business, then a response shall be communicated to the taxpayer
within two working days.
(3) The sale or supply of services shall not be eligible for the special rate of VAT
reduction.
(4) The sale or supply of alcoholic beverages, cigarettes and other tobacco products
shall not be eligible for the special rate of VAT reduction.
(5) The sale or supply of firearms and ammunition products shall not be eligible for
the special rate of VAT reduction.
(6) The sale or supply of vehicles shall not be eligible for the special rate of VAT
reduction.

### 5.

INELIGIBILITY OR DISQUALIFICATION.

(1) A VAT registered business that has not filed all of its VAT tax returns or settled all
of its outstanding tax liabilities or made arrangements with the Inland Revenue Department
to do so, prior to the Discounted VAT Rate Day, shall not be eligible to sell goods at the
special rate of 5%.
(2) The provisions of subsection (1) of this section shall also be applicable to a VAT
registered business that has submitted objections but has not satisfied those objections as
it relates to submitting 50% of the tax in dispute.
(3) If a business is subsequently discovered to have had a tax liability with the Inland
Revenue Department subsequent to the communication of acknowledgement referred to in
section 4 of this Order, that business will be denied eligibility for the special rate of VAT
and any sale on that day will be deemed to be VAT inclusive at the standard rate of 17%.

### 6.

REPORTING.

(1) Supplies at the special rate of VAT reduction by eligible VAT registered businesses
should be reported by those businesses on line (Value of Supplies at 5%) of the VAT Return
for the April 2024 tax period.
(2) VAT collected at the special rate of VAT reduction by eligible VAT registered
businesses should be reported by those businesses on line (VAT payable on supplies at 5%)
of the VAT Return for the April 2024 tax period.

### 7.

SPECIAL EXCHANGE RULES.

The special VAT rate of 5% shall only be applicable to the day indicated and shall not
extend to any subsequent exchange of goods or credits for goods bought on those days.
Made this 11th day of April, 2024.
