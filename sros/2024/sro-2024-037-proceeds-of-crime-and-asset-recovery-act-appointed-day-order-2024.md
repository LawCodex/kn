# Proceeds of Crime and Asset Recovery Act (Appointed Day) Order, 2024

SAINT CHRISTOPHER AND NEVIS
–––––––––––––
STATUTORY RULES AND ORDERS
No. 37 of 2024
–––––––––––––
Proceeds of Crime and Asset Recovery (Appointed Day) Order, 2024
–––––––––––––
In exercise of the powers conferred by section 229 of the Proceeds of Crime and
Asset Recovery Act, No. 9 of 2020, the Minister makes the following Order:
[Published 1st October 2024, Extra-Ordinary Gazette No. 60 of 2024]

### 1.

Citation.
This Order may be cited as the Proceeds of Crime and Asset Recovery Act
(Appointed Day) Order, 2024.

### 2.

Interpretation.
In this Order, “Act” means the Proceeds of Crime and Asset Recovery Act,
No. 9 of 2020.

### 3.

Appointed Day for Act No. 9 of 2020.
The Act shall come into operation on the 1st day of October, 2024.

Made this 30th day of September, 2024.

GARTH WILKIN
Attorney General and
Minister responsible for Legal Affairs
