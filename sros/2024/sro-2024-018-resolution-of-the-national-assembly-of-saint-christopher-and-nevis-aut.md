# Resolution of the National Assembly of Saint Christopher and Nevis Authorising Guarantee for the National Housing Corporation Climate Smart Homes Programme

SAINT CHRISTOPHER AND NEVIS
STATUTORY RULES AND ORDERS
No. 18 of 2024

RESOLUTION OF THE NATIONAL ASSEMBLY OF
SAINT CHRISTOPHER AND NEVIS AUTHORISING GUARANTEE
FOR THE NATIONAL HOUSING CORPORATION
CLIMATE SMART HOMES PROGRAMME
[Published 27th June 2024, Official Gazette No. 33 of 2024]
WHEREAS it is provided by section 55(b) of the Finance Administration Act,
Cap. 20.13 that a guarantee of a financial liability shall not be given by the Government
unless it is authorised by resolution of the National Assembly;
AND WHEREAS the Government, on April 28, 2023, became a party to an
Agreement with East Coast Housing Development Limited (“East Coast Housing”) and the
National Housing Corporation in which East Coast Housing, as the developer, is required to
construct up to 2,400 low-income Climate Smart Homes over a four-year period utilizing the
modern and efficient Creteform reinforced concrete construction solution by training and
engaging resident contractors and construction workers (“the Project”);
AND WHEREAS the Agreement requires East Coast Housing to sell the Climate Smart
Homes to residents of St Kitts for fixed prices of EC$111,375.00 for a one bedroom home
(450 square feet); EC$213,345.00 for a two bedroom home (862 square feet); and
EC$297,000.00 for a three bedroom home (1,200 square feet), inclusive of full size
energy-efficient refrigerator; four burner gas stove; and solar water heater;
AND WHEREAS the National Housing Corporation and the Government are satisfied
that the two model Climate Smart Homes developed by East Coast Housing, constructed by
resident contractors and construction workers, and delivered free of cost to two deserving
families in Conaree and Taylors, were built in a good workmanlike manner, meeting the
standard of construction in accordance with the Building Code of St. Christopher and Nevis
and were completed in accordance with the Construction Documents, as certified by the
issue of a certificate of completion by the National Housing Corporation;
AND WHEREAS 1,032 resident landowners of St. Kitts have already applied to the
National Housing Corporation to purchase a Climate Smart Home to be constructed on their
lands and 169 of those resident landowners have already qualified for mortgage loans through
local financial institutions or have proven that they have the cash available to purchase a
Climate Smart Home;
AND WHEREAS East Coast Housing has begun preparing 50 resident landowner lots
for construction of the Climate Smart Homes in the first phase of construction of the Climate
Smart Homes;

NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED by the National Assembly of Saint Christopher
and Nevis that the Government hereby guarantees the sum of Ten Million Two Hundred and
Sixty Thousand United States Dollars (US$10,260,000), representing a guarantee of the
sums to be contractually payable by the National Housing Corporation to East Coast Housing
to construct the first phase of Climate Smart Homes on lands owned by resident landowners
of St. Kitts who have been approved by the National Housing Corporation.
Approved by the National Assembly this 14th day of June 2024.

TREVLYN STAPLETON
