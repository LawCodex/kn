# Social Security (Benefits) (Amendment) Regulations, 2024

SAINT CHRISTOPHER AND NEVIS
STATUTORY RULES AND ORDERS
No. 3 of 2024

SOCIAL SECURITY (BENEFITS) (AMENDMENT) REGULATIONS, 2024.

In exercise of the powers conferred by section 27 of the Social Security Act, Cap. 22.10,
the Minister makes the following Regulations
[Published 18th January 2024, Official Gazette No. 5 of 2024]

### 1.

Citation and commencement.

This Order may be cited as the Social Security (Benefits) (Amendment)
Regulations, 2024 and shall come into force on the 1st day of January, 2024.

### 2.

Interpretation.

In these Regulations, “principal Regulations” means the Social Security (Benefits)
Regulations.

### 3.

Amendment of Schedule 6 to the Regulations.

Schedule 6 to the Regulations is amended in the table entitled “Table of Funeral Grant
to Insured Person or Spouse of the Insured”, immediately following the row “1997” and
“2500.00”, by adding the following row:
