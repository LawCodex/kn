# Proclamation dated the 27th day of May 2024, declaring Friday the 31st day of May, 2024 to be observed as Centenarians Day in Saint Christopher and Nevis

SAINT CHRISTOPHER AND NEVIS
STATUTORY RULES AND ORDERS
No. 13 of 2024

Proclamation dated the 27th day of May 2024, declaring Friday the 31st day of
May, 2024 to be observed as Centenarians Day in Saint Christopher and Nevis

BY THE GOVERNOR-GENERAL OF
SAINT CHRISTOPHER AND NEVIS
A PROCLAMATION
DAME MARCELLA A. LIBURD
GOVERNOR-GENERAL

[Published 30th May 2024, Official Gazette No. 28 of 2024.]
WHEREAS it is proposed to observe annually the commemoration of a Day to
celebrate the lives and contribution of our Centenarians
AND WHEREAS it is proposed to observe Friday 31st May, 2024 as Centenarians
Day to recognize our Centenarians in the Federation of Saint Christopher and Nevis
NOW THEREFORE, I, Dame Marcella Althea Liburd, Dame Grand Cross of the Order
of Saint Michael and Saint George, Justice of the Peace, Governor-General of the Federation
of Saint Christopher and Nevis, in exercise of all powers me thereunto enabling and in
accordance with the advice of the Cabinet DO HEREBY declare that Friday
31st May, 2024 be observed as Centenarians Day
AND all our officers and loving subjects in Saint Christopher and Nevis and all others
whom it may concern are hereby required to take due notice hereof and to give their ready
obedience accordingly.

LS

GIVEN under my hand and the Public Seal of
Saint Christopher and Nevis at
Government House, this 27th day of
May 2024, and in the second year of
His Majesty’s reign.

GOD SAVE THE KING!
