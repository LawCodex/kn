# Resolution of the National Assembly of Saint Christopher and Nevis to Approve Statutory Rule and Order No. 8 of 2024 Made by

SAINT CHRISTOPHER AND NEVIS
STATUTORY RULES AND ORDERS
No. 23 of 2024

RESOLUTION OF THE NATIONAL ASSEMBLY OF SAINT CHRISTOPHER AND
NEVIS TO APPROVE STATUTORY RULE AND ORDER NO. 8 OF 2024 MADE BY
THE MINISTER UNDER SECTION 27(2) OF THE VALUE ADDED TAX ACT IN
RESPECT OF A SPECIAL VAT REDUCTION ON THE 12TH DAY OF APRIL, 2024

[Published 1st August 2024, Extra-Ordinary Gazette No.46 of 2024]

WHEREAS it is provided by section 27(2) of the Value Added Tax Act that the Minister
with responsibility for finance may calculate the rates of tax at such other rate as the Minister
may by Order specify and that such order shall be subject to the Affirmative Resolution of
the National Assembly;
AND WHEREAS the Ministry of Finance considered it expedient to introduce a
measure to create a fiscal stimulus on the 12th day of April, 2024;
AND WHEREAS by Order No. 8 of 2024 made by the Minister with responsibility
for finance under section 27(2) of the Value Added Tax in respect of a Special VAT reduction
on the 12th day of April 2024 to a rate of 5% on sales of vatable goods other than those
specifically listed therein as being ineligible;
NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED that the National Assembly of
Saint Christopher and Nevis hereby affirms the Order of the Minister of Finance to reduce
the rate of VAT on the 12th day of April, 2024 to a rate of 5%.
Approved by the National Assembly this 2nd day of May 2024.

TREVLYN STAPLETON
Clerk of the National Assembly
