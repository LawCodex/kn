# Resolution of the National Assembly of Saint Christopher and Nevis Approving Recommendations of Salaries Review Commission

SAINT CHRISTOPHER AND NEVIS
STATUTORY RULES AND ORDERS
No. 7 of 2024
RESOLUTION OF THE NATIONAL ASSEMBLY
OF SAINT CHRISTOPHER AND NEVIS APPROVING
RECOMMENDATIONS OF SALARIES REVIEW COMMISSION

[Published 7th March 2024, Official Gazette No. 12 of 2024]
WHEREAS the Salaries Review Commission was established pursuant to the Salaries
Review Commission Act, Cap. 22.14 to review the salaries and other conditions of service of
the Governor-General, Members of the National Assembly, including the Speaker of the
National Assembly, the Deputy Speaker of the National Assembly, Prime Minister, Ministers
of Government, Attorney General and the Leader of the Opposition (“Senior Parliamentary
Officials”) every three years, and to make recommendations to the Government accordingly;
AND WHEREAS the Salaries Review Commission prepared a report dated September
30, 2019 in which certain recommendations were made for adjustments of salaries and other
conditions of service for Senior Parliamentary Officials based on a thorough consultative
process of analysis, economic considerations, and comparisons with other jurisdictions;
AND WHEREAS the last increase in salaries and other conditions of service for
Senior Parliamentary Officials occurred in 2005;
AND WHEREAS on the contrary, since 2005, salaries for public servants increased by
7.5% in 2008; 4% in 2014; 3% in 2015; 3% in 2016; 10% in 2022 and 8% in 2024, being an
effective total rate of increase of 41%;
AND WHEREAS there is now an anomaly, in that public servants at the salary scales of
K44 to K47 receive salaries of up to 24% more than Ministers of Government;
NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED by the National Assembly of Saint Christopher
and Nevis that the National Assembly accepts the recommendations of the Salaries Review
Commission with respect to salaries and other conditions of service for Senior Parliamentary
Officials, with effect from March 1, 2024.
Approved by the National Assembly this 22nd day of February 2024.

TREVLYN STAPLETON
Clerk of the National Assembly
