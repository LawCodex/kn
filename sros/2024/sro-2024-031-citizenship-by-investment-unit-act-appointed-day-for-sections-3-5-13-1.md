# Citizenship by Investment Unit Act (Appointed Day for Sections 3-5, 13-15, and 18-26) Order, 2024

SAINT CHRISTOPHER AND NEVIS
STATUTORY RULES AND ORDERS
No. 31 of 2024
Citizenship by Investment Unit Act (Appointed Day for Sections 3-5, 13-15
and 18-26) Order, 2024

In exercise of the powers conferred by section 1 of the Citizenship by Investment Unit
Act, 2024, the Minister makes the following Order:
[Published 6th September 2024, Extra-Ordinary Gazette No. 53 of 2024]

### 1.

Citation.

This Order may be cited as the Citizenship by Investment Unit Act (Appointed Day for
Sections 3-5, 13-15, and 18-26) Order, 2024.

### 2.

Interpretation.
In this Order, “Act” means the Citizenship by Investment Unit Act, 2024.

### 3.

Appointed Day for various sections of Act No. 11 of 2024.

All of the remaining provisions of the Act that were not brought into force by the
Citizenship by Investment Unit (Appointed Day for Sections 1-2, 6-12, 16-17 and 27-28)
Order including sections 3, 4, 5, 13, 14, 15, 18, 19, 20, 21, 22, 23, 24, 25, 26 and the
Schedules of the Act shall come into operation on the 6th day of September, 2024.
Made this 6th day of September, 2024.
TERRANCE DREW
Minister responsible for Citizenship
