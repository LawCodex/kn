# Resolution of the National Assembly of Saint Christopher and Nevis to approve the Order, No. 23 of 2024 made by the Minister under section 27(2) of the Value Added Tax Act in respect of a Special VAT…

SAINT CHRISTOPHER AND NEVIS
STATUTORY RULES AND ORDERS
No. 34 of 2024
RESOLUTION
Resolution of the National Assembly of Saint Christopher and Nevis to approve
the Order, No. 23 of 2024 made by the Minister under section 27(2) of the Value Added
Tax Act in respect of a Special VAT reduction on school supplies and air cooling devices
from the 1st day of July 2024 to the 30th day of September, 2024 to a rate of 0%, on the
16th and 17th of August, 2024, and on the 13th and 21st of December, 2024 to a rate of 5%.

[Published 26th September 2024, Extra Ordinary Gazette No. 59 of 2024]
WHEREAS it is provided by section 27(2) of the Value Added Tax Act, Cap. 18.47
that the Minister may calculate the rates of tax at such other rate as the Minister may by
Order specify and that such order shall be subject to the Affirmative Resolution of the
National Assembly;
AND WHEREAS the Ministry of Finance considers it expedient to introduce a measure
to create a fiscal stimulus;
NOW BE IT RESOLVED THEREFORE, that the National Assembly of
Saint Christopher and Nevis hereby affirms the Order of the Minister of Finance to reduce
the rate of VAT
(a) on school supplies and air conditioning devices from the 1st day of July, 2024
to the 30th day of September 2024 to a rate of 0%;
(b) on 16th August and 17th August 2024, to a rate of 5%; and
(c) on 13th and 21st of December 2024, to a rate of 5%.
Approved by the National Assembly this 18th day of September 2024.

TREVLYN STAPLETON
Clerk of the National Assembly
