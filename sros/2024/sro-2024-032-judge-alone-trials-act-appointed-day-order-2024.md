# Judge Alone Trials Act (Appointed Day) Order, 2024

SAINT CHRISTOPHER AND NEVIS
STATUTORY RULES AND ORDERS
No. 32 of 2024
Judge Alone Trials Act (Appointed Day) Order, 2024

In exercise of the powers conferred by section 1 of the Judge Alone Trials Act, 2024, the
Minister makes the following Order:
[Published 20th September 2024, Extra-Ordinary Gazette No. 56 of 2024]

### 1.

Citation.
This Order may be cited as the Judge Alone Trials Act (Appointed Day) Order, 2024.

### 2.

Interpretation.
In this Order, “Act” means the Judge Alone Trials Act, No. 19 of 2024.

### 3.

Appointed Day for Act No. 19 of 2024
The Act shall come into operation on the 20th day of September, 2024.
Made this 20th day of September, 2024.

GARTH WILKIN
Minister responsible for Legal Affairs
