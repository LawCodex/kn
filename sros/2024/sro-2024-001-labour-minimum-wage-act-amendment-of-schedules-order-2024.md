# Labour (Minimum Wage) Act (Amendment of Schedules) Order, 2024

SAINT CHRISTOPHER AND NEVIS
STATUTORY RULES AND ORDERS
No. 1 of 2024
LABOUR (MINIMUM WAGE) ACT (AMENDMENT OF SCHEDULES)
ORDER, 2024.

In exercise of the powers conferred by section 3 of the Labour (Minimum Wage) Act,
Cap. 18.19, the Minister on the advice of the Cabinet makes the following Order:
[Published 9th January 2024, Extra-Ordinary Gazette No. 2 of 2024]

### 1.

Citation and commencement.

This Order may be cited as the Labour (Minimum Wage) Act (Amendment of Schedules)
Order, 2024 and shall come into force on the 10th day of January, 2024.

### 2.

Interpretation.
In this Order, “Act” means the Labour (Minimum Wage) Act, Cap. 18.19.

### 3.

Amendment of Schedules.
The Act is amended
(a) in paragraph 2 of the Second, Third, and Sixth, Schedules, by deleting that
paragraph;
(b) in paragraph 3 of the Fourth and Seventh Schedules, by deleting that paragraph;
(c) in paragraphs 3 and 4 of the Fifth Schedule, by deleting the paragraphs;
(d) in the Eighth Schedule, by replacing paragraph 2 with the following
“National minimum wage
2\. The national minimum wage to be paid to a worker shall be ten dollars
and seventy-five cents per hour.”.
Made this 9th day of January, 2024.
MARSHA HENDERSON
Minister responsible for Labour
