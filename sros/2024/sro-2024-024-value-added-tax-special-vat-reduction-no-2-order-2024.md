# Value Added Tax (Special VAT Reduction) (No. 2) Order, 2024

SAINT CHRISTOPHER AND NEVIS
STATUTORY RULES AND ORDERS
No. 24 of 2024
Value Added Tax (Special VAT Reduction) (No. 2) Order, 2024.

In exercise of the powers conferred on him by section 27 (2) of the Value Added Tax Act,
Cap 18.47, the Minister responsible for Finance makes the following Order:
[Published 1st August 2024, Extra Ordinary Gazette No. 46 of 2024]

### 1.

CITATION.

This Order may be cited as the Value Added Tax (Special VAT Reduction) (No. 2)
Order, 2024.

### 2.

INTERPRETATION.
In this Order, “Act” means the Value Added Tax Act, Cap. 18.47.

### 3.

VAT REDUCTION DAYS.

On Friday the 16th day of August, 2024, Saturday the 17th day of August, 2024,
Friday the 13th day of December, 2024 and Saturday the 21st day of December, 2024, the
VAT rate shall be reduced to a rate of 5% on all sales of vatable goods commencing from
12.01 a.m. to 11:59 p.m. on each day.

### 4.

ELIGIBILITY FOR VAT REDUCTION DAYS.

(1) In order to qualify for the special VAT reduction, all eligible VAT registered
businesses shall submit an application to the Inland Revenue Department, to participate in
the VAT Reduction Days on or before Monday the 12th day of August 2024 for the VAT
Reduction Days of 16th and 17th August, and on or before Monday the 9th day of December
for the VAT Reduction Days of 13th and 21st December 2024.
(2) When the Inland Revenue Department receives the VAT Discounted Day Application
from the registered VAT business, then a response shall be communicated to the taxpayer
within two working days.
(3) The sale or supply of services shall not be eligible for the special rate of VAT
reduction.
(4) The sale or supply of alcoholic beverages, cigarettes and other tobacco products
shall not be eligible for the special rate of VAT reduction.
(5) The sale or supply of firearms and ammunition products shall not be eligible for
the special rate of VAT reduction.

(6) The sale or supply of vehicles shall not be eligible for the special rate of VAT
reduction on 16th and 17th August, 2024.
(7) Subject to subparagraph (6) the sale or supply of vehicles shall be eligible for the
reduced VAT rate on the first fifty thousand dollars ($50,000) of the value and VAT will be
applicable at the standard rate of 17% on the remaining balance.

### 5.

INELIGIBILITY OR DISQUALIFICATION.

(1) A VAT registered business that has not filed all of its VAT tax returns or settled all
of its outstanding tax liabilities or made arrangements with the Inland Revenue Department
to do so, prior to the Discounted VAT Rate Day, shall not be eligible to sell goods at the
special rate of 5%.
(2) The provisions of subparagraph (1) of this section shall also be applicable to a
VAT registered business that has submitted objections but has not satisfied those objections
as it relates to submitting 50% of the tax in dispute.
(3) If a business is subsequently discovered to have had a tax liability with the Inland
Revenue Department subsequent to the communication of acknowledgement referred to in
section 4 of this Order, that business will be denied eligibility for the special rate of VAT
and any sale on that day will be deemed to be VAT inclusive at the standard rate of 17%.

### 6.

REPORTING.

(1) Supplies at the special rate of VAT reduction by eligible VAT registered businesses
should be reported by those businesses on the Value of Reduced Rate at 5% Supplies line
of the VAT Return for August 2024 and December 2024 tax periods.
(2) VAT collected at the special rate of VAT reduction by eligible VAT registered
businesses should be reported by those businesses on the VAT payable on supplies at 5%
line of the VAT Return for August 2024 and December 2024 tax periods.

### 7.

SPECIAL EXCHANGE RULES.

The special VAT rate of 5% shall only be applicable to the days indicated and shall not
extend to any subsequent exchange of goods or credits for goods bought on those days.

### 8.

VAT REDUCTION ON SCHOOL SUPPLIES AND AIR-COOLING DEVICES.

(1) The VAT rate shall be reduced on the importation, sale or supply of eligible school
supplies and air-cooling devices, for the period Monday the 1 st day of July 2024 to
Monday the 30th day of September 2024.
(2) The VAT rate shall be reduced to a rate of 0%.
(3) Eligible school supplies and air-cooling devices are set out in Schedule 1.
(4) The sale or supply of eligible school supplies and air-cooling devices by VAT
registered businesses shall be reported by those businesses on the Zero-rated Supplies line
of the VAT Return for the relevant tax period.
(5) VAT registered businesses shall submit a Concession Sales Schedule with their
VAT Return for the relevant tax period, containing details of school supplies and air-cooling
devices sold for each tax period.
