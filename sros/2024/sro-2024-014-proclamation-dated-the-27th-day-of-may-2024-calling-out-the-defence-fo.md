# Proclamation dated the 27th day of May 2024, calling out the Defence Force for service in aid of the civil powers for a period not exceeding six (6) months

SAINT CHRISTOPHER AND NEVIS
STATUTORY RULES AND ORDERS
No. 14 of 2024
Proclamation dated the 27 th day of May 2024, calling out the Defence Force for service in aid
of the civil powers for a period not exceeding six (6) months
BY THE GOVERNOR-GENERAL
OF SAINT CHRISTOPHER AND NEVIS
A PROCLAMATION
DAME MARCELLA ALTHEA LIBURD
GCMG, JP
GOVERNOR-GENERAL

[Published 30th May 2024, Official Gazette No. 28 of 2024.]
WHEREAS by virtue of the provisions of section 7(1) of the Saint Christopher and Nevis
Defence Force Act, Cap. 19.14 of the Laws of Saint Christopher and Nevis the
Governor-General may, acting in accordance with the advice of Cabinet, call out the Defence Force for
service in aid of the civil powers for a period not exceeding six (6) months, with their arms and
ammunition, where it is deemed that the resources of the civil powers need to be augmented
AND WHEREAS it is further provided by section 7(5) that a member of the Defence Force
called out for services under the provisions of section 7(1) shall have and enjoy all the powers,
authorities and immunities which are vested in a police constable by law
AND WHEREAS the Cabinet at its meeting on the 27th day of May 2024, advised that the
provisions of section 7 of the Saint Christopher and Nevis Defence Force Act should be implemented
NOW, THEREFORE, I, Dame Marcella Althea Liburd, Dame Grand Cross of the Order of
Saint Michael and Saint George, Justice of the Peace, Governor-General of Saint Christopher and
Nevis, DO HEREBY acting with the advice of the Cabinet, call out the Defence Force for service in aid
of the civil powers, with their arms and ammunition, with effect from the 1st day of June, 2024 for a
period not exceeding six (6) months
AND all our officers and loving subjects in Saint Christopher and Nevis and all others whom it
may concern are hereby required to take due notice hereof and to give their ready obedience accordingly.

LS

GIVEN under my hand and the Public Seal of
Saint Christopher and Nevis, at Government
House this 27 th day of May 2024, in the
second year of His Majesty’s reign.
GOD SAVE THE KING!
