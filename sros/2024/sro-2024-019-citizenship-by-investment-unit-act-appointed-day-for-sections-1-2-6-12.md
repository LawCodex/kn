# Citizenship by Investment Unit Act (Appointed Day for Sections 1-2, 6-12, 16-17 and 27-28) Order, 2024

SAINT CHRISTOPHER AND NEVIS
STATUTORY RULES AND ORDERS
No. 19 of 2024
Citizenship by Investment Unit Act (Appointed Day for Sections 1-2, 6-12,
16-17 and 27-28) Order, 2024

In exercise of the powers conferred by section 1 of the Citizenship by Investment Unit Act,
2024, the Minister makes the following Order:
[Published 28th June 2024, Extra-Ordinary Gazette No. 34 of 2024]

### 1.

Citation.

This Order may be cited as the Citizenship by Investment Unit Act (Appointed Day for
Sections 1-2, 6-12, 16-17 and 27-28) Order, 2024.

### 2.

Interpretation.
In this Order, “Act” means the Citizenship by Investment Unit Act, 2024.

### 3.

Appointed Day for various sections of Act No. 11 of 2024.
Sections 1, 2, 6, 7, 8, 9, 10, 11, 12, 16, 17, 27 and 28 of the Act shall come into operation

on the 1st day of July, 2024.

Made this 28th day of June, 2024.
TERRANCE DREW
Minister responsible for Citizenship
