# Voluntary Bill of Indictment Act (Appointed Day) Order, 2024

SAINT CHRISTOPHER AND NEVIS
STATUTORY RULES AND ORDERS
No. 33 of 2024
Voluntary Bill of Indictment Act (Appointed Day) Order, 2024

In exercise of the powers conferred by section 1 of the Voluntary Bill of Indictment
Act, 2024, the Minister makes the following Order:
[Published 20th September 2024, Extra-Ordinary Gazette No. 56 of 2024]

### 1.

Citation.

This Order may be cited as the Voluntary Bill of Indictment Act (Appointed Day)
Order, 2024.

### 2.

Interpretation.
In this Order, “Act” means the Voluntary Bill of Indictment Act, No. 20 of 2024.

### 3.

Appointed Day for Act No. 20 of 2024
The Act shall come into operation on the 20th day of September, 2024.
Made this 20th day of September, 2024.

GARTH WILKIN
Minister responsible for Legal Affairs
