# Courts of Justice Fees Act (Appointed Day) Order, 2019

SAINT CHRISTOPHER AND NEVIS
STATUTORY RULES AND ORDERS
No. 29 of 2019
Courts of Justice Fees Act (Appointed Day) Order.

In exercise of the power conferred by section 1 of the Courts of Justice Fees Act,
No. 11 of 2019, the Attorney General makes the following Order:
[Published 20th September 2019, Extra Ordinary Gazette No. 48 of 2019]

### 1.

Citation.
This Order may be cited as the Courts of Justice Fees Act (Appointed Day) Order, 2019.

### 2.

Appointed Day.

The Courts of Justice Fees Act, No. 11 of 2019 shall come into operation on the
20th day of September, 2019.

Made this 20th day of September, 2019.

VINCENT BYRON
Attorney General
