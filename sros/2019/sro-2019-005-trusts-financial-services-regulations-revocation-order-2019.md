# Trusts (Financial Services) (Regulations) (Revocation) Order, 2019

SAINT CHRISTOPHER AND NEVIS
STATUTORY RULES AND ORDERS
No. 5 of 2019
Trusts (Financial Services) (Regulations) (Revocation) Order, 2019.

In exercise of the powers conferred by section 105 of the Trusts Act, Cap. 5.19,
the Minister makes the following Order:
[Published 24h January 2019, Extra Ordinary Gazette No. 5 of 2019]

### 1.

Citation.

This Order may be cited as the Trusts (Financial Services) (Regulations) (Revocation)
Order, 2019.

### 2.

Revocation of Financial Services (Regulations).
The Financial Services (Regulations) Order as set out in the First Schedule is revoked.
Made this 12th day of January, 2019.

TIMOTHY HARRIS
Minister responsible for Finance

GOVERNMENT PRINTERY
ST KITTS W I
