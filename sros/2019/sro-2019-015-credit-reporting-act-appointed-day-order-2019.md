# Credit Reporting Act (Appointed Day) Order, 2019

SAINT CHRISTOPHER AND NEVIS
STATUTORY RULES AND ORDERS
No. 15 of 2019

Credit Reporting Act (Appointed Day) Order.

[Published 20th February 2019, Extra Ordinary Gazette No. 9 of 2019]
In exercise of the powers conferred by section 1 of the Credit Reporting Act,
No. 11 of 2018, the Minister makes the following Order:

### 1.

Citation.
This Order may be cited as the Credit Reporting Act (Appointed Day) Order, 2019.

### 2.

Appointed Day.

The Credit Reporting Act, No. 11 of 2018 shall come into operation on the
21st day of February 2019.
Made the 19th day of February 2019.

TIMOTHY HARRIS
Minister responsible for Finance

GOVERNMENT PRINTERY
ST KITTS W I
