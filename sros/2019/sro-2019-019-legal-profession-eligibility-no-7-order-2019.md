# Legal Profession (Eligibility) (No. 7) Order, 2019

SAINT CHRISTOPHER
AND NEVIS

STATUTORY RULES AND ORDERS
No. 19 of 2019
Legal Profession (Eligibility) (No. 7) Order, 2019
In exercise of the powers conferred by section 16 of the Legal Profession Act,
No. 33 of 2008, the Minister makes the following Order:
[Published 16th May 2019 – Official Gazette No. 25 of 2019]

### 1.

Citation.
This Order may be cited as the Legal Profession (Eligibility) (No. 7) Order, 2019.

### 2.

Interpretation.
In this Order, “Act” means the Legal Profession Act, No. 33 of 2008.

### 3.

Eligibility.
Pursuant to the provisions of section 16 of the Act, Mr. Charles Dominic Kimmins, Q.C.
is declared eligible to be admitted by the High Court to Practice Law in Saint Christopher
and Nevis.

### 4.

Condition.
This Order is subject to the condition that Mr. Charles Dominic Kimmins, Q.C.
ma y pra ctice la w in Saint Christopher and Nevis solely in rela tion to
Claims No. NEVHCV 2018/0143, No. NEVHCV 2018/0144 and SKBHCVAP 2019/0006
or other related or incidental matters.
