# Child Justice Regulations, 2019

STATUTORY RULES AND ORDERS
No. 20 of 2019
CHILD JUSTICE REGULATIONS
In exercise of the power conferred by section 75 of the Child Justice Act,
No. 19 of 2013, the Minister makes the following Regulations:
[Published 16th May 2019 – Official Gazette No. 25 of 2019]

### 1.

Citation.
These Regulations may be cited as the Child Justice Regulations, 2019.

### 2.

Interpretation.
In these Regulations, the expression, “Act” means the Child Justice Act,
No. 19 of 2013.

### 3.

Forms.
(1) A police officer shall issue a notice of apprehension of a child to a
probation officer, pursuant to section 11(5) of the Act, in the form set out as
Form 1 in the Schedule.
(2) A probation officer shall issue a notice of assessment of a child to a
parent or guardian of the child officer pursuant to section 25(1) of the Act, in the
form set out as Form 2 in the Schedule.
(3) A probation officer may make a request of a police officer to provide
assistance pursuant to section 25(4) of the Act, in the form set out as Form No. 3
in the Schedule.
(4) A person may apply for permission to be absent or excluded from an
assessment of a child pursuant to section 25(3) of the Act, in the form set out as
Form No. 4 in the Schedule.
(5) A police officer may issue a notice of an initial inquiry in respect of an
offence committed by a child, pursuant to section 13 of the Act, in the form set
out as Form No. 5 in the Schedule.
(6) A police officer may issue a notice releasing a child who is in detention
from police custody, pursuant to sections 15, 16 and 17 of the Act, in the form
set out as Form No. 6 in the Schedule
(7) The Director of Public Prosecutions shall issue a certificate of the
intention to prosecute a child pursuant to section 5(3) of the Act, in the form set
out as Form No. 6 in the Schedule.

### 4.

Reports.
Where a report is required to be provided in respect of any of the proceedings
in the Act, that report shall be produced in a written form specifying the name of
the child in the question, the date of the particular proceedings, the outcome
of the proceedings and any other notable facts or findings that would be relevant
to the case in question.
