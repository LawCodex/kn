# Legal Profession (Eligibility) (Amendment of SRO No. 16 of 2017) Order, 2019

SAINT CHRISTOPHER AND NEVIS
STATUTORY RULES AND ORDERS
No. 1 of 2019
Legal Profession (Eligibility) (Amendment of SRO No. 16 of 2017) Order, 2019

In exercise of the powers conferred by section 16 of the Legal Profession Act,
No. 33 of 2008, the Minister makes the following Order:
[Published 10th January 2019, Official Gazette No. 2 of 2019]

### 1.

Citation.

This Order may be cited as the Legal Profession (Eligibility) (Amendment of SRO
No. 16 of 2017) Order, 2019.

### 2.

Interpretation.
In this Order,
“Act” means the Legal Profession Act, No. 33 of 2008;
“Order” means the Legal Profession (Eligibility) (No. 3) Order, No. 16 of 2017.

### 3.

Amendment of Order.
The Order is amended by replacing section 4 as follows
“4. Condition.
This Order is subject to the condition that Mr. Gilead Patrick Cooper, Q.C. may
practice law in Saint Christopher and Nevis solely in relation to
(a) Claim No. NEVHCV 2016/0137;
(b) Claim No. NECHCV 2018/0143; and
(c) Claim No. NECHCV 2018/0144,
or other related or incidental matters.”.
Made this 7th day of January, 2019.

VINCENT BYRON
Minister responsible for Legal Affairs
