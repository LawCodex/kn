# Proclamation dated the 24th day of May 2019, declaring Friday the 31st day of May, 2019 to be observed as Centenarians Day in Saint Christopher and Nevis

SAINT CHRISTOPHER AND NEVIS
STATUTORY RULES AND ORDERS
No. 22 of 2019
Proclamation dated the 24th day of May 2019, declaring Friday the 31st day of
May, 2019 to be observed as Centenarians Day in Saint Christopher and Nevis

BY THE GOVERNOR-GENERAL OF
SAINT CHRISTOPHER AND NEVIS
A PROCLAMATION
S. W. TAPLEY SEATON
Governor-General
[Published 24th May 2019, Extra Ordinary Gazette No. 27 of 2019]
WHEREAS it is proposed to observe annually the commemoration of a Day to celebrate
the lives and contribution of our Centenarians
AND WHEREAS it is proposed to observe Friday 31st May, 2019 as Centenarians Day
to recognize Our Centenarians in the Federation of Saint Christopher and Nevs
NOW, THEREFORE, I, SIRSAMUELWEYMOUTH TAPLEYSEATON, Knight Grand
Cross of the Most Distinguished Order of Saint Michael and Saint George, Commander of
the Royal Victorian Order, Queen's Counsel, Justice of the Peace, Governor-General of
the Federation of Saint Christopher and Nevis, in exercise of all powers me thereunto enabling
and in accordance with the advice of the Cabinet DO HEREBY declare that Friday
31st May, 2019 be observed as Centenarians Day.
AND all Our Officers and loving subjects in Saint Christopher and Nevis and all
others whom it may concern are hereby required to take due notice hereof and to give their
ready obedience accordingly.

LS

GIVEN under my hand and the Public Seal
of Saint Christopher and Nevis at
Government House this 24th day of May 2019
in the sixty-eighth year of Her Majesty’s reign.

GOD SAVE THE QUEEN!
