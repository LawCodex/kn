# Companies (Financial Services) (Fees) (Amendment) Order, 2019

SAINT CHRISTOPHER AND NEVIS
STATUTORY RULES AND ORDERS
No. 7 of 2019
Companies (Financial Services) (Fees) (Amendment) Order, 2019.

In exercise of the powers conferred by sections 219 and 240 of the Companies
Act, the Minister makes the following Order:
[Published 24th January 2019, Extra Ordinary Gazette No. 5 of 2019]

### 1.

Citation.

This Order may be cited as the Companies (Financial Services) (Fees) (Amendment)
Order, 2019.

### 2.

AMENDMENT TO FINANCIAL SERVICES (FEES) ORDER.

The Financial Services (Fees) Order as set out in the Second Schedule to the Companies
Act is amended as follows:
(a) by inserting immediately after paragraph 3, the following new paragraph
“ 4. Pursuant to section 136 (2) (b) of the Act, the fees payable to the Registrar
for a copy of a report made by inspectors shall be at the rate of two (2) dollars
per page of the report.”.
(b) by replacing the Schedule to the Order as follows:
