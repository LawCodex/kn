# Legal Profession (Eligibility) (No. 5) Order, 2019

SAINT CHRISTOPHER AND NEVIS
STATUTORY RULES AND ORDERS
No. 12 of 2019

Legal Profession (Eligibility) (No. 5) Order, 2019.

In exercise of the powers conferred by section 16 of the Legal Profession Act,
No. 33 of 2008, the Minister makes the following Order:
[Published 20th February 2019, Extra Ordinary Gazette No. 9 of 2019]

### 1.

Citation.
This Order may be cited as the Legal Profession (Eligibility) (No. 5) Order, 2019.

### 2.

Interpretation.
In this Order,
“Act” means the Legal Profession Act, No. 33 of 2008.

### 3.

Eligibility.

Pursuant to the provisions of section 16 of the Act, Mr. Stephen Atherton, Q.C. is
declared eligible to be admitted by the High Court to Practice Law in Saint Christopher and
Nevis.

### 4.

Condition.

This Order is subject to the condition that Mr. Stephen Atherton, Q.C. may practice law
in Saint Christopher and Nevis solely in relation to Claims No. NEVHCV 2018/0142 other
related or incidental matters.

Made this 19th day of February, 2019.

VINCENT BYRON
Minister responsible for Legal Affairs
