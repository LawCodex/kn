# Limited Partnerships (Financial Services) (Regulations) (Revocation) Order, 2019

SAINT CHRISTOPHER AND NEVIS
STATUTORY RULES AND ORDERS
No. 6 of 2019
Limited Partnerships (Financial Services) (Regulations) (Revocation) Order, 2019.

In exercise of the powers conferred by section 73 of the Limited Partnerships Act,
Cap. 21.12, the Minister makes the following Order:
[Published 24th January 2019, Extra Ordinary Gazette No. 5 of 2019]

### 1.

Citation.

This Order may be cited as the Limited Partnerships (Financial Services) (Regulations)
(Revocation) Order, 2019.

### 2.

Revocation.

The Financial Services (Regulations) Order as set out in the Third Schedule to the
Limited Partnerships Act, Cap 21.12 is revoked.
Made this 12th day of January, 2019.

TIMOTHY HARRIS
Minister responsible for Finance
