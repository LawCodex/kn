# Saint Christopher and Nevis (CBI Escrow Accounts) Act (Appointed Day) Notice, 2019

SAINT CHRISTOPHER AND NEVIS
STATUTORY RULES AND ORDERS
No. 14 of 2019

Saint Christopher and Nevis (CBI Escrow Accounts) Act (Appointed Day) Notice.

[Published 20th February 2019, Extra Ordinary Gazette No. 9 of 2019]
In exercise of the powers conferred by section 1 of the Saint Christopher and Nevis
(CBI Escrow Accounts) Act, No. 16 of 2017, the Minister makes the following Notice:

### 1.

Citation.

This Notice may be cited as the Saint Christopher and Nevis (CBI Escrow Accounts)
Act (Appointed Day) Notice, 2019.

### 2.

Appointed Day.

The Saint Christopher and Nevis (CBI Escrow Accounts) Act, No. 16 of 2017 shall
come into operation on the 21st day of February 2019.
Made the 19th day of February 2019.

TIMOTHY HARRIS
Minister responsible for Finance
