# Proclamation dated the 25th day of November 2019, calling out the Defence Force for Service in aid of the civil powers for a period not exceeding three (3) months

SAINT CHRISTOPHER AND NEVIS
STATUTORY RULES AND ORDERS
No. 33 of 2019
Proclamation dated the 25 th day of November 2019, calling out the Defence Force for Service
in aid of the civil powers for a period not exceeding three (3) months
BY THE GOVERNOR-GENERAL
OF SAINT CHRISTOPHER AND NEVIS
A PROCLAMATION
SIR S. W. TAPLEY SEATON
GCMG, CVO, JP, LLD
GOVERNOR-GENERAL

[Published 29th November 2019, Extra-Ordinary Gazette No. 61 of 2019.]
WHEREAS by virtue of the provisions of section 7(1) of the Saint Christopher and Nevis
Defence Force Act, Cap. 19.14 of the Laws of Saint Christopher and Nevis the
Governor-General may, acting in accordance with advice of Cabinet, call out the Defence Force for
service in aid of the civil powers for a period not exceeding six (6) months, with their arms and
ammunition, where it is deemed that the resources of the civil powers need to be augmented
AND WHEREAS it is further provided by section 7 subsection (5) that a member of the
Defence Force called out for services under the provisions of section 7(1) shall have and enjoy all the
powers, authorities and immunities which are vested in a police constable by law
AND WHEREAS the Cabinet at its meeting of the 22nd day of November 2019 advised, that
the provisions of section 7 of the Saint Christopher and Nevis Defence Force Act should be implemented
NOW, THEREFORE, I, Sir Samuel Weymouth Tapley Seaton, Knight Grand Cross of the
Most Distinguished Order of Saint Michael and Saint George, Commander of the Royal Victorian
Order, Queen's Counsel, Justice of the Peace, Doctor of Laws, Governor-General of the Federation of
Saint Christopher and Nevis, DO HEREBY acting with the advice of the Cabinet, call out the Defence
Force for service in aid of the civil powers, with their arms and ammunition, with effect from the 25th
day of November, 2019 for a period not exceeding three (3) months
AND all our officers and loving subjects in Saint Christopher and Nevis and all others whom it
may concern are hereby required to take due notice hereof and to give their ready obedience accordingly.

LS

GIVEN under my hand and the Public Seal of
Saint Christopher and Nevis, this 25th day
of November 2019 at Government House,
Basseterre in the Sixty-eighth year of Her
Majesty’s reign.
GOD SAVE THE QUEEN!
