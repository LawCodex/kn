# Proclamation dated the 23rd day of December 2019 declaring Thursday the 2nd day of January, 2020 to be a public holiday in Saint Christopher and Nevis on the occasion of the 2019/2020 Carnival

SAINT CHRISTOPHER AND NEVIS
STATUTORY RULES AND ORDERS
No. 36 of 2019

Proclamation dated the 23 rd day of December 2019 declaring Thursday the 2 nd day of
January, 2020 to be a public holiday in Saint Christopher and Nevis on the occasion of
the 2019/2020 Carnival.

BY THE GOVERNOR-GENERAL OF
SAINT CHRISTOPHER AND NEVIS
A PROCLAMATION
SIR SAMUEL WEYMOUTH TAPLEY SEATON
GOVERNOR-GENERAL

[Published 24th December 2019, Official Gazette No. 65 of 2019.]
WHEREAS it is enacted by section 5 of the Public Holidays Act (Chapter 23.23) that it shall
be lawful for the Governor-General on the advice of the Cabinet to issue a Proclamation appointing a
special day or part of a day to be reserved as a public holiday throughout Saint Christopher and Nevis
or in any island, town, district or village thereof
AND WHEREAS the Governor-General on the advice of the Cabinet of Saint Christopher
and Nevis considers it expedient that Thursday the 2nd day of January, 2020 should be observed and
kept as a public holiday in Saint Christopher and Nevis on the occasion of the 2019/2020 Carnival
NOW, THEREFORE, I, SIR SAMUEL WEYMOUTH TAPLEY SEATON, Knight Grand
Cross of the Most Distinguished Order of Saint Michael and Saint George, Commander of the Royal
Victorian Order, Queen's Counsel, Justice of the Peace, Doctors of Laws Governor-General of the
Federation of Saint Christopher and Nevis, do with the advice of the Cabinet, in exercise of the powers
conferred on me by the said section of the said Act, appoint Thursday the 2nd day of January, 2020 to
be reserved and kept as a public holiday in Saint Christopher and Nevis
AND all our officers and loving subjects in Saint Christopher and Nevis and all others whom it
may concern are hereby required to take due notice hereof and to give their ready obedience accordingly.

LS

GIVEN under my hand and the Public Seal of
Saint Christopher and Nevis at Government
House Saint Christopher this 23 rd day of
December 2019, and in the sixty-eighth year
of Her Majesty’s reign.
GOD SAVE THE QUEEN!
