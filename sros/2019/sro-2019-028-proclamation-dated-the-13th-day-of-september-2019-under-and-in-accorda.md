# Proclamation dated the 13th day of September 2019, under and in accordance with the National Honours Act, Cap. 23.17 declaring the honourees for National Heroes' Day, 2019

SAINT CHRISTOPHER AND NEVIS
STATUTORY RULES AND ORDERS
No. 28 of 2019
Proclamation dated the 13th day of September 2019, under and in accordance with the
National Honours Act, Cap. 23.17 declaring the honourees for National Heroes' Day, 2019
BY THE GOVERNOR-GENERAL
OF SAINT CHRISTOPHER AND NEVIS
A PROCLAMATION
SIR S. W. TAPLEY SEATON
GCMG, CVO, QC, JP
Governor-General
[Published 18 th September 2019, Official Gazette No. 47 of 2019.]

WHEREAS by virtue of the provisions of section 8 of the National Honours Act,
Cap. 23.17 of the Laws of Saint Christopher and Nevis, the Governor-General acting in
accordance with the advice of the Prime Minister shall make the Awards
NOW THEREFORE, I, Sir Samuel Weymouth Tapley Seaton, Knight Grand Cross of
the Most Distinguished Order of Saint Michael and Saint George, Commander of the Royal
Victorian Order, Queen’s Counsel, Justice of the Peace, Governor-General of the Federation of
Saint Christopher and Nevis in exercise of the powers conferred on me by section 8 aforesaid
and all other powers me thereunto enbling DO HEREBY PROCLAIM the following
AWARDS:Companion of the Star of Merit
Mr. Augustine Merchant – for Agriculture Development in Service to the Nation
Dr. Calvin Hazel – for spiritual Development and Advancement for our People
Bishop Joseph Henville – for Religion and Community Development
Mr. Bruce Skerritt – for Music locally, regionally and internationally
Mr. Erstein Edwards – Public Service and Diplomacy
Medal of Honour
Mrs. Marilyn Bertie-Guishard – for Education and Religion
Mr. Lester Blackett – for Sports
Mrs. Yvette Slack – for Pre-School Education and Community Activism
Mr. Antonio Liburd – for Culture
Mr. Evered (Webbo) Herbert – for Broadcasting
Mrs. Arabella Nisbett – for Farming
Mrs. Earlene Maynard – for Education
Ms. Juliette Martin – for Performing Arts (Dance)
Mrs. Alice Swanston – for Education and Community Outreach
Mr. Oswald Elliott – for Education
Mr. Michael (Mic Heyliger) Stokes – for Musical Production and Artistry
Mr. Anthony Evelyn – for Ongoing Efforts to Enhance the Cause of Building Contractors

AND all our officers and loving subjects in Saint Christopher and Nevis and all others
whom it may concern are hereby required to take due notice hereof and to give their ready
obedience accordingly.

LS

GIVEN under my hand and the Public Seal
of Saint Christopher and Nevis, this
13 th day of September 2019 at
Government House, Basseterre in
the Island of Saint Christopher in the
Federation of Saint Christopher and
Nevis.
