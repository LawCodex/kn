# Companies (Financial Services Regulations) (Revocation) Order, 2019

SAINT CHRISTOPHER AND NEVIS
STATUTORY RULES AND ORDERS
No. 4 of 2019
Companies (Financial Services Regulations) (Revocation) Order, 2019.

In exercise of the powers conferred by section 244 of the Companies Act,
Cap. 21.03, the Minister makes the following Order:
[Published 24th January 2019, Extra Ordinary Gazette No. 5 of 2019]

### 1.

Citation.

This Order may be cited as the Companies (Financial Services Regulations)
(Revocation) Order, 2019.

### 2.

Revocation.

The Financial Services (Regulations) Order as set out in the Seventh Schedule to the
Companies Act, Cap. 21.03, is revoked.
Made this 12th day of January, 2019.

TIMOTHY HARRIS
Minister responsible for Finance
