# Legal Profession (Eligibility) (No. 8) Order, 2019

SAINT CHRISTOPHER
AND NEVIS

STATUTORY RULES AND ORDERS
No. 21 of 2019
Legal Profession (Eligibility) (No. 8) Order, 2019
In exercise of the powers conferred by section 16 of the Legal Profession Act,
No. 33 of 2008, the Minister makes the following Order:
[Published 16th May 2019 – Official Gazette No. 25 of 2019]

### 1.

Citation.
This Order may be cited as the Legal Profession (Eligibility) (No. 8) Order, 2019.

### 2.

Interpretation.
In this Order, “Act” means the Legal Profession Act, No. 33 of 2008.

### 3.

Eligibility.
Pursuant to the provisions of section 16 of the Act, Mr. John Brisby, Q.C. is declared
eligible to be admitted by the High Court to Practice Law in Saint Christopher and Nevis.

### 4.

Condition.
This Order is subject to the condition that Mr. John Brisby, Q.C. may practice law
in Saint Christopher and Nevis solely in relation to Claims No. NEVHCV 2016/0137
other related or incidental matters.

Made this 10th day of May, 2019.

VINCENT BYRON
Minister responsible for Legal Affairs
