# Legal Profession (Eligibility) (No. 2) Order, 2018

SAINT CHRISTOPHER AND NEVIS
STATUTORY RULES AND ORDERS
No. 28 of 2018
Legal Profession (Eligibility) (No. 2) Order, 2018

In exercise of the powers conferred by section 16 of the Legal Profession Act,
No. 33 of 2008, the Minister makes the following Order:
[Published 21st December 2018, Extra Ordinary Gazette No. 65 of 2018]

### 1.

Citation.
This Order may be cited as the Legal Profession (Eligibility) (No. 2) Order, 2018.

### 2.

Interpretation.
In this Order, “Act” means the Legal Profession Act, No. 33 of 2008.

### 3.

Eligibility.

Pursuant to the provisions of section 16 of the Act, Mr Richard Hacker, Q.C. is declared
eligible to be admitted by the High Court to Practice Law in Saint Christopher and Nevis.

### 4.

Condition.

This Order is subject to the condition that Mr Richard Hacker, Q.C.
may practice law in Saint Christopher and Nevis solely in relation to Claims
No. NEVHCV2018/0143, No. NEVHCV 2018/0144, or other related or incidental matters.
Made this 20th day of December, 2018.

VINCENT F. BYRON
Minister responsible for Legal Affairs
