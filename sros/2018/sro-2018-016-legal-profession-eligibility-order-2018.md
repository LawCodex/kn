# Legal Profession (Eligibility) Order, 2018

SAINT CHRISTOPHER AND NEVIS
STATUTORY RULES AND ORDERS
No. 16 of 2018
Legal Profession (Eligibility) Order, 2018

In exercise of the powers conferred by section 16 of the Legal Profession Act,
No. 33 of 2008, the Minister makes the following Order:
[Published 26th July 2018, Official Gazette No. 38 of 2018]

### 1.

Citation.
This Order may be cited as the Legal Profession (Eligibility) Order, 2018.

### 2.

Interpretation.
In this Order, “Act” means the Legal Profession Act, No. 33 of 2008.

### 3.

Eligibility.

Pursuant to the provisions of section 16 of the Act, Mr Leslie Thomas, Q.C. is declared
eligible to be admitted by the High Court to practice law in Saint Christopher and Nevis.

### 4.

Condition.

This Order is subject to the condition that Mr Leslie Thomas, Q.C. may practice law in
Saint Christopher and Nevis solely in relation to Claim No. SKBHCV 2016/0134 or other
related or incidental matters.
Made this 12th day of July, 2018.

VINCENT BYRON
Minister responsible for Legal Affairs
