# Stamps (Variation of Stamp Duty) (Golden Rock Development) Order, 2018

SAINT CHRISTOPHER AND NEVIS
STATUTORY RULES AND ORDERS
No. 8 of 2018
STAMPS (VARIATION OF STAMP DUTY) (GOLDEN ROCK DEVELOPMENT)
ORDER 2018.

In exercise of the powers conferred by section 99 (3) of the Stamps Act, Cap. 20.40,
the Minister makes the following Order:
[Published 17th May 2018, Official Gazette No. 26 of 2018.]

### 1.

CITATION.

This Order may be cited as the Stamps (Variation of Stamp Duty) (Golden Rock
Development) Order, 2018.

### 2.

VARIATION OF STAMP DUTY.

The applicable stamp duty for a conveyance for transfer of land shall be varied for the
purposes of the Golden Rock Development Project located at Golden Rock St Kitts and the
applicable stamp duty for that Project shall be at the rate of 5%.

### 3.

DURATION.

The variation of the stamp duty shall be deemed to have taken effect from
23rd April, 2018 and shall be in force until the 23rd of April, 2020.
Made this 5th day of May, 2018.

TIMOTHY S. HARRIS
Minister responsible for Finance
