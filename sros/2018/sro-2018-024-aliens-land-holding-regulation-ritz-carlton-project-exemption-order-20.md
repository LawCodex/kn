# Aliens Land Holding Regulation (Ritz Carlton Project) (Exemption) Order, 2018

SAINT CHRISTOPHER AND NEVIS
STATUTORY RULES AND ORDERS
No. 24 of 2018
ALIENS LAND HOLDING REGULATION (RITZ CARLTON PROJECT)
(EXEMPTION) ORDER, 2018
In exercise of the powers conferred by section 19 of the Aliens Land Holding Regulations
Act, Cap. 10.01, the Cabinet makes the following Order.
[Published 8th November 2018, Official Gazette No. 57 of 2018]

### 1.

Citation.

This Order may be cited as the Aliens Land Holding Regulation (Ritz Carlton Project)
(Exemption) Order, 2018.

### 2.

Interpretation.
In this Order, “Act” means the Aliens Land Holding Regulation Act, Cap. 10.01.

“Ritz Carlton Project” means all that piece and parcel of land located at South Friars
Bay, South Eastern Peninsula and more particularly described in the particulars set out in
the Schedule and shown on the plan annexed thereto.

### 3.

Exemption.

Pursuant to the provisions of section 19 of the Act, the Ritz Carlton Project is hereby
granted an exemption from the payment of the alien’s land holding licence fee in respect of
lands at South Friars Bay, South Eastern Peninsula belonging to the Ritz Carlton Project.

### 4.

Duration.
The exemption shall be deemed to have taken effect from the 5th day of April, 2018.
