# Proclamation dated the 15th day of February 2018, declaring Saturday the 17th day of February, 2018 to be observed as National Volunteer Day in Saint Christopher and Nevis

SAINT CHRISTOPHER AND NEVIS
STATUTORY RULES AND ORDERS
No. 5 of 2018
Proclamation dated the 15th day of February 2018, declaring Saturday the 17th day of
February, 2018 to be observed as National Volunteer Day in Saint Christopher
and Nevis
BY THE GOVERNOR-GENERAL OF
SAINT CHRISTOPHER AND NEVIS
A PROCLAMATION
S. W. TAPLEY
SEATON
Governor-General
[Published 15th February 2018, Extra Ordinary Gazette No. 10 of 2018]
WHEREAS it is proposed to observe a new annual initiative intended to foster a
greater sense of civic identity, unity, service and responsibility in our fellowmen, women,
boys and girls
AND WHEREAS it is proposed to observe Saturday 17th February, 2018 as the first
annual National Volunteer Day to enable non-governmental and service organisations to join
with the Government in supporting and positively impacting and uplifting our communities
by beautifying our neighbourhoods, by planting trees and cleaning up their environs and
other community activities and on this day to launch The Volunteer Corps.
NOW, THEREFORE, I, SIR SAMUEL WEYMOUTH TAPLEY SEATON, Knight
Grand Cross of the Most Distinguished Order of Saint Michael and Saint George, Commander
of the Royal Victorian Order, Queen’s Counsel, Justice of the Peace, Governor-General of
the Federation of Saint Christopher and Nevis, in exercise of all powers me thereunto enabling
and in accordance with the advice of the Cabinet DO HEREBY declare that Saturday 17th
February, 2018 be observed as National Volunteer Day.
AND all Our Officers and loving subjects in Saint Christopher and Nevis and all
others whom it may concern are hereby required to take due notice hereof and to give their
ready obedience accordingly.
GIVEN at Government House under my hand
and the Public Seal of Saint Christopher and
Nevis this 15th day of February 2018 and in the
sixty-seventh year of Her Majesty’s reign.

GOVERNMENT PRINTERY
ST KITTS W I
