# Proclamation dated the 19th day of January 2018, summoning and appointing a Session of the Nevis Island Assembly

SAINT CHRISTOPHER AND NEVIS
STATUTORY RULES AND ORDERS
No. 1 of 2018
Proclamation dated the 19th day of January 2018, summoning and appointing a
Session of the Nevis Island Assembly.
BY THE GOVERNOR-GENERAL OF
SAINT CHRISTOPHER AND NEVIS
A PROCLAMATION
S. W. TAPLEY
SEATON
Governor-General
[Published 1st February 2018, Official Gazette No. 5 of 2018]
WHEREAS a general election of members of the Nevis Island Assembly was held on the
18th day of December 2017 and writs therefor duly returned;
AND WHEREAS it is provided by subsection (1) of section 46 of the Constitution of
Saint Christopher and Nevis applied with modification by section 104(1)(a) it is provided that each
session of the Nevis Island Assembly shall be held at such place and shall commence at such time as
the Governor-General may by Proclamation appoint;
AND WHEREAS it is expedient that the Nevis Island Assembly should be summoned and
the time and place for the first session therefor duly appointed
NOW, THEREFORE, I, SIR SAMUEL WEYMOUTH TAPLEY SEATON,
Knight Grand Cross of the Most Distinguished Order of Saint Michael and Saint George,
Commander of the Royal Victorian Order, Queen’s Counsel, Justice of the Peace,
Governor-General of the Federation of Saint Christopher and Nevis, in exercise of the powers in me vested hereinbefore recited and all other powers me thereunto enabling, DO, by this
my Proclamation, summon the Nevis Island Assembly and appoint Tuesday the 30th day of
January, 2018, at 10 o’clock in the forenoon at the Nevis Island Assembly Chambers at Hamilton
House in the Town of Charlestown in the Island of Nevis as the time and place at which the first session of the said Nevis Island Assembly shall be held for the despatch of public business;
AND all Our Officers and loving subjects in Saint Christopher and Nevis and all others whom it
may concern are hereby required to take due notice hereof and to give their ready obedience accordingly.
GIVEN under my hand and the Public Seal
of Saint Christopher and Nevis at
Government House this 19th day of January 2018
and in the sixty-sixth year of Her Majesty’s reign.

GOVERNMENT PRINTERY
ST KITTS W I
