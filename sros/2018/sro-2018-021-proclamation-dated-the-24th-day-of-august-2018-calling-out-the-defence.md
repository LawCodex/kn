# Proclamation dated the 24th day of August, 2018, calling out the Defence Force for service in aid of the civil powers for a period not exceeding six (6) months

SAINT CHRISTOPHER AND NEVIS
STATUTORY RULES AND ORDERS
No. 21 of 2018
Proclamation dated the 24th day of August, 2018, calling out the Defence Force for service in aid of the
civil powers for a period not exceeding six (6) months
BY THE GOVERNOR-GENERAL
OF SAINT CHRISTOPHER AND NEVIS
A PROCLAMATION
SIR S. W. TAPLEY SEATON
GCMG, CVO, QC, JP
Governor-General
[Published 24th August 2018, Extra-Ordinary Gazette No. 43 of 2018.]

WHEREAS by virtue of the provisions of section 7(1) of the Saint Christopher and Nevis
Defence Force Act, Cap. 19.14 of the Laws of Saint Christopher and Nevis the Governor-General
may, acting in accordance with advice of Cabinet, call out the Defence Force for service in aid of the
civil powers for a period not exceeding six (6) months, with their arms and ammunition, where it is
deemed that the resources of the civil powers need to be augmented
AND WHEREAS it is further provided by section 7 subsection (5) that a member of the
Defence Force called out for services under the provisions of section 7(1) shall have and enjoy all the
powers, authorities and immunities which are vested in a police constable by law
AND WHEREAS the Cabinet at its meeting of the 22nd day of August, 2018 advised that the
provisions of section 7 of the Saint Christopher and Nevis Defence Force Act should be implemented
NOW THEREFORE, I, Sir Samuel Weymouth Tapley Seaton, Knight Grand Cross of the
Most Distinguished Order of Saint Michael and Saint George, Commander of the Royal Victorian
Order, Queen’s Counsel, Justice of the Peace, Governor-General of the Federation of Saint Christopher
and Nevis DO HEREBY acting with the advice of the Cabinet, call out the Defence Force for service in
aid of the civil powers, with their arms and ammunition, with effect from the 24th day of August, 2018
for a period not exceeding six (6) months
AND all our officers and loving subjects in Saint Christopher and Nevis and all others whom it
may concern are hereby required to take due notice hereof and to give their ready obedience accordingly.

LS

GIVEN under my hand and the Public Seal of
Saint Christopher and Nevis, this
24 th day of August 2018 at
Government House, Basseterre in
the Sixty-seventh year of Her
Majesty’s reign.
