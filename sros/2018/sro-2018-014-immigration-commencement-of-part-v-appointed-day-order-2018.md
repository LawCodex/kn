# Immigration (Commencement of Part V) Appointed Day Order, 2018

SAINT CHRISTOPHER AND NEVIS
STATUTORY RULES AND ORDERS
No. 14 of 2018
Immigration (Commencement of Part V) Appointed Day Order, 2018.

In exercise of the powers conferred by section 1 subsection (2) of the Immigration
Act, Cap. 6.02 the Minister makes the following Order.
[Published 5th July 2018, Official Gazette No. 35 of 2018.]

### 1.

CITATION.

This Order may be cited as the Immigration (Commencement of Part V) Appointed
Day Order, 2018.

### 2.

INTERPRETATION.
In this Order, “Act” means the Immigration Act, Cap. 6.02.

### 3.

COMMENCEMENT OF PART V.

## Part V of the Act shall come into operation from the 1st day of July, 2018.

Made this 30th day of June, 2018.

VINCENT BYRON
Minister responsible for National Security

GOVERNMENT PRINTERY
ST KITTS W I
