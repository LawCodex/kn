# Integrity in Public Life Act Appointed Day Order, 2018

SAINT CHRISTOPHER AND NEVIS
STATUTORY RULES AND ORDERS
No. 15 of 2018
Integrity in Public Life Act (Appointed Day) Order, 2018.

In exercise of the powers conferred by section 1 subsection (2) of the Integrity in
Public Life Act, No. 18 of 2013 the Minister makes the following Order.
[Published 5th July 2018, Official Gazette No. 35 of 2018.]

### 1.

CITATION.

This Order may be cited as the Integrity in Public Life Act Appointed Day Order,
2018\.

### 2.

APPOINTED DAY.

The Integrity in Public Life Act, No. 18 of 2013 shall come into operation on the
11th day of July, 2018.
Made this 3rd day of July, 2018.

VINCENT BYRON
Minister responsible for Justice and Legal Affairs
