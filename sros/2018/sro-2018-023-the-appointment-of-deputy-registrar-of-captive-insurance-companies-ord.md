# The appointment of Deputy Registrar of Captive Insurance Companies Order, 2018 made by the Minister of Finance under section 4(1) of the Captive Insurance Companies Act, Cap. 21.20

SAINT CHRISTOPHER AND NEVIS
STATUTORY RULES AND ORDERS
No. 23 of 2018

The appointment of Deputy Registrar of Captive Insurance Companies Order, 2018
made by the Minister of Finance under section 4(1) of the Captive Insurance Companies
Act, Cap. 21.20.
[Published 8th October 2018, Extra Ordinary Gazette No. 52 of 2018]

### 1.

CITATION.

This Order may be cited as the appointment of Deputy Registrar of Captive Insurance
Companies Order, 2018.

### 2.

APPOINTMENT OF DEPUTY REGISTRAR OF CAPTIVE INSURANCE
COMPANIES.

The following person is hereby appointed Deputy Registrar of Captive Insurance
Companies for the purposes of the Captive Insurance Companies Act, Cap. 21.20.
Miss Kerstin Petty

### 3.

COMMENCEMENT.
This Order shall come into operation on the 28th day of September 2018.
Made by the Minister this 28th day of September, 2018.

HONOURABLE TIMOTHY S. HARRIS
Minister responsible for Finance
