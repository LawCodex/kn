# COVID-19 (Prevention and Control) Act (Appointed Day) Order, 2020

SAINT CHRISTOPHER AND NEVIS
STATUTORY RULES AND ORDERS
No. 57 of 2020

COVID-19 (Prevention and Control) Act (Appointed Day) Order, 2020
In exercise of the powers conferred by section 1 of the COVID-19 (Prevention and
Control) Act, No. 16 of 2020, the Minister makes the following Order:
[Published 28th December 2020, Extra-Ordinary Gazette No. 93 of 2020]

### 1.

Citation.

This Order may be cited as the COVID-19 (Prevention and Control) Act (Appointed
Day) Order, 2020.

### 2.

Interpretation.

In this Order, “Act” means the COVID-19 (Prevention and Control) Act, No. 16 of
2020\.

### 3.

Appointed Day for Act No. 16 of 2020.
The Act shall come into operation on the 1st day of January, 2021.

Made this 28th day of December, 2020.

AKILAH BYRON-NISBETT
Minister responsible for Health
