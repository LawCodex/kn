# Supreme Court (Amendment) Order, 2020

SAINT CHRISTOPHER AND NEVIS
STATUTORY RULES AND ORDERS
No. 43 of 2020
SUPREME COURT (AMENDMENT) ORDER

In exercise of the powers conferred under section 4(5) of the Supreme Court Order,
the Chief Justice, with the concurrence of the Prime Ministers, Premiers, and Chief Minister,
makes this Order:
[Published 30th September 2020, Extra-Ordinary Gazette No. 75 of 2020]

### 1.

CITATION AND COMMENCEMENT.
(1) This Order may be cited as the Supreme Court (Amendment) Order, 2020.
(2) This Order is deemed to have come into force on the 1st day of September 2019.

### 2.

AMENDMENT OF SECTION 4.
Section 4 of the Supreme Court Order is amended by replacing subsection (3) as follows:
“ (3) Subject to the provisions of subsection (5) of this section, the judges of
the Court of Appeal shall be the Chief Justice, who shall be President of the Court,
and twelve Justices of Appeal.”

Dated the 6th day of August, 2020.

DAME JANICE M. PEREIRA, DBE
Chief Justice and Chairperson of the
Judicial and Legal Services Commission
