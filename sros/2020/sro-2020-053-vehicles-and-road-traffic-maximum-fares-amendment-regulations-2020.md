# Vehicles and Road Traffic (Maximum Fares) (Amendment) Regulations, 2020

SAINT CHRISTOPHER AND NEVIS
STATUTORY RULES AND ORDERS
No. 53 of 2020

Vehicles and Road Traffic (Maximum Fares) (Amendment) Regulations, 2020

In exercise of the powers conferred by section 80 of the Vehicles and Road Traffic
Act, Cap 15.06, Revised Edition 2017, the Minister responsible for Transport makes the
following Regulations:
[Published 28th December 2020, Extra-Ordinary Gazette No. 93 of 2020]

### 1.

Citation.

These Regulations may be cited as the Vehicles and Road Traffic (Maximum Fares)
(Amendment) Regulations, 2020.

### 2.

Interpretation.
In these Regulations, unless the context otherwise requires,
“principal Regulations” means the Vehicles and Road Traffic (Maximum Fares)
Regulations as set out under the Seventh Schedule of the Vehicles and Road
Traffic Act, Cap. 15.06.
