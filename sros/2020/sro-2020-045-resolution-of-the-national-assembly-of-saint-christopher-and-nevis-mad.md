# RESOLUTION of the National Assembly of Saint Christopher and Nevis made under section 65 of the Standing Orders of the National Assembly as set out in the Fourth Schedule of the National Assembly Elections Act, Cap. 2.01

SAINT CHRISTOPHER AND NEVIS
STATUTORY RULES AND ORDERS
No. 45 of 2020
RESOLUTION
RESOLUTION of the National Assembly of Saint Christopher and Nevis made
under section 65 of the Standing Orders of the National Assembly as set out in
the Fourth Schedule of the National Assembly Elections Act, Cap. 2.01

[Published 16 th October 2020 – Extra-Ordinary Gazette No. 79 of 2020]

WHEREAS it is provided by section 65 of the Standing Orders of the National
Assembly as set out in the Fourth Schedule of the National Assembly Elections Act, that
the National Assembly may appoint any of its members to be a Select Committee for the
purpose of examining and reporting on the clauses of any proposed Bill or for any other
purpose
AND WHEREAS the National Assembly may appoint a Chairperson of a Select
Committee and in the absence of that appointment then the Select Committee shall appoint
its own Chairperson
AND WHEREAS a Select Committee shall be constituted in such a manner so as to
reflect as far as possible the balance of the parties in the National Assembly and shall make
a report to the National Assembly upon the matters referred to it
AND WHEREAS the National Assembly considers it necessary to appoint a Select
Committee to review the provisions of the Standing Orders of the National Assembly
NOW BE IT RESOLVED THEREFORE that the National Assembly of Saint
Christopher and Nevis hereby approves the appointment of a Select Committee to review
the provisions of the Standing Orders and the composition of the Committee shall be as
follows:
Honourable A. Michael Perkins - Speaker of the National Assembly – Chairperson
Eugene Hamilton
\- Leader of Government Business
Eric Evelyn
\- Representing the Government
Geoffrey Hanley
\- Representing the Opposition
Senator Joyelle Clarke
\- Representing the Opposition

Approved by the National Assembly this 15th day of October, 2020.

SONIA BODDIE-THOMPSON
Clerk of the National Assembly
