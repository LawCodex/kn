# International Trade in Wild Flora and Fauna (Designation of Management Authority) Order, 2020

SAINT CHRISTOPHER AND NEVIS
STATUTORY RULES AND ORDERS
No. 22 of 2020
International Trade in Wild Flora and Fauna (Designation of Management
Authority) Order
In exercise of the powers conferred by section 7 of the International Trade in Wild
Flora and Fauna Act, Cap. 11.08, the Minister makes the following Order:
[Published 28th May 2020 – Official Gazette No. 40 of 2020]

### 1.

Citation.
This Order may be cited as the International Trade in Wild Flora and Fauna
(Designation of Management Authority) Order, 2020.

### 2.

Designation of Management Authority.
Pursuant to the provisions of section 7 of the International Trade in Wild Flora and
Fauna Act, Cap. 11.08, the Minister designates the Management Authority to be the
Director of Agriculture.

Made this 21st day of May, 2020.
EUGENE HAMILTON
Minister responsible for Agriculture
