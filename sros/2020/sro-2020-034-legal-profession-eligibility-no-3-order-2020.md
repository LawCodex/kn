# Legal Profession (Eligibility) (No. 3) Order, 2020

SAINT CHRISTOPHER AND NEVIS
STATUTORY RULES AND ORDERS
No. 34 of 2020
Legal Profession (Eligibility) (No. 3) Order, 2020
In exercise of the powers conferred by section 16 of the Legal Profession Act,
Cap. 3.28, the Minister makes the following Order:
[Published 24th July 2020 – Extra-Ordinary Gazette No. 57 of 2020]

### 1.

Citation.
This Order may be cited as the Legal Profession (Eligibility) (No. 3) Order, 2020.

### 2.

Interpretation.
In this Order,
“Act” means the Legal Profession Act, Cap. 3.28.

### 3.

Eligibility.

Pursuant to the provisions of section 16 of the Act, Mr. Michael James Royston Ashcroft, Q.C.
is declared eligible to be admitted by the High Court to Practice Law in Saint Christopher and Nevis.

### 4.

Condition.

This Order is subject to the condition that Mr. Michael James Royston Ashcroft, Q.C.
may practice law in Saint Christopher and Nevis solely in relation to Claim
No. NEVHCV2018/0143, Claim No. NEVHCV2018/0144 or other related or incidental
matters.

Made this 24th day of July, 2020.
VINCENT BYRON
Minister responsible for Legal Affairs
