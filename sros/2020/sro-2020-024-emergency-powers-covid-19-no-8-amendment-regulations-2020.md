# Emergency Powers (COVID-19) (No. 8) (Amendment) Regulations, 2020

SAINT CHRISTOPHER AND NEVIS
STATUTORY RULES AND ORDERS
No. 24 of 2020
Emergency Powers (COVID-19) (No. 8) (Amendment) Regulations
In exercise of the power conferred by section 5 of the Emergency Powers Act,
Cap. 19.02, the Governor-General, makes the following Regulations:
[Published 4th June 2020 – Extra-Ordinary Gazette No. 43 of 2020]

### 1.

Citation.
These Regulations may be cited as the Emergency Powers (COVID-19)
(No. 8) (Amendment) Regulations, 2020.

### 2.

Interpretation.
In these Regulations
“no night curfew” means a period when limited operation of businesses
and enterprises may occur, with a duration from
(a) 8:01 p.m. of one day until 4:59 a.m. of the following day, on a
weekday; or
(b) 7:01 p.m. of one day until 4:59 a.m. of the following day, on a
Saturday or Sunday.

### 3.

Amendment of Curfew Schedule I.
Schedule I is amended by replacing the expression “night curfew” with the
expression “no night curfew” for Thursday 4 th June, Friday 5 th June and
Saturday 6th June, as follows
