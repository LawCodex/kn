# Supreme Court (Amendment of Schedule 3) Order, 2020

SAINT CHRISTOPHER AND NEVIS
STATUTORY RULES AND ORDERS
No. 44 of 2020
SUPREME COURT (AMENDMENT OF SCHEDULE 3) ORDER

In exercise of the powers conferred under section 11 (1) of the West Indies
Associated States Supreme Court Order, set out as the Fifth Schedule to the West Indies Act,
Cap. 1.01, the Judicial and Legal Services Commission, with the concurrence of the
Prime Ministers, Premiers, and Chief Minister, makes this Order
[Published 30th September 2020, Extra-Ordinary Gazette No. 75 of 2020]

### 1.

CITATION AND COMMENCEMENT.

(1) This Order may be cited as the Supreme Court (Amendment of Schedule 3)
Order, 2020.
(2) This Order is deemed to have come into force on the 1st day of September 2019.

### 2.

AMENDMENT OF SCHEDULE 3.

Section 3 of the Supreme Court Order is amended by replacing the provisions of the
Schedule as follows:
“

SALARIES OF JUDGES OF THE SUPREME COURT

Chief Justice

$285,410.00 per year

Court of Appeal Judge

$237,838.00 per year

High Court Judge

$198,198.00 per year.”

Dated the 6th day of August, 2020.

DAME JANICE M. PEREIRA, DBE
Chief Justice and Chairperson of the
Judicial and Legal Services Commission
