# Legal Profession (Eligibility) (No. 5) Order, 2020

SAINT CHRISTOPHER AND NEVIS
STATUTORY RULES AND ORDERS
No. 51 of 2020
Legal Profession (Eligibility) (No. 5) Order, 2020

In exercise of the powers conferred by section 16 of the Legal Profession Act,
Cap. 3.28, the Minister makes the following Order:
[Published 17th December 2020, Official Gazette No. 90 of 2020]

### 1.

Citation.
This Order may be cited as the Legal Profession (Eligibility) (No. 5) Order, 2020.

### 2.

Interpretation.
In this Order,
“Act” means the Legal Profession Act, Cap. 3.28.

### 3.

Eligibility.

Pursuant to the provisions of section 16 of the Act, Mr Nicholas Michael Bacon, Q.C.
is declared eligible to be admitted by the High Court to Practice Law in Saint Christopher
and Nevis.

### 4.

Condition.

This Order is subject to the condition that Mr Nicholas Michael Bacon, Q.C. may practice
law in Saint Christopher and Nevis solely in relation to Claim No. NEVHCV2020/0064 or
other related or incidental matters.
Made this 14th day of December, 2020.

VINCENT BYRON
Minister responsible for Legal Affairs
