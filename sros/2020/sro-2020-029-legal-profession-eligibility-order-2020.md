# Legal Profession (Eligibility) Order, 2020

SAINT CHRISTOPHER AND NEVIS
STATUTORY RULES AND ORDERS
No. 29 of 2020
Legal Profession (Eligibility) Order, 2020

In exercise of the powers conferred by section 16 of the Legal Profession Act,
Cap. 3.28, the Minister makes the following Order:
[Published 9th July 2020, Official Gazette No. 52 of 2020]

### 1.

Citation.
This Order may be cited as the Legal Profession (Eligibility) Order, 2020.

### 2.

Interpretation.
In this Order,
“Act” means the Legal Profession Act, Cap. 3.28.

### 3.

Eligibility.

Pursuant to the provisions of section 16 of the Act, Ms Siobhan Grey, Q.C. is declared
eligible to be admitted by the High Court to Practice Law in Saint Christopher and Nevis.

### 4.

Condition.

This Order is subject to the condition that Ms Siobhan Grey, Q.C. may practice law in
Saint Christopher and Nevis solely in relation to Criminal Appeal No. 12 of 2004. Nardis
Maynard v. The Queen or other related or incidental matters.
Made this 22nd day of May, 2020.

VINCENT BYRON
Minister responsible for Legal Affairs
