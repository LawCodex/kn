# Legal Profession (Eligibility) (Amendment of SRO No. 12 of 2019) Order, 2020

SAINT CHRISTOPHER AND NEVIS
STATUTORY RULES AND ORDERS
No. 5 of 2020
Legal Profession (Eligibility) (Amendment of SRO No. 12 of 2019) Order, 2020

In exercise of the powers conferred by section 16 of the Legal Profession Act,
No. 33 of 2008, the Minister makes the following Order:
[Published 19th March 2020, Official Gazette No. 19 of 2020]

### 1.

Citation.

This Order may be cited as the Legal Profession (Eligibility) (Amendment of SRO
No. 12 of 2019) Order, 2020.

### 2.

Interpretation.
In this Order,
“Act” means the Legal Profession Act, No. 33 of 2008;
“Order” means the Legal Profession (Eligibility) (No. 5) Order, SRO No. 12 of 2019.

### 3.

Amendment of Order.
The Order is amended by replacing section 4 as follows
“4. Condition.
This Order is subject to the condition that Mr. Stephen Atherton, Q.C. may practice
law in Saint Christopher and Nevis solely in relation to
(a) Claim No. NEVHCV 2018/0142; and
(b) Claim No. NEVHCV 2020/0016,
or other related or incidental matters.”.
Made this 17th day of March, 2020.

VINCENT BYRON
Minister responsible for Legal Affairs
