# Magistrate’s Code of Procedure (Appointed Day for Act No. 4 of 2018) Notice, 2020

SAINT CHRISTOPHER AND NEVIS
STATUTORY RULES AND ORDERS
No. 31 of 2020
Magistrate’s Code of Procedure (Appointed Day for Act No. 4 of 2018)
Notice, 2020
In exercise of the powers confirmed by section 1 of the Magistrate’s Code of Procedure
(Amendment) Act, No. 4 of 2018, the Minister makes the following Notice:
[Published 24th July 2020 – Extra-Ordinary Gazette No. 57 of 2020]

### 1.

Citation.
This Notice may be cited as the Magistrate’s Code of Procedure (Appointed Day
for Act No. 4 of 2018) Notice, 2020.

### 2.

Interpretation.
In this Notice, “Act” means the Magistrate’s Code of Procedure (Amendment) Act,
No. 4 of 2018.

### 3.

Appointed Day for Act No. 4 of 2018.
The Act shall come into operation on the 24th day of July, 2020.

Made this 21st day of July, 2020.
VINCENT BYRON
Minister responsible for Legal Affairs
