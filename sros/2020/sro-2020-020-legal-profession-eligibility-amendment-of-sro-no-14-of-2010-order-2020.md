# Legal Profession (Eligibility) (Amendment of SRO No. 14 of 2010) Order, 2020

SAINT CHRISTOPHER AND NEVIS
STATUTORY RULES AND ORDERS
No. 20 of 2020
Legal Profession (Eligibility) (Amendment of SRO No. 14 of 2010) Order, 2020
In exercise exercise of the powers conferred by section 16 of the Legal Profession
Act, Cap. 3.28, the Minister makes the following Order:
[Published 28th May 2020 – Official Gazette No. 40 of 2020]

### 1.

Citation.
This Order may be cited as the Legal Profession (Eligibility) (Amendment of SRO
No. 14 of 2010) Order, 2020.

### 2.

Interpretation.
In these Order,
“Act” means the Legal Profession Act, Cap. 3.28;
“Order” means the Legal Profession (Eligibility) Order, No. 14 of 2010 as
amended by the Legal Profession (Eligibility) (Amendment of
SRO 14 of 2010) Order No. 2 of 2012 and the Legal Profession (Eligibility)
(No. 3) Order, No. 13 of 2013.

### 3.

Amendment of section 4 of Order.
The Order is amended in section 4 by adding the following new paragraphs (e) and
(f) immediately following paragraph (d) as follows
“ (e) Claim No. NEVHCV 2018/0143;
(f) Claim No. NEVHCV 2018/0144.”.

Made this 7th day of May, 2020.
VINCENT BYRON
Minister responsible for Legal Affairs
