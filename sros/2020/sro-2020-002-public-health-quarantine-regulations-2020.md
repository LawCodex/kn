# Public Health (Quarantine) Regulations, 2020

SAINT CHRISTOPHER AND NEVIS
STATUTORY RULES AND ORDERS
No. 2 of 2020
Public Health (Quarantine) Regulations

In exercise of the power conferred by section 10 of the Public Health Act, Cap. 9.21,
the Minister, with the approval of Cabinet, makes the following Regulations:

[Published 21st February 2020, Extra Ordinary Gazette No. 13 of 2020]

### 1.

Citation.
These Regulations may be cited as the Public Health (Quarantine) Regulations, 2020.

### 2.

Interpretation.
In these Regulations
“Public Health Act” means the Public Health Act, Cap. 9.21;
“Quarantine Act” means the Quarantine Act, Cap. 9.23;
“Quarantine Authority” means the Chief Medical Officer in accordance with the
provisions of section 3 of the Quarantine Act;
“officers” means persons appointed by the Governor-General in the manner provided
under section 3 of the Quarantine Act.

### 3.

Schedule of diseases.

Pursuant to section 10(c)(i) of the Public Health Act, the diseases listed in Schedule I are
infectious, communicable or notifiable diseases dangerous to public health to which the
provisions of these Regulations apply.

### 4.

Quarantine Authority establishment and powers.

(1) The Quarantine Authority established under section 3 of the Quarantine Act shall
be the Quarantine Authority for the purposes of these Regulations.
(2) The Quarantine Authority shall have the powers conferred on him or her under the
Quarantine Act in exercise of his or her duties under these Regulations.

### 5.

Appointment of officers, quarantine guards or screening officers.

(1) Pursuant to the provisions of section 10(b) of the Public Health Act, the officers,
including, any quarantine guards necessary for giving effect to the provisions of these
Regulations, shall be appointed by the Governor-General, in accordance with the provisions
of section 3 of the Quarantine Act.

(2) The officers or quarantine guards referred to in sub-regulation (1), shall have such
duties as are directed by the Quarantine Authority.
(3) The screening officers referred to in sub-regulation (1), shall have such duties as
are directed by the Quarantine Authority.

### 6.

Duties and powers of the police.

(1) The police shall have the duties and powers conferred under the Quarantine Act for
the purposes of these Regulations.
(2) Pursuant to section 10(ee) of the Public Health Act, the police may enter, with
reasonable force if necessary, into any premises where the owner does not give permission
for the inspection or examination thereof.

### 7.

Duties and powers of the Defence Force.

For the purposes of these Regulations, the St Kitts and Nevis Defence Force shall have
such duties and powers as may be specified by the Minister, with the approval of Cabinet, by
Order published in the Gazette.

### 8.

Reporting cases.

(1) Pursuant to section 10(c)(ii) of the Public Health Act, a person may make a report of
cases or suspected cases of infectious, communicable or notifiable diseases to the Quarantine
Authority.
(2) A report shall be in the Communicable Disease Notification Form set out in
Schedule II or by telephone call to the Quarantine Authority or the officers of the Authority.

### 9.

Supplies to suppress an epidemic.

Pursuant to section 10(c)(iii) of the Public Health Act, the emergency supply chain
action plan will guide the forecasting, procurement, importing, stockpiling or issuing of
relevant medical supplies necessary for preventing, mitigating, controlling or suppressing
an epidemic or spread of any infectious, communicable, notifiable or other disease.

### 10.

Screening.
(1) The Quarantine Authority may authorise the establishment of screening stations at
designated screening sites depending on the nature of the disease.
(2) The screening process shall be in accordance with well-defined algorithms and
specific actions to be taken which shall be explained to each person being screened.
(3) A person shall submit to the examination, answer questions or other requirements
of the screening process.

### 11.

Surveillance, removal, quarantine or isolation.
Pursuant to section 10(c)(iv) of the Public Health Act, the Minister may, in consultation
with the Quarantine Authority, authorise the surveillance, removal, quarantine or isolation of
any person at a place or health facility designated by the Minister and dependent on the
nature of the infectious, communicable or notifiable disease.

### 12.

Preventing persons or conveyances.
Pursuant to section 10(c)(v) of the Public Health Act, the Minister depending on the
nature of the disease may prevent or restrict persons or conveyances from passing from one
locality to any other locality in a manner that is necessary for preventing, mitigating, controlling
or suppressing an epidemic or spread of any infectious, communicable, notifiable or other
disease.

### 13.

Detention in another island, state or country.
(1) Pursuant to sections 4, 10(c)(v), 10(c)(vi) and 10(c)(x) of the Public Health Act, the
Minister may, with the approval of Cabinet,
(a) in the national interest;
(b) in the interest of public health; or
(c) public safety,
in writing, request a person to remain outside of Saint Christopher and Nevis or outside of
one either island.
(2) The Minister shall specify the terms and conditions of the request and shall include
(a) the specified period;
(b) the resources to be provided to assist the person during the specified period
including
(i) any reasonable provisions for accommodation;
(ii) any reasonable provisions for meals;
(iii) any reasonable provisions for transportation;
(iv) any requirements for receipts.
(3) The Minister shall, if the person referred to in sub-regulation (1) makes a request in
writing, notify the person’s employer in writing of the request and the employer shall not
reduce or deduct the person’s salary for the specified period.

### 14.

Social distancing.
(1) Pursuant section 10(c)(xiv) of the Public Health Act, the Minister may, by notice
published in the Gazette, authorise
(a) the closing of schools, churches and places of public assembly or
entertainment; or
(b) any other social distancing interventions that may be appropriate depending
on the nature of the infectious, communicable or notifiable disease for the
purpose of preventing, controlling or containing the spread of disease.

### 15.

Offenses and penalties.
Pursuant to section 10(ll) of the Public Health Act, a person who contravenes the
provisions of these Regulations shall be liable on summary conviction to a fine not exceeding
eight thousand dollars.
