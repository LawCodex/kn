# Common Reporting Standard (Automatic Exchange of Financial Account Information) (Amendment) Regulations, 2020

SAINT CHRISTOPHER AND NEVIS
STATUTORY RULES AND ORDERS
No. 40 of 2020
Common Reporting Standard (Automatic Exchange of Financial Account Information)
(Amendment) Regulations, 2020

In exercise of the powers conferred by section 17 of the Common Reporting Standard
(Automatic Exchange of Financial Account Information) Act, No. 13 of 2016, the Minister
makes the following Regulations
[Published 3rd September 2020, Extra-Ordinary Gazette No. 69 of 2020]

### 1.

Citation.

These Regulations may be cited as the Common Reporting Standard (Automatic Exchange
of Financial Account Information) (Amendment) Regulations, 2020.

### 2.

INTERPRETATION.

In these Regulations, the expression, “Regulations” means the Common Reporting
Standard (Automatic Exchange of Financial Account Information) Regulations,
No. 32 of 2016.

### 3.

AMENDMENT OF SCHEDULE 2 TO THE REGULATIONS.

Schedule 2 to the Regulations is amended under the heading, “2018 Jurisdictions” by
inserting the following expressions in the correct alphabetical order:
“

Azerbaijan
Pakistan”.

### 4.

AMENDMENT OF SCHEDULE 3 TO THE REGULATIONS.

Schedule 3 to the Regulations is amended under the heading, “Reportable Jurisdictions”
by inserting the following expressions in the correct alphabetical order:
“

Austria
Azerbaijan
Barbados
Indonesia
Israel
Lithuania

Pakistan
Panama
Slovak Republic”.

Made this 24th day of August, 2020.

TIMOTHY S. HARRIS
Minister responsible for Finance
