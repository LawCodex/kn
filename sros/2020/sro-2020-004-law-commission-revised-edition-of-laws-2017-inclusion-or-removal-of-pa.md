# Law Commission (Revised Edition of Laws 2017) (Inclusion or Removal of Pages) Order, 2020

SAINT CHRISTOPHER AND NEVIS
STATUTORY RULES AND ORDERS
No. 4 of 2020

Law Commission (Revised Edition of Laws 2017) (Inclusion or Removal of Pages)
Order, 2020

In exercise of the powers conferred by section 30 of the Law Commission Act,
No. 27 of 2012, the Law Reform Commissioner makes the following Order:
[Published 2nd March 2020, Extra Ordinary Gazette No. 16 of 2020]

### 1.

Citation.

This Order may be cited as the Law Commission (Revised Edition of Laws 2017)
(Inclusion or Removal of Pages) Order, 2020.

### 2.

Interpretation.
In this Order, “Act” means the Law Commission Act, No. 27 of 2012.

### 3.

Inclusion or Removal of Pages.

Pursuant to section 30 of the Act, the laws enacted and in force in Saint Christopher
and Nevis from 1st January 2010 to 31st December 2017 shall be included in the Revised
Edition of Laws 2017 which shall also contain the 2002 and 2009 revised laws.

Made this 2nd day of March, 2020.

JOSEPHINE MALLALIEU
Law Reform Commissioner
