# Saint Christopher and Nevis Citizenship by Investment (Amendment) Regulations, 2020

SAINT CHRISTOPHER AND NEVIS
STATUTORY RULES AND ORDERS
No. 30 of 2020
Saint Christopher and Nevis Citizenship by Investment (Amendment) Regulations, 2020.

In exercise of the power conferred by section 15 of the Saint Christopher and Nevis
Citizenship Act, Cap. 1.05, the Minister makes the following Regulations:
[Published 10th July 2020, Extra Ordinary Gazette No. 53 of 2020]

### 1.

CITATION.

These Regulations may be cited as the Saint Christopher and Nevis Citizenship by
Investment (Amendment) Regulations, 2020.

### 2.

INTERPRETATION.
In these Regulations,
“principal Regulations” means the Saint Christopher and Nevis Citizenship by
Investment Regulations, No. 52 of 2011.

### 3.

AMENDMENT OF REGULATION 2.

The principal Regulations are amended in regulation 2 by inserting in the correct
alphabetical order, the following definition;
“ “International Marketing Agent” means an entity authorised by the Citizenship by
Investment Unit pursuant to regulation 9A.

### 4.

AMENDMENTOF REGULATIONS.

The principal regulations are amended by inserting immediately after paragraph 6B,
the following new paragraph 6C.
“ 6C.
Notwithstanding the provisions of paragraph 6B subparagraph (9), the
minimum contribution to the Sustainable Growth Fund for the limited time offer, shall be as
set out in paragraph 6 of the First Schedule.”.

### 5.

AMENDMENTOF REGULATIONS.

The principal regulations are amended by inserting immediately after regulation 9
thereof, the following new regulation, 9A
“ 9A. International Marketing Agents.
(1) Subject to the provisions of regulation 9, an entity may be authorised
by the Citizenship by Investment Unit, to operate as an international marketing

agent for the purpose of marketing the Citizenship by Investment Programme
to potential clients.
(2) Where an entity is desirous of becoming an international marketing
agent, it shall apply to the Citizenship by Investment Unit and satisfy the
following conditions:
(a) provide proof of registration as a body corporate;
(b) pay an annual prescribed fee;
(c) provide a copy of the management approved anti-money
laundering policy, including know your customer policies;
(d) a due diligence background report on the owners and directors
of the entity.
(3) Where an entity submits the relevant documentation pursuant to
subregulation 2 and the application is approved, the Citizenship by Investment
Unitmay issue the entity with a certificate and post the name of the successful
international marketing agent on the website.
