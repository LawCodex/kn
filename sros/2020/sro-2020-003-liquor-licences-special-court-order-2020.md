# Liquor Licences (Special Court) Order, 2020

SAINT CHRISTOPHER AND NEVIS
STATUTORY RULES AND ORDERS
No. 3 of 2020

Liquor Licences (Special Court) Order, 2020

In exercise of the powers conferred by section 4 of the Liquor Licences Act,
Cap. 18.21, the Minister makes the following Order:
[Published 21st February 2020, Extra Ordinary Gazette No. 13 of 2020]

### 1.

Citation.
This Order may be cited as the Liquor Licences (Special Court) Order, 2020.

### 2.

Interpretation.

In this Order, “Act” means the Liquor Licences Act, Cap. 18.21, as amended by Act,
No. 5 of 2020.

### 3.

Date of Additional Special Court.

Pursuant to the provisions of section 4 of the Act, the Minister hereby appoints the
28th day of February, 2020 for the holding of an additional special sitting of the Magistrate’s
Court and each Magistrate shall hold a special Court in his or her district for the purpose of
(a) receiving and considering the applications of persons for licences; or
(b) the renewal of licences,
to sell intoxicating liquors within that district, whether by wholesale or retail.

Made this 21st day of February, 2020.

TIMOTHY HARRIS
Minister responsible for Liquor Licences
