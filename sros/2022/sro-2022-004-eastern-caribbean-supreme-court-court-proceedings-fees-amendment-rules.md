(AMENDMENT) RULES, 2022
In exercise of the powers conferred by section 17 of the Eastern Caribbean Supreme
Court Order set out as the Fifth Schedule to the West Indies Act, Cap. 1.01, the Chief Justice
and two other judges make the following rules:
[Published 4th April 2022, Extra-Ordinary Gazette No. 14 of 2022]
1\.

CITATIONAND COMMENCEMENT.

(1) These Rules may be cited as the Eastern Caribbean Supreme Court (Court
Proceedings Fees) (Amendment) Rules, 2022.
(2) These Rules shall come into force on the 4th day of April, 2022.
2\.

INTERPRETATION.

In these Rules the “principal Rules” means the Eastern Caribbean Supreme Court (Court
Proceedings Fees) Rules, No. 19 of 2017.
3\.
