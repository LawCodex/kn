# Census Order, 2022

SAINT CHRISTOPHER AND NEVIS
STATUTORY RULES AND ORDERS
No. 2 of 2022

Census Order, 2022

In exercise of the power conferred by section 4 of the Statistics Act, Cap. 23.31, the
Governor-General acting in accordance with the advice of the Cabinet of Ministers, makes
the following Order:
[Published 17th March 2022, Official Gazette No. 11 of 2022]

### 1.

Citation.
This Order may be cited as the Census Order, 2022.

### 2.

Interpretation.
In this Order“Act” means the Statistics Act, 2002;
“census night” means the period of 12 hours commencing at 6:00 p.m. of
3rd April 2022 and ending at 6:00 a.m. of 4th April 2022.

### 3.

Date of Census.
A Census of St. Kitts and Nevis shall be taken on the 3rd day of April 2022.

### 4.

Persons concerning whom information required.

Information for the purpose of the census shall be furnished with respect to all persons
who are alive at midnight on census night and who are(a) on land within the Federation of St. Kitts and Nevis; or
(b) on a ship or boat within territorial waters of St. Kitts and Nevis.
5

Persons to supply information.

Information for the purpose of the census, specified in the Schedule, with respect to the
persons described in section 4 shall be furnished by(a) the householder of person in charge of every private dwelling with respect to
all persons who pass census night on the premises of that dwelling;
(b) the proprietor or manager of every hotel, motel, boarding house, boarding
school, or hostel with respect to all persons who pass census night on the
premises of that hotel, motel, boarding house, boarding school or hostel or are
usually resident therein;

(c) the chief resident officer of every public or private hospital or other institution
with respect to all persons who pass census night on the premises of that
hospital or other institution or are usually resident therein;
(d) the superintendent of every prison and the officer in charge of every lock up
with respect to all persons who pass census night on the premises of that
prison or lock up;
(e) the master of every ship or boat described in sub-section 3(b) hereof with
respect to all persons who pass census night aboard the ship or boat.
