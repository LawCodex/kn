# Vehicles and Road Traffic Act, Cap. 15.06 Traffic Notice, 2022

SAINT CHRISTOPHER AND NEVIS
STATUTORY RULES AND ORDERS
No. 13 of 2022
VEHICLES AND ROAD TRAFFIC ACT, CAP. 15.06
TRAFFIC NOTICE, 2022

In exercise of the powers conferred on the Licensing Authority by section 81 of the Vehicles
and Road Traffic Act, Cap. 15.06 the following Notice is made:
[Published 23rd June 2022, Official Gazette No. 32 of 2022]

### 1.

In order to facilitate the smooth flow of traffic at Bird Rock during the St. Kitts
Music Festival, the following traffic restrictions will be in effect from 23 rd June
until 25th June 2022:
(a) traffic restrictions will be in effect from 5 p.m. of one day until 4 a.m. of the following
day commencing on the 23rd of June, 2022 until the 25th of June 2022;
(b) traffic will flow eastwards only on the roads immediately north and south
of the Kim Collins Stadium;
(c) only authorized vehicles will be permitted on the road immediately east of
the said stadium;
(d) no parking will be permitted on the streets immediately surrounding the
Kim Collins Stadium; and
(e) public parking for patrons of the Music Festival will be at Port Zante and
the playfield known as “The Paddock” located next to the Tucker Clarke
Primary School.

### 2.

Motorists shall comply with traffic signs which will be posted to indicate the
restrictions and a person who acts in contravention of this Notice shall be liable to
(a) have his or her vehicle towed; and
(b) on summary conviction to a fine not exceeding one thousand dollars in
accordance with the provisions of section 81 (2) of the vehicles and Road
Traffic Act.
Made this 22nd day of June 2022.
HILROY BRANDY
Licensing Authority
