# Gaming (Control) (Amendment of Schedule) Order, 2022

SAINT CHRISTOPHER AND NEVIS
STATUTORY RULES AND ORDERS
No. 24 of 2022
Gaming (Control) (Amendment of Schedule) Order
In exercise of the powers granted under section 46 of the Gaming (Control) Act,
No. 11 of 2021 the Minister of Finance makes the following Order:
[Published 22nd September 2022 – Official Gazette No. 56 of 2022]

### 1.

SHORT TITLE.
This Order may be cited as the Gaming (Control) (Amendment of Schedule) Order, 2022.

### 3.

AMENDMENT OF SECOND SCHEDULE.

The Gaming Control Act is amended in the Second Schedule paragraph 2, by inserting
immediately after subparagraph (g) the following new subparagraph (i):
“ (i) two (2) certified/notarized government issued photo identification
documents.
