# Legal Profession (Eligibility) Order, 2022

SAINT CHRISTOPHER AND NEVIS
STATUTORY RULES AND ORDERS
No. 32 of 2022
Legal Profession (Eligibility) Order, 2022
In exercise of the powers conferred by section 16 of the Legal Profession Act,
Cap. 3.28, the Minister makes the following Order:
[Published 2nd December 2022, Extra Ordinary Gazette No. 75 of 2022]

### 1.

Citation.
This Order may be cited as the Legal Profession (Eligibility) Order, 2022.

### 2.

Interpretation.
In this Order,
“Act” means the Legal Profession Act, Cap. 3.28.

### 3.

Eligibility.

Pursuant to the provisions of section 16 of the Act, Mr. Thomas Paul Wentworth Grant,
K.C. is declared eligible to be admitted by the High Court to Practice Law in Saint Christopher
and Nevis.

### 4.

Condition.

This Order is subject to the condition that Mr. Thomas Paul Wentworth Grant, K.C.
may practice law in Saint Christopher and Nevis
(a) solely in relation to Claim No. NEVHCV 2020/0064;
(b) on a temporary basis which shall automatically cease on the conclusion of the
claim referred to in paragraph (a).
Made this 30th day of November, 2022.
GARTH WILKIN
Minister responsible for Legal Affairs
