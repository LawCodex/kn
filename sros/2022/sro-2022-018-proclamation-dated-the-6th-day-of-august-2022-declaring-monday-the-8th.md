# Proclamation dated the 6th day of August 2022 declaring Monday the 8th day of August, 2022 to be observed as a National Holiday in Saint Christopher and Nevis

SAINT CHRISTOPHER AND NEVIS
STATUTORY RULES AND ORDERS
No. 18 of 2022

Proclamation dated the 6 th day of August 2022 declaring Monday the 8 th day of
August, 2022 to be observed as a National Holiday in Saint Christopher and Nevis

BY THE GOVERNOR-GENERAL OF
SAINT CHRISTOPHER AND NEVIS
A PROCLAMATION
SIR SAMUEL WEYMOUTH TAPLEY SEATON

G.C.M.G., C.V.O., Q.C., J.P., LL.D
GOVERNOR-GENERAL

[Published 6th August 2022, Extra Ordinary Gazette No. 42 of 2022.]
WHEREAS it is enacted by section 5 of the Public Holidays Act (Chapter 23.23) that it shall
be lawful for the Governor-General to issue a Proclamation appointing a special day or part of a day
to be reserved as a public holiday throughout Saint Christopher and Nevis or in any island, town,
district or village thereof
AND WHEREAS the Governor-General, on the advice of the Prime Minister considers it
expedient that Monday the 8th day of August, 2022 should be kept as a public holiday throughout
Saint Christopher and Nevis on the occasion of victory at the polls in the General Elections by the
St Kitts-Nevis Labour Party
NOW, THEREFORE, I, SIR SAMUEL WEYMOUTH TAPLEY SEATON, Knight Grand
Cross of the Most Distinguished Order of Saint Michael and Saint George, Commander of the
Royal Victorian Order, Queen's Counsel, Justice of the Peace, Doctor of Laws, Governor-General of
Saint Christopher and Nevis, in exercise of all the powers conferred on me by the said Act, appoint
Monday the 8 th day of August, 2022 to be observed as a Public Holiday throughout
Saint Christopher and Nevis
AND all our officers and loving subjects in Saint Christopher and Nevis and all others whom it
may concern are hereby required to take due notice hereof and to give their ready obedience accordingly.

LS

GIVEN under my hand and the Public Seal
of Saint Christopher and Nevis at
Government House St Kitts this 6th day of
August 2022, and in the seventy-first year
of Her Majesty’s reign.
GOD SAVE THE QUEEN!
