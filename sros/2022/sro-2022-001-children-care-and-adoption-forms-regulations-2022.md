# Children Care and Adoption (Forms) Regulations, 2022

SAINT CHRISTOPHER AND NEVIS
STATUTORY RULES AND ORDERS
No. 1 of 2022

CHILDREN CARE AND ADOPTION (FORMS) REGULATIONS

In exercise of the powers conferred by section 140 of the Children Care and Adoption
Act, Cap. 12.01, the Minister makes the following Regulations
[Published 10th February 2022, Official Gazette No. 6 of 2022]

### 1.

CITATION.

These Regulations may be cited as the Children Care and Adoption (Forms)
Regulations, 2022.

### 2.

INTERPRETATION.

In these Regulations, the expression “Act” means the Children Care and Adoption Act,
Cap. 12.01.

### 3.

FORMS.

(1) The Board may, pursuant to the provisions of section 25(2) of the Act, apply to the
Court for a warrant in the form set out as Form 1 in the Schedule, where
(a) the Board believes that a child is in need of care and protection;
(b) that a less intrusive course of action will not adequately protect the health or
safety of the child; and
(c) the parent or any other person caring for the child has refused to give up the
child or to permit entry to the place or premises where the Board has reason
to believe that the child is present.
(2) Where a warrant is granted pursuant to the application in sub-regulation (1), the
warrant shall be issued in the form set out as Form 2 in the Schedule.
(3) The Board may, pursuant to the provisions of section 27(2) of the Act, apply to the
Court for an Emergency Protection Order in the form set out as Form 3 in the Schedule, where
the Board is satisfied that a child is suffering or is likely to suffer significant harm and is in
need of urgent care and protection.
(4) Where an application is granted pursuant to the application in sub-regulation (3),
the Emergency Protection Order shall be issued in the form set out as Form 4 in the Schedule.
(5) The Board may, pursuant to the provisions of section 32(1) of the Act, apply to the
Court for an Assessment Order in the form set out as Form 5 in the Schedule.

(6) Where an application is granted pursuant to the application in sub-regulation (5),
the Assessment Order shall be issued in the form set out as Form 5 in the Schedule.
(7) The Board may, pursuant to the provisions of section 36(2) of the Act, apply for a
Care Order in the form set out as Form 7, in relation to a child where it is satisfied that a child
is in need of care and protection pursuant to the provisions of section 44(1) of the Act.
(8) Where the Board makes an application for a Care Order pursuant to sub-regulation
(7), the Board shall, in accordance with section 38 of the Act, notify the parents or guardian
of the child in respect of whom the Care Order is sought, in the form set out as Form 8 in the
Schedule.
(9) Where an application is granted pursuant to the application in sub-regulation (7),
the Care Order shall be issued in the form set out as Form 9 in the Schedule.
(10) The Court may, pursuant to the provisions of section 41 of the Act, make an Interim
Care Order in relation to a child, in the form set out as Form 10, after a care application is made
and before that application is finally determined.
(11) The Board may, pursuant to the provisions of section 51 of the Act, apply for a
Supervision Order in the form set out as Form 11, where the Board is satisfied that there is
need for continuous supervision of a child.
(12) Where the Board makes an application for a Supervision Order pursuant to
sub-regulation (11), the Board shall notify the parents or guardians of the child in respect of
whom the Supervision Order is sought, in the form set out as Form 12 in the Schedule.
(13) The Court may, pursuant to the provisions of section 52 of the Act, grant a
Supervision Order in the form set out as Form 13 in the Schedule.
(14) Where the Board makes an application to the Court for a Care Order pursuant to the
provisions of section 57 of the Act, the Board shall present a Care Plan to the Court in the
form set out as Form 14 in the Schedule.
(15) The Board may, pursuant to the provisions of section 64 of the Act, apply to the
Court for a Compulsory Assistance Order, in the form set out as Form 15 in the Schedule.
(16) The Court may, subject to the provisions of 64(2), (3), (4) and 5, grant a compulsory
assistance order in the form set out as Form 16 in the Schedule.
(17) Where a child is the subject of any proceedings before a Court, any party to the
proceedings may, pursuant to the provisions of section 65(1) of the Act, apply for a Contact
Order, in the form set out as Form 17 in the Schedule.
(18) The Court may, subject to the provisions of section 65(1) and (2), grant a Contact
Order in the form set out as Form 18 in the Schedule.
(19) The Board may, pursuant to the provisions of section 72(4) and with the leave of the
Court, apply to the Court for variation or revocation of a Care Order in the form set out as
Form 19 in the Schedule.
(20) The Board may, pursuant to the provisions of sections 79 and 80 of the Act, apply
for a Recovery Order in the form set out as Form 20 in the Schedule.
(21) The Court may, pursuant to the provisions of section 79, grant a Recovery Order in
the form set out as Form 21 in the Schedule.

(22) The Court may, pursuant to the provisions of section 82 of the Act, order a parent
to contribute towards the maintenance of the child in the form set out as Form 22 in the
Schedule.
