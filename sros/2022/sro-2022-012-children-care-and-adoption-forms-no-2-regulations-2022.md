# Children Care and Adoption (Forms) (No. 2) Regulations, 2022

STATUTORY RULES AND ORDERS
No. 12 of 2022

CHILDREN CARE AND ADOPTION (FORMS) (NO. 2) REGULATIONS

In exercise of the powers conferred by section 140 of the Children Care and Adoption Act,
Cap. 12.01 the Minister makes the following Regulations.
[Published 16th June 2022, Official Gazette No. 31 of 2022]

### 1.

CITATION.

These Regulations may be cited as the Children Care and Adoption (Forms) (No. 2)
Regulations, 2022.

### 2.

INTERPRETATION.
In these Regulations, unless the context otherwise requires,
“Act” means the Children Care and Adoption Act, Cap. 12.01;
“Committee” means the Adoption Committee appointed by the Probation and Child
Welfare Board pursuant to section 85 of the Act.

### 3.

FORMS.

(1) A single person or a couple may, pursuant to the provisions of section 94 of the Act,
apply to the Adoption Committee for the placement of a child in the home of the Applicant,
in the form set out as Form 1 in Schedule 1.
(2) Where the Committee receives an application for adoption, the Committee shall
authorise the carrying out of a home study assessment on the home of the Applicant,
pursuant to section 94(2) of the Act in the form set out as Form 2 in Schedule 1.
(3) Where after conducting a home study assessment on the applicant, the Committee
decides that an Applicant is
(a) a suitable and capable person for the placement of a child in the home of that
person for the purposes of adoption, the Committee shall, pursuant to
section 95(1)(a) of the Act, issue to the Applicant, the notice of its approval of
the application in the form set out as Form 3 in Schedule 1.
(b) an unsuitable or incapable person for the placement of a child in the home of
that person for the purposes of adoption, the Committee shall, pursuant to
section 95(1)(b) of the Act, issue to the Applicant the notice of its refusal of
the application in the form set out as Form 4 in Schedule 1.

(4) Where, in accordance with section 95(1) of the Act, the Committee approves a
placement application, the Committee shall, pursuant to section 102(1)(g) of the Act, enter
into a placement agreement with the prospective adoptive parent, in the form set out as
Form 5 in Schedule 1.
(5) Where an Applicant is aggrieved by the decision of the Committee in relation to an
application for placement, the Applicant may, pursuant to section 96 of the Act, not later than
two weeks after the date of receipt of the written notice of the decision of the Committee,
apply to the Minister for review of that decision in the form set out as Form 6 in the Schedule.
(6) Where an applicant applies for a review of the decision of the Committee, the
Minister shall, pursuant to section 97(2), appoint a review panel by instrument in writing in
the form set out as Form 7 in the Schedule.
(7) Where the Committee receives the recommendation of the review panel, and reviews
that recommendation, the Committee shall, pursuant to section 99 of the Act, provide a
notice to the applicant of the outcome of the review in the form set out as Form 8.
(8) (a) Where a child is placed in the home of a prospective adoptive parent, the
Adoption Committee shall, pursuant to section 103(1) of the Act, appoint an
approved person to supervise the placement of the child in the home during
the placement period, in the form set out as Form 9(a) in Schedule 1.
(b) Upon completion of the placement period, the approved person shall, pursuant
to section 103(2) of the Act, prepare a Post Placement Report and shall submit
that report to the Committee in the form set out as 9(b) in Schedule 1.
(9) Where a prospective adoptive parent is desirous of adopting a child who was
placed in his or her home for the placement period, the prospective adoptive parent may,
pursuant to section 113(1) of the Act, apply to the Court for an adoption order in the form set
out as Form 10 in Schedule 1.
(10) Where a prospective adoptive parent applies to the Court for an adoption order, he
or she shall, pursuant to section 115(1) of the Act, issue a notice of that application to the
Adoption Committee in the form set out as Form 11 in Schedule 1.
(11) Where a consent to the adoption by the child himself or herself, is required pursuant
to sections 104(1)(a) and 105(1) of the Act, the consent shall be in the form set out as Form
12(a) in Schedule 1.
(12) Where a consent to the adoption of a child is required pursuant to sections 104(1)(b),
(c) and 105 of the Act, the consent shall be in the form set out as Form (12)(b) in Schedule 1.
(13) Where a person has consented to the adoption of a child, he or she may, in
accordance with section 107(1) of the Act, withdraw his consent in the form set out as Form
13(a) in Schedule 1.
(14) Where a child is required to give his or her consent to an adoption, the child may,
pursuant to the provisions of section 107(4) of the Act, withdraw his or her consent in the
form set out as Form 13(b) in Schedule 1.
(15) Where the Court grants an Adoption Order, authorizing the adoption of a named
child, then the Order shall be in the form set out as Form 14 in Schedule 1.

(16) Where a person who is not a citizen or resident of Saint Christopher and Nevis is
desirous of adopting a child in Saint Christopher and Nevis, that person shall, pursuant to
the provisions of section 125(1) of the Act, apply to the Adoption Committee for an intercountry adoption in the form set out as Form 15 in Schedule 1.
(17) Where a person makes an application pursuant to section 125(1) of the Act, he or
she shall, pursuant to section 125(2) of the Act, submit an affidavit in support of that
application in the form set out as Form 16 in Schedule 1.
(18) Where pursuant to section 125 (4), the Adoption Committee approves the application
for an Inter-Country Adoption and recommends that the applicant apply to the Court for an
Adoption Order, the applicant may do so, using the form set out as Form 17 in Schedule 1.
(19) Where, pursuant to section 125(10) the Court grants a Provisional Inter-Country
Adoption Order, authorising the adoption of a child by a person who is not ordinarily
resident in Saint Christopher and Nevis for the transfer of the child abroad, then the Order
shall be in the form set out as Form 18 in Schedule 1.
(20) Where the Court determines that it is necessary to postpone the determination on
an application for adoption, the Court may issue an interim order pursuant to section 119 in
the form set out as Form 19 in Schedule 1.
