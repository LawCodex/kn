# Eastern Caribbean Supreme Court (Judicial Officers Pensions) Act (Commencement) Order, 2022

SAINT CHRISTOPHER AND NEVIS
STATUTORY RULES AND ORDERS
No. 9 of 2022
Eastern Caribbean Supreme Court (Judicial Officers Pensions) Act
(Commencement) Order, 2022.

In exercise of the power conferred by section 1 (2) of the Eastern Caribbean Supreme
Court (Judicial Officers Pensions) Act, the Minister of Finance makes the following Order:
[Published 19th May 2022, Official Gazette No. 26 of 2022]

### 1.

Citation.

This Order may be cited as the Eastern Caribbean Supreme Court (Judicial Officers
Pensions) Act (Commencement) Order, 2022.

### 2.

COMMENCEMENT DAY.

The Eastern Caribbean Supreme Court (Judicial Officers Pensions) Act, No. 2 of 2021
shall come into operation on the 1st day of January, 2022.
Made this 13th day of May, 2022.

TIMOTHY S. HARRIS
Minister of Finance
