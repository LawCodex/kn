# Law Commission (Revised Edition of Laws 2020) (Inclusion or Removal of Pages) Order, 2022

SAINT CHRISTOPHER AND NEVIS
STATUTORY RULES AND ORDERS
No. 28 of 2022
Law Commission (Revised Edition of Laws 2020) (Inclusion or Removal of Pages)
Order, 2022.

In exercise of the power conferred by section 30 of the Law Commission Act,
Cap. 1.03, the Law Reform Commissioner makes the following Order:
[Published 17th November 2022, Official Gazette No. 68 of 2022]

### 1.

Citation.

This Order may be cited as the Law Commission (Revised Edition of Laws 2020)
(Inclusion or Removal of Pages) Order, 2022.

### 2.

Interpretation.
In this Order, “Act” means the Law Commission Act, Cap. 1.03.

### 3.

Inclusion or Removal of Pages.

Pursuant to section 30 of the Act, the laws enacted and in force in Saint Christopher and
Nevis from 1st January 2018 to 31st December 2020 shall be included in the Revised Edition
of Laws 2020 which shall also contain the 2002, 2009 and 2017 revised laws.
Made this 11th day of November, 2022.

JOSEPHINE MALLALIEU
Law Reform Commissioner
