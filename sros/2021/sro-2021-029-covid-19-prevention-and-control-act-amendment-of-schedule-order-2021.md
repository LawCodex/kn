# COVID-19 Prevention and Control Act (Amendment of Schedule) Order, 2021

SAINT CHRISTOPHER AND NEVIS
STATUTORY RULES AND ORDERS
No. 29 of 2021
COVID-19 Prevention and Control Act (Amendment of Schedule) Order, 2021
In exercise of the power conferred by section 27 of the COVID-19 (Prevention and
Control) Act, No. 16 of 2020 the Minister makes the following Order:
[Published 2nd July 2021 – Extra-Ordinary Gazette No. 40 of 2021]

### 1.

Citation.

This Order may be cited as the COVID-19 Prevention and Control Act (Amendment
of Schedule) Order, 2021.

### 2.

Interpretation.
“Act” means the COVID-19 (Prevention and Control) Act, No. 16 of 2020.

### 3.

Amendment of Schedule VII.

Schedule VII of the Act is amended by replacing the Fixed Penalty Notice (Ticket) as
