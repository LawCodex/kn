# Cannabis Act (Parts I and II) (Appointed Day) Order, 2021

SAINT CHRISTOPHER AND NEVIS
STATUTORY RULES AND ORDERS
No. 13 of 2021

Cannabis Act (Parts I and II) (Appointed Day) Order, 2021.

In exercise of the powers conferred by section 1 of the Cannabis Act, No. 8 of 2020, the
Minister makes the following Order:
[Published 29th April 2021, Official Gazette No. 25 of 2021]

### 1.

CITATION.

This Order may be cited as the Cannabis Act (Parts I and II) (Appointed Day)
Order, 2021.

### 2.

INTERPRETATION.
In this Order, “Act” means the Cannabis Act, No. 8 of 2020.

### 3.

APPOINTED DAY.

## Part I and Part II of the Act shall come into operation on the 30th day of April, 2021.

Made this 22nd day of April 2021.

TIMOTHY HARRIS
Prime Minister

GOVERNMENT PRINTERY
ST KITTS W I
