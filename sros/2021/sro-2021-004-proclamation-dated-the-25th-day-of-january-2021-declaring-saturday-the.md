# Proclamation dated the 25th day of January, 2021 declaring Saturday the 13th day of February, 2021 to be observed as National Volunteer Day in Saint Christopher and Nevis

SAINT CHRISTOPHER AND NEVIS
STATUTORY RULES AND ORDERS
No. 4 of 2021
Proclamation dated the 25 th day of January, 2021 declaring Saturday the 13 th day
of February, 2021 to be observed as National Volunteer Day in Saint Christopher
and Nevis
BY THE GOVERNOR-GENERAL
OF SAINT CHRISTOPHER AND NEVIS
A PROCLAMATION
SIR S. W. TAPLEY SEATON
GCMG, CVO, QC, JP, LLD
Governor-General
[Published 4 th February 2021, Official Gazette No. 6 of 2021.]

WHEREAS it is proposed to observe an annual initiative intended to foster a greater
sense of civic identity, unity, service and responsibility in our fellowmen, women, boys and girls
AND WHEREAS it is proposed to observe Saturday 13 th February, 2021 as
National Volunteer Day to enable non-governmental and service organizations to join with
the Government in supporting and positively impacting and uplifting our communities by
beautifying our neighbourhoods, by planting trees and cleaning up their environs and
other community activities and on this day to promote The Volunteer Corps
NOW THEREFORE, I, Sir Samuel Weymouth Tapley Seaton, Knight Grand Cross of
the Most Distinguished Order of Saint Michael and Saint George, Commander of the Royal
Victorian Order, Queen’s Counsel, Justice of the Peace, Doctor of Laws, Governor-General
of the Federation of Saint Christopher and Nevis, in exercise of all powers me thereunto
enabling and in accordance with the advice of the Cabinet DO HEREBY declare that
Saturday 13th February, 2021 be observed as National Volunteer Day
AND all our officers and loving subjects in Saint Christopher and Nevis and all
others whom it may concern are hereby required to take due notice hereof and to give their
ready obedience accordingly.

LS

GIVEN under my hand and the Public Seal of
Saint Christopher and Nevis, at Government
House this 25th day of January, 2021, and in
the sixty-ninth year of Her Majesty's reign.

GOD SAVE THE QUEEN!
