# Resolution of the National Assembly of Saint Christopher and Nevis to celebrate the centennial anniversary of Pioneer Reverend Dr. William M. Connor OBE

SAINT CHRISTOPHER AND NEVIS
STATUTORY RULES AND ORDERS
No. 33 of 2021
RESOLUTION
Resolution of the National Assembly of Saint Christopher and Nevis to celebrate
the centennial anniversary of Pioneer Reverend Dr. William M. Connor OBE

[Published 6 th August 2021 – Extra-Ordinary Gazette No. 48 of 2021]

WHEREAS William Manasseh Connor, also known as Pastor Connor or
Brother Connor, was born on the 21st day of June, on the first day of summer in 1921, in
Russell Village, West Basseterre and on 11th November, 1990, he made his triumphant
homeward march and went to be with the Lord.
AND WHEREAS at the tender age of ten, having lost his father, that great loss
forced him to leave school and to secure employment in the cotton fields of Needsmust
Estate.
AND WHEREAS in 1943, his career with the church began with his employment as
sexton of the St Georges Anglican Church in Basseterre, a post he held for 19 years until he
resigned in 1962.
AND WHEREAS he seriously expanded his religious and social work in
September 1949 with the initiation of various social programs, including, the establishment
of a Sunday School which would grow from thirteen children in attendance at the first
session, to include various branches being established in other parts of the Island, such as,
Old Road, St Peters and the famous Salem Sunday School under the cock and hen tree in
James Street New Town.
AND WHEREAS in 1949 he organized a Sunday School Savings Corp. which is still
in operation today.
AND WHEREAS in 1957 he began a typing school, armed with only one type writer
and one sole purpose and that was to empower young ladies with training in various
administrative skills. From these humble beginnings the school grew and was highly
operational until the advent of computers. In 1958 he then turned his attention to provide
assistance for young men with the establishment of the Wycliffe Crusaders.
AND WHEREAS in 1964, he introduced the idea of church transportation in the
Federation with the purchase of the first church van. In 1966, he also introduced the idea of
Vacation Bible School in the island of Saint Christopher.
AND WHEREAS through his interventions Sunday School would gain a solid footing
giving birth to the Baptist Movement in the Federation. This led to the establishment of the
Antioch Baptist Church which is a recognized beacon in the community and the first

established Baptist church in the Federation. Pastor Connor and 21 members painstakingly
structured the foundation of the church on 5th March, 1963.
AND WHEREAS following the formation of the Antioch Baptist Church,
Pastor Connor established the Mount Carmel Baptist Church in Bourryeau, St Kitts in 1965
and the Calvary Baptist Church in St Pauls, now the Calvary Baptist Tabernacle in
Romney Ground, Sandy Point which first began as a Sunday School in 1965, then it was
organized into a mission in 1971 and then it was structured into a church in 1975, with
Pastor Connor as the first pastor.
AND WHEREAS he received several honours during his lifetime, including, in 1979
Her Majesty Queen Elizabeth II appointed him an Officer of the British Empire. In 1979, he
was also awarded the Doctor of Divinity by the Caribbean Evangelical Association
Incorporated. In 1981, he was appointed an Honourary Deputy Sherriff of the State of
Texas, USA. In 1982, he was awarded Citizen of the Year by the Organisation of Progressive
Nationals of St Kitts as eminently and ethically qualified in the field of Sociology. In 1983,
before some twenty three thousand people, he was afforded the great honour of delivering
the keynote address on that momentous occasion when Saint Christopher and Nevis gained
her independence from England. In 1983, he was also commissioned to the Order of Kentucky
Colonels of the State of Kentucky, USA. In 1986, he was awarded an Honorary Citizenship
of the City of Lincoln in the State of Illinois, USA. In 1988, he was appointed as
Lieutenant Colonel Aide De Camp Governor, Staff by His Excellency the Governor of the
State of Georgia, USA.
AND WHEREAS he was honoured by the Government of Saint Christopher
and Nevis by having the new St Johnson Village Primary School named in his honour as the
Dr. William Connor Primary School.
AND WHEREAS every nation has its heroes, its visionaries, its champions and
Reverend Dr. William M. Connor, should be categorized as one of the champions of the
Federation of Saint Christopher and Nevis.
AND WHEREAS the National Assembly hereby wishes to join in the celebration of
the centennial anniversary of Pioneer Reverend Dr. William M. Connor OBE.
NOW BE IT RESOLVED THEREFORE that the National Assembly of
Saint Christopher and Nevis hereby joins in the celebration of the centennial anniversary of
Pioneer Reverend Dr. William M. Connor OBE.

Approved by the National Assembly this 5th day of August, 2021.

SONIA BODDIE-THOMPSON
Clerk of the National Assembly
