# Legal Profession (Eligibility) (Amendment of SRO No. 33 of 2020) Order, 2021

SAINT CHRISTOPHER AND NEVIS
STATUTORY RULES AND ORDERS
No. 19 of 2021
Legal Profession (Eligibility) (Amendment of SRO No. 33 of 2020) Order, 2021

In exercise of the powers conferred by section 16 of the Legal Profession Act,
Cap. 3.28, the Minister makes the following Order:
[Published 10th June 2021, Official Gazette No. 33 of 2021]

### 1.

Citation.

This Order may be cited as the Legal Profession (Eligibility) (Amendment of
SRO No. 33 of 2020) Order, 2021.

### 2.

Interpretation.
In this Order,

“Principal Order” means the Legal Profession (Eligibility) (No. 2) Order,
SRO No. 33 of 2020.

### 3.

Amendment of Order.
The Principal Order is amended by replacing section 4 as follows
“4. Condition.
This Order is subject to the condition that Mr. Philip Riches, Q.C. may practice
law in Saint Christopher and Nevis solely in relation to:
(a) Claim No. NEVHCV 2018/0143;
(b) Claim No. NEVHCV 2018/0144; and
(c) Claim No. SKBHCV 2021/0086,
or other related or incidental matters.”.
Made this 9th day of June, 2021.

VINCENT BYRON
Minister responsible for Legal Affairs
