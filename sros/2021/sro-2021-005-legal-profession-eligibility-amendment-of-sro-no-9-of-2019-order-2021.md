# Legal Profession (Eligibility) (Amendment of SRO No. 9 of 2019) Order, 2021

SAINT CHRISTOPHER AND NEVIS
STATUTORY RULES AND ORDERS
No. 5 of 2021
Legal Profession (Eligibility) (Amendment of SRO No. 9 of 2019) Order, 2021

In exercise of the powers conferred by section 16 of the Legal Profession Act,
Cap. 3.28, the Minister makes the following Order:
[Published 18th February 2021, Official Gazette No. 8 of 2021]

### 1.

Citation.

This Order may be cited as the Legal Profession (Eligibility) (Amendment of
SRO No. 9 of 2019) Order, 2021.

### 2.

Interpretation.
In this Order,

“Principal Order” means the Legal Profession (Eligibility) (No. 3) Order,
SRO No. 9 of 2019.

### 3.

Amendment of Order.
The Principal Order is amended by replacing section 4 as follows
“4. Condition.
This Order is subject to the condition that Mr. Colin Douglas McKie, Q.C. may
practice law in Saint Christopher and Nevis solely in relation to:
(a) Claim No. NEVHCV 2018/0143;
(b) Claim No. NEVHCV 2018/0144; and
(c) Claim No. NEVHCV 2020/0123,
or other related or incidental matters.”.
Made this 10th day of February, 2021.

VINCENT BYRON
Minister responsible for Legal Affairs
