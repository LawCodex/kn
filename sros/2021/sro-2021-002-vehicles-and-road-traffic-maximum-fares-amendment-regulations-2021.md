# Vehicles and Road Traffic (Maximum Fares) (Amendment) Regulations, 2021

SAINT CHRISTOPHER AND NEVIS
STATUTORY RULES AND ORDERS
No. 2 of 2021
Vehicles and Road Traffic (Maximum Fares) (Amendment) Regulations, 2021
In exercise of the powers conferred by section 80 of the Vehicles and Road Traffic Act,
Cap 15.06, the Minister responsible for Transport makes the following Regulations:
[Published 4th February 2021 – Official Gazette No. 6 of 2021]

### 1.

Citation.

These Regulations may be cited as the Vehicles and Road Traffic (Maximum Fares)
(Amendment) Regulations, 2021.

### 2.

Interpretation.
In these Regulations, unless the context otherwise requires,
“principal Regulations” means the Vehicles and Road Traffic (Maximum Fares)
Regulations as set out under the Seventh Schedule of the Vehicles and Road
Traffic Act, Cap. 15.06.
