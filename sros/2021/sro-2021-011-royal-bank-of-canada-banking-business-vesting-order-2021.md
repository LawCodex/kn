# Royal Bank of Canada Banking Business Vesting Order, 2021

SAINT CHRISTOPHER AND NEVIS
STATUTORY RULES AND ORDERS
No. 11 of 2021

ROYAL BANK OF CANADA BANKING BUSINESS VESTING ORDER, 2021

In exercise of the power conferred by section 175 of the Banking Act, No. 1 of 2015, the
Minister responsible for Finance makes this Order:
[Published 30th March 2021, Extra-Ordinary Gazette No. 16 of 2021]

### 1.

Citation and Commencement.

(1) This Order may be cited as the Royal Bank of Canada Banking Business Vesting
Order, 2021.
(2) This Order shall come into force on the 1st day of April 2021 (hereinafter called the
“appointed day”).

### 2.

Interpretation.
In this Order —
“Agreement” means the St. Kitts Branch Asset Purchase Agreement made on the
11th day of December 2019 between the Transferor Financial Institution and
theTransferee Financial Institution, as amended by the Amendment to St. Kitts
Branch Asset Purchase Agreement made on the 24th day of January 2020 and as
further amended by the Second Amendment to St. Kitts Branch Asset Purchase
Agreement made on the 28th day of January 2021, relating to the transfer of the
St. Kitts Branch Banking Business of the Transferor Financial Institution;
“Assumed Liabilities” has the meaning assigned to it in the Agreement;
“Purchased Assets” has the meaning assigned to it in the Agreement, as more
particularly described in Schedule 1 to this Order;
“St. Kitts Branch Banking Business” means the banking business carried on by
the Transferor Financial Institution in Saint Christopher and Nevis comprising
all the Purchased Assets, Assumed Liabilities, property, rights and obligations
as specified in and intended by the Agreement;
“Transferee Financial Institution” means The Bank of Nevis Limited, a corporation
incorporated and licensed to carry on banking business under the laws of
Saint Christopher and Nevis; and
“Transferor Financial Institution” means Royal Bank of Canada, a Schedule I
bank existing under the laws of Canada and registered and licensed to carry on
banking business in Saint Christopher and Nevis.

### 3.

Vesting of Banking Business.

The St. Kitts Branch Banking Business of the Transferor Financial Institution is, without
further assurance, hereby transferred to and vested in the Transferee Financial Institution
with effect from the appointed day.
Schedule 1
(1) Purchased Assets means the following assets of the St. Kitts Branch Banking Business:
(a) the Cash Items;
(b) the Loans;
(c) the Branch Leased Real Property and Branch Real Property Leases;
(d) the Branch Owned Real Property;
(e) the Receivables;
(f) the Fixed Assets;
(g) the Branch Contracts;
(h) the Equity Interests set forth in Section 1.1(c) of the Seller’s Disclosure Letter;
(i) the Off-Balance Sheet Items; and
(j) the Customer Books and Records.
(2) Capitalized terms used in this Schedule but not otherwise defined herein shall have the
meaning assigned to such terms in the Agreement.

Made this 30th day of March, 2021.

TIMOTHY S HARRIS
Minister responsible for Finance
