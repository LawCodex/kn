# Nevis Trust and Corporate Service Providers Ordinance (Commencement) Order, 2021

SAINT CHRISTOPHER AND NEVIS
STATUTORY RULES AND ORDERS
No. 4 of 2021
ISLAND OF NEVIS
This Order is made by the Minister with responsibility for Finance in the Nevis Island
Administration, in exercise of the powers conferred upon him by section 1 of the
Nevis Trust and Corporate Service Providers Ordinance (No. 2 of 2021) and all
other powers thereunto him enabling.

[Published 22 nd April 2021 – Official Gazette No. 24 of 2021]

### 1.

Short Title.

This Order may be cited as the NEVIS TRUST AND CORPORATE SERVICE
PROVIDERS ORDINANCE (COMMENCEMENT) ORDER, 2021.

### 2.

Interpretation.

In exercise of the powers conferred upon me by section 1 of the Nevis Trust and
Corporate Service Providers Ordinance (No. 2 of 2021) and all other powers thereunto me
enabling I, MARK A. G. BRANTLEY, Minister of Finance, do hereby appoint the 1st day of
May, 2021, as the day on which this said Ordinance shall come into force.
Made by the Minister this 20th day of April, 2021.
MARK A. G. BRANTLEY
Minister responsible for Finance

GOVERNMENT PRINTERY
