# Electronic Transactions Act Financial Services Notice

SAINT CHRISTOPHER AND NEVIS
STATUTORY RULES AND ORDERS
No. 3 of 2021
ISLAND OF NEVIS
Electronic Transactions Act Financial Services Notice

[Published 25th February 2021, Official Gazette No. 9 of 2021]
In exercise of the powers conferred by section 13(1) of the Electronic Transactions Act,
Cap. 18.44, the Minister for Finance makes the following Notice:
“ Effective 15th day of March 2021 and pursuant to the Electronic Transactions Act,
Cap. 18.44, the Registrar of Corporations, Companies, Trusts and Foundations of the
Financial Services (Regulation and Supervision) Department, Nevis Island Administration
shall process electronic records under an online registered portal called ‘Corporate Registry
Integrated Search System’ (“CRISS”) where Trust and Corporate Service Providers and
Registered Agents can file scanned copies of documents in respect of the following matters:
(i) Nevis Business Corporation Ordinance, No. 1 of 2017 (“NBCO”)
•

Section 2 - Definition of Articles of Incorporation

•

Section 4 (8) a, b, c, d - Form of Instrument and Filing

•

Section 5 - Certificates and Certified Copies as evidence

•

Section 18 (5) - Service of Process

•

Section 23 (3) - Reservation of Name

•

Section 95 (5) - Voting Trust

•

Section 110 (4) - Merger or Consolidation of Domestic Corporations

•

Section 111 (3) - Merger of Subsidiary Corporations

(ii) Nevis Limited Liability Company Ordinance, No. 2 of 2017 (“NLLCO”)
•

Section 2 (1) a - Definition of Articles of Organisation

•

Section 4 (8) a, b, c, d - Form of Instrument and Filing

•

Section 5 - Certificates and Certified Copies as evidence

•

Section 16 (5) - Service of Process

•

Section 21 - Reservation of Name

•

Section 71 (4) - Merger or Consolidation of Domestic Limited Liability Corporations

(iii) Multiform Foundations Ordinance, Cap. 7.08 (N) (“MFO”)
•

Section 2 (1) - Definition for Memorandum of Establishment

•

Section 4 - Registration Documents (Establishment and Constitution)

•

Section 6 (5) - Change in Proper Law

•

Section 8 (4) - Amendment to Constitution

•

Section 10 (3) - Amendment to the Multiform

•

Section 15 (2) - Amendment to Name

•

Section 64 (1) - Registration Documents (Constitution or Transformation)

•

Section 74 (1) - Registration Documents (Conversion or Consolidation or Merger)

•

Section 95 (2) g - Annual Returns

•

Section 111 (6) a, b, c - Form of Instrument and Filing

•

Section 112 - Certificates and Certified Copies as evidence.

Made this 24th day of February, 2021.
