# Nevis International Banking Regulations, 2021

SAINT CHRISTOPHER AND NEVIS
STATUTORY RULES AND ORDERS
No. 1 of 2021
ISLAND OF NEVIS

The Minister of Finance in exercise of the powers conferred upon him by section 84 of
the Nevis International Banking Ordinance, No. 1 of 2014, and all other powers thereunder
enabling him makes the following Regulations
[Published 18th February 2021, Official Gazette No. 8 of 2021]

### 1.

Citation.

These Regulations may be cited as the NEVIS INTERNATIONAL BANKING
REGULATIONS, 2021.

### 2.

Interpretation.
“Ordinance” means the Nevis International Banking Ordinance, No. 1 of 2014;

2\.

Applicable fees.

The prescribed fees under the Ordinance, fees for the processing of requests for regulatory
approval and fees for ancillary matters shall be non-refundable and are specified in
Schedule 1 (A) and (B) to these Regulations.

### 3.

Repeal.

Schedule 1 of the Nevis International Banking Regulations, No. 2 of 2015 is hereby
repealed and replaced with Schedule 1(A) and (B) to these Regulations.

### 4.

Commencement.
These Regulations shall come into effect on the 1st day of February, 2021.
