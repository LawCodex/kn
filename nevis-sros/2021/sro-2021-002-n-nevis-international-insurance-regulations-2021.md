# Nevis International Insurance Regulations, 2021

SAINT CHRISTOPHER AND NEVIS
STATUTORY RULES AND ORDERS
No. 2 of 2021
ISLAND OF NEVIS

The Minister of Finance in exercise of the powers conferred upon him by section 51 of
the Nevis International Insurance Ordinance, Cap. 7.07 and all other powers thereunder
enabling him makes the following Regulations.
[Published 18th February 2021, Official Gazette No. 8 of 2021]

### 1.

Citation.

These Regulations may be cited as the NEVIS INTERNATIONAL INSURANCE
REGULATIONS, 2021.

### 2.

Interpretation.
In these Regulations unless the context otherwise requires“Principal Ordinance” means the Nevis International Insurance Ordinance, Cap 7.07;

“Principal Regulations” mean the Nevis International Insurance Regulations,
No. 6 of 2004.

### 3.

Commencement.
These Regulations shall come into effect on the 1st day of July, 2021.
