# RESOLUTION of the Nevis Island Administration, the Administration, acting through its Cabinet Ministers, the Ministers;

SAINT CHRISTOPHER AND NEVIS
STATUTORY RULES AND ORDERS
ISLAND OF NEVIS
No. 7 of 2020
RESOLUTION of the Nevis Island Administration, the Administration, acting
through its Cabinet Ministers, the Ministers;
[Published 17th December 2020, Official Gazette No. 90 of 2020]
WHEREAS, the Cabinet of Ministers of the Nevis Island Administration (“the
Administration”) in its meeting on the 4th day of November, 2020, passed, adopted and consented
to the following recitals and resolution:
AND WHEREAS, the Administration, acting through its Ministry of Finance, is desirous
of obtaining an overdraft facility at the Bank of Nevis Limited (“the Bank”) with a limit not
exceeding Twenty Five Million Eastern Caribbean Dollars (EC$25,000,000.00) (“the Facility”)
for the period 1st January to 31st December, 2021;
AND WHEREAS, the new overdraft facility is to replace the previous overdraft that has
subsequently been restructured into a long term loan facility;
AND WHEREAS, the Bank has requested a Resolution from the Administration signifying
the agreement of the Administration to contract this new Facility;
AND WHEREAS the Facility will be secured by 150 acres of land situated at New
River Estate in the Parish of Saint James’ in the Island of Nevis registered in Book 49 Folio 108 of
the Register of Titles in the Nevis Circuit;
AND WHEREAS the names and specimen signatures in the Schedule listed are those
of the persons authorized to sign on the Facility;
NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED, that the Cabinet of Ministers of the
Administration approves, adopts, ratifies and confirms the new Facility.
