# Proclamation dated the 12th day of March 2020, declaring Wednesday the 1st day of April 2020, to be a public half holiday in the Island of Nevis on the occasion of the Gulf Insurance Primary Schools…

SAINT CHRISTOPHER AND NEVIS
STATUTORY RULES AND ORDERS
No. 1 of 2020
ISLAND OF NEVIS
Proclamation dated the 12th day of March 2020, declaring Wednesday the 1st day of
April 2020, to be a public half holiday in the Island of Nevis on the occasion of
the Gulf Insurance Primary Schools Championship Sports Meet.
BY THE DEPUTY GOVERNOR-GENERAL
FOR THE ISLAND OF NEVIS
A PROCLAMATION
HYLEETA LIBURD, MH
Deputy Governor-General
[Published 19th March 2019, Official Gazette No. 19 of 2020.]
WHEREAS by section 5 of the Public Holiday Act (Chapter 23.23) of the Laws of
Saint Christopher and Nevis, as amended, it is enacted that it shall be lawful for the Deputy
Governor-General from time to time on the advice of the Cabinet to appoint a special day or
part of a day to be reserved as a public holiday either throughout Nevis or in any town,
district or village thereof.
AND WHEREAS the Deputy Governor-General on the advice of the Cabinet considers
it expedient that Wednesday the 1st day of April 2020, from 12 noon should be observed and
kept as a public half holiday in the Island of Nevis to mark the occasion of the Gulf Insurance
Primary Schools Championship Sports Meet.
NOW, THEREFORE, I, HYLEETALIBURD MH, DEPUTY GOVERNOR-GENERAL
for the Island of Nevis, do with the advice of the Cabinet, in exercise of the powers conferred
on me by the said section of the said Act, appoint Wednesday the 1 st day of
April 2020, from 12 noon, to be reserved and kept as a public half holiday in the Island of Nevis.
AND All Her Majesty’s Officers and loving subjects in the Island of Nevis and all
others whom it may concern are hereby required to take due notice hereof and to give their
ready obedience accordingly.

LS

GIVEN at Government House, Nevis this
12th day of March 2020, and in the
Sixty-Ninth year of Her Majesty’s reign.
GOD SAVE THE QUEEN!
