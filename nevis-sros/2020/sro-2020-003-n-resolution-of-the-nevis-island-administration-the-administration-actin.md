# RESOLUTION of the Nevis Island Administration, the Administration, acting through its Cabinet Ministers, the Ministers;

SAINT CHRISTOPHER AND NEVIS
STATUTORY RULES AND ORDERS
ISLAND OF NEVIS
No. 3 of 2020
RESOLUTION of the Nevis Island Administration, the Administration,
acting through its Cabinet Ministers, the Ministers;
[Published 17th December 2020, Official Gazette No. 90 of 2020]
WHEREAS, the Cabinet of Ministers of the Nevis Island Administration (“the
Administration”) in its meeting on the 4th day of November, 2020, passed, adopted and consented
to the following recitals and resolution:
AND WHEREAS, the Administration, acting through its Ministry of Finance, is desirous
of restructuring a portion of its current overdraft facility of Twenty Million Eastern Caribbean
Dollars (EC$20.0 million) at The Bank of Nevis Limited (“the Bank”), into a consolidated
long term loan facility of 15 years Corporate Income Tax free interest rate of 5.25 percent;
AND WHEREAS, the Bank has requested a Resolution from the Administration signifying
the Agreement of the Administration to the restructured loan facility in the amount of EC$20
Million Dollars to be repaid over a period of 15 years;
AND WHEREAS, the restructured loan facility being extended will be secured by 150
acres of land situated at New River Estate in the Parish of Saint James’ in the Island of Nevis
registered in Book 49 Folio 108 of the Register of Titles in the Nevis Circuit;
AND WHEREAS the name and specimen signatures in the Schedule listed are those of
the persons authorized to sign on the facility;
NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED, that the Cabinet of Ministers of the
Administration having considered the said restructured loan facility, approves, adopts, ratifies and
confirms the same.
