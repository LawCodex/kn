# RESOLUTION of the Nevis Island Assembly of the Island of Nevis acting through its Cabinet Ministers, the Ministers;

SAINT CHRISTOPHER AND NEVIS
STATUTORY RULES AND ORDERS
No. 4 of 2018
ISLAND OF NEVIS
RESOLUTION of the Nevis Island Assembly of the Island of Nevis acting through
its Cabinet Ministers, the Ministers;
[Published 1st March 2018, Official Gazette No. 14 of 2018]
WHEREAS it is provided for by Part VIII, Section 48-49 of the Finance Administration
Act, No. 13 of 2007 and the Nevis Treasury Bills Ordinance, No. 5 of 1989, the Cabinet
Ministers of the Nevis Island Administration (NIA) in its meeting on the 31st day of
January, 2018, passed, adopted and consented to the following recitals and resolution;
AND WHEREAS, the NIA, acting through its Ministry of Finance, is desirous of
renewing the following Treasury Bills:

### 1.

91-Day Treasury Bills Issue (Over-the-Counter) with a limit of Ninety-Nine
Million Eastern Caribbean Dollars (EC$99,000,000.00);

### 2.

365 Day Treasury Bills issue (former RGSM) with a limit of Four Million Eastern
Caribbean Dollars (EC$4,000,000.00) and

### 3.

365 Day Treasury Bills issue (Over-the-Counter) with a limit of Five Million
Eastern Caribbean Dollars (EC$5,000,000.00)

AND WHEREAS, the names and specimen signatures in the Schedule listed are
those of the persons authorised to sign this Treasury Bill Facility;
NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED, that the Cabinet of Ministers of the NIA
approves, adopts, ratifies and confirms the renewal of the Facility.
