# The Integrity in Public Life Ordinance (Commencement) Order, 2018

SAINT CHRISTOPHER AND NEVIS
STATUTORY RULES AND ORDERS
No. 7 of 2018
ISLAND OF NEVIS
This Order is made by the Minister with responsibility for Finance in the Nevis
Island Administration, in exercise of the powers conferred upon him by
section 1 of the Integrity in Public Life Ordinance (No. 2 of 2013) and all other
powers thereunto him enabling.
[Published 28th June 2018, Official Gazette No. 34 of 2018]

### 1.

SHORT TITLE.

1\. This Order may be cited as THE INTEGRITY IN PUBLIC LIFE ORDINANCE
(COMMENCEMENT) ORDER, 2018.

### 2.

INTERPRETATION.

In exercise of the powers conferred upon me by section 1 of the Integrity in Public
Life Ordinance (No. 2 of 2013) and all other powers thereunto me enabling I,
MARK A.G. BRANTLEY, Minister with responsibility for Finance, do hereby appoint
the 20th of June 2018, as the day on which the said Ordinance shall come into force.
Made this 20th day of June 2018.

MARK A.G. BRANTLEY
Minister of Finance
Nevis Island Administration
