# RESOLUTION of the Nevis Island Administration, the Administration, acting through its Cabinet Ministers, the Ministers;

SAINT CHRISTOPHER AND NEVIS
STATUTORY RULES AND ORDERS
No. 3 of 2018
ISLAND OF NEVIS
RESOLUTION of the Nevis Island Administration, the Administration, acting through
its Cabinet Ministers, the Ministers;
[Published 1st March 2018, Official Gazette No. 14 of 2018]
WHEREAS, the Cabinet of Ministers of the Nevis Island Administration
(“the Administration”) in its meeting on the 31st day of January, 2018, passed, adopted and
consented to the following recitals and resolution:
AND WHEREAS, the Administration, acting through its Ministry of Finance, is
desirous of renewing its overdraft facility at the CIBC FirstCaribbean International Bank
(Barbados) Limited (“the Bank”) with a limit not exceeding Two Million Eastern Caribbean
Dollars (EC$2,000,000.00) (“the Facility”) for the period 1st January to 31st December 2018;
AND WHEREAS, the Bank has requested a Resolution from the Administration
signifying the agreement of the Administration to obtain this Facility;
AND WHEREAS the Facility is being extended on an unsecured basis with the Bank
reserving the right to amend the security;
AND WHEREAS the names and specimen signatures in the Schedule listed are
those of the persons authorised to sign on the Facility;
NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED, that the Cabinet of Ministers of the
Administration approves, adopts, ratifies and confirms the new Facility.
