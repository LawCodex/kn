# Proclamation dated the 12th day of March 2018, declaring Wednesday the 28th day of March 2018 to be a public half holiday in the Island of Nevis on the occasion of the Golf Insurance Primary Schools…

SAINT CHRISTOPHER AND NEVIS
STATUTORY RULES AND ORDERS
No. 6 of 2018
ISLAND OF NEVIS
Proclamation dated the 12th day of March 2018, declaring Wednesday the 28th day of
March 2018 to be a public half holiday in the Island of Nevis on the occasion of
the Golf Insurance Primary Schools Championship Sports Meet.
BY THE ACTING DEPUTY GOVERNOR-GENERAL
FOR THE ISLAND OF NEVIS
A PROCLAMATION
MARJORIE MORTON, C.S.M.
Deputy Governor-General (Ag)
[Published 22nd March 2018, Official Gazette No. 17 of 2018.]
WHEREAS by section 5 of the Public Holiday Act (Chapter 23.23), as amended, it is
enacted that it shall be lawful for the Deputy Governor-General (Ag) from time to time on the
advice of the Cabinet to appoint a special day or part of a day to be reserved as a
public holiday either throughout Nevis or in any town, district or village thereof.
AND WHEREAS the Deputy Governor-General (Ag) on the advice of the Cabinet
considers it expedient that Wednesday the 28th day of March 2018, from 12 noon should be
observed and kept as a public half holiday in the Island of Nevis to mark the occasion of the
Golf Insurance Primary Schools Championship Sports Meet.
NOW, THEREFORE, I, MARJORIE MORTON, C.S.M., DEPUTY GOVERNORGENERAL (Ag) for the Island of Nevis, do with the advice of the Cabinet, in exercise of the
powers conferred on me by the said section of the said Act, appoint Wednesday the 28th day of
March 2018, from 12 noon, to be reserved and kept as a public half holiday in the Island of Nevis.
AND All Her Majesty’s Officers and loving subjects in the Island of Nevis and all
others whom it may concern are hereby required to take due notice hereof and to give their
ready obedience accordingly.

LS

GIVEN at Government House, Nevis this
12th day of March 2018, and in the
Sixty-Seventh year of Her Majesty’s
reign.
GOD SAVE THE QUEEN!
