# RESOLUTION of the Nevis Island Administration, the Administration, acting through its Cabinet Ministers, the Ministers;

SAINT CHRISTOPHER AND NEVIS
STATUTORY RULES AND ORDERS
No. 9 of 2018
ISLAND OF NEVIS
RESOLUTION of the Nevis Island Administration, the Administration, acting through
its Cabinet Ministers, the Ministers;
[Published 19th July 2018, Official Gazette No. 37 of 2018]
WHEREAS, the Cabinet of Ministers of the Nevis Island Administration (“the
Administration”) in its meeting on the 20th day of June, 2018, passed, adopted and consented to the
following recitals and resolution;
AND WHEREAS, the Administration, acting through its Ministry of Finance is desirous of
restructuring its current loans and overdraft facilities of EC$142,000,000.00 at the St. Kitts-NevisAnguilla National Bank Limited (“the Bank”), into a consolidated long term loan facility of 25 years at
Corporate Income Tax free interest rate of 5.36% per annum and with equal monthly instalments of
EC$860,173 effective July 31, 2018;
AND WHEREAS, the Bank has requested a Resolution from the Administration signifying the
Agreement of the Administration to the restructured loan facility in the amount of One Hundred and
Forty Two Million Eastern Caribbean Dollars (EC$142,000,000.00) to be repaid over a period of
25 years;
AND WHEREAS, the restructured loan facility being extended will be secured by 86.50 acres
of land at Pinney’s Estate, Saint Thomas Parish in the Island of Nevis- Re: COT issued on
29th October 2014 registered in Book 52 and Folio # 269;
AND WHEREAS the name and specimen signature in the Schedule listed are those of the
persons authorised to sign on the Facility,
NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESSOLVED, that the Cabinet of Ministers of the Administration
having considered the said restructured loan facility approves, adopts, ratifies and confirms the same.
