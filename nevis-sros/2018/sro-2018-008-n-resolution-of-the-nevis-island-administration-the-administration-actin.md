# RESOLUTION of the Nevis Island Administration, the Administration, acting through its Cabinet Ministers, the Ministers;

SAINT CHRISTOPHER AND NEVIS
STATUTORY RULES AND ORDERS
No. 8 of 2018
ISLAND OF NEVIS
RESOLUTION of the Nevis Island Administration, the Administration, acting through
its Cabinet Ministers, the Ministers;
[Published 19th July 2018, Official Gazette No. 37 of 2018]
WHEREAS, the Cabinet of Ministers of the Nevis Island Administration (“the
Administration”) in its meeting on the 20th day of June, 2018, passed, adopted and consented
to the following recitals and resolution;
AND WHEREAS, the Administration, acting through its Ministry of Finance, is
desirous of obtaining an overdraft facility at the St. Kitts-Nevis-Anguilla National Bank
Limited (“the Bank”) with a limit not exceeding Five Million Eastern Caribbean Dollars
(EC$5,000,000.00) (“the Facility”) for the period 1st January to 31st December 2018;
AND WHEREAS, the Bank has requested a Resolution from the Administration
signifying the agreement of the Administration to establish the Facility;
AND WHEREAS the Facility will be secured by 86.50 acres of land situated at Pinney’s
Estate, Saint Thomas’ Parish in the Island of Nevis registered in Book 52 Folio 269 of the
Register of Titles in the Nevis Circuit;
AND WHEREAS the name and specimen signature in the Schedule listed are those of
the persons authorised to sign on the Facility,
NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESSOLVED, that the Cabinet of Ministers of the
Administration approves, adopts, ratifies and confirms the establishment of the Facility.
