# RESOLUTION of the Nevis Island Administration, the Administration, acting through its Cabinet Ministers, the Ministers;

SAINT CHRISTOPHER AND NEVIS
STATUTORY RULES AND ORDERS
ISLAND OF NEVIS
No. 4 of 2019
RESOLUTION of the Nevis Island Administration, the Administration, acting
through its Cabinet Ministers, the Ministers;

[Published 24th December 2019, Official Gazette No. 65 of 2019]
WHEREAS it is provided for by Part VIII, Section 48-49 of the Finance Administration Act,
No. 13 of 2007 and the Nevis Treasury Bills Ordinance, No. 5 of 1989, the Cabinet Ministers of the Nevis
Island Administration (“the Administration”) in its meeting on the 6th day of November, 2019, passed,
adopted and consented to the following recitals and resolution:
AND WHEREAS, the Administration, acting through its Ministry of Finance is desirous of
renewing the following Treasury Bills:

### 1.

91-Day Treasury Bills Issue (Over-the-Counter) with a limit of One Hundred and One
Million Eastern Caribbean Dollars (EC$101,000,000.00) for the period 1st January to
31st December 2020;

### 2.

365 Day Treasury Bills Issue (former RGSM) with a limit of Three Million Thirty Thousand
Eastern Caribbean Dollars (EC$3,030,000.00) for the period January
1st to December 31st 2020; and

### 3.

365 Day Treasury Bills Issue (Over-the-Counter) with a limit of Five Million Two Hundred
and Seventy-Five Thousand Eastern Caribbean Dollars (EC$5,275,000.00) for the period
1st January to 31st December 2020.

AND WHEREAS the names and specimen signatures in the Schedule listed are those of the
persons authorised to sign on this Treasury Bill Facility;
NOW THEREFORE BE IT RESOLVED that the Cabinet of Ministers of the Administration
approves, adopts, ratifies and confirms the renewal of the Facility.
