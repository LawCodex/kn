# The Nevis Taxi and Tour Bus Operators Ordinance (Commencement) Order, 2019

SAINT CHRISTOPHER AND NEVIS
STATUTORY RULES AND ORDERS
ISLAND OF NEVIS
No. 1 of 2019

This Order is made by the Minister with responsibility for Tourism in the Nevis
Island Administration, in exercise of the powers conferred upon him by section 1 of the
Nevis Taxi and Tour Bus Operators Ordinance (No. 1 of 2012) and all other powers thereunto
him enabling.
[Published 6th March 2019, Extra-Ordinary Gazette No. 12 of 2019]

### 1.

Citation.

This Order may be cited as THE NEVIS TAXI AND TOUR BUS OPERATORS
ORDINANCE (COMMENCEMENT) ORDER, 2019.

### 2.

Interpretation.

In exercise of the powers conferred upon me by section 1 of the Nevis Taxi and Tour
Bus Operators Ordinance (No. 1 of 2012) and all other powers thereunto me enabling I,
MARK A.G. BRANTLEY, Minister with responsibility for Tourism, do hereby appoint
the 19th of February 2019, as the day on which the said Ordinance shall come into force.
Made this 19th day of February, 2019.
MARK A.G. BRANTLEY
Minister responsible for Tourism
Nevis Island Administration
