# RESOLUTION of the Nevis Island Administration, the Administration, acting through its Cabinet Ministers, the Ministers;

SAINT CHRISTOPHER AND NEVIS
STATUTORY RULES AND ORDERS
ISLAND OF NEVIS
No. 3 of 2019
RESOLUTION of the Nevis Island Administration, the Administration,
acting through its Cabinet Ministers, the Ministers;
[Published 24th December 2019, Official Gazette No. 65 of 2019]
WHEREAS, the Cabinet of Ministers of the Nevis Island Administration (“the
Administration”) in its meeting on the 6th day of November, 2019, passed, adopted and
consented to the following recitals and resolution:
AND WHEREAS, the Administration, acting through its Ministry of Finance, is
desirous of renewing its overdraft facility at the St. Kitts-Nevis-Anguilla National Bank
Limited (“the Bank”) with a limit not exceeding Five Million Eastern Caribbean Dollars
(EC$5,000,000.00) (“the Facility”) for the period 1st January 2020 to 31st December 2020;
AND WHEREAS, the Bank has requested a Resolution from the Administration
signifying the agreement of the Administration to increase the Facility;
AND WHEREAS the Facility will be secured by 86.50 acres of land situated at
Pinney’s Estate, Saint Thomas Parish James in the Island of Nevis registered in Book 52
Folio 269 of the Register of Tiles in the Nevis Circuit;
AND WHEREAS the name and specimen signatures in the Schedule listed are
those of the persons authorised to sign on the facility;
NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED, that the Cabinet of Ministers of the
Administration approves, adopts, ratifies and confirms the renewal of the Facility.
