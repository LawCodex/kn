# Proclamation dated the 25th day of March 2024, declaring Wednesday the 3rd day of April 2024, to be a public half holiday in the Island of Nevis on the occasion of the Nevis Inter-Primary Athletic Championship

SAINT CHRISTOPHER AND NEVIS
STATUTORY RULES AND ORDERS
No. 1 of 2024
ISLAND OF NEVIS
Proclamation dated the 25 th day of March 2024, declaring Wednesday the 3 rd day of
April 2024, to be a public half holiday in the Island of Nevis on the occasion of the Nevis
Inter-Primary Athletic Championship.
BY THE DEPUTY GOVERNOR-GENERAL
FOR THE ISLAND OF NEVIS
A PROCLAMATION
HYLEETA M. LIBURD, O.B.E., MH
Deputy Governor-General for the Island of Nevis
[Published 28th March 2024, Official Gazette No. 16 of 2024.]
WHEREAS by section 5 of the Public Holidays Act (Chapter 23.23), of the Laws of
Saint Christopher and Nevis, as amended, it is enacted that it shall be lawful for the
Deputy Governor-General from time to time on the advice of the Cabinet to appoint a special day or
part of a day to be reserved as a public holiday either throughout Nevis or in any town, district or
village thereof.
AND WHEREAS the Deputy Governor-General on the advice of the Cabinet considers it
expedient that Wednesday the 3rd day of April 2024, from 12 noon should be observed and kept as
a public half holiday in the Island of Nevis to mark the occasion of the
Nevis Inter-Primary Athletic Championship.
NOW, THEREFORE, I, HYLEETA M. LIBURD, O.B.E., MH, DEPUTY
GOVERNOR-GENERAL for the Island of Nevis, do with the advice of the Cabinet, in
exercise of the powers conferred on me by the said section of the said Act, appoint Wednesday the
3rd day of April 2024, from 12 noon, to be reserved and kept as a public half holiday in the Island of
Nevis.
AND All His Majesty’s Officers and loving subjects in the Island of Nevis and all others
whom it may concern are hereby required to take due notice hereof and to give their ready obedience
accordingly.

LS

GIVEN under my hand and Public Seal
of Saint Christopher and Nevis at Government
House, Nevis, this 25th day of March 2024,
and in the Second year of His Majesty’s reign.
HYLEETA M. LIBURD, O.B.E., MH
Deputy Governor-General
