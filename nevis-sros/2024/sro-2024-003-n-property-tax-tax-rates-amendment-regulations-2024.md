# Property Tax (Tax Rates) (Amendment) Regulations, 2024

SAINT CHRISTOPHER AND NEVIS
STATUTORY RULES AND ORDERS
No. 3 of 2024
ISLAND OF NEVIS
In exercise of the powers conferred by section 44 of the Property Tax Ordinance,
CAP. 6.13(N), the Minister responsible for Finance makes the following Regulations
[Published 11th July 2024, Official Gazette No. 38 of 2024]

### 1.

Citation.

These Regulations may be cited as the Property Tax (Tax Rates) (Amendment)
Regulations, 2024 and shall come into effect on the 1st day of January, 2025.

### 2.

Interpretation.
In these Regulations, “Ordinance” means the Property Tax Ordinance, CAP. 6.13(N).

### 3.

Amendment of regulation 3(a)(i).
Regulation 3(a)(i) is amended by replacing the figure “0.156%” with the figure “0.2%”.
MADE BY the Acting Minister of Finance this 19th day of June, 2024.

ERIC EVELYN
Acting Minister responsible for Finance

Approved by Resolution of the Nevis Island Assembly this 21st day of June, 2024.

MYRA WILLIAMS
