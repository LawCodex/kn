# RESOLUTION of the Nevis Island Administration, the Administration, acting through its Cabinet Ministers, the Ministers

SAINT CHRISTOPHER AND NEVIS
STATUTORY RULES AND ORDERS
No. 2 of 2024
ISLAND OF NEVIS
RESOLUTION of the Nevis Island Administration, the Administration, acting through
its Cabinet Ministers, the Ministers
[Published 11th July 2024, Official Gazette No. 38 of 2024]
WHEREAS it is provided by section 44 of the Property Tax Ordinance, CAP. 6.13(N)
that the Minister may make, amend, add to or replace the Regulations establishing the tax
rate applicable to each class of taxable property in a financial year.
BE IT RESOLVED that the Nevis Island Assembly of the Island of Nevis approves the
Property Tax (Tax Rates) (Amendment) Regulations, 2024, made by the Minister of
Finance under section 44 of the Property Tax Ordinance, CAP. 6.13(N).
Approved by the Nevis Island Assembly this 21st day of June 2024.

MYRA WILLIAMS
Clerk of the Nevis Island Assembly
