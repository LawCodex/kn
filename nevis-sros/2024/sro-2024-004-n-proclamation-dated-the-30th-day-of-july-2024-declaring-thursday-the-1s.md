# Proclamation dated the 30th day of July 2024, declaring Thursday the 1st day of August 2024, to be a public half holiday in the Island of Nevis on the occasion of the Junior Cultural Street Parade

SAINT CHRISTOPHER AND NEVIS
STATUTORY RULES AND ORDERS
No. 4 of 2024
ISLAND OF NEVIS
Proclamation dated the 30 th day of July 2024, declaring Thursday the
1st day of August 2024, to be a public half holiday in the Island of Nevis
on the occasion of the Junior Cultural Street Parade.

BY THE DEPUTY GOVERNOR-GENERAL
FOR THE ISLAND OF NEVIS
A PROCLAMATION
HYLEETA M. LIBURD, O.B.E., MH
Deputy Governor-General for the Island of Nevis
[Published 31st July 2024, Extra-Ordinary Gazette No. 44 of 2024.]

WHEREAS by section 5 of the Public Holidays Act (Chapter 23.23), of the
Laws of Saint Christopher and Nevis, as amended, it is enacted that it shall be
lawful for the Deputy Governor-General from time to time on the advice of the
Cabinet to appoint a special day or part of a day to be reserved as a public holiday
either throughout Nevis or in any town, district or village thereof.
AND WHEREAS the Deputy Governor-General on the advice of the Cabinet
considers it expedient that Thursday the 1st day of August 2024, from 12 noon
should be observed and kept as a public half holiday in the Island of Nevis to mark
the occasion of the Junior Cultural Street Parade.
NOW, THEREFORE, I, HYLEETA M. LIBURD, O.B.E., MH,
DEPUTY GOVERNOR-GENERAL for the Island of Nevis, do with the advice
of the Cabinet, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by the said section of the
said Act, appoint Thursday the 1st day of August 2024, from 12 noon, to be reserved
and kept as a public half holiday in the Island of Nevis.
AND All His Majesty’s Officers and loving subjects in the Island of Nevis
and all others whom it may concern are hereby required to take due notice hereof
and to give their ready obedience accordingly.

LS

GIVEN under my hand and Public Seal
of Saint Christopher and Nevis
at Government House, Nevis, this
30 th day of July 2024, and in the
Second year of His Majesty’s reign.

HYLEETA M. LIBURD, O.B.E., M.H.
Deputy Governor-General

GOD SAVE THE KING!
