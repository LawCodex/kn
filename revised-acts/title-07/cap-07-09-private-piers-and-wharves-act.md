## CHAPTER 7.09

PRIVATE PIERS AND WHARVES ACT
AN ACT TO MAKE PROVISION FOR THE GRANTING OF LICENCES FOR THE ERECTION,
MAINTENANCE AND USE OF PRIVATE PIERS AND WHARVES; AND TO PROVIDE FOR
RELATED OR INCIDENTAL MATTERS.
Short title.

---

### 1.

This Act may be cited as the Private Piers and Wharves Act.

Licences to be granted.

### 2.

It shall be lawful for the Cabinet to grant to private persons licences to erect,
maintain and use piers and wharves on or over any part of the Crown’s foreshores in
the State hereinafter defined in consideration of such annual payment and subject to
such conditions as the Cabinet may think fit.
(Amended by Act 6 of 1976)

Wharfage dues to be paid as heretofore. Goods bonâ fide property of licence holders
exempt from wharfage dues.

### 3.

Except as hereinafter provided there shall be charged on and paid in respect of
all goods, wares and merchandise landed at or shipped from any pier or wharf for the
erecting and use of which such licence shall have been granted the several wharfage
dues which would under the rules and regulations in force for the time being be
payable had such goods, wares or merchandise been landed at or shipped from a
public pier or wharf in the State:
Provided that no pier or wharfage dues shall be charged on or payable in
respect of any goods, wares or merchandise landed at or shipped from any pier or
wharf in respect of which a licence shall have been granted as aforesaid which said
goods, wares or merchandise shall be the bonâ fide property of the person or persons
for the time being beneficially entitled to the privileges conferred by such licence.
Regulations.

\*

### 4.

It shall be lawful for the Minister responsible for Communications ( in this Act
called the Minister) to make regulations for the regulation of piers and wharves in
respect of which such licence shall have been granted and for assessing and securing
the wharfage dues payable in respect of goods, wares and merchandise landed thereat
or shipped therefrom.
(Amended by Act 6 of 1976)

Penalties.

### 5.

Any person who violates or refuses or neglects to comply with the regulations
framed by the Minister under the provisions of this Act shall, on summary conviction,
be liable to a fine not exceeding two hundred and fifty dollars in addition to the
amount of wharfage dues.
(Amended by Acts 7 of 1976 and 9 of 1986)

\*

Original section 5 repealed by Act 6 of 1976; section 6,7 and 9 have been renumbered
accordingly.

Definition of the Crown’s foreshores.

### 6.

The Crown’s foreshores to which this Act relates are hereby defined and
declared to be as follows—
(a) the foreshore extending along the coast of St. Christopher in front of
the town of Basseterre for a distance of half a mile in an easterly
direction from the public export pier, and for a distance of half a mile
in a westerly direction from the public import pier;
(b) the foreshore extending along the coast of St. Christopher for a
distance of one quarter of a mile on either side of the public pier at
Sandy Point;
(c) the foreshore extending along the coast of St. Christopher for a
distance of one quarter of a mile on either side of the public pier at
Dieppe Bay;
(d) the foreshore extending along the coast of Nevis which would be
included by a straight line drawn from Fort Point to the Nevis
Infirmary;
(e) the foreshore extending along the coast of Nevis within the limits of
Parbuckle Bay and for a distance of fifty feet beyond each of the
extremities of the Bay.
Act not to derogate from Crown grants made before its commencement.

### 7.

Nothing hereinbefore contained shall be construed so as to derogate from any
grant by the Crown of any rights over the foreshore made previous to the passing of
this Act.
