## CHAPTER 4.30

REMISSION OF PENALTIES ACT
AN ACT TO MAKE PROVISION FOR THE REMISSION OF PENALTIES.
Short title.

---

### 1.

This Act may be cited as the Remission of Penalties Act.

Penalties for offences may be remitted although payable to parties other than the
Crown.

### 2.

It shall be lawful for the Governor-General to remit in whole or in part any
sum of money which under any Act now in force or hereafter to be passed may be
imposed as a penalty or forfeiture on a convicted offender, although such money may
be in whole or in part payable to some party other than the Crown, and to extend the
Royal Prerogative of Mercy to any person who may be imprisoned for non-payment
of any sum of money so imposed, although the same may be in whole or part payable
to some party other than the Crown.
