## CHAPTER 4.08

EXTRADITION ACT
AN ACT TO PROVIDE FOR THE POWER OF MAGISTRATES AND THE MINISTER IN
RELATION TO EXTRADITION OF FUGITIVE CRIMINALS.
Short title.

---

### 1.

This Act may be cited as the Extradition Act.

Power of Magistrates and the Minister in relation to extradition of fugitive
criminals.

### 2.

All powers vested in and acts authorised or required to be done by a police,
Magistrate or any justice of the peace in relation to the surrender of fugitive criminals
in the United Kingdom under the Extradition Acts, 1870 and 1873 or any Act
amending or substituted for those Acts, are hereby vested in, and may in the State be
exercised and done by any Magistrate or by the Minister in relation to the surrender
of fugitive criminals under those Acts.
(Amended by Act 6 of 1976)

## SCHEDULE

EXTRADITION (EASTERN CARIBBEAN STATES) ORDER
1\.

This Order may be cited as the Extradition (Leeward Islands) Order.

2\.
The Act of the Legislature of the Leeward Islands entitled the Extradition Act,
1946, shall have effect in the State of the Leeward Islands as if it were part of the said
Extradition Act, 1870.
