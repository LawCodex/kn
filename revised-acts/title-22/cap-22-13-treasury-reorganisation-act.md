## CHAPTER 22.13

TREASURY REORGANISATION ACT
AN ACT TO MAKE PROVISION FOR THE REORGANISATION OF THE TREASURY; AND TO
MAKE PROVISION FOR RELATED OR INCIDENTAL MATTERS.
Short title.

---

### 1.

This Act may be cited as the Treasury Reorganisation Act.

Abolition of office of Treasurer.

### 2.

The office of Treasurer of the State is hereby abolished.

Comptroller of Customs.

### 3.

(1) There is established a Department of Customs, Excise and Port.

(2) It shall be lawful for the Governor-General, acting on the recommendation
of the Public Service Commission, from time to time, to appoint a Comptroller of
Customs of the State and such other officers of the Department of Customs, Excise
and Port as may, from time to time, be necessary.
(Amended by the Constitution)

(3) The Comptroller of Customs shall be charged with the administration of
the Department of Customs, Excise and Port and may, with the approval of the
Minister, make rules for the government of the said Department.
Right, etc., previously exercised by Treasurer to be exercised by Accountant
General.

### 4.

Every right, power, privilege, immunity or duty which immediately before the
commencement of this Act was or was capable of being exercised, enjoyed, or
performed by the Treasurer by virtue of any instrument having the force of law in the
State, not being an Act enacted by the Legislature of the State, shall, from and after
the commencement of this Act in relation to the State, be exercised, enjoyed and
performed by the Accountant-General.
Transfer of powers of Treasurer to Accountant-General.

### 5.

(1) Except as may otherwise be provided in any Order under section 6, every
right, power, privilege, immunity or duty which immediately before the
commencement of this Act was capable of being or was required to be exercised,
enjoyed or performed by the Treasurer pursuant to any Act in force in the State shall,
from and after the commencement of this Act, be exercised, enjoyed, and performed
by the Accountant-General.
(2) Subject to the provisions of subsection (2) of section 6, there shall be
substituted for every reference to the Treasurer in any Act in force at the
commencement of this Act a reference to the Accountant-General.
Power to allocate duties formerly performed by Treasurer between AccountantGeneral and Comptroller of Customs.

### 6.

(1) Subject to the provisions of section 4, the Minister may, from time to time,
by Order, direct that any rights, powers, privileges, immunities or duties which
immediately before the commencement of this Act were or were capable of being or

were required to be exercised, enjoyed or performed by the Treasurer (whether
pursuant to any instrument having the force of law in the State or otherwise) shall,
with effect from such day as may be specified in such Order, be exercised, enjoyed
and performed by the Accountant-General or by the Comptroller of Customs
whichever may be specified in such Order.
(Amended by Act 6 of 1976)

(2) Where any Order under subsection (1) relates to any rights, powers,
privileges, immunities or duties conferred or imposed by any Act there shall be
substituted for references in such Act to the Treasurer references to the AccountantGeneral or to the Comptroller of Customs whichever may be appropriate to bring
such Act into conformity with such Order.

(Section 6)
TREASURY REORGANISATION (ALLOCATION OF DUTIES) ORDER
Citation.

1\.
This Order may be cited as the Treasury Reorganisation (Allocation of Duties)
Order.
Allocation of Duties to Accountant-General.

2\.
The rights, powers, privileges, immunities and duties capable of being or
required to be exercised, enjoyed or performed by the Treasurer pursuant to the
provisions of any of the Acts specified in Schedule 1 hereto shall henceforth be
exercised, enjoyed and performed by the Accountant-General.
Allocation of Duties to Comptroller of Custom.

3\.
The rights, powers, privileges, immunities and duties capable of being or
required to be exercised, enjoyed or performed by the Treasurer pursuant to the
provisions of any of the Acts specified in Schedule 2 hereto shall henceforth be
exercised, enjoyed and performed by the Comptroller of Customs.
