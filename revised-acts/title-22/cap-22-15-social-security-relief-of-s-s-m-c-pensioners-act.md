## CHAPTER 22.15

SOCIAL SECURITY (RELIEF OF SSMC PENSIONERS) ACT
AN ACT TO PROVIDE FOR THE RELIEF OF PENSIONERS FORMERLY UNDER THE ST KITTS
SUGAR MANUFACTURING CORPORATION PENSIONS SCHEME BY MAKING SPECIAL
PROVISION FOR THEM UNDER THE SOCIAL SECURITY PENSIONS SCHEME.
Short title.

---

### 1.

This Act may be cited as the Social Security (Relief of SSMC Pensioners) Act.

Interpretation.

### 2.

In this Act, unless the context otherwise requires—

“Minister” means the Minister responsible for Social Security;
“SSMC pensioners” mean persons who were beneficiaries under the St Kitts Sugar
Manufacturing Corporation’s Pension Scheme at the close of the Sugar
Industry and who are named in the Schedule to this Act.
Establishment of special fund under Social Security.

### 3.

(1) There is hereby established a special fund under the Social Security
Scheme to provide relief for the SSMC pensioners whose pension payments would
have been extinguished as a consequence of the closure of the Sugar Industry in July
of 2005.
(2) The fund shall be managed and administered by the Social Security Board
in accordance with the provisions of this Act.
Government to guarantee disbursement of funds.

### 4.

The SSMC pensioners shall receive a pension from Social Security equivalent
to what they were entitled to receive from SSMC, to be paid on the basis of funds
provided by the Government to cover the disbursement.
Limited life of fund.

### 5.

(1) The fund referred to in section 3 shall be limited to the life of the SSMC
pensioners and shall not be—
(a) transferred to any other person; or
(b) extended to survivors of the beneficiaries.
(2) No person shall be eligible to receive payment out of the fund unless he
satisfies the requirements set out in this Act.
Persons already receiving assistance under Social Security Act.

### 6.

(1) Notwithstanding that a person may already be receiving assistance under
the Social Security Act, Cap. 22.10, he or she shall not be disqualified from receiving
a pension under the provisions of this Act.
(2) Subject to subsection (3), where a person is already receiving assistance
under the Social Security Act prior to the coming into force of this Act and that
person is entitled to a pension under this Act, then the assistance and pension payable
to that person may be grossed into one payment.

Revision Date: 31 Dec 2009

(3) The assistance referred to under this section may be recorded in the books
of account of the National Social Security Board for the purposes of the Social
Security Act.

### 7.

The Minister may generally make Regulations to give effect to the provisions
of this Act.
