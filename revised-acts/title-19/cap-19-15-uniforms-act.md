## CHAPTER 19.15

UNIFORMS ACT
AN ACT TO PROHIBIT THE WEARING OF MILITARY UNIFORMS WITHOUT AUTHORITY.
Short title.

---

### 1.

This Act may be cited as the Uniforms Act.

Prohibition of wearing military uniforms without authority.

### 2.

(1) It shall not be lawful for any person not serving in the armed services of
the State or Crown to wear, without the Minister’s permission, the uniform of any
armed forces or any dress having the appearance or bearing any of the regimental or
other distinctive marks of any such uniform:
Provided that this enactment shall not prevent any person from wearing any
uniform or dress in the course of a stage play, whether performed in public or private,
or in the course of a music hall or circus performance, or in the course of any bonâ
fide military representation.
(Amended by Act 6 of 1976)

(2) If any person contravenes this section, he or she shall be liable, on
summary conviction, to a fine not exceeding one hundred and fifty dollars.
(Amended by Acts 7 of 1976 and 9 of 1986)
(Sections 2 and 4 deleted by Act 6 of 1976)
