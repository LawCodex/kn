## CHAPTER 3.04

COMMISSIONERS FOR OATHS ACT
AN ACT TO PROVIDE FOR THE APPOINTMENT OF COMMISSIONERS TO ADMINISTER
OATHS; AND TO PROVIDE FOR RELATED OR INCIDENTAL MATTERS.
Short title.

---

### 1.

This Act may be cited as the Commissioners for Oaths Act.

Appointment of Commissioners to administer oaths.

### 2.

(1) The Chief Justice may appoint Commissioners to administer oaths in any
Court established by the laws of the State, and may also empower such
Commissioners to administer oaths for the purposes of the Registration and Records
Act, Cap. 23.25, and the Title by Registration Act, Cap. 10.19.
(2) Any such Commissioner may, within the area specified in his or her
commission, administer oaths in accordance with this Act and the terms of his or her
Commission.
(3) The Chief Justice may at his or her pleasure revoke any such appointment.
Proof of execution of deeds.

### 3.

(1) Any deed intended for registration which has been executed in the State
may be proved by the oath of the witness, or one of the witnesses, before any
Commissioner empowered as aforesaid to administer oaths, for the purposes of the
Registration and Records Act, and such proof shall be as valid as if made before the
Registrar.
Act.

(2) This section shall be read and construed with the Registration and Records

Oaths under Title by Registration Act.

### 4.

Any oath administered, for the purposes of the Title by Registration Act, by
any Commissioner empowered, as aforesaid, to administer oaths, for the purposes of
that Act, shall be as valid and effectual as if the same had been administered by the
Registrar of Titles under that Act.
