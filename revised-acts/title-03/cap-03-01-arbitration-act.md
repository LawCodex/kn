ARBITRATION ACT
AN ACT TO MAKE PROVISION FOR THE APPLICATION OF THE UNITED KINGDOM
ARBITRATION ACT TO APPLY TO SAINT CHRISTOPHER AND NEVIS.
Short title.

---

### 1.

This Act may be cited as the Arbitration Act.

U.K. Arbitration Act to apply to the State.

### 2.

The Arbitration Act, 1950 of the Parliament of the United Kingdom, shall be
and the same is hereby declared to be henceforth in force in this State, and all the
provisions of the said Act, so far as the same are applicable, shall mutatis mutandis
apply to all proceedings relating to arbitration within the State.
(Amended by Acts 4 of 1974 and 7 of 1976)
