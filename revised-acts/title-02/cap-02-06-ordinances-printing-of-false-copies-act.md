## CHAPTER 2.06

ORDINANCES (PRINTING OF FALSE COPIES) ACT
AN ACT TO PROHIBIT THE PRINTING OF FALSE COPIES OF ORDINANCES.
Short title.

---

### 1.

This Act may be cited as the Ordinances (Printing of False Copies) Act.
(Amended by Acts 25 of 2008 and 31 of 2008)

Penalty for printing false copies.

### 2.

Any person who—
(a) prints any paper which falsely purports to be a copy of an Ordinance;
or
(b) tenders in evidence any such paper falsely purporting to be a copy of
an Ordinance knowing the same to be false,

commits a felony and on conviction shall be liable to be imprisoned for any term not
exceeding two years with or without hard labour.
(Amended by Acts 25 of 2008 and 31 of 2008)
