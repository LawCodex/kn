## CHAPTER 2.02

GAZETTE (AUTHORISATION) ACT
AN ACT TO MAKE PROVISION RELATING TO THE PROHIBITION OF THE PRINTING OF FALSE
COPIES OF THE GAZETTE.
Short title.

---

### 1.

This Act may be cited as the Gazette (Authorisation) Act.

Name of printer to be affixed.

### 2.

All printed copies of the Gazette shall have imprinted at the end the name of
the printer who printed the same with the words “by authority” added thereafter.
Penalty for printing false copies.

### 3.

Any person who—
(a) prints any paper which falsely purports to be a duly authorised copy of
the Gazette; or
(b) tenders in evidence any such paper falsely purporting to be the
Gazette, knowing that the same was not duly printed and published,

commits a felony and on conviction shall be liable to be imprisoned for a term not
exceeding two years with or without hard labour.
