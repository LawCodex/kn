## CHAPTER 23.24

PUBLIC PARKS REGULATION ACT
AN ACT TO REGULATE PUBLIC PARKS, AND TO PROVIDE FOR RELATED OR INCIDENTAL
MATTERS.
Short title.

### 1.

This Act may be cited as the Public Parks Regulation Act.

Interpretation.

### 2.

In this Act—

“Minister” means the Minister responsible for Tourism, Culture and Sports;
(Substituted by Act 12 of 1998)

“public park” means any place mentioned in the Schedule;
“park-keeper” means any person appointed to be keeper of a public park;
“Public-Park” includes a playing field and any sporting facility designated by the
Minister for that purpose;
(Inserted by Act 12 of 1998)

“traffic” means the passage of animals and vehicles of every description.
Power to make regulations.

### 3.

(1) The Minister may make regulations for the protection, management and
good government of any public park.
(Amended by Act 6 of 1976)

(2) In particular and without prejudice to the generality of the foregoing power
such regulations may—
(a) provide for the appointment of a committee to manage and control any
public park, and prescribe the powers, duties and procedure at the
meetings of such committee;
(b) provide for the appointment of park-keepers, and prescribe their
powers and duties;
(c) regulate the use of any public park and of any buildings thereon, and
the times and conditions of admission to the same;
(d) provide for the exclusive use of portions of any public park by clubs or
other bodies of persons, and fixing the fees to be paid for such
exclusive use;
(e) fix the fees to be paid for admission to any public park or any portion
thereof or any building thereon either generally or on specified
occasions;
(f) control or prohibit traffic in any public park; and
(g) prohibit any interference with, or damage to, or the destruction of, any
trees, plants, shrubs, fruit, gates, fences, or other things within or about
any public park.

(3) Regulations made under this section shall not have any force or effect until
they have been approved by resolution of the National Assembly and published in the
Gazette.
Penalty for contravention of regulations.

### 4.

Any person who contravenes any regulation made under the authority of this
Act commits an offence and on summary conviction before a Magistrate shall be
liable to a fine not exceeding three hundred dollars.
(Amended by Acts 7 of 1976 and 9 of 1986)

Power of removal and arrest.

### 5.

It shall be lawful for any police officer or park-keeper or any person appointed
by the Minister in that behalf—
(a) to exclude or remove from any public park, any person creating a
disturbance or behaving in a disorderly or unseemly manner;
(b) to arrest without warrant any person who within his or her view
commits a breach of any regulation made under this Act, if the name
and address of such person is unknown to such police officer or parkkeeper, or if such person continues to commit such breach after being
warned to desist, and to detain him or her in custody until he or she
can be brought before a Magistrate to be dealt with according to law.
(Amended by Act 6 of 1976)

Minister to declare public park.

### 6.

The Minister may, by Order, declare any place to be a public park.
(Substituted by Act 3 of 1987)

Saving of certain rights.

### 7.

Nothing in this Act shall be deemed to authorize the interference with any
rights of way or any right whatever to which any person or persons may be by law
entitled.

## SECOND SCHEDULE

(Section 3)

PONDS EXTENSION RECREATION GROUND REGULATIONS
Short title.

1\.
These Regulations may be cited as the Ponds Extension Recreation Ground
Regulations.
Appointment of Committee.

2\.

(1) There shall be a Committee to manage and control the recreation ground.

(2) The Committee shall consist of seven persons who shall be appointed by
the Minister.

(3) If any member of the Recreation Ground Committee is absent from three
consecutive meetings of the Committee without leave of the Committee or is absent
from the Federation for more than thirty days without leave of the Committee he or
she shall ipso facto cease to be a member of the Committee.
(4) The members of the Recreation Ground Committee shall hold office
during the pleasure of the Minister and one of the members of the Committee may be
appointed by the Minister to be Chairperson.
(5) The powers and duties vested in the Committee may be exercised and
performed by the Chairperson and any two members, and in the absence of the
Chairperson, by any three members.
(6) The Minister may appoint a fit and proper person to be Secretary to the
Committee.
(7) The proceedings of the Recreation Ground Committee shall not be invalid
on account of any procedural defect therein.
Powers of Committee.

3\.

The Committee may—
(a) make roads, walks, pathways, plant trees, shrubs and carry out such
additions and improvements as they may deem advisable for the use,
maintenance and improvement of the Recreation Ground;
(b) erect fences, rails, tree guards, seats, drinking fountains, swings,
bandstands, or other structures;
(c) rent or hire for a temporary period any portion of the grounds to any
Society, club or person for the purpose of erecting thereon any tent,
booth, stand, show or any other temporary structure;
(d) allot or set apart for a period of not more than three years for the use of
any society, club or other body of persons any portion of the grounds
for the purpose of playing any game, or for any other use or purpose
that may be approved by the Committee, and also for the purpose of
erecting thereon any structure to be used as a club house provided that
the plan of any structure shall have been first approved by them; the
fees for such use shall be fixed in advance by the Committee and paid
quarterly to the Secretary thereto;
(e) grant permission to hold public meetings, processions, athletic sports,
or pyrotechnic displays or such other public gatherings as they may
deem advisable;
(f) allow the use of the grounds or any structure thereon for athletic
sports, games, concerts, theatrical performances, dances or other
entertainment, on such conditions and at such rental as they may deem
advisable;
(g) change the hour of opening or closing the Recreation Ground as
provided by Regulation 4 and on special occasions when the
Recreation Ground is used for sports or entertainments may close the
same or any part thereof to the public;
(h) prevent any person from using the Recreation Ground, or any portion
thereof if in the opinion of the Committee it may be necessary so to do
for the proper upkeep and preservation of the same;

(i) appoint a keeper of the Recreation Ground, subject to the approval of
the Minister, and the keeper shall be responsible to the Committee for
any work carried out on the Recreation Ground.
Recreation Ground Open to Public.

4\.
The Recreation Ground shall be open to the public from 6 a.m. to 10 p.m. or at
such other hours as may be approved by the Committee.
Accounts and Receipts.

5\.
(1) The Committee shall furnish the Minister in the months of July and
January of each year, in respect of the period of six months preceding, statements of
all receipts and expenditure for the period.
(2) The accounts shall be examined by the Director of Audit and his or her
report presented by the 30th June each year for the year ended 31st December.
Offences.

6\.

No person shall—
(a) pick any flowers, break the branches of any trees, shrubs, or growing
plants of any kind whatsoever in the Recreation Ground;
(b) cut or carve any tree, bench, seat, tree guard or any other article or
thing in the Recreation Ground, or damage in any way any building or
fence;
(c) be in any building or structure without the permission of the
Committee;
(d) behave in a riotous or disorderly manner in the Recreation Ground or
use any profane, blasphemous, indecent, insulting or abusive language
therein, or be found drunk therein or annoy or interfere with any
person who may be in the Recreation Ground or engage in playing any
game therein;
(e) commit any nuisance, or be indecently dressed, or commit any
indecent act in the Recreation Ground;
(f) climb over or upon any fence, rail or otherwise interfere with any
structure appertaining to the Recreation Ground.

(7) The proceedings of the Recreation Ground Committee shall not be invalid
on account of any procedural defect therein.
Powers of Committee.

3\.

The Committee may—

(a) make roads, walks, pathways, plant trees, shrubs and carry out such
additions and improvements as they may deem advisable for the use,
maintenance and improvement of the Recreation Ground;
(b) erect fences, rails, tree guards, seats, drinking fountains, swings,
bandstands, or other structures;
(c) rent or hire for a temporary period any portion of the grounds to any
society, club or body of persons for the purpose of erecting thereon
any tent, booth, stand, show or any other temporary structure;
(d) allot or set apart for a period of not more than three years for the use of
any society, club or other body of persons any portion of the grounds
for the purpose of playing any game, or for any other use or purpose
that may be approved by the Committee, and also for the purpose of
erecting thereon any structure to be used as a club house provided that
the plan of any structure shall have been first approved by them; the
fees for such use shall be fixed in advance by the Committee and paid
quarterly to the Secretary thereto;
(e) grant permission to hold public meetings, processions, athletic sports,
or pyrotechnic displays or such other public gatherings as they may
deem advisable;
(f) allow the use of the grounds or any structure thereon for athletic
sports, games, concerts, theatrical performances, dances or other
entertainment, on such conditions and at such rental as they may deem
advisable;
(g) change the hour of opening or closing the Recreation Ground as
provided by Regulation 4 and on special occasions when the
Recreation Ground is used for sports or entertainments may close the
same or any part thereof to the public;
(h) prevent any person from using the Recreation Ground, or any portion
thereof if in the opinion of the Committee it may be necessary so to do
for the proper upkeep and preservation of the same;
(i) appoint a keeper of the Recreation Ground, subject to the approval of
the Minister, and the keeper shall be responsible to the Committee for
any work carried out on the Recreation Ground.
Recreation Ground Open to Public.

4\.
The Recreation Ground shall be open to the public from 6 a.m. to 10 p.m. or at
such other hours as may be approved by the Committee.
Accounts and Receipts.

5\.
(1) The Committee shall furnish the Minister in the months of July and
January of each year, in respect of the period of six months preceding, statements of
all receipts and for the period.
(2) The accounts shall be examined by the Director of Audit and his or her
report presented by the 30th June each year for the year ended 31st December.
Offences.

6\.

No person shall—

(a) pick any flowers, break the branches of any trees, shrubs, or growing
plants of any kind whatsoever in the Recreation Ground;
(b) cut or carve any tree, bench, seat, tree guard or any other article or
thing in the Recreation Ground, or damage in any way any building or
fence;
(c) be in any building or structure without the permission of the
Committee;
(d) behave in a riotous or disorderly manner in the Recreation Ground or
use any profane, blasphemous, indecent, insulting or abusive language
therein, or be found drunk therein or annoy or interfere with any
person who may be in the Recreation Ground or engaged in playing
any game therein;
(e) commit any nuisance, or be indecently dressed, or commit any
indecent act in the Recreation Ground;
(f) climb over or upon any fence, rail or otherwise interfere with any
structure appertaining to the Recreation Ground.

TWENTIETH SCHEDULE
(Section 3)

GROVE PARK REGULATIONS
Short title.

1\.

These Regulations may be cited as the Grove Park Regulations.

Committee.

2\.
(1) Grove Park (in these Regulations referred to as the Park) shall be occupied
and under the charge and control of a committee (to be called the Grove Park
Committee) consisting of five persons who may be appointed by the Minister for
such purpose:
Provided always that the proceedings of the said committee shall not be
invalid on account of any vacancy therein.
(2) If any member of the committee is absent from three consecutive meetings
of the committee without leave of the committee, or is absent from the State for more
than thirty days without leave of the committee he or she shall ipso facto cease to be a
member of the committee.
(3) The chairperson shall be ex officio the person for the time being holding
the office of Warden, Nevis.
(4) The powers and duties vested in the committee may be exercised and
performed by the chairperson and any two members, and in the absence of the
chairperson, by any three members.
(5) The Minister may appoint some fit and proper person to be clerk to the
committee.

Powers of Committee.

3\.

The committee may—
(a) make roads, walks, pathways, plant trees, shrubs, and carry out such
additions and improvements as they may deem advisable for the use,
maintenance and improvement of the park;
(b) erect fences, rails, tree guards, seats, drinking fountains, swings,
bandstands, or other structures;
(c) rent or hire for a temporary period any portion of the grounds to any
society, club or person for the purpose of erecting thereon any tent,
booth, stand, show or any other temporary structure;
(d) allot or set apart for a period of three years for the use of any society
or club any portion of the grounds for the purpose of playing the game
of cricket or any game allowed to be played, or for any other use or
purpose that may be approved by the committee, and also for the
purpose of erecting thereon any structure to be used as a club house
provided that the plan of any structure shall have been first approved
by them;
(e) grant permission to hold public meetings, processions, athletic sports,
or pyrotechnic displays or such other public gatherings as they may
deem advisable;
(f) allow the use of the pavilion and grounds for athletic sports, games,
concerts, theatrical performances, dances or other entertainments on
such conditions and at such rental as they may deem advisable;
(g) change the hour of opening or closing the park as provided by
regulation 4 and on special occasions when the park is used for sports
or entertainments may close the same or any part thereof to the public;
(h) prevent any person from using the park or any portion thereof if in the
opinion of the committee it may be necessary so to do for the proper
upkeep and preservation of the park.

Park Open to Public.

4\.
The park shall be open to the public from 7 a.m. to 10 p.m. or at such hours as
may be approved by the committee.
Park-Keeper.

5\.
The Park-keeper shall be appointed by the committee subject to the approval
of the Minister:
Provided that in case of his or her sickness or absence on leave the chairperson
may appoint someone to perform the duties of his or her office temporarily pending
an appointment by the committee.
Duties of Park-Keeper.

6\.

The park-keeper shall—
(a) have the general superintendence and charge of the park and be
responsible for good order and behaviour in the same and for its
proper management and maintenance;

(b) attend at the park during the hours that it shall be open;
(c) collect and receive rentals and other charges and pay the same into the
Treasury, as directed by the committee.
Offences.

7\.

No person shall—
(a) pick any flowers, break the branches of any trees, shrubs or growing
plants of any kind whatsoever;
(b) cut or carve any tree, bench, seat, tree guard or any other article or
thing, or damage in any way any building or fence;
(c) be in the pavilion or any other building without the permission of the
committee;
(d) behave in a riotous or disorderly manner or use any profane,
blasphemous, indecent, insulting or abusive language, or be found
drunk or annoy or interfere with any person who may be in the park or
engaged in playing any game therein;
(e) commit any nuisance, or be indecently dressed, or commit any
indecent act;
(f) solicit alms or expose any wounds or sores for the purpose of
soliciting alms from any person;
(g) climb over or upon any fence, rail or otherwise interfere with any other
structure appertaining to the park;
(h) be in the park if afflicted with leprosy, yaws, sores or other loathsome
disease whatsoever.

(Section 3)

WARNER PARK REGULATIONS
Short title.

1\.

These Regulations may be cited as the Warner Park Regulations.

Appointment of Committee.

2\.

(1) There shall be a Committee to manage and control the Park.

(2) The Committee shall consist of seven persons who shall be appointed by
the Minister.
(3) If any member of the Committee is absent from three consecutive
meetings of the Committee without leave of the Committee or is absent from the
Federation for more than thirty days without leave of the Committee he or she shall
ipso facto cease to be a member of the Committee.

(4) The members of the Committee shall hold office during the pleasure of the
Minister and one of the members of the Committee may be appointed by the Minister
to be Chairperson.
(5) The powers and duties vested in the Committee may be exercised and
performed by the Chairperson and any two members, and in the absence of the
Chairperson, by any three members.
(6) The Minister may appoint a fit and proper person to be Secretary to the
Committee.
(7) The proceedings of the Committee shall not be invalid on account of any
procedural defect therein.
Powers of Committee.

3\.

The Committee may—
(a) make roads, walks, pathways, plant trees, shrubs and carry out such
additions and improvements as they may deem advisable for the use,
maintenance and improvement of the Park;
(b) erect fences, rails, tree guards, seats, drinking fountains, swings,
bandstands, or other structures;
(c) rent or hire for a temporary period any portion of the grounds to any
society, club or body of persons for the purpose of erecting thereon
any tent, booth, stand, show or any other temporary structure;
(d) allot or set apart for a period of not more than three years for the use of
any society, club or other body of persons any portion of the grounds
for the purpose of playing any game, or for any other use or purpose
that may be approved by the Committee, and also for the purpose of
erecting thereon any structure to be used as a club house provided that
the plan of any structure shall have been first approved by them; the
fees for such use shall be fixed in advance by the Committee and paid
quarterly to the Secretary thereto;
(e) grant permission to hold public meetings, processions, athletic sports,
or pyrotechnic displays or such other public gatherings as they may
deem advisable;
(f) allow the use of the grounds or any structure thereon for athletic
sports, games, concerts, theatrical performances, dances or other
entertainment, on such conditions and at such rental as they may deem
advisable;
(g) change the hour of opening or closing the Park as provided by
Regulation 4 and on special occasions when the Park is used for sports
or entertainments may close the same or any part thereof to the public;
(h) prevent any person from using the Park, or any portion thereof if in the
opinion of the Committee it may be necessary so to do for the proper

(i) appoint a keeper of the Park, subject to the approval of the Minister,
and the keeper shall be responsible to the Committee for any work
carried out on the Park.

Park Open to Public.

hours as may be approved by the Committee.

6\.

(a) pick any flowers, break the branches of any trees, shrubs, or growing
plants of any kind whatsoever in the Park;
(b) deface, damage, or destroy any—
(i) tree, bench, fence or similar object in the park;
(ii) building, facility or any other temporary or permanent structure in
the park;
(Substituted by S.R.O. 20/2001)

(c) be found unlawfully in the Park with any spray paint, crowbar, or any
other implements in his possession that could be used for the purposes
of vandalism or similar acts of destruction;
(d) be in any building or structure without the permission of the
Committee.
(e) behave in a riotous or disorderly manner in the Park or use any
profane, blasphemous, indecent, insulting or abusive language therein,

may be in the Park engaged in playing any game therein;
(f) commit any nuisance, or be indecently dressed, or commit any
indecent act in the Park;
(g) climb over or upon any fence, rail or otherwise interfere with any
structure appertaining to the Park;
(h) loiter on the grounds of the Park or behave in a manner consistent with
vagrancy without being able to give a satisfactory account of himself.
(Inserted by S.R.O. 20/2001)

Powers of Park-keeper to arrest without a warrant.

7\.
For the purposes of these Regulations any park-keeper or other authorised
member of the armed forces may without a warrant—
(a) arrest any person that he or she reasonably suspects to have committed
or is about to commit an offence in the Park;
(b) require the name and address of any person loitering in the Park or
behaving in a suspicious manner;

(c) stop and search any vehicle within the confines of the Park when he or
she reasonably suspects that there are grounds for so doing.
