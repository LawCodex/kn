## CHAPTER 23.06

CHARLESTOWN LIMITS ACT
AN ACT TO DEFINE THE LIMITS OF CHARLESTOWN.
Short title.

---

### 1.

This Act may be cited as the Charlestown Limits Act.

Limits of the town defined.

### 2.

The limits of the town of Charlestown in the island of Nevis shall be deemed
and taken to be as follows:
From the sea at the boundary of Pinneys Estate and the Nevis Club property then
going in an Easterly direction along the main Public Road to the road leading up to
Craddock Road then going South to the Big Rock at the corner of Craddock Road
then in a straight line to the public water supply tank at the corner of Government and
Roger Below roads then along the Public Road to the Cemetery corner on the main
Public Road, then Westward along the main Public Road continuing along the road
South of Government House and the Bath Hotel to the sea.
