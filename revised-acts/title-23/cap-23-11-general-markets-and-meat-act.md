## CHAPTER 23.11

GENERAL MARKETS AND MEAT ACT
AN ACT TO PROVIDE FOR THE REGULATION OF CERTAIN PUBLIC MARKETS AND
SLAUGHTER HOUSES; AND TO PROVIDE FOR RELATED OR INCIDENTAL MATTERS.
Short title.

### 1.

This Act may be cited as the General Markets and Meat Act.

Interpretation.

### 2.

In this Act, unless the context otherwise requires—

“animal” means a domestic animal the meat of which is intended to be used for
human consumption and includes poultry;
“butchers’ meat” includes any beef, mutton, veal, lamb, pork, kid and the meat of any
other animal slaughtered for the purpose of sale;
“Corporation” means the Central Marketing Corporation established under section 3
of the Central Marketing Corporation Act;
“existing market” includes a market within the limits of a Local Government Council
if that Council has not made by-laws regulating such market;
“existing slaughterhouse” includes a slaughter house within the limits of a Local
Government Council if that Council has not made by-laws regulating such
slaughter houses;
“Minister” means the Minister responsible for the subject of markets;
“market” means the market established under the provisions of this Act for the sale of
marketable commodities, and includes all buildings, sheds, covered and open
spaces and grounds connected therewith;
“person in charge” includes the Corporation during the currency of any agreement
between the Minister and the Corporation in pursuance of Section 7;
“slaughter house” means any place in the State declared by notice to be such by the
Governor-General under Section 4 of this Act;
“poultry” means chickens, ducks, geese, turkeys and other birds;
“regulations” means regulations made under this Act;
“slaughter” means slaughter for the purpose of processing meat into food for human
consumption.
Limitation of provisions of this Act.

### 3.

This Act shall not extend to any market or slaughter house in respect of which
by-laws have been passed by a Local Government Council under the provisions of
the Local Government Act, Cap. 2.03 for establishing, maintaining and regulating
such market or slaughter house.
Public Markets and Slaughter Houses Ordinance No. 5 of 1916.

### 4.

(1) The existing markets and slaughter houses throughout the State established
under the authority of the Markets and Meat Ordinance, 1916, shall be deemed to be
established under this Act.

(2) The Governor-General may, from time to time, by notice published in the
Gazette, define the site, limits, and extent of any existing market, or of any market
hereafter to be erected or established, and from time to time alter such site, limits and
extent.
(3) The Governor-General may, from time to time, by notice published in the
Gazette, declare any place in the State to be a slaughter house for the purpose of this
Act and may define the limits thereof and may also, from time to time, alter such
limits.
Sites of markets to remain vested in the Crown.

### 5.

The sites of all markets throughout the State, except when the same may be
private or freehold property, are hereby vested in the Governor-General for the
purposes of this Act.
General control of Minister.

### 6.

It is hereby declared that the administration of this Act and the general
superintendence of markets and slaughter houses shall be a responsibility of the
Minister.
Powers of Minister.

### 7.

It shall be lawful for the Minister to do all or any of the following matters or
things—
(a) to cause to be purchased out of public funds, all such measures, scales
and weights, furnishings and appliances as shall be necessary for
markets or slaughter houses;
(b) to cause to be made and paid for out of public funds all such repairs,
alterations, improvements and additions to markets or slaughter houses
as he or she shall deem necessary and proper;
(c) subject to the approval of the Cabinet, to enter into agreements with
the Corporation providing for—
(i) the more efficient carrying out within the State of the purpose and
intent of this Act;
(ii) the performance by the Corporation on behalf of the Government
or of the Minister of functions and services with respect to the
control and management of markets under this Act that are the
responsibility of the Minister; and
(iii) the payment of money required for functions and services
performed by the Corporation under sub-paragraph (ii).
Duties of Central Marketing Corporation.

### 8.

Every agreement made between the Minister and the Corporation under
paragraph (c) of section 7 may provide that it shall be the duty of the Corporation—
(a) to ensure that an officer or officers of that Corporation shall give
constant attendance at every market or slaughter house mentioned in
the agreement during the hours fixed for keeping open the same;

(b) to have the custody of and keep in good and proper order all measures,
scales, weights and appliances furnished by the Minister for use in
markets and slaughter houses;
(c) to cause to be inspected and tested all measures, scales, weights and
appliances used by butchers and other persons dealing in or using for
business purposes any market or slaughter house and if any such
measures, scales, weights or appliances are false or defective to
authorise the same to be seized and to report the facts to the Minister
and to the Chief of Police;
(d) to ask, demand, receive, collect and recover all rates and dues and
other moneys liable to be demanded, received, collected and recovered
under the provisions of this Act;
(e) to cause to be kept a detailed cash account book in which entries shall
be made of all moneys received by the Central Marketing Corporation,
and to furnish weekly to the Minister a copy of such account and to
pay weekly to the Accountant General any moneys so received by the
Corporation under the authority of this Act;
(f) that the Corporation will generally carry out and enforce the
provisions of this Act and any regulations made thereunder.
Power to charge rent for stalls etc.

### 9.

The person in charge of any market or slaughter house may, from time to time,
demand and take from every person occupying, or using any stand, stall, table, shed,
pen, or place, or using any weighing instrument in any market or slaughter house,
such rents, fees, and charges as may be fixed by regulations made under this Act.
Recovery of rents, fees and charges.

### 10.

If any person liable under this Act for the payment of any rent, fee, or charge
does not pay the same when required so to do by the person in charge of a market, the
same may be recovered by distress and sale of a sufficient part of the goods in respect
of which such rent, fee or charge is payable, or of any other goods in the market
belonging to or under the charge of the person liable to pay such rent, fee or charge;
or such rent, fee or charge may be recovered as a debt due to the person in charge of
the market or slaughter house in any Court of competent jurisdiction.
Regulations.

### 11.

The Minister may make regulations in respect of all or any of the following
matters, that is to say—
(a) for regulating the use of any market or markets, and of any slaughter
house or slaughter houses, and of any stalls, tables and places in any
markets;
(b) the feeding and watering of animals taken to any slaughter house;
(c) the days and hours during which any market or slaughter house shall
be kept open;
(d) for excluding and removing all persons suffering from contagious
disease, or from any skin or other disease rendering such person, in the
opinion of the person in charge of a market, unfit to sell in such
market;

(e) for prescribing the conditions subject to which stalls, tables and places
shall be held or occupied or used by any person hiring, or using, or
desirous of hiring or using the same;
(f) for regulating the inspection of any butcher’s meat exposed or
intended to be exposed or offered for sale in any part of a market or
slaughter house and of any livestock intended to be slaughtered for
meat;
(g) for maintaining order and quiet in markets and slaughter houses and
the prevention of shouting, singing and smoking and for preventing
any disturbance therein by fighting or disorderly behaviour;
(h) for fixing the rents, fees and charges to be made for the use of stalls,
tables and places, and for selling after six hours notice; butcher’s meat
or marketable commodities distrained for rent of a market stall;
(i) for regulating the sale of marketable commodities;
(j) for prescribing the areas in the vicinity of any market within which it
shall not be lawful to sell or offer for sale any marketable commodity;
(k) for the condemnation and destruction of butcher’s meat unfit for
human food, with or without compensation;
(l) for the inspection by officers or employees of the Ministry of Health,
or by medical practitioners or by sanitary inspectors or other
competent officer of the Health Department of animals brought to and
the carcasses of animals slaughtered at any slaughter house and all
articles of food exposed or offered for sale in any market, and for the
seizure and detention of any such animal, carcass or articles of food
found to be diseased, and the reporting thereof to the sanitary
inspector;
(m) for regulating the use of weighing instruments belonging to or used in
or in connection with any market or slaughter house; and preventing
the use of false or defective weights, scales or instruments by any
person selling in any market; and
(n) generally such other regulations as may be deemed necessary for the
good government of markets and slaughter houses; and for the better
and more effectual carrying out of the provisions of this Act.
(2) The regulations may impose pecuniary penalties not exceeding three
hundred dollars.
(Amended by Act 9 of 1986)

(3) Penalties for breaches of the regulations shall be recovered on summary
conviction, upon complaint made in the name of the person in charge of the market or
slaughter house, and the penalties, together with all rents, fees and charges received,
shall be paid into the Treasury for the use of the State.
(4) In this section the term “marketable commodity” means meat, fish, turtle,
game, livestock, eggs, ground provisions, vegetables, fruits and such other goods and
articles as may be notified in the Gazette by Order of the Minister.
Powers of clerk of markets.

### 12.

The clerk of a market shall have, within that market, the powers of a police
constable, and also, with respect to weights and measures, the powers of an officer of
police under the Act relating to weights and measures.

When certain markets to cease to be a market subject to this Act.

### 13.

It is hereby declared that an existing market or an existing slaughter house
within the limits of a Local Government Council shall cease to be subject to the
provisions of this Act from and after the date on which any by-laws of the Local
Government Council for regulating such market or slaughter house comes into
operation.

## FIRST SCHEDULE

(Section 11)
BASSETERRE MARKET REGULATIONS
Short title.

1\.

These Regulations may be cited as the Basseterre Market Regulations.

Interpretation.

2\.

In these Regulations—

“disposal” means sale, barter, offering or exposing for sale or barter;
“disposal of” means sell, barter, or offer or expose for sale or barter, or cause to be
sold, bartered or offered or exposed for sale, or barter;
“fish” means any kind of salt water fish;
“food” includes every article used for food or drink by man or beast;
“loiterer” includes any person in or about the market—
(a) for any lewd, idle, vicious or disorderly purpose;
(b) otherwise than in the regular performance or in pursuance of some
lawful calling or object;
(c) to the annoyance, or obstruction of any person therein;
(d) who does not leave the market when thereunto required by the Market
Clerk;
(e) who not having any authority to remain there fails to quit the market at
the hour of closing; or
(f) who is found therein after such hour;
“Inspector of Animals” or “Inspector” means the persons appointed by the Minister to
be Inspector of Animals at the public market;
“produce” means—
(a) sucking pigs;
(b) live and dead game;
(c) shell-fish, including lobsters;
(d) land and sea crabs;
(e) turtle eggs;
(f) any other article of food found, produced, grown, or manufactured in
this or any other West Indian State excepting bread, cakes, chocolate,
coffee, cooked food, drinks, honey, molasses, peanuts and sugar;
(g) brooms manufactured in the State or in any other State of the Leeward
Islands;
(h) charcoal burnt or prepared in the State or any other of the West Indian
Islands;

“market” means the market at the Bay Road Basseterre established by Proclamation
dated the 29th day of March 1930, and shall include the market buildings,
pavements, steps and approaches thereto, the slaughterhouse, tank, and any
land, or building thereon which may be erected or appropriated at any time for
use in connection with the market;
“town of Basseterre” includes the town of Basseterre and an area of one mile beyond
the limits of the said town of Basseterre;
“refuse” includes animal refuse, blood, offal, dirt, leavings, filth, shavings, sawdust,
broken bottles, or debris of any kind or description whatsoever;
“meat” includes flesh, bones, heart, lungs, tripe, or any part whatsoever of the
carcass, whether the same be fresh, corned, salted or otherwise, of any pig or
(the hide, horns and hoofs excepted) of any bull, ox, steer, cow, heifer, calf,
goat, kid, sheep, or lamb;
“mutton” means the flesh of sheep or lambs, only;
“stall” means such place in the Market as may from time to time be appointed as
such;
“stand” means such place in the Market as may from time to time be appointed as
such;
“Market Clerk” means the person holding the appointment of Market Clerk at the
commencement of these regulations or any person hereafter appointed by the
Minister as such, or any person acting in his or her place.
Market Open to Public.

3\.
(1) The market shall be open to the public from 6 a.m. to 6 p.m. except on
Sundays and Public Holidays, when it shall be open from 6 a.m. to 9 a.m. and on
Saturdays when it shall be open from 6 a.m. to 9 p.m.
(2) With respect to the closing of the market, two notice bells shall be rung,
the first, one hour, and the second one half hour, before the closing thereof; and a
closing bell at the closing hour when every person shall forthwith leave the market,
except such persons as may be authorised by the Chief Medical Officer to remain
there.
Market Clerk’s Duties.

4\.

The Market Clerk shall—
(a) be subject to the direction of the Chief Medical Officer;
(b) have the general superintendence and charge of the market, and be
responsible for the due and proper management thereof;
(c) attend at the market during the hours that the same is open;
(d) attend at the slaughterhouse at such times and under such conditions as
may be fixed by these or such other regulations as the Minister may
make from time to time for the regulation of the slaughterhouse and
with respect to the slaughtering of animals;
(e) inspect all scales, weights and measures of any description
whatsoever, used in the market, and see that the same are in
conformity with the standard and duly stamped according to law;

(f) keep a correct list and account of all property delivered to him or her
for the use of the market and be responsible for the same;
(g) make up and enter into a book every day an account under distinct and
separate heads of all his or her receipts for such day;
(h) pay into the Treasury once a week all moneys received by him or her;
(i) produce his or her book or any property under his or her charge
whenever called upon so to do by the Chief Medical Officer and give
to the Chief Medical Officer all the information as he or she may
require in respect of such books and property.
Market Clerk’s Powers.

5\.
(1) The Market Clerk may arrest, or eject or cause to be arrested or ejected
from the market, by any constable or other person—
(a) any disorderly person, or persons acting indecently or persons dirtily
or indecently dressed, or using obscene, abusive or insulting language,
or swearing or behaving in a manner calculated to disturb the peace of
the market;
(b) any person afflicted with leprosy, yaws, sores, or any other loathsome
disease whatsoever; or
(c) any loiterer.
(2) The Market Clerk may eject or have ejected any dog from the market.
Inspection of Book.

6\.
The Chief Medical Officer shall inspect the book mentioned in paragraph (g)
of regulation 4 twice a week at least at such times as may be convenient to him or
her.
Slaughterhouse Dues.

7\.
(1) Every person who desires to slaughter any of the animals mentioned in
Schedule 1, shall pay to the Market Clerk slaughterhouse dues in respect thereof, at
the rate specified in that Schedule.
(2) The Market Clerk shall, on receipt of such sums as he or she is entitled to
claim for slaughterhouse dues under the First Schedule, give a receipt, herein called a
slaughterhouse dues receipt for the same which receipt shall be produced to the
Market Clerk or other agent of the Chief Medical Officer by the slaughterer, on his or
her being requested to produce the same at any time.
(3) Every slaughterhouse dues receipt shall—
(a) state the name of the person on whose behalf it has been applied for,
and the amount for which it is issued;
(b) designate the animals to be slaughtered by virtue thereof;
(c) entitle the person mentioned in such receipt to slaughter at the
slaughterhouse the animals mentioned in such receipt but subject to
such rules and conditions as the Chief Medical Officer may from time
to time, make with respect to the slaughtering of animals.

Stall Dues.

8\.
(1) Every person who desires to use a stall shall, before using such stall,
obtain from the Market Clerk a certificate showing the number of the stall which he
or she is to use and pay to the Market Clerk stall dues in respect thereof at the rate
specified in the First Schedule.
(2) The Market Clerk shall on the receipt of such sum as he or she is entitled
to claim for stall dues under the First Schedule give a receipt in these regulations
called a “stall dues receipt” for the same, which receipt shall be produced by the stall
holder to the Market Clerk or Chief Medical Officer, whenever called upon so to do,
at any time, during the period for which the stall holder is authorised to use the stall.
(3) Every stall dues receipt shall—
(a) state the name of the person on whose behalf it has been applied for,
the amount for which it is issued, the number of the stall in respect of
which it is issued, and the period during which such stall is to be used;
(b) entitle the person mentioned therein to use the stall designated during
the period stated and not otherwise.
(4) No person shall use a stall—
(a) without having previously paid for and obtained a stall dues receipt
therefor; or
(b) after the expiration of the period mentioned in any stall dues receipt in
respect of such stall; or
(c) after having been served with a notice under regulation 16.
Ticket Dues and Stands.

9\.
(1) Every person who brings to the market any produce for disposal shall on
each occasion of his or her so doing pay in advance to the Market Clerk dues called
ticket dues, in respect thereof, at the rate specified in Schedule 1.
(2) The Market Clerk shall, on receipt of such sums as he or she shall be
entitled to claim for such ticket dues under Schedule 1, give a ticket called a market
ticket for the same purpose.
(3) Every market ticket shall entitle the holder to the use of a stand in the
market until the closing thereof on the day of issue, unless the produce in respect
whereof such ticket shall have been issued, be earlier disposed of.
Registration of Butchers.

10\. (1) No person shall exercise the trade of a butcher or act as a butcher’s
assistant within the limits of the town of Basseterre unless he or she is duly registered
as such as hereinafter provided.
(2) Every person who is desirous of exercising the trade of a butcher or acting
as a butcher’s assistant shall produce to the Chief Medical Officer a certificate in
writing signed by a medical practitioner that he or she is a fit and proper person to
exercise the trade as a butcher or act as a butcher’s assistant; and thereupon the Chief
Medical Officer shall register him or her accordingly and shall issue to him or her
free of cost a certificate of registration, and such a medical certificate will be required
periodically at intervals of six months.

(3) The Chief Medical Officer shall enter in a book to be called “the register
of butchers” the names and addresses of all persons registered as butchers or
butcher’s assistants.
(4) If any person registered as a butcher or butcher’s assistant shall have by
using any forged certificate or other fraudulent means caused himself or herself to be
so registered, or shall misconduct himself or herself in the exercise of his or her trade,
or shall be three times convicted of offences against these regulations, or shall be
convicted of a felony in connection with his or her trade, the Chief Medical Officer
may order his or her name to be expunged from the register of butchers and
thereupon such person shall be disqualified from exercising the trade of a butcher or
acting as a butcher’s assistant just as if he or she had never been registered as such,
provided that before the name of such person is removed from the register he or she
shall be given an opportunity of appearing before the Chief Medical Officer and
being heard.
(5) If any person shall exercise the trade of a butcher or act as a butcher’s
assistant without being duly registered as hereinbefore provided he or she commits an
offence against these regulations.
Slaughter of Animals in Basseterre.

11\. (1) No person shall, without the written permission of the Chief Medical
Officer, slaughter or cause to be slaughtered in the town of Basseterre, otherwise than
in the slaughterhouse, any of the animals mentioned in the First Schedule:
Provided that nothing in these regulations shall prevent any person from
slaughtering a sucking pig, lamb or kid at his or her own residence for his or her
domestic use.
(2) No person shall, without the written permission of the Chief Medical
Officer, bring or cause to be brought into the town of Basseterre any meat intended
for disposal which has been slaughtered elsewhere than at the slaughterhouse.
Disposal of Meat.

12\.

No person shall—
(a) carry any meat, otherwise than in clean receptacles;
(b) dispose of any meat, before showing the same to the Market Clerk;
(c) without the written permission of the Chief Medical Officer, dispose
of any meat at any place in the town of Basseterre other than at the
stalls provided in the market for the purpose:
Provided that this paragraph shall not apply to imported meat
other than fresh meat;
(d) dispose of goats’ flesh for mutton;
(e) remove the wool or hair from the tail of the carcass of any sheep,
lamb, goat or kid exposed for disposal.

Suspected Disposal of Meat.

13\. Any person authorised by the Chief Medical Officer or any member of the
Police Force, who reasonably suspects that any person in the town of Basseterre is
carrying or has any meat in his or her possession which is intended for disposal, and
which has not come from the market, may stop such person and search anything

carried by him or her or in his or her possession, and such person shall stop when
requested so to do, and shall allow the search mentioned in this regulation to be
made.
Meat Sellers.

### 14.

(1) Every seller of meat, turtle or fish, and every person who carries meat,
turtle or fish, into the market shall be cleanly dressed.
(2) Every butcher and every butcher’s assistant shall, whilst handling meat,
wear a clean apron to the satisfaction of the Market Clerk.
Slaughter of Animals and Inspection of Meat.

### 15.

(1) Every animal brought into the market for the purpose of being slaughtered
shall, before being slaughtered, be kept therein publicly exposed for the space of at
least twenty-four hours after being brought in, but shall not be kept therein for any
space of time longer than three days:
Provided that the Inspector of Animals may grant permission in writing to any
person to slaughter such animal before the expiration of twenty-four hours as
aforesaid.
(2) Every person bringing any animal for slaughter into the market shall at
once take the same to the Market Clerk, who shall, in addition to the other
requirements under these regulations, keep a register of the time when such animal
shall be brought in.
(3) Any person who shall slaughter or attempt to slaughter any animal
aforesaid before the expiration of twenty-four hours after such animal shall have been
brought into the market, unless the permission aforesaid shall have been first obtained
for that purpose, commits an offence against these regulations.
(4) The hours of slaughter shall be from 6 a.m. to 7.30 a.m. daily, except on
Saturday when the hours shall be from 6 a.m. to 8 a.m.:
Provided that on Friday slaughtering may take place also between the hours of
7 p.m. and 9 p.m.
(5) All animals, the meat of which is intended for local consumption, shall be
subjected to an examination by the Inspector of Animals both before and after
slaughter, and, save as is hereinafter provided, no animal shall be slaughtered unless
it has been examined and passed by the Inspector:
Provided that where, in consequence of any accident or injury an animal ought
in the opinion of the Market Clerk to be killed immediately, then it may be
considered a case of emergency and inspection before slaughter may be omitted.
(6) If slaughter does not take place within forty-eight hours after an animal has
been examined and passed by the Inspector of Animals then it shall not be allowable
unless the animal is re-examined.
(7) An animal which the Inspector of Animals declares to be unfit for
slaughter shall forthwith be removed from the market by the owner or person in
charge of the animal.
(8) The Inspector of Animals shall attend at the slaughterhouse daily at 6.30
a.m. except on Saturdays, when he or she shall attend from 6 a.m. to 8.30 a.m. for the
purpose of inspecting the meat of all animals slaughtered for local consumption.
(9) No part of a slaughtered animal shall be taken away before inspection.

(10) If the inspection shows no ground for the condemnation of the meat the
Inspector shall declare it fit for human consumption.
(11) If the inspection shows that the meat or any part of the meat is unfit for
human consumption, the Inspector shall condemn it, inform the owner or person in
charge of the meat of the fact, and immediately notify the Market Clerk.
(12) All meat condemned as aforesaid shall be immediately seized by the
Market Clerk, and shall be destroyed in such manner as the Chief Medical Officer
may, from time to time, direct.
(13) All meat remaining in the Market, which has been declared fit for human
consumption under these regulations, shall be subject to reinspection after twentyfour hours for the purpose of ascertaining whether it has in the meantime undergone
any change in its condition rendering it injurious to health.
(14) No meat shall be removed from the slaughterhouse to the stalls unless it is
properly cleaned and dressed.
(15) No person shall be allowed to enter the slaughterhouse while animals are
being slaughtered or while meat is being inspected except the butchers and their
assistants, or any person authorised by the Inspector of Animals or by the Chief
Medical Officer.
(16) The owner or person in charge of every animal and of every carcass of any
animal shall afford to the Inspector every assistance with respect to the examination
of such animal or carcass and shall furnish him or her with all information which he
or she may require in relation thereto, and every such owner or other person who
refuses to assist the Inspector or to give him or her such information, or who wilfully
gives him or her any false information, or who in any way resists, hinders, or
obstructs him or her in the execution of his or her duty commits an offence against
these regulations.
(17) Animals, the meat of which is intended for consumption on board the
Crown’s ships, may, on application of the contractor, be admitted into the market,
and slaughtered at any time in the slaughterhouse on payment of the ordinary dues;
but the examination of such animals or of the meat of such animals shall be
conducted according to the direction of the naval authorities:
Provided that no part of such meat shall be brought into or sold for local
consumption unless it is examined by the Inspector of Animals and declared by him
or her to be fit for human consumption.
(18) Whenever any animal is slaughtered out of the ordinary opening hours of
the market, the contractor shall pay to the Market Clerk, for his or her attendance, the
sum of ninety-six cents, and shall also pay the expense of washing and cleaning the
slaughterhouse after use and the cost of lighting if any.
(19) Every animal while in the market as aforesaid shall be supplied with
necessary food and water by the owner or other person in charge of the animal, and if
default be made in supplying food or water to any animal, the Market Clerk shall
cause such animal to be supplied with food or water or with both as the case may be,
and the cost of food and water shall be paid by the owner or other person in charge of
the animal, and if default be made in payment thereof the same may be summarily
recovered before a District Magistrate by the Chief Medical Officer from the owner
or other person in charge of the animal.

Stalls and Stands.

### 16.

(1) The Market Clerk may refuse any application for any stall or stand in the
market: Provided that any person aggrieved by such refusal shall be entitled to appeal
to the Minister whose decision shall be final.
(2) Except in the case of a registered firm or a bonâ fide partnership, no stall
or stand shall be rented or used by more than one person at the same time.
(3) No person shall—
(a) use any counter in the market for the purpose of chopping;
(b) use any stall or stand for any purpose other than that for which it is
appointed.
(4) Every licensee of a stall shall—
(a) before leaving the market for the day thoroughly cleanse, to the
satisfaction of the Market Clerk, his or her stall as well as his or her
counter, tools and other trade appliances, and shall remove all refuse
therefrom and place the same in the receptacle provided for the
purpose;
(b) keep his or her stall both inside and outside, and tools and all other
trade appliances, in thorough cleanliness at all times.
(5) Every licensee who shall persistently neglect or refuse to comply with the
requirements of this regulation may after one week’s notice in writing from the Chief
Medical Officer be debarred from the use of his or her stall, and shall also be liable to
be proceeded against for an offence against these regulations.
(6) No person shall—
(a) without the permission of the Market Clerk, be in any meat-stall
except the seller and his or her assistant;
(b) dispose at any meat stall of anything but meat or turtle;
(c) at any time keep in any meat stall anything but meat or turtle.
(7) No barrels, blocks, or other private property whatsoever shall be placed in
or about the market other than such as may be absolutely requisite for stall holders
and sellers in the prosecution of their business.
Miscellaneous Offences.

### 17.

(1) Every person in the market shall obey the lawful directions of the Market
Clerk.
(2) No person shall—
(a) smoke, or spit, in the market;
(b) sit or lie on any block, counter, table, or stand in the market, or on the
pavement thereof;
(c) sharpen any instrument on any part of the market structure;
(d) use blasphemous, obscene, abusive or insulting language, or swear, or
otherwise misbehave in the market;
(e) fight or create, or cause to be created, any row, riot or disturbance in
the market;

(f) throw filth, dirt, rubbish or litter in the market;
(g) open or clean the stomachs or intestines of any animal in the
slaughterhouse;
(h) break, cut, pull down, destroy, remove, injure or spoil any part of the
market;
(i) be a loiterer in or about the market;
(j) be in the market during the hours that the market is not open to the
public without permission of the Market Clerk;
(k) introduce, dispose of, or consume any wine, malt liquor, spirits or
intoxicants of any kind in the market;
(l) take or bring any dog, or permit any dog to follow, or accompany him
or her into the market;
(m) light any fire in the market, or cook therein, except in such parts
thereof and under such conditions as may be from time to time pointed
out and allowed by the Market Clerk;
(n) commit any nuisance in or at any part of the market;
(o) use any water-closet or urinal otherwise than for the purpose for which
it was intended;
(p) being a male, go into, make use of, or be in any water-closet or urinal
provided for the use of females;
(q) being a female, go into, make use of, or be in any water-closet or
urinal provided for the use of males;
(r) remove refuse from any part of the market in such a manner as to
create a nuisance in the process of such removal;
(s) destroy, deface or remove any public notice posted within the market.
Non-Liability of the Government.

### 18.

Neither the government nor the Chief Medical Officer nor any of his or her
officers or servants shall be responsible for the loss or damage of any property left in
the market.
Penalty.

### 19.

Every person who is found guilty of any offence against these regulations
shall, on being convicted of the offence before a District Magistrate, be liable to a
penalty not exceeding three hundred dollars, and in default of payment, to
imprisonment with or without hard labour for any term not exceeding two months.
Forms.

### 20.

(1) No particular form shall be necessary under these regulations.

(2) Every form in the Second Schedule shall be sufficient for the case for
which it was intended.
(3) A form may be amended at any time.

## SECOND SCHEDULE

(Section 11)
CHARLESTOWN MARKET REGULATIONS
Short Title.

1\.

These Regulations may be cited as the Charlestown Market Regulations.

Opening Hours.

2\.
(1) The public market in Charlestown, hereinafter called the market, shall be
opened daily at 6 o’clock a.m. and closed at 6 o’clock p.m., except on Saturdays and
the eves of holidays when it shall be kept open until 9 o’clock p.m.
(2) On holidays the market shall be closed at 9 p.m.:
Provided always the butchers and their assistants shall be allowed to have
access to the slaughter house at such hours as shall be arranged for with the Market
Keeper, or in case of disagreement at such hour as shall be fixed by the Chairperson
of the Central Board of Health, who shall be charged with the inspection and care of
the market.
Duties of Officer in Charge.

3\.
The officer in charge of the market shall take charge of the market and shall
keep such books as the Central Board of Health shall require.
Licensing of Tables.

4\.
(1) Tables in the market shall be marked off and numbered and shall be
licensed at such rates as are mentioned in the Schedule, and shall be paid for weekly
in advance, and any licensee refusing or neglecting to pay for his or her license as
aforesaid shall not be permitted to occupy such table, and it shall be the duty of the
Market Keeper to remove such person or persons and their goods from such table
forthwith.
(2) Every licensee of a table shall give three days’ notice of his or her
intention to quit the same, and no article shall be kept on any table except such
articles as are intended for sale.
Fees when No Tables Occupied.

5\.
Persons other than table holders bringing into the market any provisions or
other articles for sale shall place them in such part of the market as shall be pointed
out by the Market Keeper, and shall pay the fees mentioned in the Schedule.

Animals to be placed in Stock Shed.

6\.
All cattle, sheep, goats and pigs brought to the market to be slaughtered for
local consumption shall be placed in the stock shed at least fifteen hours before being
slaughtered:
Provided always that in case of emergency the time above mentioned may be
abridged by the Chairperson of the Central Board of Health.
Animals to be Inspected.

7\.
(1) The District Sanitary Officer shall inspect and approve of as fit for human
food all animals whether for local consumption or otherwise which are brought to the
market to be slaughtered, and if after the slaughter of any animal any doubt should
arise as to its fitness for human food the Sanitary Officer shall forthwith summon the
Medical Officer of the District and obtain his or her opinion as to its fitness for
human consumption.
(2) A fee of $1.20 shall be paid to the Medical Officer for the inspection and
opinion.
(3) This fee shall be payable—
(a) by the butcher when the meat is considered unfit for human food;
(b) by the government when the meat is considered fit for human food.
Collection of Fees.

8\.
The Market Keeper shall collect such fees for slaughtering and for other
purposes as are mentioned in the Schedule.
Persons Prohibited from Entering Slaughter House.

9\.
No person shall enter the slaughter house other than members of the Central
Board of Health, the servants of the Board and those actually engaged in the
slaughtering of animals.
Butchers to be Properly Dressed.

10\. Every butcher shall be cleanly dressed in his or her clothing and be provided
with clean cloths or towels when disposing of meat in his or her stall.
Stalls to be Marked with Owners Name.

11\. Butchers’ stalls shall have their owners’ names painted in legible letters and
placed in front of the same and butchers shall conform to such directions for cleaning
their stalls as the Market Keeper may direct.
Registration of Butchers.

12\. (1) No person shall exercise the trade of a butcher or act as a butcher’s
assistant within the limits of the Town of Charlestown unless he or she is duly
registered as such as hereinafter provided.
(2) Every person who is desirous of exercising the trade of a butcher or acting
as a butcher’s assistant shall produce to the Market Keeper a certificate in writing
signed by a medical practitioner that he or she is a fit and proper person to exercise
the trade as a butcher or act as a butcher’s assistant; and thereupon the Market Keeper

shall register him or her accordingly and shall issue to him or her free of cost a
certificate of registration.
(3) A medical certificate shall be produced to the Market Keeper periodically
at intervals of six months.
(4) The Market Keeper shall enter in a book to be called “The Register of
Butchers” the names and addresses of all persons registered as butchers or butchers’
assistants.
(5) If any person registered as a butcher or butcher’s assistant shall have, by
using any forged certificate or other fraudulent means caused himself or herself to be
so registered, or shall misconduct himself or herself in the exercise of his or her trade,
or shall be three times convicted of offences against these regulations, or shall be
convicted of a felony in connection with his or her trade, the Chairperson may order
his or her name to be expunged from the Register of Butchers and thereupon such
person shall be disqualified from exercising the trade of a butcher or acting as a
butcher’s assistant just as if he or she had never been registered as such, provided that
before the name of such person is removed from the Register he or she shall be given
an opportunity of appearing before the Central Board of Health and being heard.
(6) If any person shall exercise the trade of a butcher or act as a butcher’s
assistant without being duly registered as hereinbefore provided he or she commits an
offence against these regulations.
Slaughtering of Animals and Disposal of Meat.

13\.

No person shall—
(a) without the written permission of the Chairperson of the Central Board
of Health, slaughter or cause to be slaughtered in the town of
Charlestown, otherwise than in the slaughter-house, any of the animals
mentioned in the Schedule to these Regulations:
Provided that nothing in these regulations shall prevent any
person from slaughtering a sucking pig, lamb or kid at his or her own
residence for his or her domestic use;
(b) without the written permission of the Chairperson of the Central Board
of Health, bring or cause to be brought into the town of Charlestown,
any meat intended for disposal which has been slaughtered elsewhere
than at the slaughter-house;
(c) butcher any animal within the market which has died from any cause,
without the written permission of the Health Officer.

Obstructions.

14\. No person shall in any way obstruct or cause any obstruction at any gateway,
walk or passage or behave in a riotous or disorderly manner, or in any way obstruct
or impede the traffic in the market.
Tethering Horses.

15\. No person shall tether or fasten any horse or other animal to the gates, rails or
trees of, or in the market, or keep any animals in or under any sheds, except at
hereinbefore provided; or shall permit any such animal to be at large in the market.

Riding Through Market Prohibited.

16\.

No person shall ride or drive in or through the market.

Buildings Prohibited.

17\. No person shall erect any building, or shed, or plant or fix any poles in the
market without the consent of the Central Board of Health.
The Market Keeper May Eject Persons.

18\. The Market Keeper may eject, or cause to be arrested or ejected from the
market by any constable or other person—
(a) any disorderly person, or persons acting indecently, or person dirtily or
indecently dressed, or using obscene, abusive or insulting language, or
swearing or behaving in a manner calculated to disturb the peace of
the market;
(b) any person afflicted with leprosy, yaws, sores or any other loathsome
disease whatsoever.
Market Keeper May Give Directions.

19\. Every person in the market shall obey the lawful directions of the Market
Keeper.
Prohibited Acts.

20\.

No person shall—
(a) smoke, or spit in the market;
(b) sit or lie on any block, stand, counter or table, in the market, or on the
pavement thereof;
(c) sharpen any instrument on any part of the market structure;
(d) use any blasphemous, obscene, abusive or insulting language, or
swear, or otherwise misbehave in the market;
(e) fight or create or cause to be created any row, riot, or disturbance in
the market;
(f) throw filth, dirt, rubbish or litter in the market;
(g) open or clean the stomachs or intestines of any animal in the slaughterhouse;
(h) break, cut, pull down, destroy, remove, injure or spoil any part of the
market;
(i) be in the market during the hours that the market is not open to the
public without permission of the Market Keeper;
(j) sell or offer for sale any kerosene oil in the market;
(k) introduce, dispose of, consume, sell or offer for sale any wine, malt
liquor, spirits or intoxicants of any kind in the market;
(l) take or bring any dog, or permit any dog to follow, or accompany him
or her into the market;

(m) light any fire in the market, or cook therein, except in such parts
thereof and under such conditions as may be from time to time pointed
out and allowed by the Market Keeper;
(n) commit any nuisance in or at any part of the market.
No Responsibility for Loss or Damage to Property.

### 21.

Neither the government nor the Market Keeper shall be responsible for the loss
of or damage to any property left in the market.
Penalties.

### 22.

Every person who is found guilty of an offence against these regulations shall,
on being convicted thereof before a District Magistrate, be liable to a penalty not
exceeding three hundred dollars, and in default of payment, to imprisonment with or
without hard labour for any term not exceeding two months.

and in default of payment to imprisonment with or without hard labour for any term
not exceeding two months.

## FIFTH SCHEDULE

(Section 11)
SANDY POINT MARKET REGULATIONS
Short title.

1\.

These Regulations may be cited as the Sandy Point Market Regulations.

Interpretation.

2\.

In these Regulations “the market” means the public market in Sandy Point.

Open to public.

3\.
(1) The market shall be opened daily from 6 o’clock a.m. to 6 o’clock p.m.
except on Saturdays and on the eves of holidays when it shall be kept open until 9
o’clock p.m.
(2) On holidays the market shall be closed at 9 a.m.:
Provided always the butchers and their assistants shall be allowed to have
access to the slaughterhouse at such hour as shall be arranged for with the Market
Keeper, or in case of disagreement at such hour as shall be fixed by the member of
the District Board of Health who shall be charged with the inspection and care of the
market.
Officer in charge.

4\.
The Superintendent of Public Works shall take charge of the market and shall
keep such books as the District Board of Health shall require.
Market tables.

5\.
(1) Tables in the market shall be marked off and numbered and shall be let at
such rates as are mentioned in the Schedule to these regulations, and shall be paid for
weekly in advance, and any tenant or holder who refuses or neglects to pay his or her
rents as provided in this regulation shall not be permitted to occupy such table, and it
shall be the duty of the Market Keeper to remove such table forthwith.

(2) Every renter of a table shall give three days notice of his intention to quit
the same, and no article shall be kept on any table except such articles as are intended
for sale.
(3) Persons other than table holders bringing into the market any provisions or
other articles for sale shall place them in such part of the market as shall be pointed
out by the Market Keeper, and shall pay the fees mentioned in the Schedule to these
regulation.
Animals to be slaughtered in the market.

6\.
(1) No person shall, without the written permission of the Chief Medical
Officer, slaughter or cause to be slaughtered in the town of Sandy Point, otherwise
than in the slaughterhouse, any of the animals mentioned in the Schedule to these
regulations:
Provided that nothing in these regulations shall prevent any person from
slaughtering a sucking pig, lamb or kid at his or her own residence for his or her
domestic use.
(2) No person shall, without the written permission of the Chief Medical
Officer, bring or cause to be brought into the town of Sandy Point any meat intended
for disposal which has been slaughtered elsewhere than at the slaughterhouse.
(3) All cattle, sheep, goats and pigs brought to the market to be slaughtered for
consumption shall be placed in the stock shed at least fifteen hours before being
slaughtered:
Provided always that in case of emergency the time above mentioned may be
abridged by the member of the District Board of Health who shall be charged with
the inspection and care of the market.
Inspection of animals.

7\.
(1) The Sanitary Officer of the District Board of Health shall inspect and
approve of as fit for human food all animals whether for local consumption or
otherwise which are brought to the market to be slaughtered.
(2) If after the slaughter of any animal any doubt should arise as to its fitness
for human food the Sanitary Officer of the District shall forthwith summon the
Medical Officer of the District and obtain his or her opinion as to its fitness for
human consumption, and a fee of $1.20 shall be paid to the Medical Officer for this
inspection and opinion.
Collection of fees.

8\.
The Market Keeper shall collect such fees for slaughtering and for other
purposes as are mentioned in the Schedule to these regulations.
Slaughter house.

9\.
No person shall enter the slaughterhouse other than members of the District
Board of Health, the servants of the Board and those actually engaged in the
slaughtering of animals.

Butchers.

10\. (1) No person shall exercise the trade of a butcher or act as a butcher’s
assistant within the limits of the town of Sandy Point unless he or she is duly
registered as such as hereinafter provided.
(2) Every person who is desirous of exercising the trade of a butcher or acting
as a butcher’s assistant shall produce to the Chief Medical Officer a certificate in
writing signed by a medical practitioner that he or she is a fit and proper person to
exercise the trade of a butcher or act as a butcher’s assistant; and thereupon the Chief
Medical Officer shall register him or her accordingly and shall issue to him or her
free of cost a certificate of registration, and such a medical certificate will be required
periodically at intervals of six months.
(3) The Chief Medical Officer shall enter in a book to be called the “Register
of Butchers” the name and addresses of all persons registered as butchers or butchers’
assistants.
(4) If any person registered as a butcher or butcher’s assistant shall have by
using any forged certificate or other fraudulent means caused himself or herself to be
so registered, or shall misconduct himself or herself in the exercise of his or her trade,
or shall be three times convicted of offences against these regulations, or shall be
convicted of felony in connection with his or her trade, the Chief Medical Officer
may order his or her name to be removed from the Register of Butchers and
thereupon such person shall be disqualified from exercising the trade of a butcher or
acting as a butcher’s assistant, just as if he or she had never been registered as such:
Provided that before the name of such person is removed from the Register he
or she shall be given an opportunity of appearing before the Chief Medical Officer
and being heard.
(5) If any person exercises the trade of a butcher or acts as a butcher’s
assistant without being duly registered as hereinbefore provided he or she commits an
offence against these regulations.
(6) Every butcher shall be clean in his or her clothing and be provided with
clean cloths or towels when disposing of meat in his or her stall.
Butchers’ Stalls.

11\. Butchers’ stalls shall have their owners’ names painted in legible letters and
placed in front of the same and butchers shall conform to such directions for cleaning
their stalls as the Sanitary Officer of the District Board of Health may direct.
Discipline.

12\. (1) No person shall in any way obstruct or cause any obstruction at any
gateway, walk or passage or behave in a riotous or disorderly manner, or in any way
obstruct or impede the traffic of the market.
(2) No person shall tether or fasten any horse or other animal to the gates, rails
or trees in the market, or keep any animal in or under any sheds, except as
hereinbefore provided; or shall permit any such animal to be at large in the market.
(3) No person shall ride or drive in or through the market.
(4) No person shall erect any building, or shed, or plant or fix any poles in the
market without the consent of the District Board of Health.

(5) No person shall injure or destroy or remove any article of furniture or
other property belonging to the market, or tear down or deface or destroy any notice
in the market, printed or written, put up by public authority.
Market Keeper’s Powers.

13\. The Market Keeper may eject, or cause to be arrested or ejected from the
market, by any constable or other person—
(a) any disorderly person, or person acting indecently, or person dirtily or
indecently dressed, or using obscene, abusive or insulting language, or
swearing or behaving in a manner calculated to disturb the peace of
the market;
(b) any person afflicted with leprosy, yaws, sores, or any other loathsome
disease whatsoever.
Order in Market.

14\. Every person in the market shall obey the lawful directions of the Market
Keeper.
Offences.

15\.

No person shall—
(a) smoke or spit in the market;
(b) sit or lie on any block, stand, counter or table, in the market, or on the
pavement thereof;
(c) sharpen any instrument on any part of the market structure.
(d) use any blasphemous, obscene, abusive or insulting language, or
swear, or otherwise misbehave in the market;
(e) fight or create or cause to be created, any row, riot or disturbance in
the market;
(f) throw filth, dirt, rubbish or litter in the market;
(g) open or clean the stomachs or intestines of any animal in the
slaughterhouse;
(h) break, cut, pull down, destroy, remove, injure or spoil any part of the
market;
(i) be in the market during the hours that the market is not open to the
public without the permission of the Market Keeper;
(j) sell or offer for sale any kerosene oil in the market;
(k) introduce, dispose of, consume, sell or offer for sale any wine, malt
liquor, spirits or intoxicants of any kind in the market;
(l) take or bring any dog, or permit any dog to follow, or accompany him
or her into the market;
(m) light any fire in the market, or cook therein, except in such parts
thereof and under such conditions as may be from time to time pointed
out and allowed by the Market Keeper;
(n) commit any nuisance in, or at any part of the market.

Damage to Property.

16\. Neither the government nor the Market Keeper shall be responsible for the loss
of or damage to any property left in the market.
Penalties.

17\. Every person who is found guilty of any offence against these regulations
shall, on summary conviction, be liable to a penalty not exceeding three hundred
dollars, and in default of payment, to imprisonment with or without hard labour for
any term not exceeding two months.
