## CHAPTER 23.28

SANDY POINT LIMITS ACT
AN ACT TO DEFINE THE LIMITS OF SANDY POINT TOWN.
Short title.

---

### 1.

This Act may be cited as the Sandy Point Limits Act.

Limits of Sandy Point town defined.

### 2.

The limits of the town of Sandy Point in the island of St. Christopher shall be
deemed and taken to be as follows:
Commencing at a point on the sea-shore at the North West corner boundary wall of
Fort Charles, thence along the North boundary walls of the said Fort Charles to its
entrance gates on the North East, thence along the centre of Fort Charles Road to its
junction with the main public road around the island and continuing for a distance of
one hundred yards in a direction due East to a point in the lands of Bourkes Estate,
thence in a North Westerly direction along a line running one hundred yards parallel
with the highway through Sandy point to a point due East of the railway level
crossing on Mission Hill, crossing thence in a direct line to the windmill ruins on the
Pump Estate, thence in a direct line to the point West of Fig Tree Village where the
Fig Tree road meets the Belle Tete road, thence in a direct line through the ruins of
Fig Tree Fort to the sea-shore, thence along the sea-shore to the first point of
commencement aforesaid.
