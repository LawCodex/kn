## CHAPTER 23.02

BASSETERRE LIMITS ACT
AN ACT TO DEFINE THE LIMITS OF BASSETERRE.
Short title.

---

### 1.

This Act may be cited as the Basseterre Limits Act.

Boundaries of Town of Basseterre.

### 2.

The town of Basseterre shall be taken and considered to include all the land
lying between and within the lines to be drawn or considered as drawn in the
following manner, viz., beginning at the east end from a point on the sea shore lying
due south of the boundary line between the Pond Estate and the public land lately part
of the Lucas Estate and running thence in a straight line due north to the point lying
due east of the northern side of the path running east and west and leading along by
the north side of the Moravian Church towards the Taylor’s Estate; and beginning at
the west end from a point on the sea shore lying due south of the boundary line
between the La Guerite and Buckleys Estates, and running thence in a straight line
due north to a point lying due west of the said northern side of the said path leading
along by the north side of the Moravian Church and thence running due east until it
meets the northern side of the said path leading along by the north side of the
Moravian Church as aforesaid, the boundary on the south of the town of Basseterre
being the sea; and the bounds and limits of the town of Basseterre shall from
henceforth be taken and considered according to the lines to be drawn in the manner
aforesaid with the sea as the southern boundary as hereinbefore mentioned.
