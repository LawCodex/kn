## CHAPTER 15.01

CLOSURE OF STREETS ACT
AN ACT TO MAKE PROVISION FOR THE CLOSURE OF ANY STREET WHICH IS
UNNECESSARY FOR PUBLIC USE.
Short title.

---

### 1.

This Act may be cited as the Closure of Streets Act.

Interpretation.

### 2.

In this Act—

“street” means any public street, road, roadway, lane, causeway, footpath or other
way or part thereof and any bridge, culvert and drain on or used in connection
with a street.
Power of National Assembly to close streets.

### 3.

The National Assembly may, by resolution declare that any street is
unnecessary for public use and that such street shall be closed.
Notice to be given.

### 4.

When any street is declared to be closed the person authorised by the Minister
of Works shall give notice thereof in the Gazette and in the public newspapers
circulating in the State, that the said street, after an interval of three months from the
date of the publication of the Notice, shall be closed and be no longer a street for
public use.
