## CHAPTER 20.63

SAINT CHRISTOPHER AND NEVIS, GUYANA
AND BRAZIL PARTIAL SCOPE AGREEMENT ACT
AN ACT TO GIVE LEGAL EFFECT TO AND PROVIDE FOR THE IMPLEMENTATION OF THE
SAINT CHRISTOPHER AND NEVIS, GUYANA AND BRAZIL PARTIAL SCOPE AGREEMENT.
Short title.

---

### 1.

This Act may be cited as the Saint Christopher and Nevis, Guyana and Brazil
Partial Scope Agreement Act.
Interpretation.

### 2.

In this Act, “Minister” means the Minister responsible for Trade.

Agreement to have the force of law.

### 3.

(1) The Agreement set out in the Schedule to this Act shall have the force of
law in Saint Christopher and Nevis.
(2) The Minister may, by Order, publish any amendment made to the
Agreement.
(3) The Minister may, by Order, make such provision as is necessary or
expedient to give effect to any provision of the Agreement.

## SCHEDULE

(Section 3)
PARTIAL SCOPE ECONOMIC COMPLEMENTATION AGREEMENT NR.38
UNDER ARTICLE 25 OF THE 1980 MONTEVIDEO TREATY SIGNED BY THE
FEDERATIVE REPUBLIC OF BRAZIL AND THE COOPERATIVE REPUBLIC
OF GUYANA
SIXTH ADDITIONAL PROTOCOL
(Accession of the Federation of Saint Kitts and Nevis)
The Federative Republic of Brazil and the Cooperative Republic of Guyana,
Signatory Parties of the Agreement, and the Federation of Saint Kitts and Nevis, as
the acceding country;
CONSIDERING the Partial Scope Agreement signed by the Government of
the Federative Republic of Brazil and the Government of the Cooperative Republic of
Guyana on June 27, 2001, and its Articles 23 and 24, related to accession;
HEREBYAGREE:
Article 1.-To formalize, as provided under Articles 23 and 24 of the Partial Scope
Agreement signed by the Government of the Federative Republic of Brazil and the
Government of the Cooperative Republic of Guyana on June 27, 2001 (hereinafter
“the Agreement”), the accession of the Federation of Saint Kitts and Nevis through
the signature of this Protocol
Article 2. - As from the entry into force of this Additional Protocol, the title of the
Partial Scope Agreement Nr. 38 will be “PARTIAL SCOPE ECONMIC
COMPLEMENTATION AGREEMENT Nr. 38 UNDER ARTICLE 25 OF 1980
MONTEVIDEO TREATY SIGNED BY THE FEDERATIVE REPUBLIC OF
BRAZIL THE COOPERATIVE REPUBLIC OF GUYANA AND THE
FEDERATION OF SAINT KITTS AND NEVIS”.
Article 3. - The Agreement is amended so that, in its interpretation and application,
any reference to the term “Parties” must be understood as a reference to the
Federative Republic of Brazil, the Cooperative Republic of Guyana and the
Federative of Saint Kitts and Nevis.
Article 4. - The Federation of Saint Kitts and Nevis undertakes all commitments
arising from the Agreement, as modified by the Second Additional Protocol of
November 17, 2003, and extended indefinitely by the Fourth Additional Protocol of
August 26, 2008, and acquires all the rights that the Agreement grants to its
participants.
Article 5. - To Incorporate into the Agreement Annexes IV and V, which appear as
Annexes 1 and 2 to this Protocol, and contain the tariff preferences and other
conditions granted by the Federative Republic of Brazil to the Federation of Saint
Kitts and Nevis and by the latter to the Federative Republic of Brazil, respectively,

CAP. 20.63

for the import of the goods listed therein when originating from the territories of the
Parties.
Article 6. - To modify Article 20 of the Agreement as follows;
“Article 20.- The Parties agree to establish an Administrative Commission,
hereinafter referred to as “the Commission”, which shall be comprised of
representatives of the Federative Republic of Brazil, the Cooperative Republic of
Guyana and the Federation of Saint Kitts and Nevis.”
Article 7.- To modify Article 32 of the Agreement as follows:
“Article 32.- Imports by the Federative Republic of Brazil of the products from
the Cooperative Republic of Guyana and the Federation of Saint Kitts and Nevis
included in this Agreement shall be exempted from the application of the Surcharge
of Freight for the Renovation of the Merchant Marine, established by Brazilian
Decree number 2404, of December 23, 1987, in accordance with the provisions of
Brazilian Decree number 97945, of July, 11, 1989 and its modifying and
complementary provisions.”
Article 8.- This Protocol will enter into force thirty (30) days after being deposited
with the ALADI General Secretariat.
