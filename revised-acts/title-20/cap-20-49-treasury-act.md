## CHAPTER 20.49

TREASURY ACT
Short title.

---

### 1.

This Act may be cited as the Treasury Act.

Appointment of officers.

### 2.

It shall be lawful for the Governor-General, acting on the recommendation of
the Public Service Commission, to appoint an Accountant-General, and such other
officers of the Treasury Department as may from time to time be necessary, who shall
receive such salaries and allowances as may be voted by the National Assembly.
(Amended by the Constitution by necessary implication)

Moneys to be paid to the Accountant-General for the public uses of the State.

### 3.

All moneys payable to the Crown by virtue of any Act passed, or to be passed,
the appropriation of which is not otherwise directed, shall be paid to the AccountantGeneral for the public uses of the State.
(Amended by Act 6 of 1976)

Functions.

### 4.

The officers appointed under this Act shall be the Treasury Officers of the
State.
Rules.

### 5.

It shall be lawful for the Accountant-General to make rules for the government
of the Treasury Department, and such rules after being approved by the Minister and
published in the Gazette shall have the force of law.
(Amended by Act 6 of 1976)

## SCHEDULE

(Section 5)
TREASURY RULES
Short title.

1\.

These Rules may be cited as the Treasury Rules.

Hours of Attendance of Treasury Officers.

2\.
(1) The Treasury and the Revenue Offices in Nevis shall be kept open for the
transaction of business with the public on every day of the year except Saturdays,
Sundays and public holidays, from the hour of nine o’clock in the morning until
twelve o’clock noon and from one o’clock in the afternoon until two-thirty o’clock in
the afternoon, and on Saturdays from the hour of nine o’clock in the morning until
the hour of eleven o’clock in the morning.
(2) The several public officers attached to the Treasury and to the Revenue
Offices in Nevis shall give attendance at their respective offices on every day of the
year except Saturdays, Sundays and public holidays, from the hour of eight-thirty
o’clock in the morning until the hour of four o’clock in the afternoon, and they shall
attend on Saturdays from the hour of eight-thirty o’clock in the morning until the
hour of twelve o’clock noon:
Provided that it shall be lawful for the Ministry of Finance to extend the hours
for the transaction of business at the Treasury and at Revenue Offices in Nevis and
for the attendance of the Public Officers attached to any or all of those offices.
(3) Every officer attached to the Treasury or to the Revenue Offices in Nevis
shall be entitled to absent himself or herself from his or her office on every day on
which his or her attendance is required, except on Saturdays, for a period of not more
than one hour as a luncheon interval, such period to be fixed in each case by the
Accountant General.
After Hours Attendance.

3\.
The Accountant-General may require the attendance of any officer before or
after the usual office hours, and he or she may further require any officer to render
such assistance as may be necessary to prevent the work of the Department from
falling into arrear.
Control of Department.

4\.
The Accountant-General shall be the head of the Department and shall be
responsible to the Minister of Finance for the efficient performance of the duties of
the respective officers.
Out Stations.

5\.
The Accountant-General shall, as often as necessary, visit the Revenue Offices
in Nevis.
Control in Absence of Accountant-General.

### 6.

In the event of illness or absence from the office by the Accountant-General,
the control of the Department shall devolve upon the officer next in seniority unless

the Governor- General, acting upon the recommendation of the Public Service
Commission, shall otherwise direct.
Signing of Cheques.

### 7.

Cheques drawn against the Consolidated Fund of the State shall be signed by
such officers as shall be designated for that purpose by the Accountant-General.
Accountant.

### 8.

The officer performing the duties of Accountant shall be responsible for the
supervision of all entries in all books of account and for the accuracy for all returns
required by the Financial Instructions or by any law.
Cashiers.

### 9.

The officers performing the duties of Cashier shall receive all moneys paid
into the Treasury or at the Revenue Offices in Nevis and disburse from cash all
claims which are not payable by cheque. They shall also receive all moneys and
disburse all cash claims pertaining to the Savings Bank.
Savings Bank.

### 10.

The officer performing the duties of Accountant of the Savings Bank shall
keep the Savings Bank ledger and prepare the quarterly balance sheet and other
returns.
