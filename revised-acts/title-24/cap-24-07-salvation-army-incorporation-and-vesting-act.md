## CHAPTER 24.07

SALVATION ARMY INCORPORATION AND VESTING ACT
AN ACT TO PROVIDE FOR THE INCORPORATION OF THE SALVATION ARMY.
Short title.

---

### 1.

This Act may be cited as the Salvation Army Incorporation and Vesting Act.

Incorporation.

### 2.

General Eva Burrows the General of the Salvation Army and her successors in
office shall be and is hereby constituted a corporation by the name of “The General of
the Salvation Army” and by this name shall have perpetual succession and also by
that name can sue and be sued, and shall have a common seal.
Transfer and Vesting of property.

### 3.

All real and personal property in Saint Christopher and Nevis and any estate or
interest in or concerning the same respecting which are now held or vested in any
person on behalf of the members of the Salvation Army in Saint Christopher and
Nevis are hereby transferred and vested in “The General of the Salvation Army” and
her successors and assigns for the same estate and interest and to the extent which the
same were vested in the person.
Appointment of General of the Salvation Army.

### 4.

Upon the recording in the High Court Registry in Saint Christopher and Nevis
of copies certified by the proper officer or sealed by the proper Department of the
High Court of Justice in England of documents purporting to be the appointment of
and acceptance of office by a General of the Salvation Army, the person named in
such appointment and acceptance shall be deemed to be the General of the Salvation
Army until the recording of similarly certified copies of new appointment and
acceptance.
Power to acquire property.

### 5.

The General of the Salvation Army shall have full power to acquire by
purchase, transfer, donation, exchange, devise, bequest, grant, conveyance or
otherwise any real or personal property in Saint Christopher and Nevis or any estate
or interest therein.
Power to sell property.

### 6.

The General of the Salvation Army shall, subject to any restraint, reservation
or condition contained in the document under which he or she shall have acquired
title thereto, be entitled to sell, mortgage, lease and otherwise dispose of and deal
with all property which may for the time being be vested in or have been acquired by
him or her under any of the provisions of this Act and he or she may sue for and
recover all property and effects both real and personal and may assign and transfer
any monies belonging to the Salvation Army or to any person on behalf of the
Salvation Army or to any General of the Salvation Army in the capacity of General of
the Salvation Army.

Power to appoint an attorney.

### 7.

The General of the Salvation Army shall have full power to appoint an
attorney for such powers as may be stated in the power of attorney and to revoke any
such appointment and to confer on any such attorney appointed as aforesaid power to
appoint a substitute in his or her stead.
Saving of Crown and other rights.

### 8.

Nothing in this Act shall prejudice or affect the rights of the Crown, Her Heirs
and Successors or any body politic or corporate or of any other persons except such
as are mentioned in this Act and those claiming by, from, through, or under them.
