## CHAPTER 24.01

CHURCH OF GOD OF PROPHECY, NEVIS INCORPORATION ACT
AN ACT TO PROVIDE FOR THE INCORPORATION OF THE CHURCH OF GOD OF PROPHECY,
NEVIS, AND TO PROVIDE FOR RELATED OR INCIDENTAL MATTERS.
Short title.

---

### 1.

Act.

This Act may be cited as the Church of God of Prophecy, Nevis Incorporation

Incorporation.

### 2.

The Church of God of Prophecy, Nevis, shall be a body corporate, and shall, in
its corporate name—
(a) have perpetual succession;
(b) be capable of suing and being sued in a court of law;
(c) have a common seal;
(d) be capable of acquiring property by purchase, exchange, demise or
otherwise in perpetuity or for the life or lives for any term of years or
upon any terms, conditions or limitations.
Vesting of property.

### 3.

Any property that belonged, or was held in trust or on behalf of the Church of
God of Prophecy, Nevis prior to the coming into force of this Act is hereby vested in
the Church of God of Prophecy, Nevis, incorporated by this Act.
Power to dispose of property

### 4.

The Church of God of Prophecy, Nevis may, subject to the regulations made
under this Act, from time to time, by deed under its seal, sell or demise, grant,
convey, exchange or otherwise dispose of any land or other property vested in the
Church of God of Prophecy, Nevis, as it sees fit.

### 5.

Nothing in this Act shall affect the rights of Her Majesty, Her Heirs or
successor or anybody politic or corporate or any other person except such as are
mentioned in this Act and those claiming by, through, from or under them.
Regulations.

### 6.

The Church of God of Prophecy, Nevis may, with the approval of the Minister
responsible for ecclesiastic affairs, make regulations to manage it affairs.
