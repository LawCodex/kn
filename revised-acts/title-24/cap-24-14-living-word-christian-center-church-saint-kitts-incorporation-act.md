## CHAPTER 24.14

LIVING WORD CHRISTIAN CENTER CHURCH, SAINT KITTS
INCORPORATION ACT
AN ACT TO PROVIDE FOR THE INCORPORATION OF THE LIVING WORD CHRISTIAN
CENTER CHURCH, SAINT KITTS AND TO PROVIDE FOR RELATED OR INCIDENTAL
MATTERS.
Short title.

### 1.

This Act may be cited as the Living Word Chris an Center Church, Saint Kitts
Incorporation Act.
Incorporation of Living Word Christian Center Church. Saint Kitts.

2
The Living Word Christian Center Church, Saint Kitts shall be a body
corporate, and shall, in its corporate name—
(a) have perpetual succession;
(b) be capable of suing and being sued in a court of law;
(c) have a common seal;
(d) subject to the rules and regulations made under this Act, be capable of
acquiring lands or property by purchase, exchange, demise or
otherwise, in perpetuity or for the life or lives for any term of years or
upon any other terms, conditions or limitations.
Vesting of property in the Living Word Christian Center Church. Saint Kitts.

### 3.

(1) Any property that belonged to or was held in trust for or on behalf of the
Living Word Christian Center Church, Saint Kitts prior to the coming into force of
this Act is hereby vested in the Living Word Christian Center Church, Saint Kitts.
(2) All property that is devised or bequeathed by will or otherwise acquired by
voluntary gift, and all property that is vested or hereafter acquired by the Living
Word Christian Center Church, Saint Kitts shall be deemed to the property of the
Living Word Christian Center Church, Saint Kitts.
Power to dispose of property.

### 4.

The Living Word Christian Center Church, Saint Kitts may, subject to the
rules and regulations made under this Act, by deed under its seal, sell or demise,
grant, convey exchange or otherwise dispose of any land or other property vested in
the Living Word Christian Center Church, Saint Kitts.
Saving.

### 5.

Nothing in this Act shall affect the rights of Her Majesty, Her Heirs or
Successors or any body politic or corporate or any other person except such as are
mentioned in this Act and those claiming by, through, from or under them.
