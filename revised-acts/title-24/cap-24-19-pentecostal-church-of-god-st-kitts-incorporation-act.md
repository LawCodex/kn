## CHAPTER 24.19

PENTECOSTAL CHURCH OF GOD,
ST. KITTS (INCORPORATION) ACT
AN ACT TO PROVIDE FOR THE INCORPORATION OF THE PENTECOSTAL CHURCH OF GOD
ST. KITTS AND TO PROVIDE FOR RELATED OR INCIDENTAL MATTERS.
Short title.

---

### 1.

This Act may be cited as the Pentecostal Church of God St. Kitts
(Incorporation) Act.
Interpretation.

### 2.

The Pentecostal Church of God, St. Kitts shall be a body corporate, and shall
in its corporate name—
(a) have perpetual succession;
(b) be capable of suing and being sued in a court of law;
(c) have a common seal;
(d) be capable of acquiring property by purchase, exchange, demise or
otherwise in perpetuity or for the life or lives for any term of years or
upon any terms, conditions or limitations.
Vesting of property.

### 3.

Any property that belonged to or was held in trust for or on behalf of the
Pentecostal Church of God, St. Kitts prior to the coming into force of this Act is
hereby vested in the Pentecostal Church of God, St. Kitts incorporated by this Act.
Power to dispose of property.

### 4.

The Pentecostal Church of God, St. Kitts may, subject to the Regulations made
under this Act, from time to time, by Deed under its seal, sell, mortgage or demise,

the Pentecostal Church of God, St. Kitts as it sees fit.
Regulations.

### 5.

The Church Council consisting of the Bishop as the Head of Council and
Pastors or Minister of the Church may make rules and regulations to manage its
affairs.
Saving.

### 6.

Nothing in this Act shall affect the rights of Her Majesty, Her Heirs or
successors or any body politic or corporate or any other person except such as are
mentioned in this Act and those claiming by, through, from or under them.
