## CHAPTER 24.18

CHURCH OF GOD 7TH DAY INCORPORATION ACT
AN ACT TO PROVIDE FOR THE INCORPORATION OF THE CHURCH OF GOD 7TH DAY, AND
TO PROVIDE FOR RELATED OR INCIDENTAL MATTERS.
Short title.

---

### 1.

This Act may be cited as the Church of God 7th Day Incorporation Act.

Incorporation.

### 2.

The Church of God 7th Day, shall be a body corporate, and shall in its
corporate name—
(a) have perpetual succession;
(b) be capable of suing and being sued in a court of law;
(c) have a common seal;
(d) be capable of acquiring property by purchase, exchange, demise or
otherwise in perpetuity or for the life or lives for any term of years or
upon any terms, conditions or limitations.
Constitution of Board of Trustees.

### 3.

(1) The Church of God 7th Day shall have a Board of Trustees constituted by
the Council of the Caribbean Conference of Church of God 7th Day.
(2) The Board shall be responsible for the management of all the affairs of the
Church of God 7th Day.
Vesting of property in and powers of the Board.

### 4.

(1) All lands or other property, real or personal that belonged, or was held in
trust or on behalf of the Church of God 7th Day prior to the coming into force of this
Act is hereby vested in the Board of Trustees of the Church of God 7th Day
incorporated by this Act.
(2) The Board of Trustees shall have full power to acquire by purchase,
transfer, donation, exchange, devise, bequest, grant, or otherwise any real or personal
property in Saint Christopher and Nevis or any estate or interest therein on behalf of
the Church of God 7th Day.
Power to dispose of property.

### 5.

The Board of Trustees may subject to the Regulations made under this Act,
from time to time, by Deed under its seal, sell, mortgage or demise, grant, convey,
exchange or otherwise dispose of any land or other property vested in the Board of
Trustees, on behalf of the Church of God 7th Day, as it sees fit.
Savings.

### 6.

Nothing in this Act shall affect the rights of Her Majesty, Her Heirs or
successors or any body politic or corporate or any other person except such as are
mentioned in this Act and those claiming by, through, from or under them.

Regulations.

### 7.

The Church of God 7th Day, may with the approval of the Minister
responsible for Ecclesiastical Affairs make Regulations to manage its affairs.
