## CHAPTER 24.13

VALLEY BAPTIST CHURCH ST. KITTS INCORPORATION ACT
AN ACT TO PROVIDE FOR THE INCORPORATION OF THE VALLEY BAPTIST CHURCH,
SAINT KITTS, AND TO PROVIDE FOR RELATED OR INCIDENTAL MATTERS.
Short title.

---

### 1.

Act.

This Act may be cited as the Valley Baptist Church, Saint Kitts Incorporation

Incorporation of Valley Baptist Church.

### 2.

The Valley Baptist Church, Saint Kitts, shall be a body corporate, and shall, in
its corporate name—
(a) have perpetual succession;
(b) be capable of suing and being sued in a court of law;
(c) have a common seal;
(d) be capable of acquiring property by purchase, exchange, demise or
otherwise, in perpetuity or for the life or lives for any term of years or
upon any other terms, conditions or limitations.
Vesting of property in the Valley Baptist Church.

### 3.

(1) Any property that belonged to or was held in trust for or on behalf of the
Valley Baptist Church, Saint Kitts prior to the coming into force of this Act is hereby
vested in the Valley Baptist Church, Saint Kitts.
(2) The Valley Baptist Church, Saint Kitts may, subject to the rules and
regulations made under this Act acquire by purchase, exchange, demise or otherwise,
lands or property, in perpetuity or for the life or lives for any term of years or upon
any other terms, conditions or limitations.
Power to dispose of property.

### 4.

The Valley Baptist Church, Saint Kitts, may, subject to the rules and
regulations made under this Act, from time to time by deed under its seal, sell or

### 5.

Nothing in this Act shall affect the rights of Her Majesty, Her Heirs or
Successors or any body politic or corporate or any other person except such as are
