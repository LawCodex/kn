## CHAPTER 24.04

NEW TESTAMENT CHURCH OF GOD NEVIS INCORPORATION ACT
AN ACT TO PROVIDE FOR THE INCORPORATION OF THE NEW TESTAMENT CHURCH OF
GOD, NEVIS; AND TO PROVIDE FOR RELATED OR INCIDENTAL MATTERS.
Short title.

---

### 1.

This Act may be cited as the New Testament Church of God, Nevis,
Incorporation Act.
Incorporation.

### 2.

The New Testament Church of God, Nevis, shall be a body corporate, and
shall, in its corporate name—
(a) have perpetual succession;
(b) be capable of suing and being sued in a court of law;
(c) have a common seal;
(d) be capable of acquiring property by purchase, exchange, demise or
otherwise, in perpetuity or for the life or lives for any term of years or
upon any other terms, conditions or limitations.
Vesting of property.

### 3.

Any property that belonged, or was held in trust or on behalf of the New
Testament Church of God, Nevis, prior to the coming into force of this Act, is hereby
vested in the New Testament Church of God, Nevis, incorporated by this Act.
Power to dispose of property.

### 4.

The New Testament Church of God, Nevis may, subject to the regulations
made under this Act, from time to time, by deed under its seal, sell or demise, grant,
convey, exchange or otherwise dispose of, any land or other property vested in the
New Testament Church of God, Nevis, as it sees fit.

Saving.

### 5.

Nothing in this Act shall affect the rights of the Crown, Her Heirs or
successors or any body politic or corporate or any other person except such as are
mentioned in this Act and those claiming by, through, from or under them.
Regulations.

### 6.

The New Testament Church of God, Nevis may, with the approval of the
Minister responsible for ecclesiastic affairs, make regulations to manage its affairs.
