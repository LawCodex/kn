## CHAPTER 24.20

SHILOH BAPTIST CHURCH NEVIS INCORPORATION ACT
AN ACT TO PROVIDE FOR THE INCORPORATION OF THE SHILOH BAPTIST CHURCH, NEVIS
AND TO PROVIDE FOR RELATED AND INCIDENTAL MATTERS.
Short title.

### 1.

This Act may be cited as the Shiloh Baptist Church Nevis Incorporation Act.

Interpretation.

### 2.

The Shiloh Baptist Church, Nevis shall be a body corporate, and shall in its
corporate name—
(a) have perpetual succession;
(b) be capable of suing and being sued in a court of law;
(c) have a common seal;
(d) be capable of acquiring property by purchase, exchange, demise or
otherwise in perpetuity or for the life or lives for any term of years or
upon any terms, conditions or limitations.
Board of Trustees.

### 3.

The Shiloh Baptist Church, Nevis shall have a Board of Trustees which shall be
responsible for the management of all the affairs of the Shiloh Baptist Church, Nevis.
Vesting of property.

### 4.

(1) All lands or other property, real or personal, that belonged to or was held
in trust for or on behalf of the Shiloh Baptist Church, Nevis, prior to the coming into
force of this Act are hereby vested in the Board of Trustees of the Shiloh Baptist
Church, Nevis incorporated by this Act.
(2) Notwithstanding the provisions of subsection (1), the properties, more
fully described in the Schedules to this Act and held on behalf of the Shiloh Baptist
Church, Nevis, immediately prior to the coming into force of this Act are hereby
vested in the Board of Trustees of the Shiloh Baptist Church, Nevis, incorporated by
this Act.
(3) The Board of Trustees shall have full power to acquire by purchase,
transfer, donation, exchange, devise, bequest, grant, or otherwise any real or personal
property in Saint Christopher and Nevis or any estate or interest therein on behalf of
the Shiloh Baptist Church, Nevis.
Power to dispose of property.

### 5.

The Shiloh Baptist Church, Nevis may, subject to the regulations made under
this Act, from time to time, by deed under its seal, sell, mortgage or demise, grant,
convey, exchange or otherwise dispose of any land or other property vested in the
Shiloh Baptist Church, Nevis as it sees fit.

Saving.

### 6.

Nothing in this Act shall affect the rights of Her Majesty, Her Heirs or
successors or any body politic or corporate or any other person except such as are
mentioned in this Act and those claiming by, through, from or under them.
Regulations.

### 7.

The Shiloh Baptist Church, Nevis may, with the approval of the Minister
responsible for Ecclesiastical Affairs, make regulations to manage its affairs.
