## CHAPTER 24.16

NEVIS METHODIST TRUST INCORPORATION ACT
AN ACT TO PROVIDE FOR THE INCORPORATION OF THE METHODIST CHURCH OF NEVIS
AND TO PROVIDE FOR RELATED OR INCIDENTAL MATTERS.
Short title.

---

### 1.

This Act may be cited as the Nevis Methodist Trust Incorporation Act.

Interpretation.

### 2.

In this Act, unless the context indicates otherwise—

“Association” means the Wesleyan Methodist Missionary Trust Association
incorporated in England under the Imperial Companies Acts 1862 to 1893;
“Church” means the Methodist Church in the Caribbean and the Americas;
“Committee” means the Circuit Resources and Development Committee of the Nevis
Methodist Circuit;
“Corporation” means the Nevis Methodist Trust Corporation established by section 3;
“District” means the Leeward Islands District of the Methodist Church in the
Caribbean and the Americas;
Circuit Council Meeting” means the Circuit Council Meeting of the Nevis Circuit as
provided in the Constitution and Discipline of the Church.
Incorporation of the Nevis Methodist Trust Corporation.

### 3.

The Nevis Methodist Trust Corporation is hereby established as a body
corporate and shall, in its corporate name—
(a) have perpetual succession;
(b) be capable of suing and being sued in a court of law;
(c) have a common seal;
(d) be capable of acquiring property by purchase, exchange, demise or
otherwise, in perpetuity or for the life or lives for any term of years or
upon any other terms, conditions or limitations;
(e) consist of the Superintendent Minister and the members of the
Committee for the time being and the successors in office as the
Superintendent Minister and the members of the Committee; as well
as the representative of the District, and of the Connexional-Trust
Corporation.
Executive and member.

### 4.

(1) The Superintendent Minister and one of the Circuit Stewards of the Nevis
Circuit Council Meeting shall be respectively the Chairman and the Secretary of the
Corporation.
(2) In the event of the death or absence from Nevis of any member of the
Corporation, all or any of the powers conferred on the Corporation by this Act may
be exercised by the surviving or remaining members on the condition that such
powers shall not be exercised by less than seven members of the Corporation.

(3) A member shall continue in office until his tenure terminates in
accordance with the Constitution and Discipline of the Church.
Property to vest in the Corporation.

### 5.

(1) Any property in Nevis that belonged to, was held in trust for or on behalf
of or vested in the Association and all property in Nevis to which the Association is
beneficially or otherwise entitled shall be hereby vested in the Corporation without
further assurance.
(2) The properties listed in Schedule 1 are hereby vested in the Corporation.
(3) All property in Nevis that is devised or bequeathed by will or otherwise
acquired by voluntary gift by the Nevis Circuit shall be deemed to be the property of
the Corporation.
(4) All property vested in or hereafter acquired by the Corporation shall be
held by the Corporation for the use and benefit of the Nevis Circuit subject to the
Constitution and Discipline of the Church.
Execution of sealed and unsealed documents.

### 6.

(1) A document required to be formally executed by or on behalf of the
Corporation shall be deemed to be sufficiently executed if the Common Seal of the
Corporation is affixed in the presence of and is signed by the Chairman of the
Corporation, or in such other manner as may be determined by the Corporation.
(2) A document required by law to be executed under seal shall be deemed to
be duly executed if signed by a person or persons authorised by the Corporation to
sign unless the contrary is proved.
(3) A person signing as Chairman, Secretary, or member of the Corporation
shall be taken so to be, unless the contrary is proved, and any statement or certificate
that a person named therein is the Chairman, Secretary or member of the Corporation,
or that a document is a true copy of any document or entry in the books or records of
the Corporation purporting to be signed by the Chairman, the Secretary or a member
shall be admissible as prima facie evidence of the fact of the original document or
entry.
Service of documents.

### 7.

A document or process may be served on the Corporation by—
(a) delivering it to the Chairman or the Secretary of the Corporation;
(b) sending it by registered post to the address of the Corporation.

Power of Corporation to make by-laws.

### 8.

(1) The Corporation shall have authority to make by-laws for its own internal
management and control and such by-laws shall be binding on every member thereof.
(2) Subject to any by-laws made in exercise of the authority conferred by
subsection (1) above, the Corporation shall be governed and managed in accordance
with the Constitution and Discipline of the Church.

Savings.

### 9.

Nothing in this Act shall prejudice or affect the rights of Her Majesty the
Queen, Her Heirs and Successors, the Government, or any other person except as are
mentioned in the Act and those claiming by, from, through or under them.
