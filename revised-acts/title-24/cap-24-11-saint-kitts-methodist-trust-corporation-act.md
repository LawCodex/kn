## CHAPTER 24.11

SAINT KITTS METHODIST TRUST CORPORATION ACT
AN ACT TO INCORPORATE THE SAINT KITTS METHODIST TRUST CORPORATION TO HOLD
IN THE ISLAND OF SAINT KITTS THE PROPERTY OF THE METHODIST CHURCH IN THE
CARIBBEAN AND THE AMERICAS; AND TO PROVIDE FOR RELATED OR INCIDENTAL
MATTERS.
Short title.

### 1.

This Act may be cited as the Saint Kitts Methodist Trust Corporation Act.

Interpretation.

### 2.

In this Act—

“Association” means the Wesleyan Methodist Missionary Trust Association
incorporated in England under the Imperial Companies Acts 1862 to 1893;
“Church” (so written) means the Methodist Church in the Caribbean and the
Americas, but “churches” (so written) has its ordinary meaning;
“Committee” means the Finance and Property Committee of the Saint Kitts Circuit;
“Corporation” means the Corporation established by section 3;
“District” means the Leeward Islands District of the Methodist Church in the
Caribbean and the Americas;
“Quarterly Meeting” means the Quarterly Meeting of the Saint Kitts Circuit as
provided in the Constitution and Discipline of the Church.
Incorporation of the Saint Kitts Methodist Trust Corporation.

### 3.

(1) There is hereby established a body Corporate to be known as “The Saint
Kitts Methodist Trust Corporation” which shall, by that name, have perpetual
succession and a Common Seal and shall consist of the Superintendent Minister and
the members of the Committee for the time being and the successors in office as such
Superintendent Minister and as such members of the Committee.
(2) The Superintendent Minister and one of the Circuit Stewards of the Saint
Kitts Circuit designated for that purpose by the Quarterly Meeting shall be,
respectively, the Chairperson and the Secretary of the Corporation.
Death or absence from St. Kitts of members of the Corporation.

### 4.

In the event of the death or absence from Saint Kitts of any member of the
Corporation, all or any of the powers conferred on the Corporation by this Act may
be exercised by the surviving or remaining members:
Provided however that such powers may not be exercised by less than seven
members of the Corporation.
Tenure of membership.

### 5.

A member shall continue in office until his or her tenure of office terminates in
accordance with the Constitution and Discipline of the Church.

Capacity of the Corporation.

### 6.

The Corporation shall have full power—
(a) to acquire by purchase, devise or otherwise, property real and personal
of whatever kind within Saint Kitts and to hold, control, improve,
manage, mortgage, convey, assign, demise or otherwise dispose of
such property;
(b) to borrow and take up at interest any sum or sums of money for the
purpose of erecting or improving or aiding in erecting or improving
churches, schools, manses, dwelling-houses or other buildings or
structures, and for that purpose to mortgage by deed or otherwise
under the Seal of the Corporation such portions of its property as may
be necessary for securing the repayment of such sums of money with
interest thereon; and
(c) to take, hold, employ and invest all such property as they deem
advisable, but only for the purposes of the Corporation.

Vesting of property in the Corporation.

### 7.

(1) All property of whatever nature and kind in Saint Kitts which, before the
commencement of this Act, was vested in the Association and all property in Saint
Kitts to which the Association is beneficially or otherwise entitled shall be and the
same are hereby vested, without further assurance, in the Corporation.
(2) The properties listed in the Schedule are hereby vested in the Corporation.
Vesting of property bequeathed.

### 8.

All property of whatever nature and kind hereafter devised or bequeathed by
will or otherwise acquired by voluntary gift by the Saint Kitts Circuit shall be deemed
to be the property of the Corporation.
Tenure of property for use and benefit of the Circuit.

### 9.

All property vested in or hereafter acquired by the Corporation shall be held by
the Corporation for the use and benefit of the Saint Kitts Circuit subject always to the
Constitution and Discipline of the Church.
Execution of documents.

### 10.

(1) Any document requiring to be formally executed by or on behalf of the
Corporation shall be deemed to be sufficiently executed if the Common Seal of the
Corporation is affixed in the presence of and is signed by the Chairperson and the
Secretary of the Corporation, or in such other manner as may, from time to time, be
determined by the Corporation.
(2) Any other document not required by law to be executed under seal shall be
deemed to be duly executed if signed by a person or persons authorised by the
Corporation so to sign and such person or persons shall be deemed to be so
designated in such document and to be so authorised, unless the contrary is proved.
Evidence of acts.

### 11.

Any person signing as Chairperson, Secretary or member of the Corporation
shall be taken so to be, until the contrary is proved, and any statement or certificate
that any person named therein is the Chairperson, Secretary or member of the

Corporation, or that a document is a true copy of any document or of any extract from
any document or entry in the books or records of the Corporation purporting to be
signed by the Chairperson, the Secretary or a member shall be admissible as prima
facie evidence of the fact of the original document or entry.
Service of process.

### 12.

Any document or process may be served on the Corporation by—
(a) delivering it to the Chairperson or the Secretary of the Corporation; or
(b) sending it by registered post to the address of the Corporation.

Power to make By-laws.

### 13.

(1) The Corporation shall have authority to make by-laws for its own internal
management and control and such by-laws shall be binding on every member thereof.
(2) Subject to any by-laws made in exercise of the authority conferred by
subsection (1) above, the Corporation shall be governed and managed in accordance
with the Constitution and Discipline of the Church.
Rights of the Queen.

### 14.

Nothing in this Act shall prejudice or affect the rights of Her Majesty the
Queen, Her Heirs and Successors, or any other person or persons except as are
mentioned in this Act and those claiming by, from, through or under them.
