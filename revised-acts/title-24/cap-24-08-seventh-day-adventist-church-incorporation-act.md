## CHAPTER 24.08

SEVENTH DAY ADVENTIST CHURCH INCORPORATION ACT
AN ACT TO INCORPORATE THE NORTH CARIBBEAN CONFERENCE OF SEVENTH-DAY
ADVENTISTS; AND TO PROVIDE FOR RELATED OR INCIDENTAL MATTERS.
Short title.

---

### 1.

Act.

This Act may be cited as the Seventh-Day Adventist Church Incorporation

Constitution of Board of Trustees.

### 2.

A Board of Trustees consisting of the President and Secretary of the North
Caribbean Conference of Seventh-Day Adventists and one other person to be
appointed in accordance with the Constitution of the North Caribbean Conference of
Seventh-Day Adventists shall be and is hereby created a body corporate by the name
of “The Board of Trustees of the Seventh-Day Adventist Church” hereinafter called
“The Board of Trustees” and by such name as aforesaid shall have perpetual
succession and shall be capable of suing and being sued in all Courts of Justice in
Saint Kitts and Nevis and shall have and use a common seal which may from time to
time be altered by the North Caribbean Conference of the Seventh-Day Adventist
Church.
Vesting of property in and powers of Board.

### 3.

There shall be vested in the Board of Trustees all lands or other property, real,
or personal of any kind whatsoever acquired or held by or for the benefit of the
Seventh-Day Adventist Church and the Board of Trustees shall, subject to the rules,
regulations and control of the North Caribbean Conference of Seventh-Day
Adventists have full power to acquire for the said Church by purchase, exchange,
demise, gift, devise or otherwise, lands or other property, real or personal of any kind
whatsoever in perpetuity or for a life or lives or for any term of years or upon any
other terms, conditions or limitations subject to such rules and regulations and control
as aforesaid and subject also to the performance of any trust upon which any lands or
other property may have been held or acquired.
Power of Board to dispose of Property.

### 4.

It shall be lawful for the Board of Trustees, subject to such rules, regulations
and control as aforesaid from time to time by deed under their seal to sell or demise,
