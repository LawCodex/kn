## CHAPTER 24.15

REDEEMED CHRISTIAN CHURCH OF
GOD INCORPORATION ACT
AN ACT TO PROVIDE FOR THE INCORPORATION OF THE REDEEMED CHRISTIAN CHURCH
OF GOD, AND TO PROVIDE FOR RELATED OR INCIDENTAL MATTERS.
Short title.

---

### 1.

This Act may be cited as the Redeemed Christian Church of God,
Incorporation Act.
Incorporation.

### 2.

The Redeemed Christian Church of God shall be a body corporate, and shall,
in its corporate name—
(a) have perpetual succession;
(b) be capable of suing and being sued;
(c) have a common seal;
(d) be capable of acquiring property by purchase, exchange, demise or
otherwise, in perpetuity or for the life or lives for any term of years or
upon any other terms, conditions or limitations.
Membership and Executive.

### 3.

(1) The membership of the Church shall be of three classes, namely—
(a) members of the congregation (non-voting, except as provided in the
bylaws);
(b) the Board of Directors; and
(c) the Board of Trustees.

(2) The Board of Directors shall be constituted of the Chairperson (Pastor), the
Secretary and others who will be appointed in accordance with the bylaws of the
Church, and shall be responsible for the day to day management of the Church.

(3) The Board of Trustees shall be constituted by such persons as the Main
Church may appoint.
(4) For the purposes of subsection (3), “Main Church” means the Regional
Coordinating Church or Parish.
Power to dispose of property.

### 4.

The Redeemed Christian Church of God may, subject to the regulations made
under this Act, from time to time, by deed under its seal, sell or demise, grant, convey
exchange or otherwise dispose of any land or other property vested in the Redeemed
Christian Church of God as it sees fit.
Saving.

### 5.

Nothing in this Act shall affect the rights of Her Majesty, Her Heirs or
Successors or any body politic or corporate or any other person except such as are
mentioned in this Act and those claiming by, through, from or under them.
Regulations.

### 6.

The Redeemed Christian Church of God may, with the approval of the
Minister responsible for Ecclesiastical Affairs, make regulations to manage its affairs.
