## CHAPTER 24.10

ST. KITTS DISTRICT OF THE WESLEYAN HOLINESS CHURCH ACT
AN ACT TO PROVIDE FOR THE ESTABLISHMENT OF THE ST. KITTS DISTRICT OF THE
WESLEYAN HOLINESS CHURCH TO PERFORM THE FUNCTIONS FORMERLY PERFORMED BY
THE PILGRIM HOLINESS CHURCH; AND TO PROVIDE FOR RELATED OR INCIDENTAL
MATTERS.
Short title.

---

### 1.

This Act may be cited as the St. Kitts District of the Wesleyan Holiness
Church Act.
Interpretation.

### 2.

For the purposes of this Act—

“Board” means the District Board of Administration;
“Church” means the District Church of the Wesleyan Holiness Church established
under this Act;
“Conference” means the District Conference referred to in section 5;
“Discipline” means the Discipline of the Wesleyan Holiness Church (current edition);
“former Church” means the Church known as the Pilgrim Holiness Church
incorporated under the Pilgrim Holiness Church Incorporation Act (Cap. 319)
whose functions are to be performed by the District Church established under
this Act;
“General Church Conference” means the General Conference of the Wesleyan
Holiness Church of the Caribbean with headquarters in St. Michael, Barbados.
Establishment of the Wesleyan Holiness Church.

### 3.

(1) For the purposes of this Act, there is hereby established a body to be
known as the St. Kitts District of the Wesleyan Holiness Church hereinafter referred
to as “the Church”.
(2) The Church shall be a body corporate and shall consist of the officers and
members of the former church and such other persons as may hereafter become
officers and members of the Church in the manner specified in Chapter II of Part II of
the Discipline.
(3) This Act shall operate to vest in the Church—
(a) the power to sue in its corporate name;
(b) the power to enter into contracts in its corporate name, and to do so
that, in relation to third parties, the Church shall be deemed to have the
same power to make contracts as an individual has;
(c) the right to have a common seal and to alter or change that seal at
pleasure;
(d) the right to acquire and hold any real or personal property for purposes
for which the Church is constituted and to dispose of or charge such
property at pleasure as specified in the Discipline;
(Amended by Act 8 of 2010)

(e) the right to regulate its own procedure and business as specified in the
Discipline; and
(f) the right to employ such staff as may be necessary for the performance
of its functions as specified in the Discipline.
(4) This Act shall operate—
(a) to make the Church liable to be sued in its corporate name;
(b) to require that judicial notice shall be taken of the common seal of that
body, and that every document purporting to be a document sealed by
that body and to be attested to in accordance with any enactment
applicable to the attestation of documents so sealed shall, unless the
contrary is proved, be received in evidence and be deemed to be such
a document without further proof;
(c) to vest in the majority of the members of the Church the power,
subject to any quorum fixed by the Discipline or the by-laws to bind
members thereof.
Functions of the Church.

### 4.

The functions of the Church shall be inter-religious, charitable and educational
and in keeping the purposes of the Wesleyan Holiness Church as specified in the
Discipline.
By-laws.

### 5.

(1) The Conference may, subject to the approval of the General Conference
make by-laws for complying with the functions of this Act.
(2) By-laws made under this Act shall not be inconsistent with the Discipline.
Transfer of property to Church.

### 6.

(1) All property (including rights) vested immediately before the
commencement of this Act in the former Church shall, as from the commencement of
this Act, be vested in the Church.
(2) All liabilities incurred by or on behalf of the former Church in respect of
its functions under the Pilgrim Holiness Church Incorporated Act and any rules or bylaws made thereunder and subsisting immediately before the commencement of this
Act, shall as from that day, have effect as if they had been incurred by the Church.
(3) All books, records, furniture, equipment and other things relating thereto
in the possession of the former Church immediately before the commencement of this
Act shall immediately be delivered to the Church.
(4) Any contract made before the commencement of this Act by or on behalf
of the former Church, shall as from that day, be construed with such modifications
and adaptations as may be necessary to give effect to the provisions of this section.
(5) Subject to this section, the Church shall have the like rights, powers,
remedies (including, in particular, rights and powers as to taking legal proceedings)
for ascertaining, perfecting or enforcing any rights or liabilities vesting in or attaching
to the Church under this section as if they had at all times been rights and liabilities of
the Church.

7

(6) All civil proceedings commenced before the commencement of this Act in
any court in the State of Saint Christopher, Nevis and Anguilla by or against the
former Church in respect of the functions to be transferred to the Church, may be
continued by or against the Church, and any such proceedings may be amended
accordingly.
Transfer of employees of former church.

### 7.

Any minister of religion or other staff who immediately before the
commencement of this Act was employed in the service of the former Church shall,
as from that day, be transferred to the service of the Church.
Savings section.

### 8.

Nothing in this Act is intended to affect or benefit, any particular person,
association or corporate body or the rights of the Crown, all bodies politic and
corporate and all others except as are mentioned in this Act.
