## CHAPTER 24.22

LIGHTHOUSE BAPTIST CHURCH
MINISTRIES INCORPORATION ACT
AN ACT TO PROVIDE FOR THE INCORPORATION OF THE LIGHTHOUSE BAPTIST CHURCH
MINISTRIES AND TO PROVIDE FOR RELATED OR INCIDENTAL MATTERS.
Short title.

### 1.

This Act may be cited as the Lighthouse Baptist Church Ministries
Incorporation Act.
Incorporation.

### 2.

The Lighthouse Baptist Church Ministries, shall be a body corporate, and shall
in its corporate name—
(a) have perpetual succession;
(b) be capable of suing and being sued in a court of law;
(c) have a common seal;
(d) be capable of acquiring property by purchase, exchange, demise or
otherwise in perpetuity or for the life or lives for any term of years or
upon any terms, conditions or limitations.
Constitution of Board of Trustees.

### 3.

The Lighthouse Baptist Church Ministries, shall have a Board of Trustees
which shall be responsible for the management of all the affairs of the Lighthouse
Baptist Church Ministries.
Vesting of Property.

### 4.

(1) All lands or other property, real or personal, that belonged to, or were held
in trust for or on behalf of the Lighthouse Baptist Church or Lighthouse Baptist
Church Ministries, prior to the coming into force of this Act are hereby vested in the
Board of Trustees of the Lighthouse Baptist Church Ministries.
(2) Notwithstanding the provisions of subsection (1), the properties, more
fully described in the Schedule to this Act and held on behalf of the Lighthouse
Baptist Church or the Lighthouse Baptist Church Ministries, immediately prior to the
coming into force of this Act are hereby vested in the Board of Trustees of the
Lighthouse Baptist Church Ministries, referred to in subsection (3).
(3) The Board of Trustees shall have full power to acquire by purchase,
transfer, donation, exchange, devise, bequest, grant, or otherwise any real or personal
property in Saint Christopher and Nevis or any estate or interest therein on behalf of
Lighthouse Baptist Church Ministries.
Power to Dispose of Property.

### 5.

Lighthouse Baptist Church Ministries, may, subject to the regulations made
under this Act, from time to time, by Deed under its seal, sell, mortgage or demise,
grant, convey, exchange or otherwise dispose of any land or other property vested in
the Lighthouse Baptist Church or Lighthouse Baptist Church Ministries, as it sees fit.

Revision Date: 31 Dec 2020

Saving.

### 6.

Nothing in this Act shall affect the rights of Her Majesty, Her Heirs or
successors or any body politic or corporate or any other person except such as are
mentioned in this Act and those claiming by, through, from or under them.
Regulations.

### 7.

Lighthouse Baptist Church Ministries may, with the approval of the Minister
responsible for Ecclesiastical Affairs, make regulations to manage its affairs.
