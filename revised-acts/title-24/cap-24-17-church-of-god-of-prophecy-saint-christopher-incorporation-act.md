## CHAPTER 24.17

CHURCH OF GOD OF PROPHECY,
SAINT CHRISTOPHER INCORPORATION ACT
AN ACT TO PROVIDE FOR THE INCORPORATION OF THE CHURCH OF GOD OF PROPHECY,
SAINT CHRISTOPHER, AND TO PROVIDE FOR RELATED OR INCIDENTAL MATTERS.
Short title.

### 1.

The Act may be cited as the Church of God of Prophecy, Saint Christopher
Incorporation Act.
Incorporation.

### 2.

The Church of God of Prophecy, Saint Christopher, shall be a body corporate,
and shall, in its corporate name—
(a) have perpetual succession;
(b) be capable of suing and being sued in a court of law;
(c) have a common seal;
(d) be capable of acquiring property by purchase, exchange, demise or
otherwise in perpetuity or for the life or lives for any term of years or
upon any terms, conditions or limitations.
Vesting of property.

### 3.

Any property that belonged, or was held in trust or on behalf of the Church of
God of Prophecy, Saint Christopher prior to the coming into force of this Act is
hereby vested in the Church of God of Prophecy, Saint Christopher, incorporated by
this Act.
Notwithstanding the provisions of subsection (1), the properties, more fully described
in Schedule A and B to this Act, and held on behalf of the Church of God of
Prophecy, Saint Christopher immediately prior to the coming into force of this Act
are hereby vested in the Church of God of Prophecy, Saint Christopher, incorporated
by this Act.
Power to dispose of property.

### 4.

The Church of God of Prophecy, Saint Christopher may, subject to the
regulations made under this Act, from time to time, by deed under its seal, sell or
demise, grant, convey, exchange or otherwise dispose of any land or other property
vested in the Church of God of Prophecy, Saint Christopher, as it sees fit.
Saving.

### 5.

Nothing in this Act shall affect the rights of Her Majesty, Her Heirs or
successors or any body politic or corporate or any other person except such as are
mentioned in this Act and those claiming by, through, from or under them.

Revision Date: 31 Dec 2017

Regulations.

### 6.

The Church of God of Prophecy, Saint Christopher may, with the approval of
the Minister responsible for ecclesiastic affairs, make regulations to manage its
affairs.
