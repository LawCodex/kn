## CHAPTER 9.07

CEMETERIES ACT
AN ACT TO MAKE PROVISION FOR THE DECLARATION AND MAINTENANCE OF PUBLIC
CEMETERIES; AND TO MAKE PROVISION FOR RELATED OR INCIDENTAL MATTERS.
Short title.

### 1.

This Act may be cited as the Cemeteries Act.
Interpretation.

### 2.

In this Act “Minister” means the Minister responsible for Health.
(Inserted by Act 6 of 1976)

Rights under Act No. 116 protected.

### 3.

All rights, privileges, powers and control now vested in and exercised by, and
all duties, obligations and liabilities now borne, suffered, performed or carried out by
the authority of the Anglican, Roman Catholic, Wesleyan, or Moravian
Denominations respectively or in or by any person or persons under the provisions of
Act. No. 116 (A.D. 1855) entitled “An Act to provide a Burial Ground for the parish
of St. George, Basseterre,” shall continue and remain undisturbed anything in this Act
to the contrary notwithstanding.
Definition of public cemetery.

### 4.

The cemeteries at Springfield, and at Cayon, St. Kitts, the former cemetery at
Charlestown, Nevis, and the new cemetery at Bath, near Charlestown, Nevis, are
hereby declared to be public cemeteries:
Provided always that it shall be lawful for the Minister hereafter to declare any
cemetery to be a public cemetery, and thereupon the provisions of this Act shall apply
thereto.
Registrar-General of cemeteries.

### 5.

The Chief Medical Officer shall be ex officio Registrar-General of public
cemeteries and the registrars to be appointed as hereinafter mentioned shall, subject
to the provisions of this Act, be guided by all orders and directions from the
Registrar-General relative to their duties as such registrars.
(Amended by Act 6 of 1976)

Registrars of cemeteries, appointment of.

### 6.

The Minister shall from time to time appoint registrars of the respective public
cemeteries now in existence or hereafter to be established in the State.
(Amended by Act 6 of 1976)

Registrars, powers of and appointment of grave diggers and other servants.

### 7.

The registrars so appointed shall be deemed to be in actual possession of the
public cemeteries of the State to which they are respectively appointed and they shall
each employ grave diggers and other servants for caring and carrying on the
necessary work in such cemeteries respectively.
(Amended by Act 6 of 1976)

Apportionment of cemeteries among denominations-reservation of area for burial of
other persons.

### 8.

Every registrar shall, apportion the public cemetery in his or her charge among
the various Christian denominations after reserving a sufficient area for the burial of
such persons who do not belong to any of such denominations and it shall be the duty
of each registrar to see to and compel the strict observance by all persons of all such
apportionments and reservations.
(Amended by Act 6 of 1976)

Plan of cemetery to be kept.

### 9.

Every registrar shall maintain a plan of the cemetery in his or her charge on
which plan shall be marked the site of each grave with a number designating the same
which shall correspond with the number to be entered in the register hereinafter
mentioned.
(Amended by Act 6 of 1976)

Register of burials to be kept.

### 10.

Every registrar shall keep a register of all burials which take place within the
public cemetery in his or her charge and to each entry in such register shall be affixed
a number corresponding with that of the graves on the aforesaid plan in which an
interment takes place.
Form of Register.

### 11.

The register shall be in the form prescribed by the Minister and shall show the
name (if any), age and sex of each person interred and the date of such interment, and
in the case of a stillborn child the name of the father and mother of the same if they
happen to be married, and if they be unmarried then the name of the mother of such
stillborn child.
(Amended by Act 6 of 1976)

Copies of entries in register evidence of interment.

### 12.

A copy of an entry in the register certified to be a true copy by the registrar for
the time being in charge of the same or by the Registrar-General shall be received in
all legal proceedings and in all Courts of Law as evidence of the interment therein
mentioned.
Power of searching such register and extracts therefrom.

### 13.

Every registrar shall keep the register of the public cemetery of which he or
she is in charge in some convenient place so that the same may be searched by any
person desiring to do so at all reasonable hours and he or she shall at all reasonable
times furnish extracts from the register duly certified under his or her hand as correct
on being required to do so by any person and he or she shall be entitled to receive
from such person searching the same or requiring such extract such fees as may be
fixed by the Minister.
(Amended by Act 6 of 1976)

Grant of exclusive right of burial and of placing monuments and gravestones in
cemetery.

### 14.

Every registrar shall set apart a reasonable portion of each denomination’s
allotment and also of the reserved allotment hereinbefore referred to for the purpose
of granting the exclusive right of burials in such public cemetery as may be in his or
her charge and such registrar shall sell either in perpetuity or for a limited time and
subject to such conditions as he or she thinks fit such exclusive right of burial in any

part of such cemetery so set apart and he or she may also sell the right of placing any
monument or gravestone in such cemetery or any tablet or monumental inscription on
the walls of any chapel or other building within any such cemetery.
No burial to take place in ground where exclusive right of burial has been granted
except with consent of owner.

### 15.

No body shall be buried in any place wherein the exclusive right of burial shall
have been granted by the registrar except with the consent of the owner for the time
being or the representative of the owner of such exclusive right of burial.
Power to remove gravestone, monument, tablet, etc., in certain cases.

### 16.

Every registrar may take down and remove any gravestone, monument, tablet
or monumental inscription which shall have been placed without his authority within
the public cemetery under his or her charge.
Scale of charges for graves, grave-digging, etc.

### 17.

The Minister shall fix a scale of charges for the purchase of sites for vaults and
for graves in public cemeteries and of fees for grave-digging and the opening of
vaults and graves and for the use of all matters and things pertaining to the conduct of
funerals (the performance of burial rights excepted) and a table of such scale of
charges and fees shall be published and hung up in a conspicuous part of all public
cemeteries and in every such scale of charges the total amount of payments regularly
charged in respect of a burial shall be the same in whatever part of a cemetery such
burial is to take place and all such charges and fees shall be paid to the registrar in
charge.
(Amended by Act 6 of 1976)

No corpse to be disinterred except by leave.

### 18.

No corpse buried in any public cemetery shall be disinterred except by leave of
the Minister who is hereby authorised to grant such leave subject to such conditions
and stipulations as he or she may in each case prescribe, and subject as aforesaid no
grave shall be re-opened for twenty years after a corpse has been interred therein.
(Amended by Act 6 of 1976)

Fees to be paid into the Treasury.

### 19.

The registrars shall pay all fees and moneys received by them under this Act
into the Public Treasury of the State.
Expenses to be paid from general revenue.

### 20.

The expenses of carrying out the provisions of this Act shall be paid from the
general revenue of the State.
Penalty for destroying or injuring anything in cemetery.

### 21.

(1) Every person who wilfully destroys or injures any building, wall or fence
belonging to any public cemetery or destroys or injures any tree or plant therein or
daubs or disfigures any wall thereof or puts up any bill therein or on any wall thereof
or wilfully destroys, injures or defaces any monument, tablet, inscription or
gravestone or does any other wilful damage therein, shall be liable to a penalty not
exceeding one thousand one hundred and fifty dollars.
(2) A person who, within a cemetery—
(a) in any way offends against decency and decorum;

(b) takes part in a meeting other than a meeting of a religious or
commemorative character; or
(c) disturbs the peace,
commits an offence and is liable on summary conviction to a fine not exceeding one
thousand one hundred and fifty dollars.
(Amended by Acts 7 of 1976,9 of 1986 and 30 of 2009)

Penalty for playing games, discharging firearms, etc., in cemetery.

### 22.

Every person who plays at any game or sport or discharges any firearms, save
at a military funeral, in any public cemetery or who wilfully and unlawfully disturbs
any person assembled in such cemetery for the purpose of burying any body therein
or who commits any nuisance within such cemetery shall be liable to a penalty not
exceeding four hundred and fifty dollars.
(Amended by Acts 7 of 1976,9 of 1986 and 30 of 2009)

Regulations.

### 23.

The Minister shall make regulations for the general government and care of all
public cemeteries which shall be published in the official Gazette of the State and
when so published shall have the force of law and for the breach of any such
regulation the person offending shall be liable to a penalty not exceeding four
hundred and fifty dollars.
(Amended by Acts 6 of 1976, 7 of 1976 and 9 of 1986)

Penalties, how recoverable, Cap. 3.17.

### 24.

All penalties imposed by this Act or by any regulations made thereunder shall
be recoverable summarily under the provisions of the Magistrate’s Code of Procedure
Act, Cap. 3.17, on the complaint of the registrar of the public cemetery wherein or in
respect of which the offence was committed or of the Registrar-General or by any
person authorised in that behalf by writing under the hand of such registrar or
Registrar-General.

## FIRST SCHEDULE

(Section 23)
PUBLIC CEMETERIES REGULATIONS
Citation.
1\.
These Regulations may be cited as the Public Cemeteries Regulations.
Hours open to the Public.
2\.
The cemetery shall be open to the public every day from sunrise to sunset.
Excavation of graves and vaults.
3\.
All graves and vaults shall be excavated by grave diggers employed by the
registrar.
Depth of graves.
4\.
All graves for persons over 5 years of age shall be dug to a depth of 7 feet, and
for all children under 5 years of age to a depth of 5 feet.
Selection of grave spaces.
5\.
The selection of grave spaces will be subject to the approval of the registrar,
though every endeavour will be made to meet the wishes of intending purchasers.
Repair of monuments etc.
6\.
Monuments, railings, headstones and curbings shall be kept in repair by the
owner.
Removal of dilapidated monuments etc.
7\.
The registrar shall have the right to remove any monuments, railings,
headstones, or curbings, which have become dilapidated, or which, in his or her
opinion, are not kept in proper repair, or to require the owner to remove them.
Prohibition of smoking in a cemetery.
8\.
Smoking is strictly prohibited within the cemetery.
Conduct of persons in a cemetery.
9\.
All persons must conduct themselves in a quiet, orderly manner, and must on
no account stand or sit on the gravestones, nor shall any person stand or sit on the
enclosure walls of the cemetery nor enter or leave the same except through the gates.
Animals not allowed within a cemetery.
10\. No cattle, goats, sheep, pigs or feathered stock shall be allowed within the
cemetery.
Taking flowers etc. out of cemetery prohibited.
11\. No flowers, shrubs, or trees of any kind will be allowed to be taken out of the
cemetery without the permission of the registrar, and the registrar shall have the right
to remove any shrub or tree if he or she deems the removal necessary.
Fees Payable.

12\. There shall be payable to the registrar the fees as set out in the Schedule to the
Regulations, and, at the time of application for the grave, the certificate of death shall
be presented to the registrar for registration.
Graves for paupers.
13\. Upon the receipt of a duly signed order from a magistrate or of the certificate
of death from the proper officer of any public institution for the burial of any pauper
or person dying in such public institution, the registrar shall be authorised to order
that a grave be given free of charge for the burial of such person in that portion of the
cemetery set apart for the purpose.
Fees to be paid to Registrar.
14\. All fees payable under the Schedule to these Regulations, shall be paid to the
registrar, who shall receive all applications and grant permission to open any grave.

## SECOND SCHEDULE

(Section 23)
SPRINGFIELD CEMETERY REGULATIONS
Citation.
1\.
These Regulations may be cited as the Springfield Cemetery Regulations.
Care of the Cemetery.
2\.
Subject to the provisions of the Cemeteries Act, Cap. 9.07, the general
government and care of the Cemetery in the Town of Basseterre shall be entrusted to
a Committee appointed for the purpose to consist of a Chairperson and not more than
five other persons; the Chairperson and anyone of whom shall form a quorum.
Care of Graves (not enclosed by a Curb) in Sections Planted in Grass.

3\.
(1) The maintenance of all graves not enclosed by a stone curb in the part or
parts of the Cemetery in Basseterre now or hereafter planted in grass shall be by the

Committee, and such graves shall be marked by a rectangular bed cut out in the grass
and planted in small flowering shrubs and kept in proper condition.
(2) Any person who, in any way, interferes with a grave or obstructs the
Committee commits an offence.
Mounds of Earth, etc., over Graves.
4\.
No mound of earth or masonry or of any other material shall be raised or built
over any grave except by the permission and with the approval of the Committee.
Disposal of Rubbish.
5\.
No rubbish of any nature or kind shall be left or placed or thrown into the
Cemetery.
Care of Graves Enclosed by Curbs.
6\.
All persons having graves enclosed by stone curbs in the Cemetery shall keep
and maintain such graves to the satisfaction of the Committee and shall remove all
faded flowers or other matter therefrom at least once weekly.
Plants on Graves.
7\.
No shrubs or plant whatsoever not approved by the Committee shall be
planted, placed or grown upon any grave.
Wheeled Traffic.
8\.
No wheeled vehicle shall enter or be in the Cemetery in Basseterre except
along or upon the path dividing the Old from the New Cemetery.
Walking on Grass Prohibited.
9\.
No person shall walk on the cultivated grass in the Basseterre Cemetery except
for the purpose of visiting a grave and then only by the shortest way to such grave.
Hours Opened to Public.
10\. The Cemetery shall be open to the public every day from sunrise to sunset.
Excavation of Graves, etc.
11\. All graves and vaults shall be excavated by grave diggers employed for the
purpose.
Depth of Graves.
12\. All graves and vaults for persons over five years shall be dug to a depth of 7
feet, and for all children under five years to a depth of five feet.
Selection of Grave Lots.
13\. The selection of grave spaces will be subject to the approval of the Committee,
though every endeavour will be made to meet the wishes of intending purchasers.
Monuments etc., Subject to Approval.
14\. No monument, headstone, curb or railing of whatsoever description shall be
erected without the approval of the Committee.
Monuments etc., to be kept in Repair.
15\. Monuments, railings, headstones and curbings shall be kept in repair by the
owner.

Right of Committee to Remove Monuments, etc.

16\. (1) The Committee shall have the right to remove any monuments, railings,
headstones, or curbings, which have become dilapidated, or which, in their opinion
are not kept in proper repair, or which are, in their opinion, obscene or improper, or to
require the owner to remove them.
(2) Any owner failing to remove any such railing, etc., on being called upon
so to do commits an offence.
Smoking Prohibited.
17\. No person shall smoke within the Cemetery.
Conduct of Persons.
18\. No person shall conduct himself or herself in a noisy or disorderly manner in
the cemetery, nor sit or stand upon any grave or enclosure or wall thereof.
Climbing over Rails Forbidden.
19\. No person shall enter or leave the Cemetery except through a gate thereof.
Animals and Feathered Stock not Allowed in Cemetery.
20\. No cattle, goats, sheep, pigs or feathered stock shall be allowed within the
Cemetery.
Flowers, Plants, etc., not to be Removed.
21\. No flowers, shrubs, or trees of any kind shall be taken out of the Cemetery
without the permission of the Committee.
Fees Payable.
22\. There shall be payable to the Registrar of the Cemeteries the fees set out in the
Schedule to these Regulations, and, at the time of application for the grave, the
certificate of death shall be presented to the Registrar for registration.
Graves for Paupers.
23\. Upon receipt of a duly signed order from any Magistrate, Coroner or of the
certificate of death from the proper officer of any public institution for the burial of
any pauper or person dying in such public institution, the Registrar shall be
authorised to order that a grave be given free of charge for the burial of such person
in that portion of the cemetery set apart for the purpose.
Fees to be Paid to Registrar.
24\. All fees payable under the Schedule to these Regulations shall be paid to the
Registrar, who shall receive all applications and grant permission to open any grave.

## THIRD SCHEDULE

(Section 4)
CEMETERIES (DECLARATION OF PUBLIC CEMETERY) ORDER
Citation.
1\.
This Order may be cited as the Cemeteries (Declaration of Public Cemetery)
Order.
Interpretation.
2\.
In this Order, “Act” means the Cemeteries Act, Cap. 9.07.
Declaration of public cemetery.
Pursuant to the provisions of section 4 of the Act, the Minister hereby
DECLARES that certain lands situate at Farms Estate, in the parish of St Anne’s and
more particularly described in the Schedule hereto, to be a public cemetery.

## SCHEDULE

All that lot piece or parcel of land situate at Farms Estate in the parish of St Anne, in
the Island of Saint Christopher comprising 14.71 acres of the said land bounded and
measuring as follows, that is to say, On or towards the North West by an Estate Road
of Farms Estate 1377.13 feet; On or towards the East by Farms Estate 410.26 feet and
425.86 feet; On or toward the South East by Estate Road of Farms Estate 952.63 feet;
On or towards the South West by Farms Estate 51.41 feet.
