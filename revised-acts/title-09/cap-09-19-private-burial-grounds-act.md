## CHAPTER 9.19

PRIVATE BURIAL GROUNDS ACT
AN ACT TO PROVIDE FOR PRIVATE BURIAL GROUNDS AND RELATED MATTERS.
Short title.

---

### 1.

This Act may be cited as the Private Burial Grounds Act.

Prohibition of burial in unlicensed ground.

### 2.

(1) No person shall bury the dead in any place other than a public cemetery
not being a licensed burial ground.
(2) Any person who shall bury any body or in any wise act or assist in the
burial of any body in any place not being a public cemetery or a licensed burial
ground, shall, for every such offence, be liable on summary conviction to a penalty
not exceeding four hundred and fifty dollars.
(Amended by Acts 7 of 1976 and 9 of 1986)

Licence of burial grounds.

### 3.

(1) The Minister may, by Order, license as a private burial ground any parcel
of land, not being within the limits of the towns of Basseterre, Sandy Point or
Charlestown, which may be required for that purpose.
(2) The Minister may, by Order published in the Gazette, make, alter or
rescind regulations for the general government of private licensed burial grounds
within the meaning of this Act.
(3) Any person who acts in contravention of any regulation under this section
shall be liable, on summary conviction, to a penalty not exceeding three hundred
dollars.
(Amended by Acts 7 of 1976 and 9 of 1986)

Not to affect the Cemeteries Act.

### 4.

Nothing in this Act shall in any manner affect the provisions of the Cemeteries
Act, Cap. 9.07.
