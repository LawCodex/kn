## CHAPTER 9.27

SPRINGFIELD CEMETERY ACT
AN ACT TO PROVIDE FOR BURIALS AT SPRINGFIELD CEMETERY, AND FOR RELATED
MATTERS.
Short title.

---

### 1.

This Act may be cited as the Springfield Cemetery Act.

Burials in Basseterre to be only in Springfield cemetery.

### 2.

No burial or interment shall take place within the limits of the town of
Basseterre except at the cemetery called Springfield and whosoever shall contrary to
the provisions of this Act bury or cause, permit, or suffer to be buried or aid or assist
in the burying of any corpse or coffin in or under any church or churchyard, chapel,
burying ground, vault, or any other place within the limits of the said town except at
the said cemetery called Springfield, shall on summary conviction for every such
offence be liable to a fine not exceeding one thousand five hundred dollars.
(Amended by Act 7 of 1976 and 9 of 1986)
