## CHAPTER 9.05

BURIALS ACT
AN ACT TO MAKE PROVISION RESPECTING BURIAL OF DEAD PERSONS; AND TO MAKE
PROVISION FOR RELATED OR INCIDENTAL MATTERS.
Short title.

---

### 1.

This Act may be cited as the Burials Act.

Before interment a certificate of cause of death by medical practitioner to be
produced to District Registrar.

### 2.

(1) No corpse shall hereafter be buried until a certificate be produced to the
District Registrar of births and deaths signed by the medical practitioner of the
deceased person provided such medical practitioner be duly licensed, or in case there
has been no such medical practitioner, by the Medical Officer of the district in which
such person shall have died, or some other duly qualified medical man acting for such
Medical Officer, setting forth the cause of death, and until the Registrar shall have
entered the particulars relating to the death and cause thereof in the register and
issued a permit to bury.
(2) Such permit shall be in the form set out in the Schedule to this Act.
(Amended by Act 6 of 2008)

Who shall apply to medical practitioner for a certificate.

### 3.

It shall be the duty of the head of the family, or the person occupying the
house in which any person shall have died, and if there be no such person, the nearest
local constable, or in a town or village in which there is a police station, the officer in
charge of the police station, to apply to the medical practitioner of the deceased
person, or if there has been no medical practitioner, to the Medical Officer of the
district, for his or her certificate as aforesaid.
(Amended by Act 6 of 2008)

Medical practitioner to grant same.

### 4.

The medical practitioner is hereby required to grant the certificate hereinbefore
mentioned, and if there has been no such medical practitioner, the Medical Officer of
the district, upon being applied to as aforesaid, shall, with all convenient speed,
ascertain the cause of death, and grant the certificate.
(Amended by Act 6 of 2008)

Permit to bury.

### 5.

The person to whom the certificate is granted shall forthwith produce the same
to the Registrar, who shall retain it, note the contents in his or her registry, and issue
to the person presenting the certificate a permit to bury which shall be the authority to
the minister and other persons burying the corpse.
Penalty for burying corpse without permit.

### 6.

Any minister or other person who buries or causes to be buried, or assists in
burying any corpse without having first received such permit as aforesaid, shall be
liable, on summary conviction, to a fine not exceeding three hundred dollars.
(Amended by Acts 7 of 1976 and 9 of 1986)

Registrar to transmit certificate to Registrar General.

### 7.

Every Registrar shall, on the first day of each month, transmit to the Registrar
General all such certificates received by him or her during the preceding month.
Penalty for offending under this Act.

### 8.

Any person neglecting to comply with any of the requirements of this Act shall
be liable, on summary conviction, to a fine not exceeding one hundred and fifty
dollars.
(Amended by Acts 7 of 1976 and 9 of 1986)

Nothing herein contained to affect any other laws.

### 9.

Nothing contained in this Act shall interfere with or lessen the duties required
to be performed by any person under the authority of any other Act.
