SUCCESSION TO THE CROWN ACT

### 1.

This Act may be cited as the Succession to the Crown Act.

Succession to the Crown of the United Kingdom.

### 2.

The Succession to the Crown Act, 2013, of the United Kingdom or any Act
amending or replacing that Act shall apply in Saint Christopher and Nevis.
