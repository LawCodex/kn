## CHAPTER 25.03

CARIBBEAN COMMUNITY AND THE
COMMON MARKET AGREEMENT ACT
AN ACT TO MAKE PROVISION FOR MATTERS ARISING OUT OF THE ESTABLISHMENT OF
THE CARIBBEAN COMMUNITY AND THE EASTERN CARIBBEAN COMMON MARKET; AND
TO PROVIDE FOR RELATED OR INCIDENTAL MATTERS.
Short title.

### 1.

This Act may be cited as the Caribbean Community and the Common Market
Agreement Act.
Interpretation.

### 2.

In this Act, In this Act, unless the context otherwise requires—

“Community” means the Caribbean Community;
“Community origin” means, in relation to goods, that they were grown, produced or
manufactured in and consigned from a place in the Community;
“Community rate of duty” means a rate of customs duty applicable to goods on the
basis of their having been produced or manufactured in, and consigned from,
any of the Community Territories, and includes an exemption from customs
duties so applicable, and the words “CARICOM rate of duty” have a
corresponding meaning;
“Community Territories” means the Territories set out in the First Schedule to this
Act, and the words “Member Territories” have a corresponding meaning;
“The CARICOM Treaty” means the Treaty, known as the Treaty of Chaguaramas
establishing the Caribbean Community signed at Trinidad on 4th July, 1973
and any future supplementary agreements signed by or on behalf of the
Government of this State, and thereafter ratified by the National Assembly;
“The Common Market Agreement” means the Eastern Caribbean Common Market
Agreement signed on the 12th June, 1968, at Saint George’s, Grenada, and
includes any future supplementary agreements amending the same, signed by
or on behalf of the Government of this State, and thereafter ratified by the
National Assembly;
“Common Market origin” means in relation to goods, that they were grown, produced
or manufactured in, and consigned from a place in the Common Market;
“Common Market Territory” means any Territory set out in the Second Schedule to
this Act, and the words “Customs Union Territory” have a corresponding
meaning;
“Comptroller” means the person for the time being, holding the Post of Comptroller
of Customs of this State and includes any person acting for or on his or her
behalf;
“Drawback” includes any prescribed remission or repayment of, or exemption from,
duty chargeable on importation into any Community Territory;
“Less-Developed Country” means any of the Territories set out in the Second
Schedule to this Act together with Belize, and the abbreviation “LDCs” has a
corresponding meaning;

and is liable, on summary conviction, to a fine not exceeding five hundred dollars or
to a term of imprisonment not exceeding two years or both.

## THIRD SCHEDULE

(Sections 3 and 6)
COMMUNITY RULES OF ORIGIN
Citation.

1\.

These Rules may be cited as the Community Rules of Origin.

Purpose of these Rules.

2\.
The purpose of these Rules is to govern the determination of the origin and the
place of consignment of goods in the Community and in Common Market Territories.
Interpretation.

3\.

(1) In these Rules and the Appendices hereto—

“the Act” means the Customs Tariff Act No. 12 of 1984;
“Chapter” and “Tariff heading” means the Chapter and headings of the Customs
Tariff appearing in the First Schedule to the Act;
“produced from regional materials of” means the materials falling within the tariff
headings or Chapters named may be used only if they qualify to be treated as
of Community origin within the meaning of Rule 5 hereof, but does not
preclude the use of Regional materials in an earlier stage of production;
“produced from materials of” and “produced from” means the materials named or
designated, as the case may be, must be used in the condition in which they are
described, but does not preclude the use of the materials in an earlier stage of
production;
“produced from materials not included in” means the materials which fall in the tariff
headings named may not be used if they are imported from outside the
Community or are of undetermined origin;
“extra-regional materials” means materials imported from outside the Community or
of undetermined origin;
“chemical transformation” means the forming of the molecule of the finished product
by—
(i) the combination of two or more elements; or
(ii) any modification of the structure of the molecule of a compound
with the exception of ionisation and the addition or removal of
water of crystallisation.
(2) In these Rules any reference to the production of goods includes a
reference to the getting of minerals and the taking of animals (including fish); and
any reference to materials, except in sub-paragraph (g) of paragraph (6) of Rule 5
hereof, includes a reference to any raw materials, intermediate products, parts or
components used in the process of production, repair, renovation or improvement of
the goods.
Application.

4\.
(1) These Rules shall apply for determining the eligibility of goods imported
into the State for a Community rate of duty (whether chargeable on the goods as such

Repair, Renovation or Improvement of Goods.

6\.
Goods which undergo a process of repair, renovation or improvement within
the Community, having been consigned for that purpose from the State to a consignee
in another Community Territory shall, on their return to this State be treated for the
purpose of reimportation only in like manner as goods which are of Community
origin, provided that the goods are reconsigned directly to this State and the value of
materials imported from outside the Community or of undetermined origin which
have been used in the process of repair, renovation or improvement does not
exceed—
(a) in the case where the goods have undergone the process of repair,
renovation or improvement in a More Developed Country, 65 per cent
of the cost of repair, renovation or improvement;
(b) in the case where the goods have undergone the process of repair,
renovation or improvement in a Less Developed Country, 80 per cent
of the cost of repair, renovation or improvement.
Limitation of extra Regional Materials.

7\.
Where the manufacturer of goods for which the qualifying condition for
eligibility for a Community rate of duty is “wholly produced” or “produced from
regional materials” is unable by reason of circumstances beyond his or her control to
obtain supplies of the regional materials required to be used he or she shall so inform
the Minister responsible for the subject of Finance who may limit the use of extraregional materials subject to such conditions as he or she may impose.
Materials: Determination of Origin.

8\.
(1) Energy, fuel, plant, machinery and tools used in the production, repair,
renovation or improvement of goods within the Community and materials used in the
maintenance of such plant, machinery and tools, shall be regarded as wholly
produced within the Community when determining the origin of these goods.
(2) Where materials containing any element imported from outside the
Community meet the conditions specified in paragraph (1) of Rule 5, those materials
shall be regarded as containing no such element.
(3) In any case in which there is insufficient information on which to
determine whether or not any materials were imported into the Community those
materials shall be deemed to have been so imported.
Materials: Determination of Value.

9\.
The value of any materials imported into the Community or of undetermined
origin shall be the customs value determined in accordance with Section 15 of the Act
less the amount of any transport costs incurred in transit through the Member States.
Export price: Determination of Value.

10\. The export price of goods produced or manufactured in and exported from a
Member State shall be the value accepted for the purpose by the Customs Authority
in the exporting Member State, but shall not include any transport and insurance costs
incurred after the exportation of the goods.

Treatment of Packing.

11\. Except in those cases provided under the Act or under Regulations made

Natural produce of the Sea.

### 13.

(1) In determining for the purposes of these Rules where natural produce of
the sea, or goods produced or manufactured therefrom at sea, are to be treated as
produced or manufactured, anything done by or on board of a ship belonging to a
Community Territory shall be treated as done in that Community Territory and any
such produce of the sea or goods produced or manufactured therefrom at sea, if
brought direct to the State are to be deemed to be consigned from that Community
Territory.
(2) For the purpose of this Rule and paragraph (6)(f) of Rule 5, a registered
ship shall be deemed to belong to the Community Territory in which it is registered.
Determination of Questions.

### 14.

Where any question arises whether goods are to be treated as mentioned in
Rule 4 hereof, the Comptroller may require the importer of the goods to furnish him
or her, in such form as he or she may require proof of any statement made to him or
her as to any fact necessary to determine that question, and if such proof is not
furnished to his or her satisfaction, the question may be determined without regard to
that statement.
