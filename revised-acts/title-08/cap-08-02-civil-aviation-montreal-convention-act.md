## CHAPTER 8.02

CIVIL AVIATION (MONTREAL CONVENTION) ACT
AN ACT TO GIVE EFFECT TO THE CONVENTION FOR SUPPRESSION OF UNLAWFUL ACTS
AGAINST THE SAFETY OF CIVIL AVIATION SIGNED AT MONTREAL ON 23RD SEPTEMBER,
1971 AND FOR CONNECTED PURPOSES.
Short title.

---

### 1.

This Act may be cited as the Civil Aviation (Montreal Convention) Act.

Interpretation.

### 2.

In this Act, unless the context otherwise requires—

“act of violence” means any act committed in the State or outside that constitutes the
offence of murder, manslaughter, assault, theft or any similar offence;
“air navigation facility” means any building, works, apparatus or equipment—
(a) used wholly or mainly for the purpose of assisting air traffic control;
or
(b) used as an aid to air navigation, together with any land contiguous or
adjacent to any such building, works, apparatus or equipment used
wholly or mainly in connection therewith;
“in flight” includes—
(a) any period from the moment when all external doors of the aircraft are
closed following embarkation for flight until the moment when any
such door is opened for disembarkation after flight; and
(b) any period during which the aircraft makes a forced landing and
thereafter until the time when the competent authorities of the State in
which that landing takes place take over responsibility for the aircraft
and the persons and property on board that aircraft.
“in service” includes any period of time comprising the pre-flight preparation of the
aircraft by ground personnel or any period during which the aircraft’s crew
begins for a specific flight until 24 hours of any landing or any period during
which the aircraft is in flight.
Destroying, damaging or endangering safety of aircraft.

### 3.

(1) Any person who—
(a) commits on board an aircraft in flight any act of violence that is likely
to endanger the safety of the aircraft;
(b) destroys an aircraft in service;
(c) causes damage to an aircraft in service that—
(i) renders it incapable of flight, or
(ii) is likely to endanger its safety in flight;
(d) places or causes to be placed on an aircraft in service anything likely
to—
(i) destroy the aircraft;

(ii) cause damage to the aircraft rendering it incapable of flight, or
(iii) endanger the safety of the aircraft in flight;
(e) endangers the safety of an aircraft in flight by communicating to any
other person any information that the informant knows to be false,
commits an offence and shall be liable to be convicted under the provisions of this
Act.
(2) Any person who attempts or aids, abets, counsels or procures the
commission of anything in the State or elsewhere as specified in subsection (1),
commits an offence and shall be liable to be convicted under the provisions of this
Act.
(3) Subsections (1) and (2) shall not apply to an aircraft which is used in the
military, customs or police service of any other State.
Destroying, damaging or interfering with air navigation facility.

### 4.

(1) Subject to subsection (3), a person who—
(a) in a manner likely to endanger the safety of an aircraft in flight,
destroys, damages or interferes with the operation of any air
navigation facility;
(b) attempts, aids, abets, counsels or procures the commission of any of
the offences referred to in paragraph (a); or
(c) in Saint Christopher and Nevis, induces or assists in the commission
elsewhere of an act which, but for subsection (3), would be an offence
under this Act commits an offence under this Act.
(2) Subsection (1) applies where an offence under that subsection—
(a) has been committed in Saint Christopher and Nevis;
(b) has been committed outside Saint Christopher and Nevis and
endangers or is likely to endanger the safety of a Saint Christopher and
Nevis controlled aircraft; or
(c) has been committed outside Saint Christopher and Nevis and the
person who committed the offence—
(i) is a citizen of Saint Christopher and Nevis, or
(ii) is a person other than a citizen of Saint Christopher and Nevis
who is in Saint Christopher and Nevis

(3) Paragraphs (b) and (c) of subsection (2) do not apply where the air
navigation facility is used in the military, customs or police service of a State other
than Saint Christopher and Nevis, except where the aircraft likely to be endangered is
a Saint Christopher and Nevis controlled aircraft.
(Substituted by Act 31 of 2008)

Extradition.

### 5.

Any offence committed under this Act shall be deemed to be an extraditable
offence.

Penalty.

### 6.

Any person who is found guilty of an offence under this Act is liable, on
conviction, to a period of imprisonment for life or to a fine of fifty thousand dollars
or both such fine and imprisonment.
Prosecution of offences.

### 7.

No proceedings for an offence under this Act shall be instituted except by or
with the consent of the Director of Public Prosecutions.
