## CHAPTER 8.06

ROBERT LLEWELLYN BRADSHAW
INTERNATIONALAIRPORT ACT
AN ACT TO MAKE PROVISION FOR THE NAMING OF THE INTERNATIONAL AIRPORT AS
THE ROBERT LLEWELLYN BRADSHAW INTERNATIONAL AIRPORT.
Short title.

---

### 1.

This Act may be cited as The Robert Llewellyn Bradshaw International
Airport Act.
Substitution of name.

### 2.

As from the 16th day of September, 1995 the International Airport in Saint
Christopher shall be known as “The Robert Llewellyn Bradshaw International
Airport”, in substitution for the former name “Golden Rock International Airport”,
and shall be substituted in all Acts and in any regulation, contract, deed, agreement,
order or other instrument in force in Saint Christopher and Nevis.
