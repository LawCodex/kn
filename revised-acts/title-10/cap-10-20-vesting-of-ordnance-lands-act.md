## CHAPTER 10.20

VESTING OF ORDNANCE LANDS ACT
AN ACT TO MAKE PROVISION FOR THE VESTING OF CERTAIN LANDS IN THE GOVERNORGENERAL.
Preamble.

Whereas by an Act of the officer administering the Government of the Islands
of St. Christopher and Anguilla and the Council and Assembly of the same dated the
1st day of October, 1855, certain lands, messuages, tenements, estates and
hereditaments therein mentioned became absolutely vested in the officer
administering the said Government for the time being, the President of the Legislative
Council of the said Islands for the time being and the Speaker of the House of
Assembly of the same for the time being and their successors in office respectively to
and for such uses and upon such trusts and to and for such ends, intents and purposes
for the public service of the said Islands as should be from time to time declared,
ordered or directed by Act of the Legislature of the said Islands subject to certain
leases or agreements for lease in the said Act mentioned and to the proviso in the last
section of the said Act contained.
And whereas by section 18 of the Act of the Leeward Islands No. 2 of 1882
entitled “An Act for the union of the Presidencies of St. Christopher and Nevis” the
powers, authorities and rights of the aforesaid Legislature in respect of the premises
were now vested in the Legislative Council.
And whereas it is expedient for the more convenient future dealing with the
aforesaid Ordnance lands, buildings and hereditaments for the public service of the
Islands of Saint Christopher and Nevis and for removing doubts as to the title to the
said lands, buildings and hereditaments that the same should be declared to be vested
as is hereinafter ordained and to declare certain trusts in respect thereof.
Short title.

---

### 1.

This Act may be cited as the Vesting of Ordinance Lands Act.

Ordnance lands in Saint Christopher vested in the Governor-General.

### 2.

(1) All lands, messuages, tenements, estate and other hereditaments situated in
Saint Christopher vested by the above recited Act dated 1st October, 1855, in the
trustees mentioned in that Act shall by virtue of this Act be absolutely vested in the
Governor-General for the time being and his or her successors in office to and for
such uses and upon such trusts for the public service of the Islands of St. Christopher

(2) Nothing contained in subsection (1) shall be construed to affect the rights

heretofore in force relating to the premises.
The lands so vested to be held in trust for public purposes.

### 3.

The lands, messuages, tenements, estates and other hereditaments hereby
vested in the Governor-General and his or her successors shall be held by him or her
and his or her successors in trust to be used for such public purposes or to be leased
for such term or terms of years not exceeding twenty-one years for such rents and on
such conditions as the Cabinet may direct.
