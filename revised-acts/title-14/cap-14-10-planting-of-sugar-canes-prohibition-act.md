## CHAPTER 14.10

PLANTING OF SUGAR CANES (PROHIBITION) ACT
AN ACT TO PROHIBIT THE PLANTING OF SUGAR CANES IN OR WITHIN A DISTANCE OF ONE
HUNDRED FEET OF ANY TOWN OR VILLAGE.
Short title.

---

### 1.

This Act may be cited as the Planting of Sugar Canes (Prohibition) Act.

Sugar canes planting of in or within one hundred feet of towns and villages.

### 2.

Any person who, without the permission of the Minister responsible for
Agriculture, plants or causes to be planted sugar canes in or within one hundred feet
of any town or village in the State commits an offence and shall be liable, on
summary conviction, to a fine not exceeding one thousand five hundred dollars or to
imprisonment for a term not exceeding six months.
(Amended by Acts 7 of 1976 and 9 of 1986)
