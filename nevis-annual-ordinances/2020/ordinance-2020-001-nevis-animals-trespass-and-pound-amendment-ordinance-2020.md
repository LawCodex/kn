BE IT ENACTED by the Queen's Most Excellent Majesty, by and with the advice and
consent of the Nevis Island Assembly and by the authority of the same, as follows:–
1\.

Short Title.
(a) This Ordinance may be cited as the Nevis Animals (Trespass and Pound)
(Amendment) Ordinance, 2020.
(b) In this Ordinance, ‘the principal Ordinance’ means the Nevis Animals (Trespass
and Pound) Ordinance, Chapter 3.02 (N).

2\.

Interpretation.
Section 2(1) of the principal Ordinance is amended in the definition of(a) “animal”, by adding the words “dog and monkey” after the word “sheep”;
(b) inserting in appropriate alphabetical sequence, the following new definitions“authorised person” means any person appointed by the Minister to shoot
stray or diseased animals and includes a police officer and an animal
warden;
“feathered stock” means any fowl, turkey, peafowl, guinea-bird, duck or
goose;
“particular property” means an animal or feathered stock owned by a person;

3\.

Amendment to Section 3.

Section 3 of the principal Ordinance is amended by inserting after subsection 3(3) the
new subsections as follows:

“3(4) Where any animal cannot be impounded under subsection (1), it shall be
lawful for any authorised person to shoot the said animal.
3(5) Where any animal has been shot under subsection (4), the authorised

3(7) No authorised person shall be accountable to the owner of any animal shot
under the provisions of this section and shall not be liable to be sued by
such owner in respect of such shooting.
3(8) All authorised persons shall be licensed to shoot under the provisions of
the Firearms Act, Cap. 19.05.”

### 4.

Insertion of new Section 4A.

The principal Ordinance is amended by inserting after Section 4 a new Section 4A as
follows:
“4A Shooting of any animal
(1) It shall be lawful for any proprietor or any person authorised by any
proprietor to shoot any animal or feathered stock found trespassing
upon his property and doing actual damage thereon.
(2) Where any animal or feathered stock is shot, if it is identified as particular
property, notice of such shooting shall be given by the proprietor or
any person authorised by the proprietor, to the owner of the animal or
feathered stock within six hours of such identification.
(3) If the proprietor, possessor, agent or other person in charge fails to give
such notice he or she shall be liable to pay a fee of seventy-five dollars
to the Department of Agriculture.
(4) Where the owner of such animal or feathered stock does not remove the
same within six hours after the receipt of the notice or if the animal or
feathered stock cannot be identified, it shall be considered as abandoned
and be deemed the property of the party aggrieved.
(5) No person shooting any animal or feathered stock or authorising the
shooting of any animal or feathered stock in accordance with this section

Nevis Animals (Trespass and Pound) (Amendment) Ordinance, 2020 – 1.
shall be accountable to the owner of any animal or feathered stock shot
nor be liable to be sued by such owner in respect of such shooting.
(6) Any person taking part in the shooting of any animal or feathered stock
shall be licensed to shoot under the provisions of the Firearms Act,
Cap. 19.05.”

### 5.

Amendment to Section 13.
Section 13 of the principal Ordinance is amended:
(a) in subsection 13(1), by replacing the word “Order” with the word “instrument”;
(b) in subsection 13(1), by deleting the words “published in a newspaper of
general circulation in Nevis”;
(c) by inserting after subsection 13(2) a new section 13A as follows:
“13A. Powers of the Director of Agriculture
(1) The Director of Agriculture may:
(a) seize any animal or feathered stock and detain it in order to
ascertain whether an offence under this Ordinance is being
or has been committed and may enter any premises for the
purposes of such seizure and detention;
(b) enter any premises for the purpose of preventing or ending
an attack of any animal on any person or any property.
(2) The Director of Agriculture, in exercising a power of entry into
any premises under this Ordinance, may bring with him into such
premises such other persons as he believes to be necessary for
the purpose of assisting him in the exercise of his powers and
functions under this Ordinance and the Director of Agriculture
and any such other person may take with them into the premises
such equipment as they consider to be necessary.
(3) Any person who obstructs or impedes the Director of Agriculture
in the exercise of his functions under this Ordinance shall be
liable on summary conviction to a fine not exceeding $5,000.00,
or to imprisonment for a term not exceeding three months, or, at
the discretion of the court, to both such fine and such
imprisonment.”
