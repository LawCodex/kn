ISLAND OF NEVIS
No. 4 of 2018
A BILL to amend the Nevis Investment Promotion Agency Ordinance, Chapter 6. 12 (N)
and for matters related thereto or connected therewith.
[Published 27th December 2018, Official Gazette No. 65 of 2018.]
BE IT ENACTED by the Queen's Most Excellent Majesty, by and with the advice and
consent of the Nevis Island Assembly and by the authority of the same, as follows:–

### 1.

Short Title.

This Ordinance may be cited as the Nevis Investment Promotion Agency (Amendment)
Ordinance, 2018.

### 2.

Substitution of the term “Chief Executive Officer”.

The Principal Ordinance is amended by deleting the term “Executive Director” wherever
it appears and substituting the term “Chief Executive Officer”.

### 3.

Interpretation.
The Interpretation Section of this Ordinance is amended by
a)

inserting, in the appropriate alphabetical sequence, the following new
definitions“”Ordinance” means the Nevis Investment Promotion Agency Ordinance,

## Chapter 6. 12 (N)”

### 4.

Amendment to Section 3.
Section 3 of the Principal Ordinance is amended by
1\.

adding a new subsection (3) as follows:
(3) The Agency shall consist of three (3) functioning arms which shall respectively
be responsible for:
(a) promoting Nevis’ Financial Services Sector;

(b) promoting Nevis as an attractive destination for investment; and
(c) facilitating investment development applications.

### 5.

Amendment to Section 4.
Section 4 of the Principal Ordinance is amended by
Inserting in section 4 paragraph (a) following the word, “investment” the words,
“and financial services”.

### 6.

Amendment to Section 6.
Section 6 of the Ordinance is amended by
1\.

deleting from subsection (2) (a) the words “a member of Cabinet nominated by
the Minister of Finance.”

2\.

adding to subsection (2) (a) the words “the Permanent Secretary in the Ministry
of Finance or a Financial Advisor in the Ministry of Finance.”

3\.

deleting sub-section (2) (b) and replacing it with:
“one representative with knowledge and expertise in Finance, Economics or a
related field who shall be nominated by the Minister of Finance or any
other person selected by the Minister of Finance.”

4\.

deleting subsection (2) (c) and replacing it with:
“one representative with knowledge and expertise in Planning and Development
who shall be nominated by the Minister of Physical Planning.”

5\.

deleting the current subsection (2) (d) and replacing it with
“(d) two representatives of the private sector, one person with business
development expertise to be nominated by the Chamber of Industry and
Commerce and one person with expertise in financial services who shall be
nominated by the Minister of Finance”.

6\.

inserting a new paragraph (e) as follows and renumbering the remaining
paragraphs accordingly:
(e) one member of the Bar Association of Saint Christopher and Nevis; and

### 7.

Amendment to Section 8.
Section 8 of the Ordinance is amended by
1\.

deleting paragraphs (a), (b), (c), (d), (e), (f) and (g) from subsection (2) and
renumbering the remaining paragraphs (a) to (d) accordingly.

2\.

adding a new subsection (3) as follows:
(3) (1) The Board may appoint committees or sub-committees to examine
and report to it on any matter whatsoever arising out of or connected
with any of its functions under this Ordinance.

(2) Any such committee shall consist of at least one member of the Board
together with such other persons whether members or not, whose
assistance or service the Board may desire.
(3) The Board may by resolution reject the report of any such committee
or adopt it either wholly or with such modifications, additions or
adaptations as the Board may think fit.

### 8.

Amendment to Section 15.
Section 15 of the Ordinance is amended by
1\.

deleting from sub-section (1) the words “the Minister of Finance upon the
recommendation of”.

2\.

deleting from subsection (2) the words “and such other duties as the Board
may determine”.

3\.

adding following the word Ordinance in subsection (2) the word “including”

4\.

adding the following new paragraphs to subsection (2):
(a) determining goals and objectives for the Agency;
(b) establishing and updating the constitution and by-laws for the Agency;
(c) defining roles and responsibilities of board members, committees and staff;
(d) defining organisational structure and reporting relationships;
(e) recommending policies relating to finances, personnel, programs, reporting
protocols and public relations;
(f) preparing and updating the policy manual;
(g) establishing and approving the budget and committing to securing the
required revenues; and
(h) performing such other duties as the Board may determine.

### 9.

Amendment to Section 17.
Section 17 of the Ordinance is amended by
1\.

deleting the word “and” from subsection (1) paragraph (e)

2\.

deleting paragraph (f) and adding the following new paragraphs:
(f) two (2%) percent levy on the Net Income of Entities operating in the
Financial Services Sector licensed by the Minister of Finance;
(g) ten percent of the Planning Fees collected by the Ministry of
Communications et al. for approved plans for development in the
commercial sector; and
(h) all other money lawfully received by or made available to the Agency.

### 10.

Amendment to Section 19.
Section 19 of the Principal Ordinance is amended by
1\.

deleting the word “and” from subsection (1) (a).
