ISLAND OF NEVIS
No. 5 of 2018
ABill to appropriate certain sums of money for the use of the Public Service of the
Island of Nevis for the financial year commencing on the 1st day of January, 2019 and ending
on the 31st day of December, 2019.
[Published 21st December 2018, Extra Ordindary Gazette No. 65 of 2018.]
BE IT ENACTED by the Queen's Most Excellent Majesty, by and with the advice and
consent of the Nevis Island Assembly and by the authority of the same, as follows:–

### 1.

Short Title and Commencement.

This Ordinance may be cited as the NEVIS APPROPRIATION (2019)
ORDINANCE, 2018 and shall come into force on the 1st day of January, 2019.

### 2.

Appropriation.

There shall be and there is hereby granted to Her Majesty the Queen for and during the
period of one year commencing from the 1st day of January, 2019, the sum of Two Hundred and
Sixty-Nine Million Nine Hundred and Fifty-Two Thousand Two Hundred Dollars
($269,952,200.00) over and above the sums granted and provided under the Acts, Ordinances
and other laws in force in the Island of Nevis, to be applied and expended in the manner and
for the services set forth in the Schedule hereto.

### 3.

Sums charged on Consolidated Fund.

The said sum of Two Hundred and Sixty-Nine Million Nine Hundred and Fifty-Two
Thousand Two Hundred Dollars ($269,952,200.00) hereby declared to be a charge on and
payable out of the Nevis Island Consolidated Fund.

### 4.

Authority to pay.
The Treasurer for the Island of Nevis is hereby authorised and required from time to time
upon the order or warrant of the Minister of Finance to pay the several sums appropriated by
and in the said Schedule to the several services therein mentioned, as the said order or
warrant shall direct, out of the Nevis Island Consolidated Fund without further order or
formality.
