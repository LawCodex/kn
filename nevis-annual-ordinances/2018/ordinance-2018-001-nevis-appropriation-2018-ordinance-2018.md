ISLAND OF NEVIS
No. 1 of 2018

An Ordinance to appropriate certain sums of money for the use of the Public Service
of the Island of Nevis for the financial year commencing on the
1st day of January, 2018 and ending on the 31st day of December, 2018.
[Published 1st March 2018, Official Gazette No. 14 of 2018.]
BE IT ENACTED by the Queen's Most Excellent Majesty, by and with the advice and
consent of the Nevis Island Assembly and by the authority of the same, as follows:–

### 1.

Short Title and Commencement.

This Ordinance may be cited as the NEVIS APPROPRIATION (2018)
ORDINANCE, 2018.

### 2.

Appropriation.

There shall be and there is hereby granted to Her Majesty the Queen for and during the
period of one year commencing from the 1st day of January, 2018, the sum of Two Hundred and
Twenty-Seven Million Three Hundred and Twenty-Four Thousand Two Hundred and Fifty
Dollars ($227,324,250.00) over and above the sums granted and provided under the Acts,
Ordinances and other laws in force in the Island of Nevis, to be applied and expended in the
manner and for the services set forth in the Schedule hereto.

### 3.

Sums charged on Consolidated Fund.

The said sum of Two Hundred and Twenty-Seven Million Three Hundred and TwentyFour Thousand Two Hundred and Fifty ($227,324,250.00) hereby declared to be a charge on
and payable out of the Nevis Island Consolidated Fund.

### 4.

Authority to pay.
The Treasurer for the Island of Nevis is hereby authorised and required from time to time
upon the order or warrant of the Minister of Finance to pay the several sums appropriated by
and in the said Schedule to the several services therein mentioned, as the said order or
warrant shall direct, out of the Nevis Island Consolidated Fund without further order or
formality.

Nevis Appropriation (2018) Ordinance, 2018 – 1.
