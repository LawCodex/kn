ISLAND OF NEVIS
No. 3 of 2019
An Ordinance to amend the Nevis Taxi and Tour Bus Ordinance, No. 1 of 2012 and for
matters related thereto or connected therewith.
[Published 24th October 2019, Official Gazette No. 53 of 2019]
BE IT ENACTED by the Queen’s Most Excellent Majesty, by and with the advice
and consent of the Nevis Island Assembly and by the authority of the same as follows:

### 1.

Short Title.

This Ordinance may be cited as the Nevis Taxi and Tour Bus (Amendment)
Ordinance, 2019.

### 2.

Interpretation.

In this Ordinance, unless the context otherwise requires, “Ordinance” means the
Nevis Taxi and Tour Bus Ordinance No.1 of 2012.

### 3.

Amendment to Section 2.

Section 2 of the principal Ordinance is amended by repealing the definition of
“Association” and substituting the following definition:
“Association” means any body authorised by the Ministry of Tourism and established
under section 17 of the Ordinance to act as a taxi association;

### 4.

Amendment to Section 4.
Section 4 of the principal Ordinance is repealed and substituted as follows:
“4. Prescribed Areas
(1) The areas prescribed for the operation of taxis and tour buses are set out in
Schedule 1 of this Ordinance.
(2) Any operator operating in the prescribed areas as set out in Schedule 1 must
hold a valid ID and taxi permit issued by the Ministry of Tourism.

(3) Operators licensed and or trained by the Ministry of Tourism in the Federal
Government or the St Kitts Tourism Authority that seek to operate in the
prescribed areas must produce a valid taxi permit ID and shall be granted a
temporary pass from the Ministry of Tourism in Nevis.”

### 5.

Insertion of new subsection 6(3).
Following Subsection 6(2), a new subsection 6(3) is inserted:
“6(3) Any body corporate wishing to operate a Taxi and Tour Bus business must
seek written approval from the Ministry of Tourism.
1\.

The applicant or manager of the company must:
(a) be a certified taxi operator with a minimum of 5 years of
experience; and
(b) be a member of a taxi association established under this
Ordinance.

Amendment to Section 7
Section 7 (3) of the principal Ordinance is amended by
(a) deleting the words “subject to certain terms and conditions as the
Minister thinks fit” and substituting therefore the words, “subject
to the terms and conditions as prescribed by the Regulations”.”

### 6.

Amendment to Section 13.
Section 13 (3) of the principal Ordinance is amended by
(a) deleting the words “complies or”.

### 7.

Amendment to Section 14.
Section 14 (1) of the principal Ordinance is amended by
(a) inserting immediately after subsection (e) the following new subsection:
“(f) fails to return a taxi permit to the Ministry within three days of the permit
having being suspended.”

### 8.

Amendment to Section 17.
Section 17 (1) of the principal Ordinance is amended by
(a) repealing subsection (1) and substituting the following new subsection (1):
“(1) Any association comprising of not less than fifteen operators may be
authorised by the Ministry of Tourism to function as a recognised
association under this Ordinance.”

Amendment to Section 19.
The principal Ordinance is amended in section 19 by

(a) deleting the word “exceed” in subsection (2) and substituting therefore the
word, “exceeds”
(b) inserting immediately after subsection (4) the following new subsection:
“

### 10.

(5) An operator shall not be allowed to use a vehicle which has an “R” or
“P” plate. An “H” plate shall only be allowed where the operator is in
possession of a valid temporary taxi permit.”

Amendment to Section 21.
Section 21 of the principal Ordinance is amended by
(a) inserting immediately after subsection (2) the following new subsection:
“(3) A “T” plate assigned to a vehicle shall not be transferable to another
vehicle without the written permission of the Ministry of Tourism.”

### 11.

Repeal and Substitution of Section 22.
Section 22 of the principal Ordinance is repealed and substituted as follows:
“22. Nevis Taxi Regulatory Committee
(1) There is hereby established a committee to be known as the Nevis Taxi
Regulatory Committee which shall comprise five members appointed by
the Minister as prescribed by the Regulations, of whom one shall be
appointed as Chairman.
(2) Any three members of the Committee including the Chairman appointed
under subsection (1) shall constitute a quorum for meetings of the
Committee.
(3) The Committee shall hear and determine disputes between an operator
and the Ministry.
(4) An operator who is aggrieved by a decision of the Ministry has a right
to appeal to the Committee in writing within 15 days of the date of
service of the notice of the decision on him.
(5) A person who is dissatisfied with the ruling of the Committee under this
section may appeal to a Judge of the High Court and the Judge may
make such order as he thinks fit.”

### 12.

Amendment to Section 23.
Section 23 (1) of the principal Ordinance is amended by
(a) inserting immediately after paragraph (b) the following new paragraph (c):
“(c) make amendments to this Ordinance and to the Regulations.”

Amendments to Section 25.
Section 25 (1) of the principal Ordinance is amended by

(a) inserting immediately after the word “Operators” the words “that are in
operation for a minimum of three years”.

Repeal of Section 30.
Section 30 of the principal Ordinance is hereby repealed.

### 15.

Amendments to Section 31.
Section 31 of the principal Ordinance is amended
(a) in section 31 (5) by inserting immediately after the words “ a police officer,
dispatcher, inspector” the words “or a Ministry of Tourism official”
(b) in section 31 (6) by inserting immediately before the words “a Nevis Air and
Seaport Authority” the words “a Ministry of Tourism official and Nevis
Tourism Authority official”.
