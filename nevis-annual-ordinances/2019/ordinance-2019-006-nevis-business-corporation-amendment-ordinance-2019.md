ISLAND OF NEVIS
No. 6 of 2019
AN ORDINANCE to amend the Nevis Business Corporation Ordinance, No. 1 of 2017 and
for matters related thereto or connected therewith.
[Published 5th December 2019, Official Gazette No. 62 of 2019.]
BE IT ENACTED by the Queen's Most Excellent Majesty, by and with the advice and
consent of the Nevis Island Assembly and by the authority of the same, as follows:–

### 1.

Short Title.
(a) This Ordinance may be cited as the Nevis Business Corporation (Amendment)
Ordinance, 2019.
(b) In this Ordinance, “the principal Ordinance” means the Nevis Business Corporation
Ordinance, No. 1 of 2017.

### 2.

Amendment to Section 136.

The principal Ordinance is amended in section 136 by repealing sub-section (5) and
substituting the following:
“(5) Notwithstanding the provisions of subsection (1), (2), (3) and (4) the tax exemptions
granted to all corporations shall cease to take effect on the earlier of
(a) 30th June, 2021 and,
(b) if applicable, the date the corporation acquires, holds, owns or deals with any
new assets or engages in any new business activity that would give rise to
assessable income in the absence of the exemption.”
