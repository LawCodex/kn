ISLAND OF NEVIS
No. 8 of 2019
An Ordinance to appropriate certain sums of money for the use of the Public Service
of the Island of Nevis for the financial year commencing on the 1st day of January, 2020 and
ending on the 31st day of December, 2020.
[Published 24th December 2019, Official Gazette No. 65 of 2019.]
BE IT ENACTED by the Queen's Most Excellent Majesty, by and with the advice and
consent of the Nevis Island Assembly and by the authority of the same, as follows:–

### 1.

Short Title and Commencement.

This Ordinance may be cited as the NEVIS APPROPRIATION (2020)
ORDINANCE, 2019 and shall come into force on the 1st day of January, 2020.

### 2.

Appropriation.

There shall be and there is hereby granted to Her Majesty the Queen for and during the
period of one year commencing from the 1st day of January, 2020, the sum of Two Hundred and
Thirty-Nine Million, Seven Hundred and Thirteen Thousand ($239,713,000.00) over and
above the sums granted and provided under the Acts, Ordinances and other laws in force in
the Island of Nevis, to be applied and expended in the manner and for the services set forth
in the Schedule hereto.

### 3.

Sums charged on Consolidated Fund.

The said sum of Two Hundred and Thirty-Nine Million, Seven Hundred and Thirteen
Thousand ($239,713,000.00) hereby declared to be a charge on and payable out of the
Nevis Island Consolidated Fund.

### 4.

Authority to pay.
The Treasurer for the Island of Nevis is hereby authorised and required from time to time
upon the order or warrant of the Minister of Finance to pay the several sums appropriated by
and in the said Schedule to the several services therein mentioned, as the said order or
warrant shall direct, out of the Nevis Island Consolidated Fund without further order or
formality.
