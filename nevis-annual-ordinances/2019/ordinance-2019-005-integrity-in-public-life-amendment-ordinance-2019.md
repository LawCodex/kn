ISLAND OF NEVIS
No. 5 of 2019
AN ORDINANCE to amend the Integrity in Public Life Ordinance, No. 2 of 2013 and for
matters related thereto or connected therewith.
[Published 5th December 2019, Official Gazette No. 62 of 2019.]
BE IT ENACTED by the Queen's Most Excellent Majesty, by and with the advice and
consent of the Nevis Island Assembly and by the authority of the same, as follows:–

### 1.

Short Title.
(a) This Ordinance may be cited as the Integrity in Public Life (Amendment)
Ordinance, 2019.
(b) In this Ordinance, “the principal Ordinance” means the Integrity in Public Life
Ordinance, No. 2 of 2013.

### 2.

Amendment to Section 2.
Section 2 of the principal Ordinance is amended
(a) by deleting the definition of “person in public life” and replacing it with the
following:
“person in public life” means a person who holds an office specified in
Schedule I except in the case of Permanent Secretaries and Heads of
Departments, where such persons hold an acting appointment for a period
not less than twelve months.
(b) by inserting, in the appropriate alphabetical sequence, the following new
definition:
“child” means the child of a person who has not attained the age of eighteen
years and is not married or having attained that age, is under the age of
twenty-five and not married and is pursuing a course of higher education
and includes a step-child or an adopted child; and, in respect of a man,
includes a child born out of wedlock of whom the man had been adjudged
the father by a court of competent jurisdiction or whom the man had
acknowledged to be his own child.

### 3.

Amendment to Section 3.

Section 3 of the principal Ordinance is amended by inserting after sub-section 3(3), the
following new sub-section:
3(4) Every member of the Commission and staff of the Commission shall, before
assuming the functions of his or her office or as soon as practicable thereafter, make
and subscribe the Oath of Secrecy in the form prescribed in Schedule IV.

### 4.

Amendment to Section 13.

Section 13 (1) of the principal Ordinance is amended by deleting the words, “who shall
be a public servant”.

### 5.

Amendment to Section 15.

Section 15 of the principal Ordinance is amended by inserting after sub-section (5) the
following new sub-sections:
(6) Where a person who is required to do so fails to file a declaration in accordance
with this section or without reasonable cause, fails to furnish further particulars in
accordance with section 20, the Commission shall publish such fact in the Gazette and at
least one newspaper in general circulation on the island of Nevis.
(7) The Commission may, at any time after the publication referred to in subsection (6), make an ex parte application to the High Court for an order directing such
person to comply with the Ordinance and the Court may in addition to making such an
order, impose such conditions as it thinks fit including costs.
(8) A person who fails to comply with the directions of the Court, commits an
offence and is liable on conviction to a fine of fifty thousand dollars.

### 6.

Amendment to Section 27.
Section 27 of the principal Ordinance is repealed and the following is substituted:
(1) A Commissioner or an employee of the Commission who performs any function
in the service of the Commission shall treat all declarations or information
relating to such declarations as secret and confidential and shall not disclose
or communicate to an unauthorised person or allow any such person to have
access to any such declarations or information.
(2) Any person who contravenes sub-section (1) commits an offence and is liable
on summary conviction to a fine of fifty thousand dollars or to a term of
imprisonment not exceeding five years or to both such fine and imprisonment.
(3) An authorised person who publishes information which comes to his or her
knowledge pursuant to subsection (1) to an unauthorised person commits an
offence and is liable on summary conviction to a fine of fifty thousand dollars
or to a term of imprisonment not exceeding five years or to both such fine and
imprisonment.

(4) For the purposes of this section, an unauthorised person is a person other
than a person authorised to receive information:
(a) under this Ordinance in relation to the financial affairs of a person in
public life; or
(b) by reason of an Order of a Judge or the High Court.
