ISLAND OF NEVIS
No. 2 of 2019
An Ordinance to amend the Nevis Investment Promotion Agency Ordinance,
No. 4 of 2018 and for matters related thereto or connected therewith.
[Published 24th October 2019, Official Gazette No. 53 of 2019]
BE IT ENACTED by the Queen’s Most Excellent Majesty, by and with the advice
and consent of the Nevis Island Assembly and by the authority of the same as follows:

### 1.

Short Title.

This Ordinance may be cited as the Nevis Investment Promotion Agency (Amendment)
Ordinance, 2019.

### 2.

Interpretation.

In this Ordinance, “Principal Ordinance” means the Nevis Investment Promotion
Agency Ordinance, No. 4 of 2018.

### 3.

Amendment to Section 6.
The principal Ordinance is amended in Section 6 as follows:
(a) in subsection (2) (a) by adding the words “or any other person selected by
the Minister of Finance” after the word “Finance” in line two.
(b) in subsection (3) by deleting the words “or any other person selected by the
Minister of Finance” after the word “Finance” in line two.
