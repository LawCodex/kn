No. 4 of 2023
AN ORDINANCE to amend the Integrity in Public Life Ordinance Cap. 1.02 and for matters
related thereto and connected therewith.
[Published 30th November 2023, Official Gazette No. 68 of 2023.]
BE IT ENACTED by the King's Most Excellent Majesty, by and with the
advice and consent of the Nevis Island Assembly and by the authority of the
same, as follows:–

### 1.

Short Title.
This Ordinance may be cited as the Integrity in Public Life (Amendment)
Ordinance, 2023.

### 2.

Interpretation
In this Ordinance, the “Principal Ordinance” means the Integrity in Public Life Ordinance,
Cap. 1.02.

### 3.

Amendment of Section 3
The Principal Ordinance is amended in Section 3 (2) by inserting a new subsection (c)
immediately after 3 (2)(b)(ii):
(c) If there is no Leader of the Opposition, then the Deputy Governor-General may
appoint a member on the advice of the member or members of the Opposition,
provided that where there is no member of the Opposition or the members of the
Opposition cannot agree on a nominee, then a member will be appointed on the
advice of the Premier after consultation with the members of the Opposition, if any.

### 4.

Amendment of Section 5
Section 5 of the Principal Ordinance is amended in subsection (1) by deleting the word
“five” as it appears therein and replacing it with the word “three.”

### 5.

Amendment of Section 7
Section 7 of the Principal Ordinance is amended in subsection (1) paragraph (b) by
deleting the word “five” and replacing it with the word “three.”

### 6.

Amendment of Section 8
Section 8 of the Principal Ordinance is amended as follows:
(a) in subsection (1) by adding the words “if there is an incumbent in the position”
after the word “and” as it appears in line two of the subsection.”
(b) in subsection (4) by adding the words “if there is an incumbent in the position”
after the word “and” as it appears in line three of the subsection.”
