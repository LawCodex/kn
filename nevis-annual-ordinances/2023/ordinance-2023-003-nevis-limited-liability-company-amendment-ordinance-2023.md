ISLAND OF NEVIS
No. 3 of 2023
AN ORDINANCE to amend the Nevis Limited Liability Company Ordinance, Chapter 7.04
and for matters related thereto and connected therewith.
[Published 21st September 2023, Official Gazette No. 54 of 2023.]
BE IT ENACTED by the King's Most Excellent Majesty, by and with the
advice and consent of the Nevis Island Assembly and by the authority of the
same, as follows:–
1\. Short Title.
This Ordinance may be cited as the Nevis Limited Liability Company
(Amendment) Ordinance, 2023.
2\. Interpretation.
In this Ordinance, the “Principal Ordinance” means the Nevis Limited Liability
Company Ordinance, Cap. 7.04.
3\. Amendment of Section 2.
The Principal Ordinance is amended in section 2 by inserting the following
new definition in the correct alphabetical order:
“electronic signature” means an electronic indication attached to a document
which indicates a person’s intent to agree to the content of the document
to which the signature relates and this may include a wet signature;”.

### 4.

Amendment of Section 4.
Section 4 of the Principal Ordinance is amended as follows:
(a) by deleting subsection (8) in its entirety and replacing it with the following:
(8) Where a provision of this Ordinance requires an instrument to be filed
with the Registrar of Companies, such requirement means that:

(a) the instrument shall be filed electronically on the electronic portal
designated by the Registrar of Comp anies for the filing of
instruments;
(b) upon receipt of the instrument filed electronically, the Registrar
of Companies shall certify that the instrument has been filed by
endorsing the word “Filed” upon the instrument and the date of
filing shall be the date of payment of the relevant fee;
(c) t h e e n d o r s e me n t b y t h e R e g i s t r a r o f C o mp a n i e s u nd e r
paragraph (c) constitutes the certificate of the Registrar of
Companies that the document is a true duplicate of the instrument
filed electronically with his office and that it was filed as of the
date stated in the endorsement;
(d) an instrument filed in accordance with paragraph (b) shall be
effective as of the filing date stated thereon; and
(e) upon the filing of any instrument, the Registrar of Companies shall
issue an Endorsement Certificate under an authorized signature
and seal certifying that the instrument is filed,
and may provide copies of such documents to the registered agent for
maintenance at the office of the registered agent.”
(b) by adding a new subsection (12) immediately after subsection (11) as
follows:
“(12) An instrument filed with the Registrar of Companies may contain an
electronic signature and the Registrar of Companies shall accept
such instrument for filing which contains an electronic signature.”

### 5.

Amendment of Section 16.
Section 16 of the Principal Ordinance is amended as follows:
(a) by deleting subsection (4) and replacing it as follows:
“(4) Where a limited liability company which is registered under this
Ordinance fails to maintain a registered agent, then service of
process in a legal action on that limited liability company shall be
at the last known registered office of the limited liability company.
(b) by de leting sub sec tio ns (5), (6 ), (7) and ( 8) and renumberi ng
subsection (9) as the new subsection (5).

### 6.

Insertion of new section 67A.
The Principal Ordinance is amended by inserting the following new section
immediately after section 67:

“67A.

Documents to be kept by a limited liability company.

“(1) A limited liability company shall keep correct and complete copies
of the following documents:
(a) the Articles of Organization;
(b) an operating agreement, if in writing;
(c) the register of members and class of membership;
(d) the register of managers which contains the name and address
of each manager;
(e) minutes of meetings of the members and action taken;
(f) minutes of meeting of committees, if any;
(g) copies of all notices and other documents filed by the limited
liability company”
and may provide copies of such documents to the registered agent
for maintenance at the office of the registered agent.
(2) Where a limited liability company keeps a copy of the register of
members at the office of its registered agent, it shall –
(a) within 15 days of any change in the register, notify the
registered agent in writing of the change, and
(b) provide the registered agent with a written record of the
physical address of the place or places at which the original
register of members is kept.
(3) Where the place at which the original register of members is
changed, the limited liability company shall provide the registered
agent with the physical address of the new location of the records
within 15 days of the change of location.
(4) Any information and records required to be kept and maintained
at the office of the registered agent under this section shall be
maintained for a minimum of six (6) years after the date on which
the limited liability company is dissolved or otherwise ceases to
exist.
(5) a limited liability company that contravenes subsections (1), (2),
(3) and (4) shall be liable to a penalty not exceeding $10,000.00.

### 7.

Amendment of Section 102.
Section 102 of the Principal Ordinance is amended by deleting the section in
its entirety and replacing it with the following new Section 102:

“(1) A limited liability company found in default of one or more of the
provisions of this Ordinance for which no specific penalty is applicable,
shall be liable to a penalty not exceeding $10,000.00.
(2) An individual found in default of one or more of the provisions of this Ordinance
for which no specific penalty is applicable shall be liable to a penalty not
exceeding $10,000.00.”
