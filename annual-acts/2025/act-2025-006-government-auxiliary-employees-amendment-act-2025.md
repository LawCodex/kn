SAINT CHRISTOPHER AND NEVIS
No. 6 of 2025
AN ACT to amend the Government Auxiliary Employees Act, Cap. 22.16.
[Published 2nd May 2025, Extra Ordinary Gazette No. 22 of 2025.]
BE IT ENACTED by the King’s Most Excellent Majesty, by and with the advice and
consent of the National Assembly of Saint Christopher and Nevis, and by the authority of
the same as follows:

### 1.

Short title.
This Act may be cited as the Government Auxiliary Employees (Amendment) Act, 2025.

### 2.

Interpretation.
In this Act
“Act” means the Government Auxiliary Employees Act, Cap. 22.16.

### 3.

Amendment of section 7.
The Act is amended in section 7 by
(a) renumbering it as subsection (1); and
(b) by inserting the following new subsection (2)
“ (2) The provisions of Part III of the Pensions Act, Cap. 22.06 shall apply
mutatis mutandis to the following employees
(a) employees who were employed on or after 18th May 2012 and who
shall contribute to the Pensions Fund in accordance with the
provisions of Part III of the Pensions Act, Cap. 22.06; and
(b) Government Auxiliary Employees under the Government Auxiliary
Employees Act, Cap. 22.16 (formerly known as non-established

Government Auxiliary Employees (Amendment) Act, 2025 – 6.

workers) who, before 18th May, 2012, could not be appointed into the
public service and who shall
(i) elect to contribute to the Pensions Fund in accordance with the
provisions of this Act, including employees who are over
forty-five years; and
(ii) who have not reached the age of fifty-five years.”.
