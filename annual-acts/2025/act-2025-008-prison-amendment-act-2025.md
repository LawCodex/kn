SAINT CHRISTOPHER AND NEVIS
No. 8 of 2025
AN ACT to amend the Prison Act, Cap. 19.08.
[Published 29th May 2025, Official Gazette No. 27 of 2025.]
BE IT ENACTED by the King’s Most Excellent Majesty, by and with the advice and
consent of the National Assembly of Saint Christopher and Nevis, and by the authority of
the same as follows:

### 1.

Short title.
This Act may be cited as the Prison (Amendment) Act, 2025.

### 2.

Interpretation.
In this Act, the expression,
“Act”, means the Prison Act, Cap. 19.08.

### 3.

Amendment of section 23.
The Act is amended in section 23 as follows
(a) by inserting immediately after the expression, “tobacco”, the expression
“cannabis, pharmaceutical drugs or controlled drugs as defined by the Drugs
(Prevention and Abatement of the Misuse and Abuse of Drugs) Act”;
(b) by replacing the expression “six months” with the expression “ten years”; and
(c) by replacing the expression “one thousand” with the expression, “fifty
thousand”.

### 4.

Amendment of section 24.
The Act is amended in section 24 as follows:

Prison (Amendment) Act, 2025 – 8.

(a) by replacing the expression, “letter or any other thing” with the expression,
“sharp object, knife, weapon, letter, mobile phone, sim card, any other component
parts of a mobile phone, or any other chattel”; and
(b) by replacing the expression, “five hundred dollars.” with the expression, “fifty
thousand dollars and to imprisonment for a term not exceeding ten years.”.
