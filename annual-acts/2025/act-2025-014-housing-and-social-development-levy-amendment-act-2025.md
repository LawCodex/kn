SAINT CHRISTOPHER AND NEVIS
No. 14 of 2025
AN ACT to amend the Housing and Social Development Levy Act, Cap. 20.21 and
for related matters
[Published 11th July 2025, Extra-Ordinary Gazette No. 39 of 2025.]
BE IT ENACTED by the King's Most Excellent Majesty, by and with the advice and
consent of the National Assembly of Saint Christopher and Nevis, and by the authority of the
same as follows:

### 1.

Short title.

This Act may be cited as the Housing and Social Development Levy (Amendment) Act,
2025\.

### 2.

Amendment of section 3 of the Act.

The Act is amended in section 3 subsection (2) by replacing paragraphs (a) and (b) as
follows:
“(2) (a) in the case of an employee whose wages are up to $27,040.00 (520.00) per
week), 0% by the employee, and 3% by the employer in respect of the
wages the employer pays to the employee; and
(b) in paragraph (b), by replacing the expression, “23,400.00”, with the
expression, “$27,040.00”.
