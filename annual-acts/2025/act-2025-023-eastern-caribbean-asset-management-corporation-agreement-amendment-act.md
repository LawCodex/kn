SAINT CHRISTOPHER AND NEVIS
No. 23 of 2025
AN ACT to amend the Eastern Caribbean Asset Management Corporation Agreement
Act, Cap. 21.23.
[Published 28th August 2025, Official Gazette No. 47 of 2025.]

BE IT ENACTED by the King’s Most Excellent Majesty, by and with the advice and
consent of the National Assembly of Saint Christopher and Nevis, and by the authority of
the same as follows:

### 1.

Short Title.
This Act may be cited as the Eastern Caribbean Asset Management Corporation
Agreement (Amendment) Act, 2025.

### 2.

Interpretation.
In this Act, the expression, “Act” means the Eastern Caribbean Asset Management
Corporation Agreement Act, Cap. 21.23.

### 3.

Amendment of section 5.
The Act is amended in Section 5 by deleting paragraph (a) and by substituting the
following —
“(a) The Banking Act, Cap. 21.01, except Part X and section 178;”.
