MARCELLA ALTHEA LIBURD

31st July, 2025.

SAINT CHRISTOPHER AND NEVIS
No. 18 of 2025
AN ACT to amend the Gaming (Control) Act, Cap. 17.01.
[Published 7th August 2025, Official Gazette No. 43 of 2025.]

BE IT ENACTED by the King’s Most Excellent Majesty, by and with the advice and
consent of the National Assembly of Saint Christopher and Nevis, and by the authority of
the same as follows:

### 1.

Short title.
This Act may be cited as the Gaming (Control) (Amendment) Act, 2025.

### 2.

Interpretation.
In this Act
“Act” means the Gaming (Control) Act, Cap. 17.01.

### 3.

Amendment of section 18.
The Act is amended in section 18 by inserting a new subsection (4) as follows
“(4) The provisions of this section do not apply to the National Lotteries Authority
or an operator under the National Lotteries Authority Act, 2025.”.
LATOYA JONES
Deputy Speaker
