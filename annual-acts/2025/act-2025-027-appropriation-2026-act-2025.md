SAINT CHRISTOPHER AND NEVIS
No. 27 of 2025
AN ACT to provide for the services of Saint Christopher and Nevis for the financial
year commencing 1st January, 2026 and ending on 31st December, 2026.
[Published 29th December 2025, Extra-Ordinary Gazette No. 69 of 2025.]
BE IT ENACTED by the King's Most Excellent Majesty by and with the advice and
consent of the National Assembly of Saint Christopher and Nevis and by the authority of the
same as follows:–

### 1.

Short title and Commencement.

This Act may be cited as the Appropriation (2026) Act, 2025 and shall come into force on
the first day of January, 2026.

### 2.

Appropriation.

There shall be and there is hereby granted to His Majesty the King for and during the
period of one year commencing from the 1st day of January 2026 and ending on the
31st day of December 2026, the sum of Nine Hundred and Forty-Eight Million, Nine Hundred
and Twenty-Two Thousand and Seventy-Five Dollars ($948,922,075) over and above the
sums granted and provided under the Acts and other laws in force in Saint Christopher and
Nevis, to be applied and expended in the manner and the services set forth in the Schedule
hereto.

### 3.

Sums charged on Consolidated Fund.

The said sum of Nine Hundred and Forty-Eight Million, Nine Hundred and Twenty-Two
Thousand and Seventy-Five Dollars ($948,922,075), shall be and the same is hereby declared
to be charged upon and payable from and out of the Consolidated Fund.

### 4.

Authority to pay.

The Accountant General of Saint Christopher and Nevis is hereby authorised and required
from time to time upon the order or warrant of the Minister of Finance to pay the several
services therein mentioned, as the said order or warrant shall direct, out of the Consolidated
Fund without further order or formality.
