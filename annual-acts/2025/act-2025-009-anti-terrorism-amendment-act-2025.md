the authority of the same as follows:

### 1.

Short Title.
This Act may be cited as the Anti-Terrorism (Amendment) Act, 2025.

### 2.

Interpretation.
In this Act, the expression,
“Act” means the Anti-Terrorism Act, Cap. 4.02.

The Act is amended in section 118 subsection (1), by inserting immediately
after paragraph (d), the following new paragraph (e)
“ (e) should be identified as targets for designation based on any other

counter-terrorism sanctions lists”.
