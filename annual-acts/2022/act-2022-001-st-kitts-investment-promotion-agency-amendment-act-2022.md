No. 1 of 2022
AN ACT to amend the St Kitts Investmentt Promotion Agency Act,Act, Cap 20.26.
[Published 17th Novembe 2022, Official Gazette No. 68 of 2022.]
BE IT ENACTED by the King's Most Excellent Majesty, by and with the advice and
consent of the National Assembly of Saint Christopher and Nevis, and by the authority of
the same as follows:

### 1.

Short title.

This Act may be cited as the St Kitts Investment Promotion Agency (Amendment)
Act, 2022.

### 2.

Interpretation.
In this Act, unless the context otherwise requires,
“Act” means the St Kitts Investment Promotion Agency Act, Cap. 20.26.

### 3.

Amendment of section 2.

The Act is amended in section 2 by replacing the definition of the expression "Minister"
with the following new definition.
“ “Minister” means the Minister responsible for Investment;”.
