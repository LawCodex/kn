SAINT CHRISTOPHER AND NEVIS
No. 6 of 2022
AN ACT to provide for the Incorporation of the Evangelistic Faith Church of the Alley
Sandy Point, St Kitts and to provide for related or incidental matters.
[Published 2nd December 2022, Extra Ordinary Gazette No. 75 of 2022.]
BE IT ENACTED by the King's Most Excellent Majesty, by and with the advice and
consent of the National Assembly of Saint Christopher and Nevis, and by the authority of
the same as follows:

### 1.

Short title.
This Act may be cited as the Evangelistic Faith Church (Incorporation) Act, 2022.

### 2.

Incorporation.

The Evangelistic Faith Church shall be a body corporate, and shall in its corporate
name—
(a) have perpetual succession;
(b) be capable of suing and being sued in a court of law;
(c) have a common seal;
(d) be capable of acquiring property by purchase, exchange, demise or otherwise
in perpetuity or for the life or lives for any term of years or upon any terms,
conditions or limitations.

### 3.

Vesting of Property.

Any property that belonged to or was held in trust for or on behalf of the Evangelistic
Faith Church prior to the coming into force of this Act is hereby vested in the Evangelistic
Faith Church Incorporated, by this Act.

Evangelistic Faith Church (Incorporation) Act, 2022 - 6.

### 4.

Power to dispose of property.

The Evangelistic Faith Church Incorporated may, from time to time, by Certificate of Title
or by Deed, under its seal, sell, mortgage or demise, grant, convey, exchange or otherwise
dispose of any land or other property vested in the Evangelistic Faith Church Incorporated
as it sees fit.

### 5.

Regulations.

The Church Board consisting of the Pastor and the other members of the Church Board
may make Rules and Regulations to manage the church’s affairs.

### 6.

Saving.

Nothing in this Act shall affect the rights of His Majesty, His Heirs or successors or any
body, politic or corporate or any other person except such as are mentioned in this Act and
those claiming by, through, from or under them.
