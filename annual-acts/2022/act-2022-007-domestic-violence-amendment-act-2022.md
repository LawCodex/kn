SAINT CHRISTOPHER AND NEVIS
No. 7 of 2022
AN ACT to amend the Domestic Violence Act, Cap. 12.04.
[Published 2nd December 2022, Extra Ordinary Gazette No. 75 of 2022.]
BE IT ENACTED by the King's Most Excellent Majesty, by and with the advice and
consent of the National Assembly of Saint Christopher and Nevis, and by the authority of
the same as follows:

### 1.

Short title.
This Act may be cited as the Domestic Violence (Amendment)Act, 2022.

### 2.

Interpretation.
In this Act, unless the context otherwise requires,
“Act” means the Domestic Violence Act, Cap. 12.04.

### 3.

Amendment of section 2.
The Act is amended in section 2 titled “Interpretation”
(a) by replacing the definition of “Court” as follows
“

“Court” means
(a) the Eastern Caribbean Supreme Court or any Court with similar
jurisdiction established in succession to that Court; or
(b) the Magistrate’s Court or any Court with similar jurisdiction
established in succession to that Court.

(b) by replacing the definition of “Director” as follows—
“

“Director” means Director of Gender Affairs; “

(c) by replacing the definition of “economic abuse” as follows—
“

“economic abuse” includes—

Domestic Violence (Amendment) Act, 2022 - 7.

(a) the avoidance of financial obligations owed to the applicant and
child or dependent of the respondent, including mortgage or rental
obligations; or
(b) situations where, without the consent of the applicant, the respondent
exercises full access and control over the financial resources of the
applicant in a manner that is not in the best interest of the applicant.”.
