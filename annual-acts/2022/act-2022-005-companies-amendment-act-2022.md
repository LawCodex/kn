SAINT CHRISTOPHER AND NEVIS
No. 5 of 2022
AN ACT to amend the Companies Act, Cap. 21.03.
[Published 2nd December 2022, Extra Ordinary Gazette No. 75 of 2022.]
BE IT ENACTED by the King's Most Excellent Majesty, by and with the advice and
consent of the National Assembly of Saint Christopher and Nevis, and by the authority of
the same as follows:

### 1.

Short title.
This Act may be cited as the Companies (Amendment) Act, 2022.

### 2.

Interpretation.
In this Act, unless the context otherwise requires
“Act” means the Companies Act, Cap. 21.03.

### 3.

Amendment of section 224.
The Act is amended in section 224 titled “Exemption from taxes”, as follows
(a) in subsection 224(11), by replacing the expression “224A” with the expression
“224(7)”;
(b) by inserting after subsection (11) the following new subsections 12, 13, 14, 15
and 16
“(12) For the purposes of this section
(a) the expression “exempt company” means a company which was
exempted from taxes under the repealed subsection 225(1) of this Act;
(b) the expression “struck exempt company” means an exempt company
struck from the Register pursuant to subsection (9) or otherwise
pursuant to the Act; and

(c) the expression “assets of a struck exempt company” means any
outstanding property, real or personal, including things in action,
whether within or outside the Federation—
i.

vested in the struck exempt company;

ii.

to which the struck exempt company was entitled; or

iii. over which the struck exempt company had a disposing power,
at the time it was struck from the Register but which has not been realised
or otherwise disposed of or dealt with by the struck exempt company.
(13) Where there remain assets of a struck exempt company, the Court may, at
any time within four years of the date of removal of the struck exempt
company from the Register, on an application made for the purpose
(a) by a creditor of the struck exempt company; or
(b) by any other person appearing to the Court to be interested,
make orders directing the assets of a struck exempt company be sold, assigned,
transferred, or otherwise disposed of or dealt with, either solely or in concurrence
with any other person, in such manner for such consideration, by public auction,
public tender or private contract upon such terms and conditions as, in whichever
order of priority and in whichever manner, the Court deems just and equitable.
(14) An applicant to the Court pursuant to subsection 224(13) shall—
(a) serve all creditors of the struck exempt company with the application
and any supporting documents; or
(b) if there are no creditors of the struck exempt company, submit a sworn
declaration to the Court under the penalty of perjury that no such
creditors exist.”
(15) The Court shall not make any orders pursuant to subsection 224(13) until
the Court is satisfied that any and all creditors of the company have been
served with the application.
(16) The claimant in any claim, suit, or action pursuant to subsection 224(13) shall
be the applicant and the defendant shall be the struck exempt company.”.
