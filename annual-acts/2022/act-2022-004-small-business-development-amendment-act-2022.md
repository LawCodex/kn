SAINT CHRISTOPHER AND NEVIS
No. 4 of 2022
AN ACT to amend the Small Business Development Act, Cap. 20.61.
[Published 2nd December 2022, Extra Ordinary Gazette No. 75 of 2022.]
BE IT ENACTED by the King's Most Excellent Majesty, by and with the advice and
consent of the National Assembly of Saint Christopher and Nevis, and by the authority of
the same as follows:

### 1.

Short title.
This Act may be cited as the Small Business Development (Amendment) Act, 2022.

### 2.

Interpretation.
In this Act, unless the context otherwise requires—
“Act” means the Small Business Development Act, Cap. 20.61.

### 3.

Amendment of section 2.

The Act is amended in section 2 titled “Interpretation” by replacing the definition of
“Minister” as follows—
“

“Minister” means the Minister responsible for small business;”.
