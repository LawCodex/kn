SAINT CHRISTOPHER AND NEVIS
No. 9 of 2018
AN ACT to provide for the incorporation of the Lighthouse Baptist Church
Ministries and to provide for related or incidental matters.
[Published 16th August 2018, Official Gazette No. 41 of 2018.]

BE IT ENACTED by the Queen's Most Excellent Majesty, by and with the advice
and consent of the National Assembly of Saint Christopher and Nevis, and by the
authority of the same as follows:

### 1.

Short title.
This Act may be cited as the Lighthouse Baptist Church Ministries Incorporation,
Act 2018.

### 2.

Incorporation.
The Lighthouse Baptist Church Ministries, shall be a body corporate, and shall in
its corporate name,
(a) have perpetual succession;
(b) be capable of suing and being sued in a court of law;
(c) have a common seal;
(d) be capable of acquiring property by purchase, exchange, demise or
otherwise in perpetuity or for the life or lives for any term of years or
upon any terms, conditions or limitations.

### 3.

Constitution of Board of Trustees.
The Lighthouse Baptist Church Ministries, shall have a Board of Trustees which shall
be responsible for the management of all the affairs of the Lighthouse Baptist Church
Ministries.

### 4.

Vesting of Property.
(1) All lands or other property, real or personal, that belonged to, or were held in
trust for or on behalf of the Lighthouse Baptist Church or Lighthouse Baptist Church
Ministries, prior to the coming into force of this Act are hereby vested in the Board of
Trustees of the Lighthouse Baptist Church Ministries.
(2) Notwithstanding the provisions of subsection (1), the properties, more fully
described in the Schedule to this Act and held on behalf of the Lighthouse Baptist
Church or the Lighthouse Baptist Church Ministries, immediately prior to the coming
into force of this Act are hereby vested in the Board of Trustees of the Lighthouse
Baptist Church Ministries, referred to in subsection 3.
(3) The Board of Trustees shall have full power to acquire by purchase, transfer,
donation, exchange, devise, bequest, grant, or otherwise any real or personal property
in Saint Christopher and Nevis or any estate or interest therein on behalf of Lighthouse
Baptist Church Ministries.

### 5.

Power to Dispose of Property.
Lighthouse Baptist Church Ministries, may, subject to the regulations made under
this Act, from time to time, by Deed under its seal, sell, mortgage or demise, grant,
convey, exchange or otherwise dispose of any land or other property vested in the
Lighthouse Baptist Church or Lighthouse Baptist Church Ministries, as it sees fit.

### 6.

Saving.
Nothing in this Act shall affect the rights of Her Majesty, Her Heirs or successors
or any body politic or corporate or any other person except such as are mentioned in
this Act and those claiming by, through, from or under them.

### 7.

Regulations.
Lighthouse Baptist Church Ministries may, with the approval of the Minister
responsible for Ecclesiastical Affairs, make regulations to manage its affairs.
