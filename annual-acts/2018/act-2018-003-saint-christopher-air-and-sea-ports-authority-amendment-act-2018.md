SAINT CHRISTOPHER AND NEVIS

AN ACT to amend the Saint Christopher Air and Sea Ports Authority Act,
Cap. 8.07.
[Published 19th February 2018, Extra-Ordinary Gazette No. 11 of 2018.]

BE IT ENACTED by the Queen's Most Excellent Majesty, by and with the advice
and consent of the National Assembly of Saint Christopher and Nevis and by the authority
of the same as follows:

### 1.

Short title.

This Act may be cited as the Saint Christopher Air and Sea Ports Authority
(Amendment) Act, 2018.

### 2.

Interpretation.
In this Act,
“Act” means the Saint Christopher Air and Sea Ports Authority Act, Cap. 8.07.

### 3.

Amendment of section 24.
The Act is amended in section 24 subsection (2) (a) by replacing it with the following

...................................
new subsection

“(2) (a) to borrow and reborrow moneys pursuant to loan agreements whether
secured or otherwise, or by the issue and sale of debentures or debenture stock
or other security, or promissory notes, or any combination thereof, for the
purpose of raising the funds of the Authority for the effectual exercise by it of
the powers conferred by this Act.”.

### 4.

Amendment of section 75.
The Act is amended in section 75 by
(a) renumbering section 75 as section 75 subsection (1);

Saint Christopher Air and Sea Ports Authority (Amendment) Act, 2018 - 3.

(b) in subsection (1), replacing the expression “Where, after
the commencement of this Act” with the expression “Subject to
sub-section (2), where after the commencement of this Act”;
(c) inserting new subsections (2) and (3) as follows
“(2) Subsection (1) (b) shall not apply to any action or legal proceeding
commenced against the Authority arising out of, related to, or
connected with any borrowing by the Authority pursuant to its powers
under section 24 or section 25.
(3) Section 2(1) (a) of the Public Authorities Protection Act, Cap 5.13 shall
not apply to any action or legal proceeding commenced against the
Authority arising out of, related to, or connected with any borrowing
by the Authority pursuant to its powers under section 24 or section
25, and the provisions of the Limitation Act, Cap. 5.08 shall apply.”.

### 5.

Amendment of section 76.
The Act is amended in section 76 by
(a) renumbering section 76 as section 76 subsection (1);
(b) in subsection (1), replacing the expression “Notwithstanding anything to
the contrary in this Act” with the expression “Subject to sub-section (2)
and notwithstanding anything to the contrary in this Act”;
(c) inserting a new subsection (2) as follows
“(2) Subsection (1) shall not apply to:
(a)

any judgment or order obtained against the Authority arising out
of, related to, or connected with any borrowing by the Authority
pursuant to its powers under section 24 or section 25, including
the enforcement or realisation of any security provided by the
Authority;

(b) any seizure or taking of any property of the Authority by any
person, including a receiver appointed out of court, having by law
power to attach or distrain property, where such seizure or distress
arises out of, is related to, or is connected with any borrowing by
the Authority pursuant to its powers under section 24 or section
25; or
(c) the enforcement or realisation of any security provided by the
Authority where the grant of such security arises out of, is related
to, or is connected with any borrowing by the Authority pursuant
to its powers under section 24 or section 25.”.

### 6.

Amendment of section 78.

Saint Christopher Air and Sea Ports Authority (Amendment) Act, 2018 - 3.

The Act is amended in section 78 by adding a new subsection (5) as follows
“(5) All lands and other property within the limits of the ports including
the beds and shores vested in the Saint Christopher Air and Sea Ports
Authority pursuant to section 78(1) (a) may be dealt with, including
but not limited to being conveyed, disposed of or mortgaged, under
and in accordance with the provisions of the Conveyancing and
Law of Property Act, Cap. 10.04.”.
