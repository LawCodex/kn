SAINT CHRISTOPHER AND NEVIS
No. 8 of 2018
AN ACT to provide for the incorporation of the Apostolic Faith Mission Church and to
provide for related or incidental matters.
[Published 16th August 2018, Official Gazette No. 41 of 2018.]

BE IT ENACTED by the Queen's Most Excellent Majesty, by and with the advice and
consent of the National Assembly of Saint Christopher and Nevis, and by the authority of
the same as follows:

### 1.

Short title.
This Act may be cited as the Apostolic Faith Mission Church, Incorporation Act 2018.

### 2.

Incorporation.
The Apostolic Faith Mission Church, shall be a body corporate, and shall in its corporate name,
(a) have perpetual succession;
(b) be capable of suing and being sued in a court of law;
(c) have a common seal;
(d) be capable of acquiring property by purchase, exchange, demise or otherwise
in perpetuity or for the life or lives for any term of years or upon any terms,
conditions or limitations.

### 3.

Constitution of Board of Trustees.

The Apostolic Faith Mission Church, shall have a Board of Trustees which shall be
responsible for the management of all the affairs of the Apostolic Faith Mission Church for
Saint Christopher and Nevis.

### 4.

Vesting of Property.

(1) All lands or other property, real or personal, that belonged to, or were held in trust
for or on behalf of the Apostolic Faith Church, the Apostolic Faith Mission or the Apostolic

Faith Mission Inc. prior to the coming into force of this Act are hereby vested in the Board
of Trustees of the Apostolic Faith Mission Church, established by this Act.
(2) Notwithstanding the provisions of subsection (1), the properties, more fully
described in the Schedule to this Act and held on behalf of the Apostolic Faith Church, the
Apostolic Faith Mission or the Apostolic Faith Mission Inc. immediately prior to the coming
into force of this Act are hereby vested in the Board of Trustees of the Apostolic Faith
Mission Church, established by this Act.
(3) The Board of Trustees shall have full power to acquire by purchase, transfer,
donation, exchange, devise, bequest, grant, or otherwise any real or personal property in
Saint Christopher and Nevis or any estate or interest therein on behalf of the Apostolic
Faith Mission Church.

### 5.

Power to Dispose of Property.

The Apostolic Faith Mission Church, may, subject to the regulations made under this
Act, from time to time, by Deed under its seal, sell, mortgage or demise, grant, convey,
exchange or otherwise dispose of any land or other property vested in the Apostolic Faith
Mission Church, as it sees fit.

### 6.

Saving.

Nothing in this Act shall affect the rights of Her Majesty, Her Heirs or successors or
any body politic or corporate or any other person except such as are mentioned in this Act
and those claiming by, through, from or under them.

### 7.

Regulations.

The Apostolic Faith Mission Church, may, with the approval of the Minister responsible
for Ecclesiastical Affairs, make regulations to manage its affairs.
