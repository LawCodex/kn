SAINT CHRISTOPHER AND NEVIS
AN ACT to provide for the services of Saint Christopher and Nevis for the financial
year commencing 1st January 2019 and ending on 31st December 2019.
[Published 31st December 2018, Extra Ordinary Gazette No. 68 of 2018.]
BE IT ENACTED by the Queen's Most Excellent Majesty by and with the advice
and consent of the National Assembly of Saint Christopher and Nevis and by the authority
of the same as follows:–

### 1.

Short title and commencement.

This Act may be cited as the Appropriation (2019) Act, 2018 and shall be deemed
to have come into force on the first day of January, 2019.

### 2.

Appropriation.

There shall be and there is hereby granted to Her Majesty the Queen for and
during the period of one year commencing from the 1st day of January 2019 and ending
on the 31st day of December 2019, the sum of Six Hundred and Seventy-Seven Million,
Nine Hundred and Ninety-Four Thousand, and Seventy-Three Dollars ($677,994,073) over
and above the sums granted and provided under the Acts and other laws in force in Saint
Christopher and Nevis, to be applied and expended in the manner and the services set forth
in the Schedule hereto.

### 3.

Sums charged on Consolidated Fund.

The said sum of Six Hundred and Seventy-Seven Million, Nine Hundred and
Ninety-Four Thousand, and Seventy-Three Dollars ($677,994,073), shall be and the same
is hereby declared to be charged upon and payable from and out of the Consolidated Fund.

### 4.

Authority to pay.

The Accountant General of Saint Christopher and Nevis is hereby authorised
and required from time to time upon the order or warrant of the Minister of Finance to pay
the several services therein mentioned, as the said order or warrant shall direct, out of the
Consolidated Fund without further order or formality.
