SAINT CHRISTOPHER AND NEVIS

AN ACT to amend the Development Control and Planning Act, Cap. 20.07.
[Published 5th February 2018, Extra-Ordinary Gazette No. 6 of 2018.]

BE IT ENACTED by the Queen's Most Excellent Majesty, by and with the advice and
consent of the National Assembly of Saint Christopher and Nevis and by the authority of
the same as follows:

### 1.

Short title.

This Act may be cited as the Development Control and Planning (Amendment)
Act, 2018.

### 2.

Interpretation.
In this Act,
“Act” means the Development Control and Planning Act, Cap. 20.07.

### 3.

Amendment of First Schedule.

The Act is amended in the First Schedule by replacing section 1 subsection (1) with
the following new subsection
