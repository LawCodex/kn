SAINT CHRISTOPHER AND NEVIS
No. 8 of 2023
AN ACT to amend the Eastern Caribbean Central Bank Act, Cap. 21.06 and for related
matters.
[Published 25th May 2023, Official Gazette No. 28 of 2023.]
BE IT ENACTED by the King's Most Excellent Majesty, by and with the advice and
consent of the National Assembly of Saint Christopher and Nevis, and by the authority of
the same as follows:

### 1.

Short title.

This Act may be cited as the Eastern Caribbean Central Bank (Amendment)
Act, 2023.

### 2.

Interpretation.
In this Act, unless the context otherwise requires,
“Act” means the Eastern Caribbean Central Bank Act, Cap. 21.06.

### 3.

Amendment of the Act.

The Act is amended by inserting the following new section 4A immediately after
section 4.
“ 4A. Inconsistency with other legislation
In the event of any inconsistency between the provisions of this Act and the
operation of any other enactment, the provisions of this Act shall prevail to the
extent of the inconsistency.”
