SAINT CHRISTOPHER AND NEVIS
No. 10 of 2023
AN ACT to amend the Crown Proceedings Act, Cap. 5.06.
[Published 16th June 2023, Extra Ordinary Gazette No. 34 of 2023.]
BE IT ENACTED by the King's Most Excellent Majesty, by and with the advice and
consent of the National Assembly of Saint Christopher and Nevis, and by the authority of
the same as follows:

### 1.

Short title.
This Act may be cited as the Crown Proceedings (Amendment) Act, 2023.

### 2.

Interpretation.
In this Act,
“Act” means the Crown Proceedings Act, Cap. 5.06.

### 3.

Amendment of section 14.
The Act is amended in section 14 by
(a) renumbering the section as subsection (1);
(b) adding the following new subsection (2)
“ (2) All documents required to be served–
(a) on the Nevis Island Administration for the purpose of, or in connection
with, any civil proceedings against the Nevis Island Administration shall
be served on the Legal Department of the Office of the Premier of the
Nevis Island Administration.

(b) on an officer of the Nevis Island Administration, as distinct from the Nevis
Island Administration itself, shall be served on that officer.”
