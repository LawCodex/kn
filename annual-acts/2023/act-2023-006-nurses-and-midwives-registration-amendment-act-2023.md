SAINT CHRISTOPHER AND NEVIS
No. 6 of 2023
AN ACT to amend the Nurses and Midwives (Registration) Act, Cap. 9.17
and for related matters.
[Published 20th April 2023, Official Gazette No. 20 of 2023.]

BE IT ENACTED by the King's Most Excellent Majesty, by and with the
advice and consent of the National Assembly of Saint Christopher and Nevis, and
by the authority of the same as follows:

### 1.

Short Title.
This Act may be cited as the Nurses and Midwives (Registration) (Amendment)
Act, 2023.

### 2.

Interpretation.
In this Act, the expression, “Act” refers to the Nurses and Midwives
(Registration) Act, Cap. 9.17

### 3.

Amendment of Second Schedule.
The Act is amended in the Second Schedule in paragraph 11 by replacing the
title and subparagraph (1) as follows:
" Biennial Registration.
(1) W here a nurse is desirous of having his or her name
retained on the Register, he or she shall, every two years before the

Nurses and Midwives (Registration) (Amendment) Act, 2023 - 6.

31 st day of December, forward to the Registrar notification in
Form 5 in Schedule 2 of his or her intention to remain on the Register.".
