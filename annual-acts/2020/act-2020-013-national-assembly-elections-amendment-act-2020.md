SAINT CHRISTOPHER AND NEVIS
No. 13 of 2020
AN ACT to amend the National Assembly Elections Act, Cap. 2.01.
[Published 9th November 2020, Extra-Ordinary Gazette No. 83 of 2020.]
BE IT ENACTED by the Queen's Most Excellent Majesty, by and with the advice and
consent of the National Assembly of Saint Christopher and Nevis and by the authority of the
same as follows:

### 1.

Short title.
This Act may be cited as the National Assembly Elections (Amendment) Act, 2020.

### 2.

Interpretation.
In this Act,
“principal Act” means the National Assembly Elections Act, Cap. 2.01.

### 3.

Amendment of Section 2.

The principal Act is amended in section 2 by adding the following definition of the
term “Commonwealth Caribbean Country” in the correct alphabetical order
““Commonwealth Caribbean Country” means any country specified in Part I of
the First Schedule;”.

### 4.

Amendment of section 37.
The principal Act is amended in section 37,
(a) by replacing subsection (1) (d) and adding paragraphs (e) as follows
“(d) a citizen of a Commonwealth Caribbean Country of eighteen years or
upwards and is ordinarily resident in Saint Christopher and Nevis at least
twelve months immediately before the registration date;
(e) a Commonwealth citizen, not being a citizen of Saint Christopher and
Nevis or a citizen of a Commonwealth Caribbean Country, of eighteen

years or upwards who has been ordinarily resident in Saint Christopher
and Nevis at least three years immediately before the registration date;”.
(b) by replacing subsection (3) as follows
“37 (3). Where a person who is ordinarily resident in Saint Christopher and
Nevis is registered as a voter for a constituency and he or she has ceasedd to
reside in that constituency then he or she shall
(a) continue to be registered as a voter for that constituency, for a period
not exceeding six months; and
(b) register in the other constituency that he or she is qualified to be
registered in, as a voter.”.

### 5.

Amendment of the First Schedule.
The principal Act is amended by
(a) renumbering the First Schedule as Part II to the First Schedule and
(b) inserting a new Part I to the First Schedule as follows

## Part I

Commonwealth Caribbean Countries
(Section 2, 37)

Antigua and Barbud, Barbados, Belize, Dominica, Grenada, Guyana, Jamaica, Saint Lucia,
Saint Vincent and the Grenadines, The Bahamas and Trinidad and Tobago.
