SAINT CHRISTOPHER AND NEVIS
No. 6 of 2020
AN ACT to amend the Drugs (Prevention & Abatement of the Misuse and Abuse of
Drugs) Act, Cap. 9.08.
[Published 21st February 2020, Extra-Ordinary Gazette No. 13 of 2020.]
BE IT ENACTED by the Queen's Most Excellent Majesty, by and with the advice and
consent of the National Assembly of Saint Christopher and Nevis and by the authority of the
same as follows:

### 1.

Short title.

This Act may be cited as the Drugs (Prevention & Abatement of the Misuse and Abuse
of Drugs) (Amendment) Act, 2020.

### 2.

Interpretation.
In this Act,

“Act” means the Drugs (Prevention & Abatement of the Misuse and Abuse of Drugs)
Act, Cap. 9.08.

### 3.

Amendment of Section 6.

The Act is amended in section 6 in subsection (4) paragraph (e), by replacing the
expression “fifteen grammes of cannabis or five grammes of cannabis resin” with the expression
“fifty-six grammes of cannabis or fifteen grammes of cannabis resin”.

### 4.

Amendment of Section 6A.

The Act is amended in section 6A paragraph (a), by replacing the expression “fifteen
grammes of cannabis or five grammes of cannabis resin” with the expression “fifty-six grammes
of cannabis or fifteen grammes of cannabis resin”.

Drugs (Prevention & Abatement of the Misuse and Abuse of Drugs) (Amendment) Act, 2020 - 6.

### 5.

Amendment of section 7.
Section 7 is amended by deleting subsection (3).
