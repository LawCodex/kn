SAINT CHRISTOPHER AND NEVIS
No. 17 of 2020
AN ACT to provide for the services of Saint Christopher and Nevis for the financial
year commencing 1st January 2021 and ending on 31st December 2021.
[Published 29th December 2020, Extra Ordinary Gazette No. 94 of 2020.]
BE IT ENACTED by the Queen's Most Excellent Majesty by and with the advice and
consent of the National Assembly of Saint Christopher and Nevis and by the authority of the
same as follows:–

### 1.

Short title and commencement.

This Act may be cited as the Appropriation (2021) Act, 2020 and shall be deemed to have
come into force on the first day of January, 2021.

### 2.

Appropriation.

There shall be and there is hereby granted to Her Majesty the Queen for and during the
period of one year commencing from the 1st day of January 2021 and ending on the 31st day of
December 2021, the sum of Seven Hundred and Seventy Million, One Hundred and ThirtySix Thousand and Fifty-Three Dollars ($770,136,053) over and above the sums granted and
provided under the Acts and other laws in force in Saint Christopher and Nevis, to be applied
and expended in the manner and the services set forth in the Schedule hereto.

### 3.

Sums charged on Consolidated Fund.

The said sum of Seven Hundred and Seventy Million, One Hundred and Thirty-Six
Thousand and Fifty-Three Dollars ($770,136,053), shall be and the same is hereby declared
to be charged upon and payable from and out of the Consolidated Fund.

### 4.

Authority to pay.

The Accountant General of Saint Christopher and Nevis is hereby authorised and
required from time to time upon the order or warrant of the Minister of Finance to pay the
several services therein mentioned, as the said order or warrant shall direct, out of the
Consolidated Fund without further order or formality.
