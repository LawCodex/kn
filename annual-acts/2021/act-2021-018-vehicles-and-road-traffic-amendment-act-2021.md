SAINT CHRISTOPHER AND NEVIS
No. 18 of 2021
AN ACT to amend the Vehicles and Road Traffic Act, Cap. 15.06.
[Published 30th December 2021, Official Gazette No. 81 of 2021.]

BE IT ENACTED by the Queen's Most Excellent Majesty, by and with the
advice and consent of the National Assembly of Saint Christopher and Nevis and
by the authority of the same as follows:

### 1.

Short Title.
This Act may be cited as the Vehicles and Road Traffic (Amendment) Act,
2021\.

### 2.

Interpretation.
In this Act,
“Act” means the Vehicles and Road Traffic Act, Cap. 15.06.

### 3.

Amendment of section 62.
The Act is amended in section 62 by replacing sub-section (5), as follows
“(5)
Every motor vehicle, other than a motorcycle or agricultural tractor,
shall be fitted with a windscreen that provides ample protection to the
driver of the motor vehicle and the windscreen shall
(a) be kept in such condition as to allow an unobstructed view of
the road at the front of the vehicle; and

(b) not be tinted more than ten inches from the top of the
windscreen for the purpose of providing protection as a
sun visor and that tinting shall be in accordance with the
provisions of sub-section (6).”.
