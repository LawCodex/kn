SAINT CHRISTOPHER AND NEVIS
No. 16 of 2021
AN ACT to validate the Land Registry Act No. 10 of 2017 and to give retroactive effect
to the provisions of the Act.
[Published 6 th August 2021 – Extra-Ordinary Gazette No. 48 of 2021]

BE IT ENACTED by the Queen’s Most Excellent Majesty, by and with the advice and
consent of the National Assembly of Saint Christopher and Nevis and by the authority of
the same as follows:

### 1.

Short Title.
This Act may be cited as the Land Registry (Validation) Act, 2021.

### 2.

Interpretation.
In this Act,
“Act” means the Land Registry Act, No. 10 of 2017.

### 3.

Commencement and Validation of Act.

The Act shall come into operation on the 9th day of August, 2021 and any action taken or
done by the Government in relation to its enactment prior to the coming into force of the Act is
hereby validated and declared to have been lawfully taken or done from 13th November, 2017.
