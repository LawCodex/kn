SAINT CHRISTOPHER AND NEVIS
No. 5 of 2021
AN ACT to amend the Magistrate’s Code of Procedure Act, Cap 3.17.
[Published 19th March 2021, Extra-Ordinary Gazette No. 13 of 2021.]
BE IT ENACTED by the Queen's Most Excellent Majesty, by and with the advice and
consent of the National Assembly of Saint Christopher and Nevis and by the authority of the
same as follows:

### 1.

Short title.

This Act may be cited as the Magistrate’s Code of Procedure (Amendment)
Act, 2021.

### 2.

Interpretation.
In this Act, unless the context otherwise requires, the expression
“Act” means the Magistrate’s Code of Procedure Act, Cap 3.17.

### 3.

Amendment of Act.

The Act is amended by inserting a new section 249A, immediately after section 249, as
follows
“249A. The Chief Justice may make rules of practice regulating proceedings in criminal
causes and matters and in all matters of criminal procedure not provided for by this
Act or any other enactment.”.
