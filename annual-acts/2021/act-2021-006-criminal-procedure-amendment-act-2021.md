SAINT CHRISTOPHER AND NEVIS
No. 6 of 2021
AN ACT to amend the Criminal Procedure Act, Cap 4.06.
[Published 19th March 2021, Extra-Ordinary Gazette No. 13 of 2021.]
BE IT ENACTED by the Queen's Most Excellent Majesty, by and with the advice and
consent of the National Assembly of Saint Christopher and Nevis and by the authority of the
same as follows:

### 1.

Short title.
This Act may be cited as the Criminal Procedure (Amendment) Act, 2021.

### 2.

Interpretation.
In this Act, unless the context otherwise requires, the expression
“Act” means the Criminal Procedure Act, Cap 4.06.

### 3.

Amendment of Act.

The Act is amended by inserting a new section 99, immediately after section 98, as
follows
“99. The Chief Justice may, in the exercise of powers conferred by section 17 of the
Supreme Court Order, Fifth Schedule to the West Indies Act, Cap 1.01, make rules
regulating the practice and procedure of the High Court in all matters of criminal
procedure not provided for by this Act or any other enactment.”.
