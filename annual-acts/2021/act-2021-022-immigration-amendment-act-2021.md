No. 22 of 2021
AN ACT to amend the Immigration Act, Cap. 6.02.
[Published 30th December 2021, Official Gazette No. 81 of 2021.]
BE IT ENACTED by the Queen's Most Excellent Majesty, by and with the advice and
consent of the National Assembly of Saint Christopher and Nevis and by the authority of the
same as follows:

### 1.

Short Title.
This Act may be cited as the Immigration (Amendment) Act, 2021.

### 2.

Interpretation.
In this Act, “Act” means the Immigration Act, Cap. 6.02.

### 3.

Amendment of section 2.

The Act is amended in section 2 by inserting the following new definition in the correct
alphabetical order
“digital resident” includes
(a) Digital Nomads who are remote workers who travel to different locations
on a regular basis and who use modern technology to work from hotels,
co-working spaces, coffee shops or libraries with a WiFi connected Laptop
or smartphone from anywhere in the world;
(b) Freelancers who are self-employed or working for a company as an
independent contractor and who can travel while they work or work from
home while living in one place on a long-term basis;
(c) Consultants who are professional consultants that work remotely;
(d) Retired Persons who are persons who have retired and who have sufficient
funds to support themselves during their 12 months stay in
Saint Christopher and Nevis;

(e) Digital Workers or Tech Startups who are workers in the technology
industry who are working from home including but not limited to; content
specialist, software developers, graphic designers, innovators employed
with large corporations that allow them to work remotely;
(f) High-Net-Worth Individuals who are individuals that are seeking places
for investment opportunities and would like to explore the islands and
want to experience life in the islands for an extended period of a maximum
of 12 months.

### 4.

Amendment of section 5.
The Act is amended in section 5 by inserting a new paragraph 5 (2) (b) (iv) as follows
“(iv) is likely to be a digital resident of Saint Christopher and Nevis who is
retired, a high- net-worth individual or is in employment on a full-time basis in
a country other than Saint Christopher and Nevis and wishes to work remotely
in Saint Christopher and Nevis for a maximum period of twelve months;”.

### 5.

Amendment of section 6.
The Act is amended in section 6 by
(a) in paragraph (f), replacing the full stop with a semicolon;
(b) inserting a new paragraph (g) immediately following paragraph (f) as follows
“(g)

digital resident.”.

(c) inserting new sub-sections (6) and (7) as follows
“(6) Subject to the provisions of this Act and Regulations made under this
Act a person may, on application made to the Minister in the form set out in the
Eighth Schedule, be granted permission to become a digital resident of
Saint Christopher and Nevis, if he or she satisfies the requirements specified in
sub-section (7) and the Eighth Schedule.
(7) The person referred to in sub-section (6) shall be a person who
(a) wishes to work remotely in Saint Christopher and Nevis for a
maximum period of twelve months and
(i) who is employed on a full-time basis in a country other
than Saint Christopher and Nevis; and
(ii) who is employed by a company registered and operating
outside of Saint Christopher and Nevis;
(b) is not a citizen, an approved CARICOM citizen or a citizen of a
Protocol Member State;
(c) is a retired person who are persons who retired and who have
sufficient funds to support themselves during their 12 months
stay in Saint Christopher and Nevis;

(d) is a high-net-worth individual who is an individual that is
seeking places for investment opportunities and would like to
explore the islands and wants to experience life in the islands
for an extended period of a maximum of 12 months;
(e) is dependent on a principal applicant under this sub-section.

### 6.

Amendment of section 14.
The Act is amended in section 14 by inserting a new sub-section (9) as follows
“(9) Notwithstanding anything contained in this section, a digital resident, as
long as such a person is not a prohibited person, shall be permitted to enter and
remain in Saint Christopher and Nevis for a period of twelve months.”.

## PART A

Digital Residents

1\.

Employment of domestic workers.

A digital resident, referred to under section 6, may employ a person who is a citizen of
Saint Christopher and Nevis, or an approved CARICOM citizen or a citizen of a Protocol
Member State residing in Saint Christopher and Nevis, to work as a domestic worker or
support staff.
2\.

Application for Digital Resident

(1) An applicant for digital residence shall make the application online while the person
is outside of Saint Christopher and Nevis, except that this provision shall not apply in
respect of an application for renewal of a digital residence or any other case as the Minister
may, by Order, direct.
(2) Any person being a Digital Resident, who contravenes or fails to comply with any
condition subject to which the permit was granted, commits an offence.
(3) The Minister may, at any time
(a) modify or cancel any condition specified; or
(b) revoke, the Digital Residence.
