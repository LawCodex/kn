No. 15 of 2021
AN ACT to amend the Non-Government Organisations Act, Cap. 20.29.
[Published 26 th March 2021, Extra-Ordinary Gazette No. 15 of 2021.]

BE IT ENACTED by the Queen’s Most Excellent Majesty, by and with the advice and
consent of the National Assembly of Saint Christopher and Nevis and by the authority of
the same as follows:

### 1.

Short Title.
This Act may be cited as the Non-Government Organisations (Amendment) Act, 2021.

### 2.

Interpretation.

In this Act, unless the context otherwise requires the expression “Act” means the
Non-Government Organisations Act, Cap. 20.59

### 3.

Amendment of Section 3.
The Act is amended in section 3 by replacing subsection (1) as follows:
“ (1) An NGO is a non-profit, non-governmental body, organisation or
association whose aims, nature and objectives, are primarily for the benefit of the
public and shall include activities that are patriotic, religious, charitable, for scientific
inquiry, community development, for sustainable development, human rights,
artistic, for protection of animals, social, professional or sporting character, or the
like.”.

### 4.

Amendment of Section 6.

The Act is amended in section 6 subsection (2) paragraph (b), by replacing the
expression,

“company limited by guarantee”, with the expression, “that is a not for
profit company”;.

### 5.

Amendment of Section 17.B.

The Act is amended in section 17.B by inserting immediately after subsection (2), a new
subsection as follows
“ (3) The provisions of subsection (1), shall be subject to the power of the
Attorney General as the Central Authority for receiving and responding to requests
in criminal matters pursuant to section 4 of the Mutual Assistance in Criminal
Matters, Cap. 4.19.”.
