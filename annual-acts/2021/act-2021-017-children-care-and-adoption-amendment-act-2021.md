SAINT CHRISTOPHER AND NEVIS
No. 17 of 2021
AN ACT to amend the Children (Care and Adoption) Act, Cap. 12.01.
[Published 6 th August 2021 – Extra-Ordinary Gazette No. 48 of 2021]

BE IT ENACTED by the Queen’s Most Excellent Majesty, by and with the advice
and consent of the National Assembly of Saint Christopher and Nevis and by the
authority of the same as follows:

### 1.

Short Title.
This Act may be cited as the Children (Care and Adoption) (Amendment) Act, 2021.

### 2.

Interpretation.
In this Act,
“Act” means the Children (Care and Adoption) Act, Cap.12.01.

### 3.

Amendment of section 2.
The Act is amended in section 2 by replacing the definition of the term “Director” with
the following new definition
“

“Director” means the Director of the Department of Probation and
Child Protection Services;”.

### 4.

Amendment of section 87.
The Act is amended in section 87 by replacing subsection (3), as follows:
“ (3) The members of the Adoption Committee shall elect a deputy
chairperson from amongst the members appointed pursuant to the provisions

Children (Care and Adoption) (Amendment) Act, 2021 – 17.

of subsections 85(3)(b) and 85(3)(c) and if the chairperson is unable to preside
at a meeting, the deputy chairperson shall preside at that meeting.”.
