SAINT CHRISTOPHER AND NEVIS
No. 4 of 2021
AN ACT to amend the COVID-19 (Prevention and Control) Act, No. 16 of 2020
[Published 19th March 2021, Extra-Ordinary Gazette No. 13 of 2021.]
BE IT ENACTED by the Queen's Most Excellent Majesty, by and with the advice and
consent of the National Assembly of Saint Christopher and Nevis and by the authority of the
same as follows:

### 1.

Short title.
This Act may be cited as the COVID-19 (Prevention and Control) (Amendment)
Act, 2021.

### 2.

Amendment of section 8.
The Act is amended in section 8 by replacing subsection (1) as follows
“8. (1) Notwithstanding the provisions of the Small Charges Act, Cap. 4.36, in an
effort to prevent the spread of COVID-19, when in a public place a person shall wear
a face-mask, covering his or her
(a) nose,
(b) mouth; and
(c) chin.”.
