SAINT CHRISTOPHER AND NEVIS
No. 1 of 2021
AN ACT to amend the Saint Christopher and Nevis (Mutual Exchange of Information on
Taxation Matters) Act, Cap. 20.60.
[Published 18th February 2021, Official Gazette No. 8 of 2021.]
BE IT ENACTED by the Queen's Most Excellent Majesty, by and with the advice and
consent of the National Assembly of Saint Christopher and Nevis and by the authority of the
same as follows:

### 1.

Short title.

This Act may be cited as the Saint Christopher and Nevis (Mutual Exchange of Information
on Taxation Matters) (Amendment) Act, 2021

### 2.

Interpretation.

In this Act, unless the context otherwise requires, “the expression, “Act” means the
Saint Christopher and Nevis (Mutual Exchange of Information on Taxation Matters) Act,
Cap 20.60.

### 3.

Amendment of Section 4.

The Act is amended in section 4 subsection (1) by replacing the expression, “Financial
Secretary” with the expression, “Comptroller of Inland Revenue”.
