SAINT CHRISTOPHER AND NEVIS
No. 13 of 2024
AN ACT to amend the Immigration Act, Cap. 6.02 and for related matters.
[Published 4th July 2024, Official Gazette No. 36 of 2024.]
BE IT ENACTED by the King’s Most Excellent Majesty, by and with the advice and
consent of the National Assembly of Saint Christopher and Nevis, and by the authority of
the same as follows:

### 1.

Short title.
This Act may be cited as the Immigration (Amendment) Act, 2024.

### 2.

Interpretation.
In this Act, the expression, “Act” refers to the Immigration Act, Cap. 6.02.

### 3.

Amendment of Section 2.

Section 2 of the Act, is amended by inserting the following definition in the correct
alphabetical order
“financial or other material benefit” includes any type of financial or non-financial
inducement, payment, bribe, reward, advantage, privilege or service, including
sexual or other services;”.

### 4.

Amendment of section 27.
The Act is amended in section 27 by replacing subsection 6 as follows:
“(6) In the exercise of his or her functions under this Act an immigration officer shall
act in accordance with the directions and instructions of the Chief Immigration
Officer.

### 5.

Amendment of Act.

The Act is amended by inserting immediately after section 27, the following new
section 27A:

Immigration (Amendment) Act, 2024 – 13.

“27A.

Jurisdiction of Chief Immigration Officer.

(1) Further to the provisions of Section 26, the Chief Immigration Officer, shall
be responsible for the investigation, arrest and charging of persons who
commit offences under this Act.
(2) In the exercise of his or her powers pursuant to subsection (1), the Chief
Immigration Officer may consult and collaborate with the Commissioner of
Police in such manner as the Chief Immigration Officer may deem fit.”.

### 6.

Amendment of Section 32.
The Act is amended in section 32 as follows:
(a) in subsection (1) by replacing the expression, “fifty” with the expression, “one
hundred”; and
(b) in subsection (2), by replacing the expression, “one hundred” with the
expression, “two hundred and fifty”.
