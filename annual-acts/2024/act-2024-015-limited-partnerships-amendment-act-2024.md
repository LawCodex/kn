BE IT ENACTED by the King’s Most Excellent Majesty, by and with the advice and
consent of the National Assembly of Saint Christopher and Nevis, and by the authority of
the same as follows:

### 1.

Short Title.
This Act may be cited as the Limited Partnerships (Amendment) Act, 2024.

### 2.

Interpretation.

In this Act, unless the context otherwise requires, the expression, “Act” means the
Limited Partnerships Act, Cap. 21.12.

Amendment of section 8.
The Act is amended in section 8 by replacing subsection (4) as follows
“(4) Where default is made in compliance of subsection (1) of this section, every
general partner who is in default commits an offence and is liable to a fine not
exceeding seventy-five thousand dollars and in the case of a continuing offence
to a further fine not exceeding two hundred and fifty dollars for each day on
which the offence so continues.

### 5.

Amendment of section 21.
The Act is amended in section 21 as follows
(a) in subsection (2) by inserting, immediately after the expression, “Registrar”,
the expression, “within 21 days.”.
(b) in subsection (4) paragraph (a), by inserting the expression, “general and”
immediately before the expression, “limited” wherever it occurs.
(c) in subsection (4) paragraph (c), by inserting, immediately after the expression,
“made to it”, the expression, “and any amendment to the partnership agreement
shall be submitted to the Registrar within 21 days of that amendment;”.
(d) in subsection (6) by replacing it as follows:
“(6) Where default is made in compliance with this section, the Registrar
may impose on every general partner who is in default, a penalty to be paid
to the Registrar as follows:
(a)

upon the issuance of a first notice informing of the noncompliance, a penalty not exceeding five thousand dollars and
in the case of a continuing offence, to a fine not exceeding one
hundred dollars for each day on which the non-compliance so
continues;

(b)

upon the issuance of a second notice, to a penalty not exceeding
ten thousand dollars and in the case of a continuing offence, to
a fine not exceeding one hundredand fifty dollars for each day
on which the non-compliance so continues;

(c)

upon the issuance of a third notice, to a penalty not exceeding
twenty-five thousand dollars and in the case of a continuing
offence to a fine not exceeding two hundred and fifty dollars
for each day on which the non-compliance so continues.

(e) by adding immediately after subsection (6), the following new paragraphs (7)
and (8):

“(7) The Register shall be made available to the Registrar within a time
period not exceeding 24 hours.
(8)
All information and documents in thissection shall be kept accurately
and updated accordingly once changes are made.”.

### 6.

Amendment of section 22.
The Act is amended in section 22 by replacing subsection (4) as follows:
“(4) Every general partner who is in default of subsection (1) paragraph (a)
commits an offence and shall be liable
(a) on summary conviction to a fine not exceeding one hundred
thousand dollars; and
(b) in the case of an offence under subsection (1) paragraph (b), to a fine
not exceeding fifty thousand dollars and to a fine of two hundred
dollars for every day that the offence is permitted to continue; and;”.
(c) the registration of the declaration may be cancelled in accordance
with section 61, the provisions of which shall apply accordingly.".

### 7.

Amendment of section 70.

The Act is amended in section 70 by inserting immediately after the expression,
“a fine”, the expression, “not exceeding one hundred and fifty thousand dollars.”.
