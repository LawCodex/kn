SAINT CHRISTOPHER AND NEVIS
No. 1 of 2024
AN ACT to amend the Housing and Social Development Levy Act, Cap. 20.21 and for
related matters.
[Published 29th February 2024, Official Gazette No. 11 of 2024.]
BE IT ENACTED by the King's Most Excellent Majesty, by and with the advice and
consent of the National Assembly of Saint Christopher and Nevis, and by the authority of
the same as follows:

### 1.

Short title.
This Act may be cited as the Housing and Social Development Levy (Amendment) Act, 2024.

### 2.

Amendment of section 3 of the Act.
The Act is amended in section 3 subsection (2) as follows:
(a) by replacing paragraph (a) as follows
“

(a) in the case of an employee whose wages are up to $23,400.00 (450.00
per week), 0% by the employee, and 3% by the employer in respect of
the wages the employer pays to the employee;” and

(b) in paragraph (b), by replacing the expression, “18720.00”, with th expression,
“23,400.00”.
