SAINT CHRISTOPHER AND NEVIS
No. 18 of 2024
AN ACT to amend the Freedom of Information Act, No. 6 of 2018.
[Published 25th July 2024, Extra-Ordinary Gazette No. 43 of 2024.]
BE IT ENACTED by the King’s Most Excellent Majesty, by and with the advice and
consent of the National Assembly of Saint Christopher and Nevis, and by the authority of
the same as follows:

### 1.

Short Title.
This Act may be cited as the Freedom of Information (Amendment) Act, 2024.

### 2.

Interpretation.

In this Act, unless the context otherwise requires, “Act” means the Freedom of
Information Act, No. 6 of 2018.

### 3.

Amendment of section 2.

The Act is amended in section 2 by adding the following new definitions in the
correct alphabetical order
“ “Ombudsman” means the Ombudsman appointed under section 4 of the Ombudsman
Act, Cap. 3.22;
“Special Prosecutor” means the Special Prosecutor appointed under section 5 of the
Anti-Corruption Act, No. 5 of 2023;”.

### 4.

Amendment of section 35.
The Act is amended in section 35(a) by replacing subsection (2)(b) with the expression “holds or is acting in a public
office except if he or she is or is acting as the Ombudsman or the Special
Prosecutor;”; and
