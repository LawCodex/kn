AN ACT to amend the Foundations Act, Cap. 5.19.
[Published 25th July 2024, Extra-Ordinary Gazette No. 43 of 2024.]
BE IT ENACTED by the King’s Most Excellent Majesty, by and with the advice
and consent of the National Assembly of Saint Christopher and Nevis, and by the
authority of the same as follows:

### 1.

Short Title.
This Act may be cited as the Foundations (Amendment) Act, 2024.

### 2.

Interpretation.
In this Act, unless the context otherwise requires, the expression, “Act” means
the Foundations Act, Cap. 5.19.

### 3.

Amendment of section 10.
The Act is amended in section 10 by replacing subsection (2) as follows
“(2) Where a foundation changes its name under this section, the councillors
or secretary shall notify the Rgistrar within 21 days and the Registrar shall:
(a) enter the new name on the Register in place of the former name;
(b) issue a certificate of establishment altered to meet the circumstances
of the case; and
(c) issue an altered certificate with the change of name which shall have
effect from the date that it is issued.".
