SAINT CHRISTOPHER AND NEVIS
AN ACT to provide for the services of Saint Christopher and Nevis for the financial
year commencing 1st January 2020 and ending on 31st December 2020.
[Published 30th December 2019, Extra Ordinary Gazette No. 67 of 2019.]
BE IT ENACTED by the Queen's Most Excellent Majesty by and with the advice
and consent of the National Assembly of Saint Christopher and Nevis and by the authority
of the same as follows:–

### 1.

Short title and commencement.

This Act may be cited as the Appropriation (2020) Act, 2019 and shall come
into force on the first day of January, 2020.

### 2.

Appropriation.

There shall be and there is hereby granted to Her Majesty the Queen for and
during the period of one year commencing from the 1st day of January 2020 and ending on
the 31st day of December 2020, the sum of Seven Hundred and Forty-Six Million, Seven
Hundred and Sixty-Eight Thousand, Nine Hundred and Sixty-Five Dollars ($746,768,965)
over and above the sums granted and provided under the Acts and other laws in force in
Saint Christopher and Nevis, to be applied and expended in the manner and the services set
forth in the Schedule hereto.

### 3.

Sums charged on Consolidated Fund.

The said sum of Seven Hundred and Forty-Six Million, Seven Hundred and SixtyEight Thousand, Nine Hundred and Sixty-Five Dollars ($746,768,965), shall be and the same
is hereby declared to be charged upon and payable from and out of the Consolidated Fund.

### 4.

Authority to pay.

The Accountant General of Saint Christopher and Nevis is hereby authorised
and required from time to time upon the order or warrant of the Minister of Finance to pay
the several services therein mentioned, as the said order or warrant shall direct, out of the
Consolidated Fund without further order or formality.
