## CHAPTER 8.01 (N)

CHARLESTOWN VENDING
PROHIBITION ORDINANCE
AN ORDINANCE TO PROHIBIT VENDING IN ANY STREET, SIDEWALK, PUBLIC SQUARE
AND OTHER PUBLIC AREAS IN CHARLESTOWN AND FOR MATTERS INCIDENTAL THERETO
OR CONNECTED THEREWITH.
Short title.

---

### 1.

This Ordinance may be cited as the Charlestown Vending Prohibition
Ordinance.
Interpretation.

### 2.

In this Ordinance—

“Charlestown” means from the sea at the boundary of Pinneys Estate and the Nevis
Club property then going in an Easterly direction along the main Public Road
to the road leading up to Craddock Road then going South to the Big Rock at
the corner of Craddock Road then in a straight line to the public water supply
tank at the corner of Government and Roger Below roads then along the
Public Road to the Cemetery corner on the main Public Road, then Westward
along the main Public Road continuing along the road South of Government
House and the Bath Hotel to the sea.”
“Minister” means Member responsible for Finance;
“Vend” or “Vending” includes—
(a) exposing for sale;
(b) attempted sale or;
(c) offering for sale;
(d) active sale;
“Charlestown Market” includes market street, the public market, the fish market and
the farmer’s co-operative;
“special events” include but is not limited to culturama, national sporting activities,
public holiday events or such other activities as prescribed by the Minister.
Prohibition of Vending on streets, sidewalks, public squares and other public area
in Charlestown.

### 3.

Subject to section 4, no person shall vend on or along the streets, sidewalks,
public squares and any other public area in Charlestown.
Vending only in Charlestown market and designated area.

### 4.

Vending in Charlestown shall take place only at the Charlestown market and
any other area from time to time designated by the Minister.

Penalty.

### 5.

Any person who acts in contravention of section 3 commits an offence and is
liable on summary conviction to a fine not exceeding one thousand dollars or to
imprisonment for a period not exceeding three (3) months.
Permits for Special Events.

### 6.

(1) Where there are special events being held in and around Charlestown
every person vending on or along the streets, sidewalks, public squares and any other
public area (save and except the Culturama Complex) must obtain a permit for each
special event from the Ministry of Finance.
(2) Any person who vends on or along the streets, sidewalks, public squares
and any other public area in Charlestown during any special event without a permit
commits an offence and is liable on summary conviction to a fine not exceeding one
thousand dollars or to imprisonment for a period not exceeding three (3) months.
Application for licence.

### 7.

(1) Every person desirous of occupying any stall, booth or other space in the
Charlestown market or any designated area unless exempted under this Ordinance or
its regulations shall make an application for a licence to the Ministry of Finance in
accordance with the provisions of the Licenses on Businesses and Occupation Act,
Cap. 18.20.
(2) Notwithstanding, subsection (1) where upon the commencement of this
ordinance a person vending on any street, sidewalk, public square or other public
place in Charlestown applied for and obtained a licence under the Licences on
Businesses and Occupation Act that person need not apply for a new licence to
occupy any stall, booth or other space in the Charlestown market or any designated
area, but is deemed to have applied for a licence under subsection (1) of this section.
Rental of booths, stalls and public areas.

### 8.

Booths, stalls or other areas provided shall be let to the vendors on terms and
conditions to be determined by the Ministry of Agriculture under the Markets and
Meats Ordinance and its regulations.
Regulations.

### 9.

(1) The Minister may after consulting with the Ministry of Agriculture (from
time to time) make regulations for the proper carrying out of the provisions of this
Ordinance from time to time.
(2) Regulations made under this section may prescribe penalties not exceeding
one thousand dollars or to imprisonment for a period not exceeding three (3) months
for the breach of any such regulation.

## SCHEDULE

EXEMPTED PERSONS
The following category of persons are exempted from applying for a licence:
1\. Vendors of locally grown or produced vegetables and provisions.
2\. Vendors of locally produced meats, poultry and eggs.
