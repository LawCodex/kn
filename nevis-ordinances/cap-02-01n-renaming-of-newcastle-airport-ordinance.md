## CHAPTER 2.01 (N)

RENAMING OF
NEWCASTLE AIRPORT ODINANCE
AN ORDINANCE TO MAKE PROVISION FOR THE RENAMING OF THE NEWCASTLE
AIRPORT AS THE VANCE W AMORY INTERNATIONAL AIRPORT.
Short title.

---

### 1.

This Ordinance may be cited as the Renaming of Newcastle Airport
Ordinance.
Substitution of name.

### 2.

As from the date of commencement of this Ordinance the Airport in Nevis
shall be known as the Vance W. Amory International Airport, in substitution for the
former name the Newcastle Airport, and shall be substituted in all Ordinances and in
any regulation, contract, deed, agreement, order or other instrument in force in Nevis.
