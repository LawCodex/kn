## CHAPTER 1.01 (N)

TRANSFER OF POWERS AND FUNCTIONS (LOCAL GOVERNMENT
ACT, 1967) ORDINANCE
AN ORDINANCE TO PROVIDE FOR THE TRANSFER OF STATUTORY POWERS AND
FUNCTIONS OF THE NEVIS LOCAL COUNCIL AS SPECIFIED IN PART III OF THE LOCAL
GOVERNMENT ACT; TO PROVIDE FOR THE VALIDATION OF ANY EXERCISE OF SUCH
POWERS; AND TO PROVIDE FOR RELATED OR INCIDENTAL MATTERS.
Short title.

---

### 1.

This Ordinance may be cited as the Transfer of Powers and Functions (Local
Government Act 1967) Ordinance.
Transfer of powers and functions.

### 2.

The Nevis Island Assembly shall exercise the powers and functions of the
Nevis Local Council as specified in Part II of the Local Government Act, 1967 in
addition to and not in derogation of the powers and functions already vested in it by
the Saint Christopher and Nevis Constitution.
Validation.

### 3.

All powers and functions as may have been exercised under the Local
Government Act, 1967 by the Nevis Island Assembly with effect from the date of
commencement of this Ordinance shall be deemed to have been valid and shall be
valid and effectual as if this Ordinance has been in force during this period.
