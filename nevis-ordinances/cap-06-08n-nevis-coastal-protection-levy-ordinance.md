## CHAPTER 6.08 (N)

NEVIS COASTAL PROTECTION LEVY ORDINANCE
AN ORDINANCE TO PROVIDE FOR THE PRESERVATION AND PROTECTION OF THE
COASTAL AREAS OF NEVIS AND FOR THE IMPOSITION OF AN IMPROVEMENT LEVY AND
FOR MATTERS CONNECTED THEREWITH OR INCIDENTAL THERETO.
Short title.

---

### 1.

This Ordinance may be cited as the Nevis Coastal Protection Levy Ordinance.

Interpretation.

### 2.

In this Ordinance—

“Administration” means the Nevis Island Administration;
“Coastal Areas” means all those areas, bordered by the sea;
“Hotel” includes inns, condominiums, villas and apartments offering rooms for rent;
“Minister” means the Member of the Administration having responsibility for
Environment.
Advisory Committee.

### 3.

(1) The Minister shall appoint an Advisory committee to advise him on the
matters specified in section 4.
(2) The provisions of the Schedule herein shall have effect with regard to the
constitution, practice and procedure of the Advisory Committee and the said
Schedule may, from time to time, be amended by the Minister by Order published in
the Gazette.
Functions of Advisory Committee.

### 4.

The Advisory Committee shall advise the Minister on—
(a) the appropriate measures for the protection of Coastal Areas against
the ravages of hurricanes and any other natural or man-made disasters;
(b) the protection and preservation of the marine environment of the
Coastal Areas;
(c) the causes, nature, extent and prevention of damage to the Coastal
Area.

Power to enter land and buildings.

### 5.

(1) Subject to the provisions of this Ordinance, it shall be lawful for members
of the Advisory Committee, in carrying out their duties, to have the right to enter at
all reasonable times any land or building adjacent to the Coastal Areas and to require
the person(s) in charge to provide them with all relevant information and to assist
them in their duties under this Ordinance.
(2) A member of the Advisory Committee shall not enter a dwelling house
except—
(a) with the consent of the occupant of the house; or
(b) under the authority of a warrant issued by a Magistrate.

Imposition of improvement tax.

### 6.

(1) A tax herein called the improvement tax at a rate to be determine by the
Minister after consulting with the Advisory Committee for each night or part of a
night shall be paid by every hotel guest who occupies a room or suite of a hotel that is
located adjacent to the Coastal Areas and which is named by the Minister by Notice
in the Gazette.
Use of Improvement tax.

### 7.

The tax shall be used exclusively by the Administration for the care, upkeep
and protection of the Coastal Areas.
Regulations.

### 8.

(1) The Minister may make regulations for the better carrying out of the
provisions of this Ordinance.
(2) Without prejudice to the generality of the foregoing, the Minister may
make regulations for all or any of the following—
(a) protecting the coastal and marine resources of the Coastal Areas;
(b) controlling and minimising damage to the Coastal Areas;
(c) the preservation or protection of any natural reef;
(d) the construction, preservation or protection of any artificial reef;
(e) to provide that a breach of any of the regulations shall be a criminal
offence, and for the imposition of a penalty therefor (not exceeding
twenty thousand dollars) for any such offence.
Provisions of this Ordinance to apply notwithstanding other laws.

### 9.

The provisions of this Ordinance shall have effect notwithstanding anything to
the contrary in any other law, rule or regulation.

## SCHEDULE

APPOINTMENT OF MEMBERS AND PROCEEDINGS
OF THE ADVISORY COMMITTEE
Constitution of Advisory Committee.

1\.
The Advisory Committee shall consist of five members who shall be appointed
by the Minister.
2\.
The Minister shall appoint one Member as Chairman of the Advisory
Committee.
Terms of appointment of Members.

3\.
A Member shall hold and vacate office in accordance with the terms of his
appointment.
4\.
A Member shall be appointed by instrument in writing for a period not
exceeding two years and upon expiry of such period shall be eligible for reappointment.
5\.
A Member may at any time resign his office by giving not less than one
month’s notice in writing to the Chairman except that in the case of the Chairman he
shall tender his resignation in writing to the Minister.
6\.

If the Minister is satisfied that a Member—
(a) has been absent from six consecutive meetings of the Advisory
Committee without the permission of the Committee;
(b) has become bankrupt;
(c) is incapacitated by physical or mental illness; or
(d) is unable or unfit to discharge the functions of a Member,

he may terminate his appointment.
Procedure at meetings.

7\.

At a meeting of the Advisory Committee—
(a) a simple majority of the Members shall form a quorum;
(b) the Chairman shall preside at all meetings and in his absence the
quorum of Members present shall elect one of their number to perform
the duties of Chairman;
(c) each Member shall have one vote on each matter for deliberation; and
(d) in the event of an equality in the votes the Chairman of the meeting
shall have a casting vote.

Transaction of business without meeting.

8\.
A resolution is a valid resolution of the Advisory Committee, even though it
was not passed at a meeting of the Committee, if—
(a) it is signed or assented to by a majority of Members; and
(b) proper notice of the proposed resolution was given to all Members.

Minutes of Meetings.

9\.
The Advisory Committee shall keep proper minutes of its proceedings
including minutes of any business transacted in accordance with paragraph 7.
